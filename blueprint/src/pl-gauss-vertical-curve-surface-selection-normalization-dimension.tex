\begin{theorem}[A genuine finite dominant integral map preserves the whole original dimension]
\label{thm:pl-gauss-original-finite-dominant-whole-dimension}
\uses{thm:pl-gauss-original-whole-dimension-equals-affine,lem:actual-injective-integral-ring-dimension-bound}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionNormalizationDimension.whole_dimension_eq_of_finite_dominant}
\leanok
Let $k$ be any original field, let the actual integral $Y$ have an
actual locally finite-type structure $p:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}k$,
and let $f:D\to Y$ be an actual finite dominant morphism with
integral $D$. Then $\dim D=\dim Y$ for the SAME whole original schemes.
No normality, flatness, prescribed affine map or dimension certificate
is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A finite map is closed. Its actual dominant image is dense, hence its
closed original range is the whole target. Derive actual surjectivity.
Choose a genuine nonempty affine neighbourhood $U$ of the original
generic point of $Y$. The original inverse image $V=f^{-1}(U)$ is
affine because $f$ is finite, and is NONEMPTY by the just-derived
actual surjectivity. The SAME actual field structure $f\gg p$ makes
$D$ locally finite type, internally.

Dominance over the reduced original $Y$ gives scheme-theoretic
dominance: its actual whole kernel has full support and is zero.
The original quasi-compact map's actual section map
$\Gamma(Y,U)\to\Gamma(D,V)$ is therefore injective. Finiteness makes
this SAME actual affine section homomorphism integral. Incomparability
bounds the actual target-ring prime-chain dimension by that of the
source ring. Conversely genuine lying-over and going-up lift every
actual source prime chain along this original injective integral map,
giving the reverse dimension inequality. Thus the two actual rings
have equal Krull dimensions, with no supplied coefficient algebra
identification. Apply the proved whole original scheme/affine
dimension equality to $Y,U$ and $D,V$. This proves the claimed equality
of the SAME whole scheme dimensions, without any dimension-one restriction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The SAME actual generic-field normalization preserves whole dimension]
\label{cor:pl-gauss-original-normalization-whole-dimension}
\uses{thm:pl-gauss-original-finite-dominant-whole-dimension,scheme:actual-integral-generic-normalization-input,thm:actual-finite-type-scheme-normalization-finite,scheme:actual-integral-normalization-surjective}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionNormalizationDimension.normalization_dimension_eq}
\leanok
For the same original integral locally field-finite-type $Y$, its
existing actual generic-field normalization $Y^\nu$ satisfies
$\dim Y^\nu=\dim Y$. This is the original normalization and original
projection, without a new normal model or finite-normalization premise.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The original generic-field normalization is integral. The existing
arbitrary-field finite-type normalization theorem makes its SAME
actual projection finite, internally. The actual lying-over theorem
for its original affine normalization charts makes that SAME projection
surjective. Its image is therefore dense, giving actual dominance.
Apply the preceding genuine finite-dominant whole dimension theorem
to this original projection and the original field structure. Every
finiteness, surjectivity and dominance fact is derived from actual
normalization geometry, not passed as a desired comparison certificate.
\end{proof}

This is a dimension-preserving prerequisite for normalization at EVERY
horizontal-cut induction stage. The original closed central curve
must still be lifted as a CLOSED integral curve through the finite
projection, with its actual finite dominant map, and relative projectivity,
normality, flatness and the stage triangles retained in the induction.
No surface, Gauss, residual positivity, nef or LocalPositivity conclusion
is claimed here.
