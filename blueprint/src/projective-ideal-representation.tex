\subsection{Actual ideal sheaves from original homogeneous ideals}
\label{subsec:projective-ideal-representation}

This concerns actual scheme IdealSheafData and genuine quotient kernel ideals.
The constructed homogeneous representative need not recover a previously chosen
graded ideal, and no analytic coherent sheaf comparison is asserted.

\begin{theorem}[Whole homogeneous form sections on actual coordinate overlaps]
\label{projective:actual-homogeneous-form-overlap-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeCoordinateIdeal,

EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeFormSection,
EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.homogeneousChartRatioHom_dehom_of_isHomogeneous,
EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeFormSection_germ,
EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeFormSection_X_unit_iff,

EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeFormSection_germ_ratio}
\leanok
\uses{projective:native-homogeneous-chart-coordinates,projective:native-scheme-coordinate-sections}
For an arbitrary commutative ring $R$, actual polynomial Proj coordinate
ideals and homogeneous form sections are defined through the true coordinate
section-ring isomorphisms. Every coordinate polynomial has a genuine
homogeneous numerator. On overlaps, original homogeneous section germs satisfy
$F/X_\ell^d=(X_j/X_\ell)^d(F/X_j^d)$ in the actual projective stalk, and
the basic open of $X_j/X_\ell$ is precisely $U_\ell\cap U_j$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the actual degree-zero homogeneous-localization fraction representation
to the genuine coordinate-ratio image of every polynomial, then dehomogenize
through the proved whole-ring inverse. The real section/germ isomorphism maps
these fractions into the actual degree-zero prime localization, whose embedding
into the ordinary prime localization is injective. Unit denominator cancellation
there proves the full overlap identity. Its unit criterion proves the actual
basic-open equality, without any reducedness or domain assumption on $R$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual ideal sheaves represented by original homogeneous ideals]
\label{projective:actual-homogeneous-ideal-sheaf-representation}

\leanok
\uses{projective:actual-homogeneous-form-overlap-sections,scheme:ideal-sheaf-data-stalk,graded:native-polynomial-quotient-coordinate-ideal}
Every actual IdealSheafData on polynomial Proj is the genuine quotient kernel
ideal sheaf of a constructed original homogeneous ideal, with equality on
entire original affine ideals and no radical replacement. No Noetherianity or
finite generation is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Intersect the pullbacks of the original whole coordinate ideals under each
actual dehomogenization map, and take its genuine homogeneous core. Its image
in each coordinate ideal is contained in the original ideal. Conversely lift
any element of one original chart ideal to a genuine homogeneous form $F$.
On every other coordinate chart, the true overlap germ identity puts the
corresponding form section in the localized original ideal. Actual affine
ideal membership detection by its stalk images and the genuine ideal-sheaf
localization law yield a power of the actual coordinate ratio that clears this
membership. There are finitely many original coordinates, so choose one common
exponent $N$. The actual homogeneous form $X_j^N F$ then belongs to every
original chart ideal, and hence to the constructed homogeneous core, while its
de-homogenization on chart $j$ is the original chosen element. Thus all whole
coordinate ideal images agree. The genuine homogeneous quotient kernel chart
comparison identifies these with the real quotient ideal-sheaf coordinate
ideals. The actual coordinate cover detects equality of whole IdealSheafData,
proving the statement while retaining all nilpotents and powers.
\end{proof}
