\subsection{Actual completed-disc restrictions and translations}
\label{subsec:gauss-disc-restrictions}

These are genuine Banach-algebra completion constructions underlying
the usual disc restrictions in Berkovich geometry (Berkovich~1990,
\S\S1.2, 2.1; BGR~1984, \S\S5.1.1--5.1.2).
No analytic sheaf or restricted-series presentation is assumed.

\begin{theorem}[Genuine whole translated closed discs]
\label{spectrum:actual-translated-closed-disc}
\uses{scheme:relative-affine-valued-point-homeomorphism,spectrum:pullback,spectrum:relative}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.CoordinateDisc,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.TranslatedDisc,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.discTranslationHomeomorph,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.discTranslationHomeomorph_val_apply,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.isClosedEmbedding_discTranslation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.discTranslationHomeomorph_native}
\leanok
For arbitrary commutative coefficient rings and any prescribed
coefficient seminorm, the ACTUAL polynomial automorphism
$T\mapsto T+a$ gives a genuine homeomorphism from the closed
radius-$s$ coordinate locus $|T|\leq s$ to the true translated
locus $|T-a|\leq s$. Every original polynomial value follows
its entire actual substitution. This is the restriction of a
proved whole polynomial-spectrum homeomorphism, and its native
affine square is the actual scheme automorphism's square.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the genuine polynomial algebra automorphism with inverse
$T\mapsto T-a$. Pullback on the whole seminorm spectrum is an
actual homeomorphism; coefficient algebra laws preserve every
original prescribed coefficient. Its value on $T-a$ is exactly
the original value on $T$, because the ENTIRE substituted
polynomial is $T$. Therefore this homeomorphism restricts to the
specified actual closed loci, including zero radius. The genuine
affine native naturality theorem identifies its whole point map
with the actual Spec of that same original algebra automorphism.
No topology, native bijection or generator-only seminorm equality
is declared as a certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine contractive translation and restriction of completed functions]
\label{analytic:actual-gauss-disc-restriction}
\uses{analytic:actual-polynomial-gauss-completion,spectrum:gauss-maximality}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.gauss_val_translate_le,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.polynomialTranslation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.nnnorm_polynomialTranslation_le,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.lipschitz_polynomialTranslation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.translationRingHom,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.translationRingHom_polynomialMap,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.translation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.continuous_translation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.translation_polynomialMap,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.nnnorm_translation_le}
\leanok
Let $K$ be an ultrametric normed field, $r,s>0$, $s\leq r$, and
$\|a\|\leq r$. There is an ACTUAL contractive $K$-algebra map
from the radius-$r$ Gauss completion to the radius-$s$ completion,
extending the entire original polynomial substitution
$p(T)\mapsto p(T+a)$. It preserves every original coefficient.
For $a=0$ this is the actual completed-function restriction map.
No field completeness or injectivity of function restriction is
asserted or assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pull the original radius-$s$ Gauss point back along the genuine
whole polynomial translation. Its coefficient values are the
original $K$ norm, and its value on $T$ is at most
$\max(s,\|a\|)\leq r$. The actual whole polynomial Gauss
maximality theorem therefore bounds EVERY substituted polynomial's
radius-$s$ norm by its original radius-$r$ norm. Compose this
true polynomial map with the actual target polynomial embedding
into its complete Gauss algebra. On the genuine radius-$r$
normed polynomial copy it is contractive, hence continuous.
Its actual ring completion extension constructs the entire map.
Completion density preserves the bound on EVERY completion
element. The actual polynomial coefficient law proves that the
whole original $K$ coefficient action is preserved, giving the
claimed genuine algebra map.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole composition and identity of actual completed restrictions]
\label{analytic:actual-gauss-disc-restriction-composition}
\uses{analytic:actual-gauss-disc-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.polynomialTranslate_comp,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.denseRange_polynomialMap,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.center_add_bound,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.translation_comp,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.translation_zero}
\leanok
For $t\leq s\leq r$ positive radii and centers satisfying
$\|a\|\leq r$, $\|b\|\leq s$, the ACTUAL completed
translation by $a$ followed by translation by $b$ is precisely
translation by $a+b$ from radius $r$ to radius $t$.
Zero translation on a fixed radius is the whole identity map.
These are equalities on EVERY completed function.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The ultrametric inequality derives the required genuine combined
center bound $\|a+b\|\leq\max(\|a\|,\|b\|)\leq r$.
On every original polynomial, actual substitution composition
sends $p(T)$ to $p((T+b)+a)=p(T+(a+b))$, while zero
translation fixes the entire polynomial. These are equalities
of true whole polynomial homomorphisms. The original polynomial
image is genuinely dense in its norm completion, and both actual
ring maps are continuous. Equality on ALL those original
polynomials therefore proves equality of the whole completion
maps. This does not infer injectivity of restriction to a smaller
disc from polynomial injectivity or selected coordinate values.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual completed-function evaluation naturality on genuine subdiscs]
\label{analytic:actual-gauss-subdisc-evaluation-naturality}
\uses{spectrum:actual-translated-closed-disc,analytic:actual-gauss-disc-restriction-composition,analytic:actual-gauss-disc-function-values,spectrum:completed-residue-field-pullback}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.pointTranslation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.pointTranslation_val_apply,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.isClosedEmbedding_pointTranslation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.evaluationToH_translation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.value_translation}
\leanok
The corresponding genuine point map sends every actual
radius-$s$ point $x$ to its translated radius-$r$ point $y$.
It preserves the entire polynomial seminorm and is a continuous
closed embedding onto the actual translated subdisc. For EVERY
completed radius-$r$ function, its value after actual function
restriction at $x$ equals its original value at $y$.
More strongly, actual evaluations into the true original
completed residue fields commute with their genuine field map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first theorem supplies the actual translated point and whole
polynomial value formula. Its original coordinate value is at
most $\max(s,\|a\|)\leq r$, so it lies in the true target
disc. The genuine whole polynomial automorphism proves point
injectivity. The translated locus is closed because it is the
inverse image of a closed interval under a continuous whole
polynomial evaluation. Its inclusion composed with the actual
translation homeomorphism is therefore a closed embedding.
Cancelling the embedding of the radius-$r$ disc into the full
relative spectrum proves the asserted closed embedding into
that disc, even at zero point radius. The native affine square retains this SAME
original point map and every polynomial value.

The actual point identity $y=\operatorname{comap}(T\mapsto T+a)(x)$
gives a genuine isometric map $\mathcal H(y)\to\mathcal H(x)$.
Compare the two ACTUAL ring maps from the radius-$r$ completion
into this true $\mathcal H(x)$: function restriction followed
by original-$x$ evaluation, and original-$y$ evaluation followed
by this completed residue-field map. Both are continuous.
On EVERY original polynomial, actual completed-function
restriction is genuine polynomial substitution and genuine
completed-field functoriality preserves that entire original
value. Therefore the two whole ring maps agree on the true dense
polynomial image, hence on EVERY completion element. Taking
norms, using the genuine field map's isometry, and the previously
proved spectral-value/original-evaluation formula proves the
asserted whole completed-function value naturality. No residue
fields are identified by fiat, and no analytic sheaf or harmonic
structure is asserted.
\end{proof}
