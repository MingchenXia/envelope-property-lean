\section{Actual formal functions for the whole original projective section ring}

\begin{theorem}[The canonical original infinitesimal section map]
\label{thm:fibre-projective-original-section-map}
\uses{thm:fibre-global-kernel-quotient-reconstruction,lem:fc-projective-source-separated,lem:fc-projective-finite-ordered-affine-cover}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.sectionMap,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.sectionMap_apply,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.sectionMap_injective_of_cover,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.sectionMap_injective}
\leanok
The original whole global-section ring maps to the ring of compatible
sections of the literal positive infinitesimal models by every actual
projection. Over the actual complete Noetherian base, for a genuine
projective factorization, this map is injective.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual projection and transition composition law proves every
compatibility equation. Ring operations commute with every original
projection. If two sections have the same coordinates, their difference
belongs to every original global projection kernel. Hence they have the
same entire original kernel quotient in every order. The genuine stable
kernel-filtration reconstruction equivalence then identifies the two
original sections.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The entire original coefficient triangle]
\label{lem:fibre-projective-formal-coefficient}
\uses{thm:fibre-projective-original-section-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.projection_coefficient_quotient,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.sectionMap_coefficient}
\leanok
For each order, restriction of an original coefficient to the original
infinitesimal model is its quotient coefficient followed by that model's
structure coefficient. Consequently the canonical section map sends an
original coefficient to its entire original compatible coefficient family.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The two structure morphisms of the defining pullback have the same
composite to the original affine base. Apply global sections and the
canonical affine global-section identification. This gives the equality
of the two whole coefficient homomorphisms at every order. Equality at
every positive coordinate then gives the asserted equality in the ring
of compatible sections.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole original projective formal functions]
\label{thm:fibre-projective-formal-sections}
\uses{thm:fibre-projective-original-section-map,thm:fibre-global-kernel-quotient-reconstruction,lem:fc-infinitesimal-compatible-section-boundary,cor:fc-formal-functions-positive-compatible-zero}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.compatible_coordinate_lifts,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.sectionMap_surjective_of_cover,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.sectionMap_surjective,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.formalEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.formalEquiv_toRingHom,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.formalEquiv_apply,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.formalEquiv_coefficient,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.formalEquiv_symm_apply}
\leanok
For an actual projective factorization over an $I$-adically complete
Noetherian coefficient ring, the same original global-section ring is
canonically isomorphic to the whole ring of compatible sections of its
actual positive infinitesimal models. There is no extra lifting,
cohomology, reducedness or normality assumption.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the original projective coordinate affine cover, deriving
separatedness and its finite affine cover internally. A compatible
family of original infinitesimal sections has genuine connecting
classes in the first cohomology of the original ideal powers. Actual
boundary naturality makes these classes compatible under the original
power inclusions. The proved coherent-to-literal Rees comparisons
preserve these precise maps. Genuine finite projective Rees cohomology
and separation over the actual complete Noetherian base force this
entire compatible obstruction family to vanish. Exactness therefore
lifts every given coordinate to an original whole global section.

The classes of these lifts in the entire original projection-kernel
quotients are independent of the lifts and form a compatible family,
because the projections commute with the original transitions. The
genuine kernel-filtration reconstruction gives one original global
section with every prescribed quotient class, hence every prescribed
original infinitesimal section. This proves surjectivity of the actual
canonical map. Its ring operations were already proved, so its
bijectivity gives the asserted whole original ring isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal closed-fibre idempotent restriction]
\label{thm:fibre-projective-formal-idempotents}
\uses{thm:fibre-projective-formal-sections,scheme:actual-original-infinitesimal-idempotent-family}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.idempotentRestrictionEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.FibreProjectiveFormalSections.idempotentRestrictionEquiv_apply}
\leanok
Under the same actual complete Noetherian projective hypotheses,
restriction of original whole global sections to the actual order-one
model bijects idempotents. This refers to the entire original rings,
including their nilpotents.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The whole formal-functions ring isomorphism induces a bijection on
idempotents because a ring homomorphism preserves $e^2=e$, and its
inverse preserves the same equation. Actual unique idempotent lifting
across every nilpotent infinitesimal transition identifies idempotents
of the entire compatible-section ring with idempotents on the order-one
model. Their composition is the original order-one section restriction:
the formal-functions forward map has every coordinate equal to the
original section pullback, and the second bijection evaluates its
zero-index coordinate. This proves the asserted bijection for that
literal restriction.
\end{proof}
