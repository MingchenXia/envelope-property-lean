\section{From actual geometric ampleness to original Kodaira vanishing}

The positivity target in this chapter is the original Kodaira theorem:
for a smooth projective $n$-fold over a characteristic-zero field and an
actual ample line bundle $L$, the whole original
$H^q(Y,\omega_Y\otimes L)$ vanishes for $q>0$. Ampleness is geometric:
a positive power is the pullback of the actual $\mathcal O(1)$ under an
actual closed projective embedding. Following \cite{DI87}, Theorem~1.2, Theorem~2.1, Corollary~2.8,
Lemma~2.9 and Corollary~2.11, the route uses algebraic de Rham
complexes, the characteristic-$p$ Cartier isomorphism, a derived splitting
from a genuine lift modulo $p^2$, and spreading out. The ordinary Serre
and rounded-canonical objects are preceding prerequisites. Logarithmic
and relative Kawamata--Viehweg vanishing, needed for local and Nadel
vanishing, require a further logarithmic/cyclic-cover argument beyond
this Kodaira target.

\begin{lemma}[Actual top Cartier exactness on a finite $p$-basis]
\label{lem:vanishing-cartier-top-exactness}
\leanok
Let $A$ be an actual commutative ring of prime characteristic $p$ with an
actual finite $p$-basis $y_i$. Write $\partial_i$ for its actual coordinate
derivations and $C$ for its actual top Cartier coefficient operator. Then
\[
 C(f)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 f=\sum_i\partial_i(g_i)\ \text{for some actual }g_i\in A.
\]
For the actual polynomial ring over a perfect field this applies to its
original polynomial Cartier trace and its original standard partial
derivatives, with the coordinate $p$-basis derived internally.
Moreover $C$ is surjective, and every original $f$ decomposes as
\[
 f=C(f)^p\prod_i y_i^{p-1}+\sum_i\partial_i(g_i).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand the original $f$ in its unique finite $p$-basis expansion
$f=\sum_a c_a^py^a$, with $0\le a_i<p$. The coefficient $c_{(p-1,\ldots,p-1)}$
is exactly $C(f)$. For every other $a$, choose an $i$ with $a_i<p-1$.
The integer $a_i+1$ is a unit in $A$, since it is prime to $p$. Its inverse
has zero derivative: differentiate its product with $a_i+1$, use that
integer derivatives vanish, and cancel this unit. Thus
\[
 \partial_i\bigl((a_i+1)^{-1}c_a^py^{a+e_i}\bigr)=c_a^py^a.
\]
Add these finitely many antiderivatives coordinatewise to obtain the
stated whole decomposition. Every coordinate derivative has top Cartier
coefficient zero, because differentiation cannot produce a digit $p-1$
in that coordinate. Hence the decomposition gives both directions of
the kernel equality. Finally
$C(g^p\prod_i y_i^{p-1})=g$ for every actual $g$, proving surjectivity.
The argument includes an empty coordinate family. On the actual
polynomial ring its original variables form a derived $p$-basis. The
standard partial derivative and its $p$-basis coordinate derivative agree
on every variable; the uniqueness of derivations on a $p$-basis proves
equality of the whole actual derivatives. This gives the asserted
standard-polynomial specialization.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The whole algebraic Cartier complex on actual smooth charts]
\label{lem:vanishing-cartier-smooth-complex}
\uses{lem:vanishing-cartier-top-exactness}
\notready
For a smooth scheme over a perfect field of characteristic $p$, the actual
cohomology sheaves of its original Frobenius-pushed de Rham complex are
canonically its original exterior cotangent sheaves on the relative
Frobenius twist.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On polynomial coordinates the actual de Rham complex decomposes by
exponents modulo $p$. In one coordinate its differential sends
$c^py^a$ to $a c^py^{a-1}dy$. For $1\le a<p$ this is an isomorphism of the
two indicated Frobenius coefficient summands, with inverse multiplication
by $a^{-1}y$ on their monomials. The remaining degree-zero summand is
$A^p$ and the remaining degree-one summand is
$A^p y^{p-1}dy$. Tensor the coordinate complexes. Every tensor summand
containing one of those two-term isomorphisms has the explicit contraction
obtained by applying its inverse in the first such coordinate, with the
usual exterior sign. The surviving summands are indexed by subsets of
the coordinates and are exactly the original exterior cotangent basis
classes. This proves the entire local Cartier isomorphism in all degrees,
including the top-degree equality of the preceding lemma.

The maps are intrinsic: inverse Cartier sends $df$ to the cohomology class
of $f^{p-1}df$, and sends exterior products to their products. The product
rule and characteristic-$p$ binomial formula show the required additive
and Leibniz relations modulo exact forms. Thus the polynomial-coordinate
maps agree on overlaps. Smooth actual schemes have etale coordinate
neighbourhoods, and etale base change preserves the original cotangent
modules and these Frobenius complexes. The local maps therefore glue to
the stated actual cohomology-sheaf isomorphisms.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Derived splitting from an actual lift modulo $p^2$]
\label{lem:vanishing-deligne-illusie-splitting}
\uses{lem:vanishing-cartier-smooth-complex}
\notready
Let $Y$ be smooth over a perfect field of characteristic $p$, of dimension
$n<p$, and suppose its actual smooth scheme lifts over the length-two
Witt ring. Then its whole original Frobenius-pushed de Rham complex is
isomorphic in the derived category to
$\bigoplus_{i=0}^n\Omega^i_{Y^{(p)}}[-i]$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual affine cover of the lift on which the relative Frobenius
has lifts. On such an open, its differential is divisible by $p$.
Division by $p$ followed by reduction modulo $p$ gives a map of original
one-forms to closed one-forms inducing the inverse Cartier map. Two
Frobenius lifts differ by $p$ times a derivation; their divided
differentials differ by the differential of this derivation. These
actual homotopies satisfy the Cech cocycle relation on triple overlaps,
since differences of the original lifts add. Consequently the local
maps and their homotopies define a morphism
$\Omega^1[-1]\to F_*\Omega^\bullet$ in the original derived category.
Take its exterior powers and divide by $i!$ to antisymmetrize. These
integers are invertible because $i\le n<p$. Together with the actual
Frobenius map in degree zero, their sum gives the stated morphism from
the direct sum. On every actual affine chart its cohomology maps are
exactly the Cartier maps proved above. It is therefore a quasi-isomorphism
of the whole original complexes, which gives the required derived
isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Original Serre vanishing for a geometrically ample line]
\label{lem:vanishing-original-ample-serre}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeAmpleCoherentSerreVanishing.exists_sheafCohomology_bound}
\leanok
\uses{thm:coherent-projective-serre-vanishing}
For an actual projective scheme over a field, an original coherent
module $E$ and an actual geometrically ample line $L$, there is $N$ such
that the WHOLE original $H^q(Y,E\otimes L^m)$ is zero for all $m\ge N$
and all $q>0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual closed projective embedding $i:Y\hookrightarrow
\mathbb P^s$ and a positive integer $r$ with the actual isomorphism
$L^r\simeq i^*\mathcal O(1)$. For each of the finitely many residues
$0\le a<r$, the SAME original $E\otimes L^a$ has coherent closed
pushforward. The actual projection formula for the original invertible
$\mathcal O(d)$ follows on every standard affine chart from its genuine
local frame; the actual section maps and frame transitions glue the
formula. It identifies the pushforward of
$E\otimes L^{a+rd}$ with that coherent pushforward tensored by the
original $\mathcal O(d)$. Closed pushforward retains the WHOLE original
cohomology: use the original standard affine cover and its inverse
images, the actual section equality for pushforward, and genuine
quasicoherent affine-intersection acyclicity. Thus the actual Cech
complexes compute the SAME original sheaf cohomology on both schemes.
Apply the proved original polynomial-projective Serre theorem to each
of these finitely many coherent pushforwards. Taking their maximum
bound and using Euclidean division $m=a+rd$ proves the assertion for
EVERY sufficiently large actual power of the original $L$. The
embedding, projection formula, pushforward/cohomology comparison and
ordinary ampleness transport are all derived from the original
geometric data in this Serre argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Positive twisted Cartier comparison by zero-group induction]
\label{lem:vanishing-charp-positive-ample}
\uses{lem:vanishing-deligne-illusie-splitting,lem:vanishing-original-ample-serre}
\notready
Under the actual lift and dimension hypotheses above, every original
actual ample line $L$ satisfies
$H^j(Y,\Omega_Y^i\otimes L)=0$ for $0\le i\le n$ and $i+j>n$.
No Serre duality or cohomology-dimension finiteness is a premise.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $F:Y\to Y^{(p)}$ for the ACTUAL relative Frobenius, and $L'$ for
the actual base change of the SAME original line to its field Frobenius
twist. Derive the actual isomorphism $F^*L'\simeq L^p$ from the original
line transition functions. The actual projection formula identifies
$F_*\Omega_Y^\bullet\otimes L'$ with the Frobenius pushforward of the
original de Rham complex twisted by $F^*L'$. This latter complex uses
its genuine Cartier connection: the actual local frames pulled back by
Frobenius are horizontal, since their transition functions are original
$p$th powers with zero derivative. It is NOT an arbitrary tensoring of
an $\mathcal O_Y$-linear de Rham differential. Its actual terms are
precisely the original $\Omega_Y^i\otimes L^p$.

Fix $t>n$. By original ample Serre vanishing, there is an exponent $e$
such that EVERY group
$H^{t-i}(Y,\Omega_Y^i\otimes L^{p^e})$, $0\le i\le n$, is zero.
Only finitely many actual coherent exterior modules occur, all the
indices $t-i$ are positive, and $p^e$ tends to infinity. Suppose these
zero-group assertions hold at exponent $a+1$. The bounded original
hypercohomology spectral sequence for the ACTUAL connection complex
with terms $\Omega_Y^i\otimes L^{p^{a+1}}$ has zero entries on the
ENTIRE total-degree-$t$ diagonal. Every later entry on that diagonal
is a subquotient of zero. Its actual finite filtration therefore gives
zero total-degree-$t$ hypercohomology.

Tensor the ACTUAL full Deligne--Illusie splitting by $(L')^{p^a}$ and
use that genuine projection/connection comparison. It identifies this
zero group with the finite direct sum
\[
 \bigoplus_{i=0}^n
 H^{t-i}(Y^{(p)},\Omega_{Y^{(p)}}^i\otimes (L')^{p^a}).
\]
Each summand is zero. Base change by the ACTUAL perfect-field
Frobenius automorphism identifies its underlying group with the
corresponding original group on $Y$, with the original field action
semilinearly changed. This is a derived comparison to construct, not
a replacement of the original $Y$ or $L$. Faithfulness of this field
base change gives the zero-group assertion at exponent $a$. Descend
the finitely many exponents from $e$ to zero. For $t=i+j>n$ this is
exactly the asserted WHOLE original sheaf-cohomology vanishing.
No dimension inequality, finite-dimensionality, negative ample power
or Serre duality is used. Full Cartier/DI, the actual Frobenius twist,
connection and projection comparisons, bounded hypercohomology and
original ample Serre transport all remain genuine mathematical
obligations before this node can become ready.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Spreading the actual smooth projective variety and ample line]
\label{lem:vanishing-original-spreading}
\notready
A smooth projective variety and an actual ample line bundle over a
characteristic-zero field descend to a smooth projective family over a
nonempty smooth integral scheme of finite type over $\mathbb Z$. After
shrinking, the actual canonical and exterior cotangent sheaves and their
cohomology have the original fibre identifications. There is a closed
fibre of characteristic $p>n$ admitting an actual smooth lift modulo
$p^2$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the finitely many coefficients in a projective presentation of the
original variety, its actual smooth affine charts, the transition maps
of $L$ and its inverse, and an actual embedding identifying $L^r$ with
$\mathcal O(1)$. The finitely many polynomial identities among these data
hold over a finitely generated integral $\mathbb Z$-subalgebra after
inverting finitely many coefficients. Smoothness is open, so shrink to a
smooth base where the same charts and embeddings give the asserted
family and actual relatively ample line. The finite projective Cech
complexes give coherent cohomology semicontinuity and base change on a
further open. Infinitely many rational primes occur on this nonempty
finite-type integral characteristic-zero base; remove the finitely many
primes $p\le n$ and take a closed point. Its finite residue field is
perfect. Smoothness of the base over $\mathbb Z$ lifts its residue-field
point over the length-two Witt ring by the infinitesimal lifting property.
Pull back the original smooth projective family along this lift. This is
the required actual lift of the chosen original closed fibre.
\end{proof}

The global Witt decomposition is supplied by
Corollary~\ref{cor:di-actual-witt-full-decomposition}. The original arithmetic
model, canonical comparison and specialization in
Theorem~\ref{thm:kn-original-characteristic-zero-kodaira} therefore give
the original characteristic-zero vanishing without a decomposition premise.

\begin{theorem}[Original Kodaira vanishing from geometric positivity]
\label{thm:vanishing-original-kodaira}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.KodairaCharacteristicZero.kodaira}
\uses{thm:kn-original-characteristic-zero-kodaira}
\leanok
For an actual smooth projective $n$-fold over a characteristic-zero field
and an actual ample line bundle $L$, the whole original
$H^q(Y,\omega_Y\otimes L)$ is zero for every $q>0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual positive-power projective embedding expressing
ampleness of $L$. Apply the original characteristic-zero Kodaira theorem
to this same scheme, structural morphism, smooth dimension and line.
Its canonical module is the original top exterior cotangent sheaf.
For $q>0$, write $q=r+1$ and apply that theorem at $r$. The resulting
whole native sheaf cohomology group is the group displayed here.
\end{proof}
