\section{Actual tensor untwisting and the original residue Frobenius action}
\label{sec:witt-two-residue-untwist}

Let $W=W_2(k)$, let $\sigma:W\simeq W'$ be the actual Witt-Frobenius
coefficient equivalence into the copied ring, and let $A$ be any original
$W$-algebra. Retain the actual twisted tensor $T=W'\otimes_W A$, its
native residue presentation $C=k\otimes_W T$, and $B=k\otimes_W A$.

\begin{theorem}[The actual twisted tensor has its genuine original algebra untwisting]
\label{thm:witt-two-source-native-untwist}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoResidueUntwist.baseAlgEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoResidueUntwist.sourceUntwist,EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoResidueUntwist.sourceUntwist_tmul,EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoResidueUntwist.sourceUntwist_one_tmul}
The actual coefficient equivalence gives a genuine $W$-algebra equivalence
$u:T\simeq A$. On every original pure tensor,
$u(w\otimes a)=\sigma^{-1}(w)a$, and $u(1\otimes a)=a$.
No smoothness or freeness hypothesis on $A$ is required.
\uses{thm:witt-two-frobenius-equivalence}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual structural map $W\to W'$ is the already proved genuine
Witt coefficient ring equivalence. It is therefore a $W$-algebra
equivalence, whose inverse is also an actual $W$-algebra map.
Apply native tensor congruence to this inverse on the first original
factor and the identity on the second. This gives an actual equivalence
$W'\otimes_W A\simeq W\otimes_W A$. Compose with the genuine native
left-unit algebra equivalence $W\otimes_W A\simeq A$.
The original pure-tensor formulas of those two maps give the asserted
value and its coefficient-one specialization, since the actual inverse
coefficient equivalence sends one to one.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine residue presentation is the original residue algebra]
\label{thm:witt-two-native-residue-untwist}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoResidueUntwist.residueUntwist,EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoResidueUntwist.residueUntwist_tmul,EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoResidueUntwist.residueUntwist_one_coordinate}
The same original tensor maps produce a genuine $k$-algebra equivalence
$\bar u:C\simeq B$, sending every $\lambda\otimes t$ to
$\lambda\otimes u(t)$. In particular it retains every original
coefficient-one coordinate $1\otimes(1\otimes a)\mapsto1\otimes a$.
\uses{thm:witt-two-source-native-untwist}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Tensor the actual original $W$-algebra equivalence $u$ with the identity
on the same residue field $k$. Native tensor congruence with that fixed
left factor gives an actual $k$-algebra equivalence between the same
two original residue tensors. Its original pure-tensor value formula
is the stated formula. The preceding coefficient-one source identity
gives the final coordinate value without any identification premise.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same residue relative Frobenius has the genuine original pth-power action]
\label{thm:witt-two-residue-frobenius-untwist}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoResidueUntwist.residueFrobenius_untwist}
For every original $t\in T$, its actual residue relative Frobenius is
exactly the $p$th power of its same untwisted original residue:
\[
 r(t)=\bigl(1\otimes u(t)\bigr)^p.
\]
This is equality of the actual original ring-map values, including zero
algebras, not a supplied Frobenius comparison.
\uses{thm:witt-two-native-residue-untwist,lem:witt-two-frobenius-reduction,thm:witt-two-residue-relative-frobenius}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual relative Frobenius and the $p$th-power map of the untwisted
residue are genuine original ring maps. Their values at zero agree;
additivity reduces equality for sums to equality for their summands.
It remains to check each original pure tensor $w\otimes a$.
The actual relative Frobenius value is
$\rho(w)(1\otimes a)^p$. The untwist value before taking a $p$th power
is $\rho(\sigma^{-1}w)(1\otimes a)$. The proved original Witt/residue
Frobenius identity gives
$\rho(\sigma^{-1}w)^p=\rho(w)$, by applying it to that actual inverse
coefficient and the genuine inverse identity. Multiplicativity of the
original residue map and of the genuine Frobenius therefore gives
exactly the same pure-tensor value. Original tensor additive generation
proves equality for every $t$.
\end{proof}

This is the actual original algebra and coefficient transport needed to
compare the constructed divided maps with native ordinary Frobenius modules.
Native Kähler/exterior transport and scheme-relative twisting/gluing remain
subsequent constructions.
