\section{Actual original coproduct generating families}

\begin{lemma}[The original summand generators generate the whole coproduct]
\label{lem:scheme-original-coproduct-generating-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleGeneratingCoproduct.evaluation,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleGeneratingCoproduct.evaluation_epi,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleGeneratingCoproduct.generatingSections,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleGeneratingCoproduct.generatingSections_isFiniteType}
\leanok
For any family of original scheme modules with original generating families,
the union of these families defines a genuine free evaluation epimorphism
onto the same whole original coproduct. A finite family of finite generating
families gives an actual finite generating family of that coproduct.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the genuine free-Hom equivalence on the images of all original summand
generators under the actual coproduct inclusions. On the free module of each
summand's generators, this actual evaluation factors as its original
surjective evaluation followed by the original summand inclusion. If two
maps out of the whole coproduct agree after the evaluation, precomposition
with these factors and cancellation of each original evaluation show that
they agree on every original summand. Coproduct extensionality gives equality,
which proves epimorphism. The actual generator index is the dependent sum of
the original indices, hence finite under the stated finite hypotheses.
\end{proof}
