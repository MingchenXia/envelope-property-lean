Let $K$ be an ultrametric normed field and $r>0$ a real radius.
Give $K[T]$ its ACTUAL weighted Gauss absolute value
\[
 \left\|\sum_i a_iT^i\right\|_r=\max_i\|a_i\|r^i.
\]
The genuine separated norm completion of this ring is the
weighted one-variable Gauss algebra. It is an actual complete
commutative normed ring, with a genuine original coefficient and
polynomial map. Every original polynomial retains its entire
weighted Gauss norm. Completeness of $K$ is not required to construct
this ring; when $K$ is complete, it is the usual closed-disc
Banach algebra over $K$. The radius-zero case is excluded here
because the polynomial seminorm would not be a norm. We define
this algebra by norm completion; identification with a separately
constructed restricted formal-power-series presentation is not
asserted here.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The proved Gauss valuation is multiplicative for the full finite
coefficient maximum. Positive radius and faithful coefficient norm
imply that zero Gauss value forces every polynomial coefficient
to vanish; hence it is a genuine absolute value. Use the actual
normed copy of the polynomial ring specified by this absolute
value, then its actual uniform ring completion. The normed ring
and complete-space structures are the genuine completion
structures. Its original polynomial embedding preserves the whole
norm by the real completion norm law, and its coefficient embedding
is isometric since the constant-polynomial Gauss norm is the
original coefficient norm. The ultrametric inequality extends
from all original polynomial pairs by actual completion density.
No formal-power-series evaluation theorem with a linear-topology
hypothesis on the valued field is used.
\end{proof}

The contractive spectrum of this ACTUAL completion, with every
original $K$ coefficient prescribed its true norm, is genuinely
homeomorphic to the closed radius-$r$ locus $|T|\leq r$ of the
existing affine prescribed-base spectrum of $K[T]$. Restriction
is along the original polynomial map and preserves EVERY original
polynomial value. Every disc point extends uniquely to a
contractive multiplicative ultrametric seminorm on the completion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A genuine completion point restricts to the actual weighted
normed polynomial copy. Its coefficient values stay prescribed,
and its true contractivity gives $|T|\leq\|T\|_r=r$.
Conversely, a real affine point with these coefficient values
and $|T|\leq r$ satisfies the bound on EVERY original polynomial:
write the polynomial as its actual finite coefficient sum,
apply the true ultrametric inequality, and bound each monomial
by $\|a_i\|r^i$. The original weighted normed-copy ring
equivalence therefore identifies its entire polynomial seminorm
with a genuine contractive point, retaining every scalar value.
Both constructions are true inverse maps on all polynomial
elements. Their evaluation-coordinate descriptions give continuous
maps, hence a genuine homeomorphism with the norm-bounded original
polynomial spectrum. Compose this with the actual completion
homeomorphism of the preceding theorem. Its genuine extension
through the true completed residue field supplies existence and
uniqueness on EVERY completion element, rather than assigning
values only to original coordinates. Nonemptiness follows from
the actual original Gauss point, including trivial field norms.
\end{proof}

For every ACTUAL element of the Gauss completion, its value at
every closed-disc point is the unique completed seminorm value.
Its nonnegative absolute value and extended logarithm are
continuous on the whole genuine disc. The ACTUAL original Gauss
point realizes the norm of every completion element and dominates
its value at every other actual disc point. Original polynomials
recover their entire original seminorm functions, including
zeros and logarithmic value $-\infty$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First construct an actual completion evaluation into the true
$\mathcal H(x)$ of the ORIGINAL polynomial disc point: its original
polynomial evaluator, composed with the genuine normed-copy ring
equivalence, is contractive on every polynomial by the whole disc
bound. Its actual ring completion extension preserves every original
polynomial and remains contractive on every completion element.
Actual density gives uniqueness among continuous ring maps with
these whole original polynomial values. The true completed residue
field is not identified definitionally with that of the normed
polynomial copy. Instead, the two continuous seminorm functions
agree on ALL original polynomials, so actual completion density
proves equality on EVERY completion element. Thus the spectral
value is exactly the norm of this true original-$\mathcal H(x)$
evaluation.

Evaluate the actual completed spectrum point furnished by the
inverse of the just-proved homeomorphism on the given completion
element. Every such spectrum evaluation is genuinely continuous
for the original evaluation topology. Compose with the real
homeomorphism inverse. Continuity of the extended nonnegative
logarithm gives the logarithmic assertion, including zero.
The whole polynomial restriction identity gives the original
polynomial formula. The real original Gauss point is a witness in
the disc. Its value on every polynomial is the whole weighted
Gauss norm. Density of the entire original polynomial image and
continuity of both completed seminorm and actual norm extend that
equality to EVERY completion element. Contractivity of every other
completion point then makes this same Gauss point dominate its
value on each actual completed function. These are genuine analytic disc functions
constructed from norm convergence; no analytic structure sheaf,
rational-domain gluing, boundary, skeleton, harmonic comparison,
subharmonicity or maximum-principle structure has yet been asserted.
Those remain distinct producers required for GR25 curve theory.
\end{proof}
