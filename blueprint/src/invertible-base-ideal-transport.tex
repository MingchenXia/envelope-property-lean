\section{Transport and grading of the whole original base ideals}

\begin{lemma}[Whole coefficient transport under an original line isomorphism]
\label{lem:actual-base-ideal-iso-transport}
For any original isomorphism $e:L\simeq M$ of invertible sheaves on any
scheme, $\mathfrak b(L)=\mathfrak b(M)$. Evaluation against the original
contravariant dual map is the evaluation of the same original functional
on the original image section, on every open set.
\uses{prop:actual-line-global-section-evaluation,prop:actual-invertible-sheaf-base-ideal-affine}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.InvertibleBaseIdealTransport.evaluation_dualMap,EnvelopeProperty.InvertibleBaseIdealTransport.sectionEvaluation_map,EnvelopeProperty.InvertibleBaseIdealTransport.baseIdeal_eq_of_iso}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A local functional on $M$ precomposes with the restriction of $e$.
Evaluating at a section of $L$ therefore evaluates the original functional
at its image under $e$. This is the literal definition of the dual map,
and naturality identifies the image of a restricted global section with
the restriction of its original global image. To compare the two whole
base ideals, transport each original global section along $e$ and each
local functional along its inverse. Their evaluation coefficients agree.
Since every affine coefficient ideal is the sum over all original global
sections, this gives both inclusions on every affine open.
\end{proof}
