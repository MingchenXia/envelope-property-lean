\section{Actual chosen Witt restrictions have identity and composition coherence}
\label{sec:witt-two-etale-restriction-coherence}

Retain the original perfect characteristic-$p$ field, the actual
Witt algebras, and actual etale Witt algebra maps. A prescribed
source lift has its actual original relative Frobenius reduction.
Every target at which a lift is produced is smooth over the
original length-two Witt ring.

\begin{definition}[The actual restricted lift]
\label{def:witt-two-chosen-etale-restricted-lift}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleRestrictionCoherence.restrictedLift, EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleRestrictionCoherence.restrictedLift_reduction, EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleRestrictionCoherence.restrictedLift_commutes}
\leanok
The restricted lift along an actual etale map is the actual
copied-Witt-base algebra homomorphism produced by the preceding
existence theorem. Its entire reduction is the original target
relative Frobenius, and its composite with the SAME twisted
restriction is the actual coefficient map composed with the
prescribed source lift.
\uses{thm:witt-two-etale-restricted-lift-exists}
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Choose the genuine algebra homomorphism from the proved existence
theorem. The two whole equations are the two properties of that
same chosen witness. No reduction or commutation certificate is
supplied for the target map.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original twisted maps preserve composition]
\label{lem:witt-two-original-twist-restriction-composition}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleRestrictionCoherence.twistMap_comp}
\leanok
For two actual composable Witt algebra maps, the twisted map of
their composite agrees on EVERY original twisted element with
the composite of their actual twisted maps.
\uses{def:witt-two-actual-twist-restriction-map}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On every actual pure tensor both maps keep the original copied
coefficient and apply the composite Witt algebra map to its
original algebra factor. Both maps preserve zero and addition,
so genuine tensor induction proves equality everywhere.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Identity of actual restricted lifts]
\label{thm:witt-two-etale-restricted-lift-identity}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleRestrictionCoherence.restrictedLift_id}
\leanok
Restricting an actual lift along the actual identity Witt algebra
map gives the SAME original whole lift.
\uses{def:witt-two-chosen-etale-restricted-lift,thm:witt-two-etale-restricted-lift-unique}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual identity twisted map is the identity on every pure
tensor, hence everywhere by tensor induction. The prescribed
source lift itself has its supplied original reduction and the
required actual commutation along the identity. The constructed
restricted lift has those same properties. Uniqueness of the
actual etale restricted lift identifies the two whole algebra
homomorphisms.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Composition of actual restricted lifts]
\label{thm:witt-two-etale-restricted-lift-composition}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleRestrictionCoherence.restrictedLift_comp}
\leanok
Restriction of an actual source lift along the composite of two
actual etale Witt algebra maps equals its two successive actual
restrictions, as whole original target lift homomorphisms.
\uses{def:witt-two-chosen-etale-restricted-lift,lem:witt-two-original-twist-restriction-composition,thm:witt-two-etale-restricted-lift-unique}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two successive restricted lifts are produced by the first
definition and have their entire actual residue Frobenius
reductions. Their actual commutation equations compose. The
previous lemma identifies the intervening SAME actual twisted
maps with the twisted map of the original composite. Thus the
twice restricted lift has exactly the original reduction and
whole commutation required for a restriction along the composite.
That composite is etale. Uniqueness identifies the direct and
successive restrictions as genuine entire homomorphisms.
\end{proof}

This proves algebraic identity and composition coherence for
actual restricted Witt lifts. Construction of the corresponding
scheme overlap restrictions and higher coherent exterior
homotopies, and global Deligne--Illusie, remain further producers.
