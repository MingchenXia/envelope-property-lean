\section{Genuine eventual global generation on original projective space}

\begin{theorem}[Ordinary eventual global generation by actual global sections]
\label{thm:coherent-projective-serre-global-generation}
\uses{lem:coherent-projective-original-twist-addition,thm:scheme-original-invertible-tensor-exact,thm:coherent-projective-nonnegative-twist-global-generation,lem:scheme-original-coproduct-generating-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.CoherentProjectiveGlobalGeneration.exists_eventual_generatingSections,EnvelopeProperty.CoherentProjectiveGlobalGeneration.exists_eventual_free_epi,EnvelopeProperty.CoherentProjectiveGlobalGeneration.canonicalEvaluation,EnvelopeProperty.CoherentProjectiveGlobalGeneration.exists_eventual_canonicalEvaluation_epi}
\leanok
Let $R$ be any commutative ring and $M$ an original finite-type
quasicoherent module on actual finite-coordinate polynomial projective
space over $R$. Every sufficiently large original twist
$M\otimes\mathcal O(d)$ is the quotient of a finite free structure-module
sum by a morphism arising from its actual original global sections.
In particular its entire canonical evaluation map from all original
global sections is an epimorphism.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the genuine finite negative-twist quotient
$\bigoplus_i\mathcal O(-a_i)\twoheadrightarrow M$, and let $A=\max_i a_i$
(or zero for the empty source). For $d\ge A$, tensor this actual
surjection by $\mathcal O(d)$ and use the original twist-addition
isomorphisms. Its source becomes $\bigoplus_i\mathcal O(d-a_i)$.

For each $e=d-a_i\ge0$, the degree-$e$ coordinate monomials give actual
whole global sections of this same original $\mathcal O(e)$. On the
$j$th standard coordinate open, the monomial $X_j^e$ has coefficient
one in its original standard frame. Thus these finitely many global
sections generate the whole original module on every standard open,
so their finite free evaluation map is an epimorphism. Take the finite
sum over $i$ and compose with the original tensor quotient. This gives
the required finite free surjection to $M(d)$, with every component an
actual global section of $M(d)$. The canonical evaluation from all
global sections factors this finite free map through the corresponding
inclusion of its finite list of sections. A morphism through which an
epimorphism factors is itself an epimorphism, proving the final claim.
\end{proof}
