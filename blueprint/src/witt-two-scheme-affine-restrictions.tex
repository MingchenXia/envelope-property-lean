\section{Actual affine restrictions on an original smooth Witt scheme}
\label{sec:witt-two-scheme-affine-restrictions}

Let $\widetilde X\to\operatorname{Spec}W_2(k)$ be an actual smooth
scheme over the original length-two Witt ring of a perfect
characteristic-$p$ field. All affine opens and section rings below
are those of this SAME actual scheme. Their coefficient structures
come from its actual structure morphism through the original
spectrum global-sections equivalence.

\begin{definition}[Actual structural sections and restriction]
\label{def:witt-two-scheme-affine-section-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoSchemeAffineRestrictions.sectionAlgebra,EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoSchemeAffineRestrictions.restrictionAlgebraHom}
\leanok
For every actual inclusion $U\subseteq V$, the actual structure
sheaf restriction $\Gamma(V)\to\Gamma(U)$ is a homomorphism over
the original Witt ring with these actual structural coefficient
actions.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The coefficient map on an open is the original spectrum
section identification followed by the actual structure
morphism's section map to that open. Naturality of this map
under original structure sheaf restriction identifies both
composites on every original coefficient. Thus the actual
restriction ring map has precisely its original Witt algebra
homomorphism structure.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual smooth affine rings and etale affine restrictions]
\label{thm:witt-two-smooth-affine-sections-and-etale-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoSchemeAffineRestrictions.affine_smooth,EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoSchemeAffineRestrictions.restriction_etale}
\leanok
Every actual affine section ring is smooth over the original Witt
ring for its actual structural algebra action. Every inclusion
between actual affine opens induces an etale original ring map.
\uses{def:witt-two-scheme-affine-section-restriction}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Smoothness of the actual scheme morphism gives smoothness of its
section map on an actual affine source open and the entire affine
base. Composing with the actual spectrum global-sections ring
equivalence gives smoothness for exactly the structural algebra
action in the first definition. The actual identity scheme
morphism is an open immersion and hence etale. Its actual affine
section map from $V$ to $U$ is exactly the original structure
sheaf restriction, since its section map is the identity. The
scheme definition of etaleness therefore gives etaleness of the
SAME original affine restriction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual local lifts and their affine restrictions are produced]
\label{thm:witt-two-scheme-affine-restriction-lift-exists}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoSchemeAffineRestrictions.exists_affineLift,EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoSchemeAffineRestrictions.exists_restrictedLift}
\leanok
Each actual affine open admits a genuine original relative
Frobenius lift on its actual Witt algebra of sections. Given ANY
such lift on $V$, an inclusion of actual affine opens
$U\subseteq V$ produces an actual lift on $U$ whose ENTIRE
original residue Frobenius reduction and literal lifted
restriction commutation hold on EVERY twisted source element.
\uses{thm:witt-two-smooth-affine-sections-and-etale-restriction,thm:witt-two-actual-affine-relative-lift,thm:witt-two-etale-restricted-lift-exists}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual section smoothness just proved allows the original
smooth infinitesimal lifting construction on each actual affine
section ring. Its entire reduction is the proved actual relative
Frobenius. For a prescribed lift on $V$, use the SAME actual
restriction algebra homomorphism from the first definition.
The second theorem supplies genuine etaleness of this actual
map and genuine smoothness of the target section ring. The
proved etale lifting construction therefore produces its actual
restricted lift with both whole equations. Thus neither target
lift nor its reduction or commutation is supplied as a premise.
\end{proof}

The residue algebras in this theorem are the actual tensors
$k\otimes_{W_2(k)}\Gamma(\widetilde X,U)$. Identifying them with
sections on the actual scheme special fibre, transporting its
native forms, higher coherent exterior homotopies and global
