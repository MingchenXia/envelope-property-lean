\section{The genuine closed constituent of the original simultaneous projective embedding}
\label{sec:generated-line-family-closed-projection}

\begin{theorem}[One original embedding constituent and every unchanged original generated line {\cite[\S1.2]{BJ22}}]
\label{thm:generated-line-family-closed-projection}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GeneratedLineFamilyClosedProjection.exists_embedding_with_closed_first_projection}
\leanok
\uses{thm:generated-line-family-projective-embedding,thm:finite-relative-projective-products}
For any finite actual generated-line family on an integral
native-projective $X$, the simultaneous original closed
projective product embedding can be constructed with its
first constituent itself an actual closed embedding.
Every remaining constituent retains the whole original
$\mathcal O(1)$ pullback isomorphism and every original
coordinate-section/generator equality. All original
coefficient triangles are exact.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose a genuine original closed polynomial projective
embedding from native projectivity. For each actual original
finite generating family use its original generated-line
projective morphism, entire original line isomorphism and
all original section identities. Put the unchanged closed
embedding first and these original maps after it. Their
whole native finite relative-product map is closed because
its first constituent was closed. Its original structure
triangle is exact by the native product lift construction.

Projection of this whole map onto the first factor is
literally the unchanged original closed embedding, by the
actual native product projection identity. Thus that first
projection is genuinely closed as well. Projection onto
each remaining factor is exactly the constructed
original generated-line map. Substitute this original
morphism identity into its whole line isomorphism and every
original coordinate-section identity. All original lines,
generators, coefficient maps and the same original source
$X$ are retained. The closed first constituent can therefore
be used for actual whole native function-field generation.
\end{proof}
