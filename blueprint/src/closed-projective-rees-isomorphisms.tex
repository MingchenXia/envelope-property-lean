\subsection{Whole identification for reduced closed projective birational witnesses}

\begin{lemma}[The constructed genuine fraction-field inverse is dense]
\label{scheme:actual-partial-iso-fraction-lift-dense}
\uses{scheme:actual-partial-iso-fraction-lift}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PartialIso.denseRange_fractionLift}
For any actual partial isomorphism from $Y$ to $\operatorname{Spec}A$ with
$A$ a domain and any actual fraction field $K$, the CONSTRUCTED map
$\operatorname{Spec}K\to Y$ has dense image. No supplied generic-density
certificate or reducedness of $Y$ is required.
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The true fraction inclusion is injective, so the spectrum closure formula
makes its image dense in $\operatorname{Spec}A$. It lies in the actual dense
target open. Its whole lift into that target open is dense there: the open
inclusion is inducing, and the closure of the image after that inclusion is
the entire base, so the inducing closure criterion gives density in the open.
The inverse actual scheme isomorphism maps this dense set to a dense subset
of the source open. The source-open inclusion has dense range by the genuine
partial-isomorphism data. Composition of continuous dense maps proves density
of the ENTIRE constructed original fraction-field inverse into $Y$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The constructed whole original Rees factor is an isomorphism for a reduced witness]
\label{scheme:actual-reduced-projective-rees-isomorphism}
\uses{scheme:actual-partial-iso-fraction-lift-dense,scheme:actual-closed-projective-rees-domination}
For ANY domain $A$, retain an ACTUAL closed embedding $\iota:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_A$,
its whole coefficient projection $f$, and genuine partial-isomorphism data
over this SAME $f$ and the identity of $\operatorname{Spec}A$. If $Y$ is
reduced, it is actually isomorphic over $\operatorname{Spec}A$ to a CONSTRUCTED
same original finite Rees scheme, with an actual nonzero selected generator. The entire isomorphism followed by its original closed embedding
is the real standard Rees immersion, and its coefficient projection is the
whole original projection. No source/target isomorphism or point-bijection
certificate is supplied.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the proved original coordinate construction, whole generic comparison
and reduced closed-factor producers of the checked domination construction.
They construct the original family and actual factor map $\delta$. Its composite with the genuine closed immersion agrees
with the actual generic inverse on the original generic Rees lift. Cancellation
of that true closed immersion gives the whole generic inverse as a composite
through $\delta$. The preceding lemma makes this generic inverse dense, so
$\delta$ has dense image. It is an actual closed immersion by the proved
factor theorem, hence its closed image is the whole target; thus it is
scheme-surjective. A surjective closed immersion into a reduced scheme is a
true isomorphism: its actual ideal-sheaf kernel has full support, hence is zero
on that reduced target, and the closed-immersion isomorphism criterion applies.
The resulting scheme isomorphism retains both already proved ENTIRE original
triangles. This remains a theorem for an ACTUAL closed projective embedding;
the arbitrary-proper Chow/projective-modification boundary remains separate.
\end{proof}
