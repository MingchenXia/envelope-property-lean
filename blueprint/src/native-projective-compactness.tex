\subsection{Actual native projective compactness for arbitrary coefficient seminorms}
\label{subsec:native-projective-compactness}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine fixed-base projective compactness]
\label{projective:native-fixed-base-compactness}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.compactSpace_nativeProjective,
  EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.isCompact_nativeProjective_relativeLocus,
  EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nonempty_nativeProjective}
\leanok
\uses{projective:normalized-native-homeomorphism,projective:general-relative-normalized-charts}
For every commutative coefficient ring $R$, every prescribed real
seminorm $v$ on $R$ and every $n$, the actual native valued-point space
of polynomial projective $n$-space above the whole base point $v$ is
compact and nonempty. Its actual fixed-base locus is a compact subset
of the absolute native valued-point space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine normalized fixed-base spectrum is compact: the coordinate
values are bounded by one, while the fixed coefficient seminorm gives
an individual bound for every polynomial evaluation. This requires no
uniform bound on all base coefficients. The actual native homeomorphism
transports this genuine compactness to the native fixed-base space.
By the compact-subtype criterion its original fixed-base locus is a
compact subset of the independently defined absolute native space.
The true zero-coordinate inverse Gauss chart gives an actual normalized
point above $v$, including when $n=0$. Its image under the genuine native
homeomorphism proves nonemptiness. No field, completeness, noetherianity,
coefficient bound or compactness certificate is assumed.
\end{proof}
