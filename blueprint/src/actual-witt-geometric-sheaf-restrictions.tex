\section{The same actual Witt maps under genuine sheaf restriction}

\begin{theorem}[The actual geometric restriction squares]
\label{thm:actual-witt-geometric-xi-homotopy-restriction}
\uses{thm:actual-witt-geometric-xi-homotopy-readback,thm:actual-witt-geometric-section-restrictions,lem:di-affine-local-map-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.ActualWittGeometricXiHomotopy.xiSheafMap_restrict, EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.ActualWittGeometricXiHomotopy.homotopySheafMap_restrict}
\leanok
Under an actual affine inclusion the geometric individual map of an original selected lift restricts to the geometric individual map of its same canonical restricted lift. The geometric canonical homotopy restricts to the canonical term for the same two directly restricted lifts.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the proved entire original section squares for the individual map and the canonical difference term. On the larger original twisted affine open, compare the two original restriction adjoints with values in the original pushforward from the smaller open. The actual source is the original exterior cotangent sheaf of the second projection; this projection is the genuine smooth pullback of the actual special fibre, so source quasicoherence is derived internally. The original associated-sheaf/global-sections counit then makes sections on this larger affine detect the adjoint morphisms. The exact original readbacks compute their section maps. Naturality of the original factor unit and adjoint identifies their equality with precisely the already-proved original section restriction square. Restriction in the smaller-open pushforward along these two containing opens is an isomorphism, so cancellation gives the whole adjoint equality. Apply the original restriction adjunction to obtain the displayed equality of actual local sheaf maps. Both target lifts and their reduction equalities are the same canonical restrictions used in the section square. No target quasicoherence, localization-generation hypothesis, matching morphism, or desired restriction identity is supplied.
\end{proof}
