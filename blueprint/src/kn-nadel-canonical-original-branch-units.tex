\section{Every original ratio equation is a unit on the actual dense open}

\begin{definition}[The entire original coefficient product]
\label{def:kn-canonical-original-ratio-product}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTraceGenericBranch.coefficientProduct}
\leanok
For an actual original SNC ratio chart, put $a=\prod_i c_i$, the product
of the coefficients of every original ratio-root equation, including
all absent unit-root factors.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Use the native finite product of the same original coefficient family.
This is a construction, with no separate source-theorem credit.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The same original coefficients and their product are nonzero]
\label{lem:kn-canonical-original-ratio-coefficients-nonzero}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTraceGenericBranch.ratioCoefficients_ne_zero,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTraceGenericBranch.coefficientProduct_ne_zero}
\leanok
Original smooth integral SNC geometry makes each original ratio
coefficient $c_i$ nonzero and hence makes their full product $a$ nonzero.
No nonvanishing certificate is a premise.
\uses{def:kn-canonical-original-ratio-product,lem:kn-nadel-whole-native-ratio-model}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each coefficient is its genuine original unit ratio times the original
uniform SNC generator selected by its original label. SNC component
ideals internally prove the active coordinate generators nonzero.
Absent components have generator one. Integrality of the actual
nonempty affine chart makes every product nonzero, including the empty
product when there are no root equations.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual generic field inverts every original coefficient]
\label{lem:kn-canonical-original-ratio-generic-units}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTraceGenericBranch.ratioCoefficients_generic_isUnit}
\leanok
The actual original germ of each $c_i$ is a unit in
$\mathcal O_{X,\eta}$.
\uses{lem:kn-canonical-original-ratio-coefficients-nonzero}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original germ map on regular functions of this nonempty original
affine chart is injective. It therefore sends the proved nonzero
coefficient to a nonzero element of the actual generic field.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole original ratio algebra is etale on its actual coefficient open]
\label{thm:kn-canonical-original-ratio-localized-etale}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTraceGenericBranch.localizedRatio_etale}
\leanok
Let $A$ be the same actual coefficient ring and put $S=A[1/a]$.
For the original positive tame root degrees, the entire actual algebra
$S\otimes_A B$, where $B$ is the original ratio algebra, is etale over
$S$. No etaleness or expected tensor comparison is supplied.
\uses{def:kn-canonical-original-ratio-product}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every original coefficient divides $a$, so its actual image in $S$ is
a unit. The original degrees remain positive, and their original
field-unit witnesses give genuine unit witnesses in $S$. The existing
whole tensor comparison identifies the entire basechanged algebra with
the original simultaneous root quotient on these mapped coefficients.
The already proved whole tame unit-root producer proves that quotient
etale. Transport this property through the same actual algebra
isomorphism; every original root factor is retained.
\end{proof}
