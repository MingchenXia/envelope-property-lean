\section{Actual section homology and original restriction maps}
\label{sec:scheme-section-homology}

Let $K$ be any cochain complex of actual module sheaves on a
scheme $X$. No quasicoherence, affineness, or exactness of
global sections is assumed in this section.

\begin{definition}[The actual section complex and canonical homology map]
\label{def:scheme-original-section-homology}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomology.sectionComplex,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomology.comparison,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomology.comparison_cycle}
For each original open $U$, evaluate every term and differential
of $K$ on $U$. The resulting actual complex of
$\mathcal O_X(U)$-modules has a canonical map
$H^q(\Gamma(U,K))\to\Gamma(U,H^q(K))$ in every degree.
On an actual native cycle it is the preserved-kernel comparison
followed by the original sheaf homology projection evaluated
on $U$.
\uses{prop:left-exact-original-homology-comparison}
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Evaluation on $U$ preserves finite limits: limits of sheaves of
modules are computed by the limits of their underlying sheaves,
and limits of sheaves are evaluated pointwise. Evaluation is
additive and in particular preserves zero morphisms. Apply the
canonical left exact comparison to the actual short complex
of $K$ in degree $q$. Its cycle formula gives exactly the
stated original evaluated homology projection.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Every original restriction map is the actual coefficient map]
\label{prop:scheme-original-section-complex-restriction}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomology.restriction,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomology.restrictionComplexMap,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomology.restrictionComplexMap_f_apply}
An inclusion $U\subseteq V$ gives the whole original cochain
map from sections on $V$ to sections on $U$, with scalars
restricted along the actual structural map
$\mathcal O_X(V)\to\mathcal O_X(U)$. Every component sends
every original section to its actual sheaf restriction.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The restriction maps of the original presheaf of modules are
linear into scalar restriction along this same structural ring
map. Naturality of any original sheaf morphism says that its
components commute with these restriction maps. Apply this to
every original differential of $K$. These exact squares are
the cochain-map identities, and the construction retains the
whole original coefficient map in every degree.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The original section-homology map commutes with restriction]
\label{prop:scheme-original-section-homology-restriction}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomology.comparison_restriction}
The native homology map of that entire original restriction
complex, followed by the canonical homology comparison for
evaluation on $U$ with scalars restricted to
$\mathcal O_X(V)$, equals the comparison on $V$ followed by
the actual restriction of the original homology sheaf.
\uses{def:scheme-original-section-homology,prop:scheme-original-section-complex-restriction,prop:left-exact-original-homology-comparison-naturality}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The original restriction maps form a natural transformation
from evaluation on $V$ to evaluation on $U$ followed by actual
restriction of scalars. Both functors preserve finite limits
and zero morphisms. Apply the proved natural-transformation
square for the canonical homology comparison to the actual
degree-$q$ short complex of $K$. Its induced short-complex map
is exactly the one induced by the whole restriction cochain
map, while its component on original homology is exactly the
original homology-sheaf restriction. This proves the complete
claimed square, not merely a square on selected representatives.
\end{proof}

No comparison in this section is asserted to be invertible.
The affine quasicoherent isomorphism and the compatibility of
the canonical Cartier maps still require their own proofs.
