\section{Actual reversal of a bounded cochain complex}

\begin{lemma}[Reversal retains original tensor homology]
\label{lem:kn-derived-cochain-reversal}
\uses{thm:kn-derived-finite-free-triples}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedReversal.reverseBaseChangeHomologyIso}
\leanok
For an actual integer-indexed cochain complex $K$ and integer $N$,
restriction along $q\mapsto N-q$ constructs an actual nonnegative
chain complex.  If the actual term $K^{N+1}$ is zero, its native
degree-$q$ homology after any algebra base change is isomorphic to
the native homology of the original scalar-extended three terms
in degree $N-q$.  If $K$ has flat terms and finite native homology,
the constructed reverse complex has those properties as well.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the native complex-shape embedding from nonnegative descending
indices to increasing integer indices.  In positive chain degrees,
all three neighboring indices and actual maps are retained literally.
At chain degree zero, the outgoing differential is zero; the actual
original target term $K^{N+1}$ is zero too, so the cycle comparison
is an isomorphism and the incoming comparison is the identity.
These are comparisons of the actual scalar-extended original maps.
Flatness is termwise, and finiteness transfers along the constructed
native homology isomorphisms.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Specialization for the original bounded cochain complex]
\label{cor:kn-derived-cochain-homology-specialization}
\uses{lem:kn-derived-cochain-reversal,cor:kn-derived-chain-homology-specialization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedReversal.exists_cochain_homology_zero_neighborhood,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedReversal.generic_cochain_homology_isZero_of_closed_fibre}
\leanok
Under these hypotheses, zero native homology at an original closed
residue fibre in a degree $N-q$ persists on a produced principal open,
and over an original domain it reaches the original fraction field.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Transfer to the constructed reverse chain complex, apply the proved
native chain specialization producer, and transfer back along its
actual scalar-extended comparisons.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Geometric base change remains separate]
The nonflat geometric ordered-complex comparison is proved in
Theorem~\ref{thm:kn-geometric-cech-base-change}.  Its interpretation
as actual positive fibre sheaf cohomology is proved in
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-derived-geometric-fibre-cohomology}, separately
from this reversal construction.
\end{remark}
