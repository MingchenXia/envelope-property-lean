\begin{remark}[Retaining the complete ample-model coefficients]
The original power comparison and its inverse now descend by the actual
finite-coefficient and Hom-detection construction above; that comparison,
rather than the tensor-difference expression alone, proves model ampleness.
A further coefficient enlargement need not preserve smoothness of the
coefficient ring over the integers. The separate arithmetic-stage producer
therefore smooth-localizes the resulting coefficient stage before selecting
the large-characteristic point and its Witt lift, retaining all model and
power-comparison coefficients. Kodaira vanishing is not asserted here.
\end{remark}
