\begin{definition}\label{def:actual-kummer-unit-root-algebra}

\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The original polynomial is monic because its positive-degree leading term is $T^n$ and its only other term is constant. Its class is zero in its actual principal-ideal quotient; evaluating that exact relation gives $t^n=a$. No root or algebra realization is a premise.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
The original monic power basis makes the whole quotient finite and free. The exact root relation makes $t^n$ a unit, hence $t$ a unit. Since this element is already a unit in the original quotient, that actual localization is the whole original quotient. Transport the genuine proved standard étale structure through this actual algebra isomorphism.
\end{proof}
