\section{Actual original line sheaves over local spectra}

\begin{lemma}[Every actual line sheaf on a local spectrum is trivial]
\label{lem:actual-local-spectrum-line-triviality}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.nonempty_iso_unit_on_localSpec}
\leanok
For an arbitrary original local ring $A$ and an actual invertible
module sheaf $\mathcal L$ on $\operatorname{Spec}A$, there is an actual
global module-sheaf isomorphism $\mathcal L\simeq\mathcal O$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual local trivializing cover of $\mathcal L$ covers the closed
point corresponding to the maximal ideal of $A$. Choose a member
containing this point. Every prime of a local ring specializes to its
maximal ideal, so an open containing the closed point contains every
prime; this member is the entire spectrum. Its genuine trivialization
is therefore a trivialization of the pullback along the actual
inclusion of the top open. Pull this actual isomorphism back by the
inverse of the canonical top-open isomorphism. The actual pullback
composition comparison, the native equality comparison for the
identity composite and the native pullback identity comparison
identify its source with the original $\mathcal L$. The actual
structure-unit pullback comparison identifies its target with the
original $\mathcal O$. Their composite is the required global
isomorphism on the entire original scheme.
\end{proof}
