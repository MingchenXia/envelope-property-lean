\begin{theorem}[Original geometric ampleness and positive actual tensor powers]
\label{thm:fc-original-ample-line-positive-powers}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.AmpleLinePositivePowers.isAmple_tensorPower, EnvelopeProperty.AmpleLinePositivePowers.isAmple_of_tensorPower, EnvelopeProperty.AmpleLinePositivePowers.isAmple_tensorPower_iff}
\leanok
\uses{lem:fc-original-ample-eventual-very-ample}
Let $A$ be an original invertible line on an original integral scheme
over $R$, and let $s>0$ be an original natural number. Then $A$ is
geometrically ample exactly when its actual original tensor power
$A^{\otimes s}$ is geometrically ample.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $A$ is ample, all its sufficiently large actual powers are very ample.
Choose a positive $t$ for which $st$ is beyond that original threshold;
for example, if the threshold is $N$, take $t=N+1$. The canonical whole
original multiplied-power isomorphism identifies
$(A^{\otimes s})^{\otimes t}$ with this original very ample
$A^{\otimes st}$. The original $A^{\otimes s}$ is invertible, so its
positive very ample $t$th power proves its geometric ampleness.
Conversely, geometric ampleness of $A^{\otimes s}$ gives an original
positive $t$ whose actual tensor power is very ample. Its same whole
multiplied-power isomorphism identifies this line with the actual
$A^{\otimes st}$. Since $st>0$ and $A$ is originally invertible, this
is the original positive-power geometric ample criterion for $A$.
\end{proof}
