\begin{lemma}[Geometric ampleness restricts to original closed subschemes]
\label{lem:fc-original-ample-closed-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeAmpleClosedRestriction.veryAmple_of_iso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeAmpleClosedRestriction.veryAmple_pullback, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeAmpleClosedRestriction.ample_pullback}
\leanok
\uses{def:fc-original-geometric-line-ampleness}
Let $p:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ and let $f:Y\hookrightarrow X$ be an
actual closed immersion. Whole line isomorphisms preserve very ampleness.
If $L$ is very ample, respectively ample, on $X$ over $R$, its whole
original pullback is very ample, respectively ample, on $Y$ over $R$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Transport the actual closed projective embedding line comparison across
the given line isomorphism. For restriction, compose the actual embedding
of $X$ into projective space with $f$. A composite of closed immersions is
a closed immersion, and the whole pullback composition isomorphism gives
the required original embedding line on $Y$. For an ample line choose
the positive very ample power in its geometric definition. Pullback
preserves invertibility. The actual tensor-power pullback isomorphism
identifies the pulled very ample power with that same positive tensor
power of the pulled line. This supplies the geometric ample criterion
on $Y$.
\end{proof}
