\subsection{Whole-scheme scalar extension on actual native points}

\begin{lemma}[Literal scalar pullback and intrinsic psh pullback]
\label{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-pullback}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.model, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.projection, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.structureMap, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.projection_comp_structure, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.nativeMap, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.nativeMap_coe, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.continuous_nativeMap, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.isLocallyPSHOn_comp_nativeMap}
\uses{lem:fc-affine-base-change-psh,lem:local-basic}
Let $R\to S$ be an actual coefficient-ring map and let the prescribed
real seminorms agree on $R$. For every actual scheme
$X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$, its literal scheme pullback to
$\operatorname{Spec}S$ has the actual continuous native scalar-restriction
map. The entire original point is its image under the whole scheme
projection. Pullback through this actual map preserves intrinsic local
psh defined by genuine regular units on Zariski opens.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual scheme pullback projections and their whole coefficient
triangle. The compatible prescribed base points give the native map.
Its whole original projection is continuous. Genuine regular units and
their logarithms pull back along this actual scheme morphism; the
intrinsic local-psh pullback theorem for a whole coefficient diagram
therefore applies.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole actual affine tensor scheme comparison]
\label{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-affine-tensor}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.affineTensorIso, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.affineTensorIso_hom_projection, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.affineTensorIso_hom_structure, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.affineTensorHomeomorph, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.affineTensorHomeomorph_nativeMap, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.isProperMap_nativeMap_affine}
\uses{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-pullback,lem:fc-affine-base-change-psh}
The literal scalar pullback of $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is actually
isomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec}(S\otimes_RA)$, preserving both whole
projections. On prescribed native points this is a homeomorphism and
identifies the actual scalar map exactly with tensor scalar restriction.
This native scalar map is proper for arbitrary rings and compatible
seminorms, without boundedness, finite type or properness of the scheme.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Actual scheme pullback symmetry followed by the genuine Spec tensor
pushout comparison gives the isomorphism and both projection triangles.
The entire new coefficient triangle restricts its native homeomorphism
to the exact prescribed seminorm locus. The original projection triangle
identifies the whole scalar-restriction map. The genuine affine tensor
restriction is proper by its ultrafilter lifting theorem. Transport
that actual proper map through these two homeomorphisms.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual affine-source scalar restriction and algebraic-completion lifts]
\label{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-affine-source}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.isProperMap_nativeMap_of_isAffine, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.nativeMap_surjective_algebraicCompletion_of_isAffine}
\uses{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-affine-tensor,lem:fc-algebraic-completion-lift}
For an arbitrary actual affine source scheme over $R$, its true native
scalar restriction is proper. If the base is a complete ultrametric
normed field and the new field is the completion of an algebraic normed
extension, this actual native map is also surjective.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual source structure map induces the entire coefficient action
on its genuine global-section ring. The actual source isomorphism with
Spec of this ring gives an actual isomorphism of scalar pullbacks,
with full coefficient and original-source projection triangles. Thus
the whole scalar map is conjugate to the actual affine tensor
restriction. Use its proved properness and, in the algebraic-completion
case, the constructed actual tensor point lifts.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[True scalar-changed chart and whole native inverse image]

Every actual original open immersion has a genuine scalar-changed open

coefficient and original projection triangles commute. Its image is
exactly the original projection inverse image of the original chart.
Consequently its native points are homeomorphic to the whole native

\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine iterated-pullback isomorphism followed by the actual
pullback chart projection constructs this morphism. The true pullback
identities prove both whole triangles, and the genuine base-change
open-range identity computes its entire image. Apply the actual native
open-immersion homeomorphism and use this exact range equality. Native
functoriality of the whole original projection triangle proves the
asserted commutation.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Proper and closed scalar restriction on every actual scheme]
\label{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-proper}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.isProperMap_nativeMap_restrict_affine, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.isProperMap_nativeMap, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.isClosedMap_nativeMap, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.isCompact_preimage_nativeMap, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.isCompact_fibre_nativeMap}
\uses{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-affine-source,lem:fc-scheme-scalar-open-chart}
For every actual scheme over $R$, its native scalar restriction along
arbitrary compatible prescribed coefficient seminorms is proper and
closed. Compact native sets have compact inverse images, and every
whole point fibre is compact. No separation, finite-type, boundedness
or proper-model hypothesis occurs.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On every actual affine support-open, the preceding true chart
homeomorphism identifies the entire inverse image with the actual
scalar-changed affine chart. Thus the restricted map is conjugate to
the already proved proper scalar map of an actual affine source. The
canonical original affine cover covers all actual native points.
Genuine proper-map locality on this actual open target cover proves
properness of the whole scalar map. Closedness and compact inverse
images and fibres follow from this actual proper map.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole-scheme algebraic-completion scalar lifts]
\label{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-algebraic-completion-lift}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.nativeMap_surjective_algebraicCompletion}
\uses{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-affine-source,lem:fc-scheme-scalar-open-chart}
Let $F$ be a complete ultrametric normed field and $L/F$ an algebraic
normed extension. For every actual $F$-scheme, native scalar restriction
from its literal $\widehat L$-scheme pullback is surjective.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a genuine original affine chart through the support of the
prescribed whole point. The actual native open-immersion homeomorphism
lifts that whole point to the original chart. The constructed actual
affine-source scalar lift gives a point of its literal completed
scalar pullback. Map it through the genuine scalar-changed chart
inclusion. The proved whole native projection commutation recovers
exactly the original prescribed whole point.
\end{proof}

The general Gauss-extension, rational-lift and nontrivial complete
algebraically closed scalar-extension producers remain separate from
this construction.
