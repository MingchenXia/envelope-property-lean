\section{The intrinsic full coherent Euler characteristic}

\begin{definition}[The full actual coherent Euler characteristic]
\label{def:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-characteristic}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.coverEuler, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.eulerCharacteristic, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.normalizedEulerCharacteristic}
Take the actual finite affine cover of the original quasi-compact
separated scheme. Define $\chi(M)$ to be the alternating sum of its
actual field-linear Cech cohomology dimensions in degrees less than
one plus the number of cover members. Define the normalized Euler
characteristic of the original line $L$ as $\chi(L)-\chi(\mathcal O)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Derived boundedness and independence]
\label{thm:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-bounded}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.fieldCech_isZero_of_card_le, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.fieldCech_finite, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.coverEuler_eq_of_card_le, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.fieldCech_finrank_eq_of_affine_openCovers, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.eulerCharacteristic_eq_on_cover, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.eulerCharacteristic_eq_of_iso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.normalizedEulerCharacteristic_eq_of_iso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.normalizedEulerCharacteristic_unit, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.coverEuler_eq_partialEuler}
\uses{def:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-characteristic, thm:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-index}
The actual field Cech cohomology vanishes in every degree at least the
cardinality of any finite affine cover. For projective coherent original
modules every group is finite dimensional. Every sufficiently long
finite alternating sum on any other finite affine cover is the same
$\chi(M)$, and original module isomorphisms preserve $\chi$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Give the finite index type any linear order. In degrees at least its
cardinality there is no strictly increasing tuple, so every actual
ordered Cech term vanishes. The genuine native-to-ordered Cech homotopy
equivalence makes the original native Cech homology vanish in those
degrees. The original scalar restriction is exact and preserves zero.
Projective coherent finiteness applies in every positive degree, while
actual degree-zero Cech cohomology is actual global sections. To compare
two covers, extend both finite sums to the larger bound; the added
terms are zero. The actual degree-zero section isomorphism and genuine
two-cover positive-degree cohomology isomorphisms then identify every
term. An original module isomorphism likewise induces linear
isomorphisms on every actual cohomology group.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional coherent Euler additivity]
\label{thm:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-characteristic-exact}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerCharacteristic.eulerCharacteristic_add}
\uses{thm:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-bounded, thm:fc-cohomology-finite-euler, thm:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-zero}
For every actual short exact sequence of original finite-type
quasicoherent modules on an actual projectively factored scheme over
the original field, $\chi(B)=\chi(A)+\chi(C)$. No vanishing or numerical
certificate is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual finite affine cover sends the original module short exact
sequence to a true short exact sequence of the original base-linear
Cech complexes. Restriction to the original field remains exact. All
actual cohomology dimensions are finite and vanishing at the cover
bound is derived by the preceding theorem. The genuine finite Euler
relation of the actual long exact cohomology sequence therefore gives
the result directly.
\end{proof}
