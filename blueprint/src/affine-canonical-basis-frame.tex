\begin{theorem}[Original differential freeness derives the whole canonical line]
\label{sc0-affine-canonical-basis:whole-frame}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.CanonicalGeometry.affineCanonicalBasisFrame,EnvelopeProperty.CanonicalGeometry.affineCanonical_isInvertible_of_basis}
\leanok
Let $X$ be ANY original affine scheme over the original field $k$.
An actual basis of size $n$ of its SAME global section algebra's
Kaehler differential module derives a genuine ENTIRE original
canonical sheaf frame and actual cover-local invertibility.
No finite presentation, smoothness, desired frame or
sheaf-comparison hypothesis is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the genuine native isomorphism between the SAME original affine
scheme and the spectrum of its original global section ring.
Its actual structural morphism is the original affine algebra
structural morphism, by the existing full structural comparison.
The true exterior cotangent open-immersion comparison identifies
pullback of the SAME original canonical sheaf with the genuine
canonical sheaf on that spectrum. The previously constructed full
associated-sheaf comparison and the actual top-wedge basis give
an entire frame there. Pull back that actual frame by the SAME
inverse affine isomorphism, retaining the true native pullback
composition, congruence, identity and unit comparisons. This gives
the asserted entire original frame, not merely a global-section
bijection. Actual structure-module invertibility transfers across
that genuine whole frame and proves the claimed invertibility.
\end{proof}

This includes the actual original localization spectra required by
the valuative canonical-section argument, without adding a false
finite-presentation or smoothness premise to those localizations.
The actual whole localization differential isomorphism, canonical
module pushforward and vanishing remain further obligations.
