\subsection{Actual polynomial spectral units in a closed Gauss disc}

This slice concerns the ACTUAL completed Gauss disc algebra $D_r$ over an
arbitrary ultrametric normed field $K$, with $r>0$. The original coefficient
ring $A$ may be any ultrametric seminormed commutative ring. It does not assert
the full spectral-image universal property for arbitrary Banach targets.
The one substantive target restriction is that the denominator image is an
ORIGINAL polynomial in $D_r$; numerator images may be arbitrary completed
functions. No unit, power-bound, tuple-bound or extension certificate is input.

\begin{lemma}[Actual split roots detect strict constant dominance]
\label{analytic:actual-disc-polynomial-root-dominance}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.gaussFun_X_sub_C,
EnvelopeProperty.gaussFun_eq_leadingCoeff_mul_prod_roots,
EnvelopeProperty.gaussFun_eq_coeff_zero_of_splits,
EnvelopeProperty.gaussFun_tail_lt_coeff_zero_of_splits,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.polynomial_tail_lt_of_split_extension,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.polynomial_tail_lt_of_no_disc_zero}
\leanok
\uses{analytic:actual-polynomial-gauss-completion,spectrum:complete-norm-extension}
Let $p\in K[T]$ and $r>0$. If $p$ is nonzero at EVERY whole point of the true
prescribed-$K$ closed disc of radius $r$, then $p(0)\ne0$ and
\[
 \|p-C(p(0))\|_r<|p(0)|.
\]
The hypothesis quantifies over the actual point space, not only rational
$K$-points or original supports. No completeness, algebraic closedness,
nontrivial norm or separability of $K$ is required.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual evaluation-at-zero point lies in the original disc, so $p(0)\ne0$
and $p\ne0$. Embed $K$ into its genuine complete field $\widehat K$, form the
ACTUAL finite splitting field $L$ of the image of $p$, and extend the complete
$\widehat K$ norm to $L$ using the checked algebraic norm-extension producer.
The composite original map $K\to\widehat K\to L$ preserves EVERY coefficient
value, with both actual coefficient triangles retained; no norm on an unrelated
field is supplied. Every root $a$ in this splitting field defines the WHOLE
original polynomial point by $q\mapsto |q(a)|$. If $|a|\le r$, its entire
coefficient restriction is the original norm of $K$, its $T$ value is at most
$r$, and $p$ has value zero, contrary to the hypothesis. Thus all the actual
finitely many roots have absolute value STRICTLY greater than $r$.

Choose a real nonnegative $s>r$ strictly below all root values; when the root
multiset is empty choose any $s>r$. Factor the actual image of $p$ as its
leading coefficient times the full multiset product of $T-a$. Genuine Gauss
multiplicativity and the exact Gauss value $\max(s,|a|)=|a|$ give
$\|p\|_s=|p(0)|$, preserving the original coefficients. For every $i>0$,
\[
 |p_i|r^i\le |p(0)|(r/s)^i<|p(0)|.
\]
The zero coefficient of $p-C(p(0))$ vanishes, and the actual finite coefficient
maximum therefore gives the claimed STRICT inequality on the WHOLE tail.
Zero polynomials and constant polynomials are covered by the same zero-point
and empty-root arguments; no residue-only inference is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A polynomial has no spectral zero precisely when it is a genuine disc unit]
\label{analytic:actual-disc-polynomial-spectral-unit}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.isUnit_polynomialMap_of_no_disc_zero,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.GaussCompletion.isUnit_polynomialMap_iff_no_disc_zero}
\leanok
\uses{analytic:actual-disc-polynomial-root-dominance,analytic:actual-gauss-completion-disc-homeomorphism,analytic:actual-gauss-disc-function-values}
For EVERY original polynomial $p$, its actual image in $D_r$ is a UNIT if and
only if $p$ has nonzero value at every actual prescribed-$K$ disc point.
In the forward direction all actual spectral evaluations are retained; in the
reverse direction an inverse is genuinely constructed in the SAME completed
Gauss ring, not in a scalar extension or a field of rational functions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual point evaluations are multiplicative and send one to one, so a unit has
nonzero value at EVERY point. Conversely the previous lemma gives $p_0\ne0$
and $\|1-C(p_0^{-1})p\|_r<1$. The actual complete normed ring $D_r$ admits the
convergent geometric series for this element. Its true unit $1-(1-C(p_0^{-1})p)$,
multiplied by the actual constant unit $C(p_0)$, has value the ORIGINAL polynomial
image $p$. This constructs a genuine unit in $D_r$ and proves its entire element
identity. No maximum-modulus or spectral-invertibility theorem is assumed.
\end{proof}
