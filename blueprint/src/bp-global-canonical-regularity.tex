\section{All global coefficients of the same original BP canonical modules are regular}
\label{sec:bp-global-canonical-regularity}

\begin{theorem}[Every original BP relative canonical global section has an original coefficient]
\label{sc0-bp-global-canonical-regularity:all-line-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.BPGlobalCanonicalRegularity.exists_originalRing_fractionCoefficient}
\leanok
For the original domain with actual etale polynomial coordinates
over the original characteristic-zero field, any original nonzero
ideal and the SAME prescribed original fraction field, consider
the SAME literal original BP sequence. The SAME prescribed rational
coefficient of EVERY global section of its ENTIRE relative canonical
line belongs to the SAME original source ring. No regularity,
normality, target integrality, source basis, properness, nonzero
differential, valuation lift or coefficient range premise is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual etale coordinates derive source smoothness and regularity
and its SAME spectrum-section cotangent basis. The actual BP
sequence derives its target integrality and smoothness, properness
over the original locally Noetherian source, and the original
nonempty off-centre isomorphism open. That SAME original open
also derives nonvanishing of the SAME canonical differential.
Apply the preceding full regular-DVR intersection theorem to
EVERY original global section. Its normalized coefficient and
prescribed field equivalence are precisely the pre-existing
original BP coefficient maps; no comparison formula is assumed.
\uses{sc0-proper-canonical-regularity:all-original-sections}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original rounded BP global section has an original coefficient]
\label{sc0-bp-global-canonical-regularity:all-rounded-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.BPGlobalCanonicalRegularity.exists_originalRing_roundedCoefficient}
\leanok
For EVERY rational exponent, the SAME rational coefficient of
EVERY global section of the ENTIRE actual rounded BP relative
canonical module belongs to the SAME original source ring.
Here the rounded module is the pre-existing intrinsic actual
one; nonnegative exponents have the usual
$\mathcal O(-\lfloor qF\rfloor)$ meaning.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding all-section theorem to the image of an
ARBITRARY original rounded global section under its SAME entire
original rounded inclusion. The pre-existing rounded coefficient
is definitionally the coefficient of this very inclusion image.
No section-image, regularity or coefficient certificate is supplied.
\uses{sc0-bp-global-canonical-regularity:all-line-sections}
\end{proof}

This proves regularity for ALL actual original global sections,
including the entire rounded module, beyond the previous quotient
kernel ideal. The full original multiplier-module comparison and
whole pushforward sheaf assembly remain further work; no local or
Nadel vanishing is inferred.
