\section{Actual smooth local Kummer pullbacks}

Write $P_R^n=\operatorname{Spec}R[t_0,\ldots,t_{n-1}]$ with its
actual coefficient projection to $\operatorname{Spec}R$.
For positive integers $m_j$, let $q_m:P_R^n\to P_R^n$ be the actual
morphism induced by $t_j\mapsto t_j^{m_j}$ and the identity on $R$.

\begin{lemma}[The same polynomial space is smooth of its original dimension]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-kummer-polynomial-smooth}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.polynomialAlgebra_standardSmooth,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.polynomialProjection_smooth}
The actual projection $P_R^n\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is smooth of
relative dimension $n$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the genuine polynomial presentation with its $n$ original variables
and no relations.  Its relation Jacobian is the empty matrix, whose
determinant is $1$, so this presentation is standard smooth of
dimension $n-0=n$.  The actual coefficient homomorphism is therefore
locally standard smooth of dimension $n$.  The native affine scheme
criterion proves the assertion for this precise spectrum and projection.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The literal independent-coordinate pullback]
\label{def:kn-nadel-kummer-local-pullback}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinatePowerHom,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinatePowerMap,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.pullbackScheme,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.chartProjection,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.affineProjection,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.structureMap}
For an actual etale chart $g:U\to P_R^n$, define
\[
 C(g,m)=U\times_{P_R^n,q_m}P_R^n.
\]
Its actual projections are $a:C(g,m)\to U$ and
$b:C(g,m)\to P_R^n$, with $g\circ a=q_m\circ b$.
Its coefficient structure is the actual composite of $b$ with the
original polynomial-space projection.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[The literal pullback has a smooth source of the same dimension]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-kummer-pullback-smooth}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.affineProjection_etale,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.structureMap_smooth}
\uses{def:kn-nadel-kummer-local-pullback,lem:kn-nadel-kummer-polynomial-smooth}
The actual map $b$ is etale and the actual source $C(g,m)$ is smooth
of relative dimension $n$ over $R$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The projection $b$ is the literal base change of the given original
etale chart $g$ along $q_m$, so it is etale.  In particular it is smooth
of relative dimension zero.  Compose it with the preceding polynomial
projection, which is smooth of relative dimension $n$.  The native
composition theorem gives relative dimension $0+n=n$ for precisely
the displayed structure map.  The other projection $a$ is the branched
map to the original chart; its etaleness is not used in this argument.
\end{proof}

The actual SNC chart producer supplies such $g$ by using the original
regular parameters as etale coordinates.  Distinct components through
the chosen closed point are distinct coordinate hyperplanes, and every
other original component is absent on that whole chart.  The
construction above therefore adjoins roots of those original coordinate
equations individually.  It changes the local covering construction
from a single monomial root to independent coordinate roots.

\begin{lemma}[Actual roots of the original coordinate equations]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-kummer-original-roots}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinateRoot,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinateRoot_pow,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.chart_coordinateRoot_pow,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.originalProjection_over}
\uses{def:kn-nadel-kummer-local-pullback}
Let $y_j=b^*t_j$ be the actual coordinate section on $C(g,m)$.
Then $y_j^{m_j}=a^*g^*t_j$.  For the original chart coordinates $v_j$,
this is $y_j^{m_j}=a^*v_j$ under the original open-subscheme section
comparison.  The new coefficient structure is the original composite
$C(g,m)\to U\to X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the actual top-section contravariant functor to the native
pullback square $a\,g=b\,q_m$.  The true comorphism of $q_m$ takes
the original section $t_j$ to $t_j^{m_j}$ and fixes every coefficient.
The native coordinate-chart identity identifies $g^*t_j$ with the
original open-subscheme section $v_j$.  Composing the same square
with the original coefficient projection proves the structure identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The genuine finite surjective local covering projection]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-kummer-finite-surjective}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinatePowerHom_integral,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinatePowerHom_finiteType,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinatePowerHom_finite,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinatePowerHom_injective,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinatePowerMap_finite,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.coordinatePowerMap_surjective,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.chartProjection_finite,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerCover.chartProjection_surjective}
\uses{def:kn-nadel-kummer-local-pullback}
If every $m_j>0$, the original coordinate-power morphism $q_m$ and
the actual first pullback projection $a:C(g,m)\to U$ are finite and
surjective.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use on the target polynomial ring the algebra structure induced by
the actual power homomorphism, rather than its identity algebra
structure.  Each target coordinate is integral, since its positive
$m_j$-th power is the image of the corresponding original source
coordinate.  Constants are images too; polynomial induction using
addition and multiplication by coordinates proves integrality of
every element.  The homomorphism is of finite type: its composite
with the original coefficient map is the original finite-type
polynomial coefficient homomorphism.  Integral and finite type gives
module finiteness for precisely this power-map algebra structure.

For injectivity put $N=\prod_jm_j>0$ and $c_j=N/m_j$.
The composite power homomorphism with exponents $c_j$ after the
original one is the uniform expansion $t_j\mapsto t_j^N$, which is
injective by the actual polynomial coefficient calculation.  Thus
the original power homomorphism is injective.  Integral lying over
now proves surjectivity on the entire original prime spectrum.
The native affine finite criterion and native base-change theorems
give the assertions for $q_m$ and its literal pullback projection.
\end{proof}

These results supply a genuine smooth finite local covering source.
The finite free basis is constructed below from the actual
independent-root tower.  Extension to the normalized single cyclic
covering and the global auxiliary-divisor construction and its
gluing remain separate producers.  None is an assumption of the
local results above.

\begin{lemma}[Actual SNC geometry produces the local smooth finite cover]

at each original closed point the native SNC chart gives an actual
affine neighborhood with a finite surjective literal independent-root
cover whose source is smooth of relative dimension $d$ over the
original field.  Every original component through the point remains
the span of its specified distinct original coordinate, and every
other original component is absent on the whole neighborhood.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take the actual affine etale coordinate chart from the original SNC

all component ideal equalities, and all absence equalities.  Apply
the preceding literal pullback construction to these original
coordinates.  Its second projection is etale, its structural map
is the actual composite to the original scheme and field, and its
first projection is finite and surjective.  The root equations hold
for these same original component coordinates.  The actual affine
pullback is affine since both polynomial spaces and the chart are
affine.  No replacement divisor family or covering-existence input
is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
The coordinate and unit power identities already proved give

 r^N=\alpha^N\prod_j(y_j^N)^{a_j}
     =u|_T\prod_j(x_j|_T)^{a_j}=s|_T.

Apply the actual quotient universal property of

is exactly its structure triangle over the original chart's
affinization.  The existing native raw cyclic equation is the

with the original cyclic projection is exactly the constructed

the cyclic equation of that same specified coefficient, retaining

\end{proof}

The last target is the original \emph{raw} cyclic equation.

original coefficient polynomial algebra acting by the power map.
Its basis index has exactly $\prod_i m_i$ elements.  The original
power morphism and its literal branched pullback projection are

\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At stage zero use the original singleton basis of $A$ over itself.

$1,z_i,\ldots,z_i^{m_i-1}$ of that actual root quotient over the

tower, so the native product-of-bases construction gives a basis
over the same original $A$.  Its actual index is recursively the

\end{proof}
