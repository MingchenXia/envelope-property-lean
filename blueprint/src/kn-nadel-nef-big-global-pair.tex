\section{The same twisted generator map on an entire affine base blowup}

The affine exceptional-line embedding uses ideal generators on the original
base.  The global construction instead starts with generators of an ideal
tensored by a line.  The comparison below retains the projective map of
these actual twisted generators, rather than choosing a replacement map.

\begin{lemma}[The actual twisted generator map over a framed affine base]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-framed-affine-pair-closed}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.affineFramedMap,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.affineFramedMap_eq,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.affineFramedMap_isClosedImmersion}
\leanok
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-global-frame-untwist,lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-affine-ideal-generators,lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-exceptional-projective-closed}
Let $I$ be an ideal of a commutative ring $A$, let $B$ be an invertible
line on $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with an actual frame
$e:B\simeq\mathcal O_X$, and let $G$ be an actual finite generating
family of $\widetilde I\otimes B$.  Put
$\pi:V=\operatorname{Bl}_I X\to X$ and $J=I\mathcal O_V$.
Pull $G$ back and apply the canonical ideal-comap epimorphism, giving the
same actual generating family of $J\otimes\pi^*B$.  Its original
projective map $q:V\to\mathbf P_A^s$ is a closed immersion.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Compose each original generator with the actual untwisting
$\widetilde I\otimes B\simeq\widetilde I$.  The resulting family still
generates the whole ideal module.  The whole affine ideal-section theorem
therefore says that its original coefficients $g_i\in I$ generate the
entire original ideal $I$, with the same original index set.  The genuine
spectrum section-ring isomorphism is used in recovering these elements
of $A$; no new ideal or generator-spanning condition is chosen.

Let $G_I$ be the actual exceptional generating family of these same
$g_i$.  The whole ideal-comap untwisting square identifies each basis map
of the original transported $G$, followed by the actual target
untwisting, with the actual exceptional section of $g_i$.  On the original
Rees chart $A[I/a]$, compose that whole identity with the actual inverse
pivot frame.  Each coefficient of the original $q$ is then exactly
$g_i/a$, and its added padding coefficient is zero.  Thus its affine
projective tuple equals the entire original tuple of the actual
exceptional map of $G_I$, with the same coefficient homomorphism and
same indices.  The actual chart cover detects equality of the two whole
scheme morphisms.  The proved exceptional map of $G_I$ is a
closed immersion, so the same is true of the original $q$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual global generated pair is proper]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-same-global-pair-proper}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.globalMap,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.globalMap_structure,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.globalPair,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.globalPair_fst,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.globalPair_snd,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairClosed.globalPair_isProper}
\leanok
Let $X$ be projective over the original field, $D$ an original coherent
ideal, $B$ an original invertible line, and $G$ an actual finite
generating family of $D\otimes B$.  On the actual blowup $\pi:Y\to X$,
form the original generated-line map $q$ of its canonical transported
family of $D\mathcal O_Y\otimes\pi^*B$.  Its original structure triangle
constructs the actual pair $(\pi,q)$ into $X\times_k\mathbf P_k^s$.
Both projections of this pair are the specified original maps, and the
whole pair is proper.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original generated-line construction proves the structure triangle,
so the original fibre-product universal property gives the pair and both
projection identities.  The actual blowup remains projective over the
original field by the proved projective blowup theorem.  Its composition
with $X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is therefore proper.  Since the original
structure map of $X$ is separated, its actual blowup projection is proper.
The first projection $X\times_k\mathbf P_k^s\to X$ is separated, as a
base change of the original separated projective-space structure map.
The pair composed with this first projection is exactly the proper
blowup projection.  Properness of a composite with a separated target
morphism implies properness of the original morphism, giving the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The same original generated pair is a closed immersion]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-same-global-pair-closed}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairBaseOpenComposition.basePair_isClosedImmersion,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairBaseOpenComposition.globalPair_isClosedImmersion,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigGlobalPairBaseOpenComposition.globalPair_mono}
\leanok
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-framed-affine-pair-closed,lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-same-pair-base-open,lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-global-frame-untwist}
Let $X$ be an arbitrary scheme over the original field $k$, let $D$ be
an original ideal, let $B$ be an original invertible line, and let $G$ be
an actual finite generating family of $D\otimes B$.  Put
$\pi:Y=\operatorname{Bl}_D X\to X$ and let $q$ be the original generated-line
map of the same canonically transported family of
$D\mathcal O_Y\otimes\pi^*B$.  The original pair
$(\pi,q):Y\to X\times_k\mathbf P_k^s$ is a closed immersion, hence a
monomorphism.  Its actual base change to every original affine base open
on which $B$ has its actual frame is a closed immersion on the entire
original affine-base blowup.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On an original affine open $U$, canonically restrict the original source
family $G$ and use the literal original ideal-comap equality to identify
its ideal with the ideal of $D(U)$ on $\operatorname{Spec}\Gamma(X,U)$.
The actual pullback-composition and ideal-comap maps construct a whole
isomorphism between the original global transported line restricted to
$Y_U$ and the line of the same affine source family.  The whole identity
on every original basis map is proved before specializing the arbitrary
base scheme to the section-ring spectrum.  Propositional elimination of
the original ideal equality then seals the whole isomorphism together
with that identity.  No comparison, coefficient equality or closedness
result is supplied by a caller.

On each original Rees pivot chart, pull this actual basis identity back
and compose with the actual original pivot frame.  The resulting whole
frame identity preserves each projective coefficient, including its
original index and the added zero coefficient.  The actual structure
triangle preserves the original coefficient homomorphism as well.
Consequently the restriction of the same original $q$ is the original
framed-affine map followed by the original projective coefficient map.
The actual pivot cover detects equality on the entire affine-base
blowup.  The canonical Cartesian projective coefficient square, using
both its original structure and coefficient projections, identifies the
actual base-changed pair with this same framed-affine map.  The proved
whole framed-affine theorem therefore makes the base-changed pair closed.

Invertibility of the original $B$ internally supplies a cover of $X$ by
original framed affine opens.  Pull this cover back to the original
target $X\times_k\mathbf P_k^s$.  The actual Cartesian blowup squares
identify each restriction of the original pair with the preceding
closed map.  Closed immersion is local on this target cover, proving
closedness of the original whole pair.  Its monomorphism follows from
closedness.  Projectivity, compactness and integrality of $X$ are not
needed for this conclusion.
\end{proof}

In particular this applies to the actual Serre families of
$D\otimes H^d$ supplied by the controlled-generators theorem on the
original projective base, without choosing a replacement projective map.

\begin{corollary}[The same global exceptional line has a positive twist]
\label{cor:kn-nadel-nef-big-same-global-twist-very-ample}
\notready
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-same-global-pair-closed}
For some original positive $N$, the whole line
$(D\mathcal O_Y)\otimes\pi^*H^N$ is very ample over the original field.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Compose the first factor of the preceding pair with the original
projective embedding of $X$, then use the actual Segre embedding.
Its hyperplane line is the tensor product of the original first-factor
hyperplane and the same generated-line hyperplane.  These are
$\pi^*H$ and $(D\mathcal O_Y)\otimes\pi^*H^d$, respectively, by their
original whole hyperplane comparisons.  Tensor symmetry and the actual
power-addition isomorphism identify their product with
$(D\mathcal O_Y)\otimes\pi^*H^{d+1}$.  The composite is closed and
$d+1>0$, giving the assertion without a positivity certificate.
\end{proof}

These statements concern the actual global Rees map and its exceptional
line.  They neither define numerical bigness nor establish the numerical
Kodaira lemma, nef-plus-ample ampleness, or Kawamata--Viehweg vanishing.
