\section{Actual total-model norms through the noncentral product}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:native-tc-product-polynomial-gauss-source}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.productGaussSource, EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.productGaussSource_map}
\leanok
For every original valued point $x$ and positive radius $r$, the
actual product Gauss point extends the entire polynomial Gauss
valuation through the actual composite map from the noncentral
product into the total test configuration and then the parameter
line. Its native image is exactly the constructed
relative total-space Gauss point, as a whole valued point.
\uses{thm:native-tc-global-laurent-sections}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Composition of the native point maps identifies the image under
the composite with the image of the actual total-space Gauss point
under the original parameter projection. The latter has the whole
original polynomial Gauss valuation by construction. This proves
the prescribed-base condition. The same whole point identity makes
the two relative total-space points equal, since their base proofs
are propositions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:native-tc-product-frame-true-norm}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.exists_productResidueFrame_gaussPowerSectionNorm}
\leanok
Let the original field be trivially valued and let $0<r\leq1$.
Assume the actual total-space module $M$ is invertible, with its
original action linearization and fibre-one identification with $L$.
For every integer $n\geq0$ and original point $x$, there exists a
native residue-fibre frame of the actual product pullback of
$L^{\otimes n}$ at the product Gauss point such that, simultaneously
for every original $s\in H^0(\mathcal X,M^{\otimes n})$, the true
proper-total-model norm of $s$ at the actual total Gauss point equals
the native valuation of the coefficient of its actually transported
product section in that frame. The frame is derived from the actual
proper center, and no proper-model norm is assigned to the noncentral
Laurent product.
\uses{thm:native-tc-product-polynomial-gauss-source,thm:proper-model-norm-source-frame-existence,thm:native-tc-global-laurent-sections}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The polynomial Gauss valuation is bounded by one because the field
valuation is trivial and the radius is at most one. The original
test-configuration projection is proper, and $M^{\otimes n}$ is
invertible. Apply the proper-target source-frame existence theorem
to the actual product-to-total morphism, its actual relative product
Gauss point and the genuine whole line-power isomorphism derived
from the original action and fibre data. This yields one native
source residue frame computing every original whole section norm.
The preceding whole relative-point equality identifies the target
with the original total Gauss point. The actual global pullback unit
is the section obtained by pulling the original section morphism
and evaluating at one; the two canonical original section-morphism
constructions coincide by definition. Hence the target norm is
exactly the already defined original total-space section norm.
The source coefficient is precisely the defined actual
transported product section. Every zero section and degree zero are
retained throughout.
\end{proof}
