\begin{theorem}[Entire tensor comparison with the original quotient]
\label{algebra:surjective-base-change-original-quotient}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.ideal,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.quotientRingEquiv,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.quotientRingEquiv_mk,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.coefficientMap,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.quotientAlgebra,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.tensorQuotientEquiv,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.tensorQuotientEquiv_one_tmul,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.coefficientMap_algebraMap,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.coefficientMap_eq_of_comp,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.tensorQuotientEquiv_tmul,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.quotient_isScalarTower}
\leanok
Let $R\to S$ be a genuinely surjective homomorphism of arbitrary
commutative rings and $B$ any actual $R$-algebra. Put
$I=\ker(R\to S)B$, retaining the original ideal rather than its radical.
There is a canonical descended $S$-action on $B/I$ and an actual
$S$-algebra isomorphism $S\otimes_R B\simeq B/I$. Every original
$1\otimes b$ maps to the original class of $b$, and $s\otimes b$
maps to the product of the descended $s$ and that original class.
The entire original coefficient square commutes and characterizes
the descended coefficient map. The actual scalar tower is retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine tensor/quotient isomorphism identifies $B/I$ with
$B\otimes_R(R/\ker(R\to S))$. Commute the factors and apply the
actual first isomorphism theorem for the given surjective coefficient
map. This constructs a whole ring isomorphism with $S\otimes_R B$
which retains every original $b$ as $1\otimes b$. Transport the
actual $S$ coefficient inclusion through its inverse to define the
descended action. The inverse ring map is an $S$-algebra isomorphism
for this actual action. Multiplicativity gives the full pure-tensor
formula, with no argument restricted to generators.

The genuine tensor balance identity identifies both images of every
original $r\in R$, proving the entire coefficient square and actual
scalar tower. Surjectivity of $R\to S$ makes any other coefficient
map with that same whole square equal to this constructed map on
every $s\in S$. No finiteness, flatness, domain, reducedness or rank
comparison is assumed, and every original nilpotent is retained.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine finite, free, rank and etale pullback]
\label{algebra:surjective-base-change-free-etale}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.finite,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.free,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.finrank,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.etale,
EnvelopeProperty.SurjectiveBaseChange.isReduced_of_field}
\leanok
\uses{algebra:surjective-base-change-original-quotient}
The rank values here are Lean's natural-valued \texttt{Module.finrank};
for an infinite free basis this convention gives zero. In every finite
module application it is the usual finite free rank.
The actual quotient action above preserves module finiteness, module
freeness and etaleness of the original $R$-algebra. When the original
module is free and the coefficient rings satisfy the strong rank
condition, its free rank equals the $S$-free rank of the whole original
quotient. If $S$ is a field and the original algebra is etale, the
actual original quotient is reduced.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Tensor base change preserves actual finite modules, actual free
modules and etale algebras. Transport these structures through the
proved whole $S$-algebra isomorphism. Its actual linear equivalence
identifies ranks, and the genuine tensor basis rank theorem identifies
the scalar-extended free rank with the original free rank. Over a
field, the true formally unramified algebra is reduced. No finite
module assumption is required for the free-rank equality itself, and
no generic-degree or normalization comparison is inferred here.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual etale domain algebras are reduced]
\label{algebra:etale-domain-generic-reduced}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.isReduced_of_etale_of_domain}
\leanok
If $R$ is any domain and $B$ an actual etale $R$-algebra, then $B$ is
reduced. There is no Noetherian or finite-module assumption.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $K$ be the genuine fraction field of $R$. The actual algebra
$K\otimes_R B$ is etale over $K$, hence formally unramified and
reduced. Etaleness makes the original $B$ flat over $R$, while the
actual fraction field is a faithful $R$-algebra. Flat tensoring of the
injective original coefficient map therefore makes the whole map
$B\to K\otimes_R B$, $b\mapsto1\otimes b$, injective. Every original
nilpotent maps to zero in the reduced generic algebra and is therefore
zero by this actual injection. This is a proof through actual generic
base change, with no reducedness certificate as an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal line tensor equals the entire original unreduced preimage]
\label{algebra:line-tensor-original-unreduced-preimage}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLine_ker_le,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLineHom,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLineHom_comp,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLineHom_principalLineHom,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.linePreimageIdeal_eq_surjectiveBaseChange,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.lineTensorPullbackEquiv,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.lineTensorPullbackEquiv_one_tmul,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.lineTensorPullbackEquiv_symm_mk,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.normalizedLineProductHom_eq_originalNormalization_comp}
\leanok
\uses{algebra:surjective-base-change-original-quotient,algebra:localized-line-original-preimage,algebra:whole-normalized-line-product-map}
Let $F$ be any field, let $a,b$ be the actual affine-line coefficients,
and suppose the chosen direction coordinate $b_j$ is nonzero. Write
$R=F[\mathbf X]_g$ and $C=F[T]_{g(a+bT)}$, and let
$\phi:R\to B$ be any actual $F$-algebra homomorphism. For the original
transverse equation image ideal $I\subseteq B$, there is an actual
whole coefficient homomorphism $C\to B/I$ and a genuine
$C$-algebra isomorphism $C\otimes_R B\simeq B/I$.
Both directions retain every original $B$ element. The actual line
map to the whole component normalization product factors through this
entire original unreduced quotient, with its proved original map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual localized line substitution is surjective because the
nonzero direction coordinate gives a true polynomial preimage of $T$,
and the original principal-localization denominators are retained.
Its exact kernel is the localized original transverse equation ideal.
Extending this exact ideal along $\phi$ is exactly the original $I$,
by the actual composition law for ideal images; there is no radical.
Consequently the original composite $R\to B\to B/I$ kills the entire
line substitution kernel. The genuine surjective universal property
constructs $C\to B/I$ and proves its whole original $R$ triangle.

Apply the preceding actual surjective base-change construction and
the proved ideal equality. The two genuine quotient actions agree:
on every element of $C$, choose an actual $R$ preimage and use the
entire original coefficient square. This makes the ring comparison an
algebra isomorphism for the actual descended line homomorphism, not
an action assumed to agree. Its original-element formulas and both
inverse laws prove the entire asserted comparison. Finally choose an
actual $R$ preimage of each element of $C$. The existing full original
ambient formula for the normalized line product and the actual
unreduced normalization-map quotient formula prove equality of the
entire line homomorphisms through $B/I$. No component, reducedness,
normalization identification or rank certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual line pullback transports original free rank and etaleness]
\label{algebra:line-tensor-free-rank-etale}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLineHom_finite,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLineHom_free,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLineHom_etale,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLineHom_finrank,
EnvelopeProperty.AffineLine.originalLineQuotient_isReduced_of_etale}
\leanok
\uses{algebra:line-tensor-original-unreduced-preimage,algebra:surjective-base-change-free-etale,algebra:etale-domain-generic-reduced}
Actual finiteness, freeness or etaleness of the given ambient map
passes to the entire original line pullback. Rank means the same
natural-valued \texttt{Module.finrank} convention above. If $g$ and $g(a+bT)$
are nonzero and the original module is free, the free rank of that
original module equals the line-free rank of the actual unreduced
preimage. Actual ambient etaleness and $g(a+bT)\ne0$ make the whole
original line quotient reduced.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The literal comparison transports the genuine tensor finite, free and
etale structures to the actual line action on $B/I$. Nonzero $g$ and
nonzero line restriction make the actual ambient and line localizations
domains, so both satisfy the strong rank condition. The actual linear
equivalence and true tensor free-rank theorem give the equality of
these two free ranks. For reducedness, the actual line algebra is
etale over the genuine domain $C$; apply the preceding proved generic
reducedness producer.

These statements may be applied on a genuine chosen etale or free
principal open by using its actual localized homomorphism. They do
not construct such an open, identify normalization with the original
quotient on that open, compare the normalized product rank with an
original generic degree, or assert degree preservation when the
original generic pullback is nonreduced. Those next comparisons
require genuine further algebra; nilpotent and inseparable cases
are not erased by a claimed degree identity.
\end{proof}
