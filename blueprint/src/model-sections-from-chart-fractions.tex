\section{Whole model sections are exactly actual regular chart fractions}
\label{sec:model-sections-from-chart-fractions}

\begin{lemma}[Actual original chart fractions admit genuine homogeneous denominator clearing {\cite[\S1.2]{BJ22}}]
\label{lem:model-chart-fraction-clearing}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.exists_clear_chart_fraction, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.exists_uniform_clear_chart_fractions}
\leanok
\uses{lem:gl-charts,def:gl-sections}
For an actual original multiprojective model, a rational coefficient $f$ whose
fraction $f/D_j(a)$ belongs to its whole actual chart algebra admits an actual
nonnegative clearing exponent $b$, with $a+b$ nonnegative and $fD_j(b)$ in the
actual homogeneous piece of degree $a+b$. If these fractions are regular on
every nonempty original coordinate chart, one common original exponent
works on every such chart. No denominator-clearing premise is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Represent the whole chart-algebra element by an actual homogeneous fraction
$g/D_j(c)$. Choose a nonnegative exponent $n$ with $a+n$ nonnegative. The original
fraction identity implies $fD_j(c+n)=gD_j(a+n)$. The right side belongs to the
actual homogeneous piece by multiplication of actual pieces, giving one
original clearing exponent. For all charts, there are only finitely many
actual coordinate chart indices. Sum their internally obtained clearing
exponents and add a nonnegative exponent offsetting every negative entry of
$a$. The resulting exponent dominates every original chart exponent. Multiply
each genuine homogeneous numerator by the remaining actual chart monomial;
this retains its precise original degree and gives one common exponent.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole original graded sections are precisely whole regular chart fractions {\cite[\S1.2]{BJ22}}]
\label{thm:model-sections-iff-chart-fractions}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.mem_sections_iff_chart_fractions}
\leanok
\uses{lem:model-chart-fraction-clearing,lem:gl-sections,lem:gl-model-change}
For every actual original integral multiprojective model, every integer
multidegree $a$, and every coefficient $f$ in its original whole function field,
$f$ is a genuine model global section exactly when its original fraction
$f/D_j(a)$ belongs to the whole actual algebra of every original nonempty
coordinate chart.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A genuine section has actual regular chart fractions by the original section
chart theorem. Conversely, the preceding internally derived common exponent
$C$ clears all actual nonempty charts. The genuine coordinate pigeonhole
argument makes $((M_c+1)C_c)_c$ admissible: every monomial of that multidegree
either contains a zero original coordinate, when the product is zero, or
has in each factor a coordinate with exponent at least $C_c$. Divide out those
actual chart powers and multiply the remaining actual coordinate monomial
by the already cleared whole homogeneous numerator. This gives precisely
the genuine original admissibility condition defining the whole model
section space. The original function field, homogeneous algebra and all
coordinate charts are retained throughout, with no normality assumption.
\end{proof}
