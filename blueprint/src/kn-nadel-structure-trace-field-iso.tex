\section{The native finite-field trace respects whole scheme isomorphisms}

\begin{lemma}[Whole original finite-field trace invariance]
\label{lem:kn-structure-trace-field-iso}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.fieldStructureTrace_iso_top}
\leanok
Let $q:Z\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ be finite over the field $K$ and
$e:W\simeq Z$ any actual scheme isomorphism. For every original whole
section of $Z$, its native trace for $q$ equals the native trace of its
actual pullback for the original composite $W\to Z\to\operatorname{Spec}K$.
No coefficient-map or trace-matching certificate is assumed.
\uses{lem:kn-structure-trace-field-producer}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual map on whole sections of $e$ is a ring isomorphism. It is an
isomorphism of the original $K$-algebras: the coefficient action is the
original map on sections composed with the inverse spectrum--sections
isomorphism for $K$, and the formula for sections of a composite scheme
map makes this square commute on every coefficient. Native algebra
trace is invariant under this algebra isomorphism, since the two
multiplication endomorphisms are conjugate. Apply the proved whole
section formula for the actual finite-field sheaf trace on each side.
This gives the asserted equality for every original section.
\end{proof}
