\section{The genuine multiplicative-group scheme}

\begin{definition}[The actual Laurent multiplicative-group object]
\label{def:native-multiplicative-group}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeMultiplicativeGroup,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeMultiplicativeGroup_isGrpObj,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeLaurentTensorEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeMultiplicativeGroupProductIso}
\leanok
The actual scheme $\operatorname{Spec}R[z,z^{-1}]$ over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$ is a genuine group object, supplied by its actual
Laurent Hopf algebra.  The actual iterated Laurent scheme is isomorphic
to the whole product of two copies of this group scheme.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The Laurent group algebra has the actual Hopf structure
$\Delta(z)=z\otimes z$, $\epsilon(z)=1$, and $S(z)=z^{-1}$.
The established Hopf-algebra-to-affine-group-scheme functor gives all
actual group-object axioms.  The genuine scalar tensor equivalence
$R[z,z^{-1}]\otimes_R R[w,w^{-1}]
\simeq R[z,z^{-1}][w,w^{-1}]$, followed by the actual affine scheme
tensor pullback comparison, gives the whole group-product isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual group laws are the original Laurent coordinate maps]
\label{lem:native-multiplicative-group-coordinate-laws}
\uses{def:native-multiplicative-group,lem:native-laurent-action-coordinate-laws}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.parameterLaurentUnit_zpow,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.parameterLaurentProductUnit_zpow,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeLaurentCounit_eq_eval,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeLaurentComul_eq_multiplication,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeMultiplicativeGroup_one,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeMultiplicativeGroup_mul}
\leanok
The actual group-object unit is the genuine Laurent evaluation at $1$.
Under the whole double-Laurent product isomorphism, its actual
multiplication is precisely the previously constructed Laurent
coefficient map $z\mapsto wz$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Both unit ring maps fix original coefficients and send every Laurent
power to $1$, so Laurent polynomial induction identifies the counit
with the actual evaluation homomorphism.  The comultiplication sends
each Laurent monomial $az^m$ to $a(z^m\otimes z^m)$.
The genuine tensor equivalence sends this to
$a\,z^m w^m$, exactly the actual Laurent evaluation at the product unit.
Laurent polynomial induction identifies the entire ring maps.
Contravariance of Spec and cancellation of the two inverse actual
tensor-pullback comparisons give the full group-object identities.
\end{proof}
