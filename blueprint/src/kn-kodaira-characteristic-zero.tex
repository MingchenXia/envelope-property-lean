\section{Kodaira vanishing on the original characteristic-zero scheme}

\begin{definition}\label{def:kn-global-witt-di-input}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.KodairaCharacteristicZero.GlobalWittDecompositionInput}
\leanok
The global Witt decomposition interface asserts, for every prime $p$ and every
field $K$ of characteristic $p$, the actual global derived decomposition
when $K$ is perfect, on the literal special fibre of every separated smooth $W_2(K)$ model of
relative dimension $n<p$.  Its derived category is the standard one in
universe $u+1$.  It contains
no projection formula, connection, canonical comparison, specialization,
or cohomological vanishing conclusion.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[The actual Witt decomposition supplies the global interface]
\label{lem:kn-proved-global-witt-di}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.DeligneIllusieInput.provedWittDecompositionInput,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.KodairaCharacteristicZero.globalWittDecomposition}
\uses{cor:di-actual-witt-full-decomposition,def:kn-global-witt-di-input}
\leanok
The global Witt decomposition interface holds. Its source consists of the
original exterior cotangent sheaves on the actual relative Frobenius twist,
and its target is the original relative de Rham complex extended by zero
to integer degrees.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix a prime, a perfect field of that characteristic, and an actual smooth
separated Witt-two model of relative dimension $n<p$. The proved full
Deligne--Illusie decomposition applies to precisely that model. Its source
is the finite coproduct of the original exterior sheaves in degrees zero
through $n$, extended to integer degrees. These are exactly the summands
of the stated interface. The target is the same original relative de
Rham complex on the same special fibre and Frobenius twist. Thus the
proved isomorphism inhabits the interface for this model. Quantifying
over all such models, fields and primes proves the assertion; choosing
the standard derived category gives the indicated universe.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kodaira vanishing from the proved global decomposition]
\label{thm:kn-original-characteristic-zero-kodaira}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.KodairaCharacteristicZero.kodaira_tensorObj,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.KodairaCharacteristicZero.kodaira}
\leanok
\uses{lem:kn-proved-global-witt-di,thm:kn-arithmetic-original-ample-model,thm:kn-arithmetic-original-dimension-witt,thm:kn-ample-scalar-extension,cor:kn-actual-witt-stage-kodaira,thm:kn-arithmetic-canonical-specialization}
Let $k$ be a characteristic-zero field, $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ a
smooth scheme of relative dimension $n$, and $L$ an ample line in the
original positive-power projective embedding sense. Then
\[
 H^{q+1}(X,\omega_{X/k}\otimes L)=0\qquad(q\geq0).
\]
No arithmetic model, canonical fibre comparison, vanishing certificate,
or separate projectivity premise is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the positive-power embedding contained in the original ample datum.
The original ample model theorem constructs a finitely generated integer
subalgebra $S\subset k$, the actual canonical coefficient family of that
same embedding, and an ample model $M$ of the original $L$, smooth of
relative dimension $n$.  The model includes the actual whole pullback
isomorphism to $L$; it is constructed from descended sections and actual
descended inverse maps, rather than assumed.

Apply the dimension-preserving Witt stage producer with bound $n$ after
all these line coefficients have been chosen.  It internally localizes
the integer base to make it smooth and produces a closed point and its
actual $W_2$ lift at a prime $p>n$.  Its further coefficient ring $R$ still
contains $S$.  Pull back $M$ along the actual coefficient transition.
The proved arbitrary scalar extension theorem preserves ampleness, and
pullback composition preserves its whole original comparison to $L$.
The resulting base is Noetherian because it is isomorphic to a principal
localization of a finite-type integer ring.

On the actual residue family, scalar extension again gives the actual
ample residue line.  Apply Witt Kodaira vanishing to the produced smooth
Witt model and its original dimension bound. The preceding lemma supplies
the global decomposition at its actual finite residue field. The proved comparison with the literal DI
special fibre retains its original structural morphism and original line.
Choose standard derived categories internally.

The arithmetic canonical object tensored with the actual model line is
invertible and has its proved whole canonical tensor comparison on every
field fibre.  The literal quotient residue field of the Witt stage and
the localization residue field used in specialization are identified by
the actual bijective quotient-to-residue algebra map, not by definitional
equality.  Actual fibre isomorphism and functorial cohomology transport
provide the required closed-fibre vanishing.  The proved geometric
specialization theorem now gives vanishing on the original Cartesian
$k$-fibre: the map $R\hookrightarrow k$ is injective, and the actual model
has its constructed projective factorization and flatness from smoothness.
Its original Cartesian square and the actual line comparison identify the
answer with the stated original $X,f,L$.  Transport along the whole line
isomorphism and the native tensor comparison gives the displayed claim.
\end{proof}
