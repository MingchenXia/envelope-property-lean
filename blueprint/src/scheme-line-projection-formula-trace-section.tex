\section{Every local coefficient of the same inverse-PF trace}

\begin{lemma}[All local coefficients of the original line trace]
\label{lem:line-pf-unit-trace-native-local-coefficient}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeLineProjectionFormulaUnit.lineProjectionFormulaObj_unitTrace_app_smul_unit}
\uses{lem:line-pf-native-pure-tensor-readback}
\leanok
For an arbitrary original scheme map $g:Y\to X$, an invertible original
line $L$, and any actual morphism $\tau:g_*\mathcal O_Y\to\mathcal O_X$,
the composite formed from the original left unitors, the inverse of the
original line projection formula and $\tau\otimes\mathrm{id}_L$ sends
$b\eta_L(m)$ to $\tau(b)m$ on every original open. Both local sections
$b$ and $m$ are arbitrary.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original PF sends $b\otimes m$ to $b\otimes\eta_L(m)$ by the
preceding native pure-tensor formula. The original actual structure
left unitor sends this tensor to $b\eta_L(m)$. Composing that same PF
and left unitor with their inverses cancels them. Tensor naturality
then sends $b\otimes m$ to $\tau(b)\otimes m$, whose original left
unitor is precisely $\tau(b)m$. No projection formula or scalar
matching identity is assumed.
\end{proof}
