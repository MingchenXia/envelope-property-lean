\section{Geometric source of the Laurent family}

\begin{lemma}[The whole actual coefficient-extended product]
\label{lem:native-parameter-family-product}
\uses{lem:native-parameter-family-morphisms}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.isPullback_parameterPolynomialBaseChange,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.isPullback_nativeParameterProduct,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeParameterProductIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeParameterProductIso_hom_fst,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeParameterProductIso_hom_snd}
\leanok
For every actual coefficient-ring extension $R\to S$, the native scheme
$\mathbb P^M_{S[t]}$ is the whole actual base product
$\mathbb P^M_{R[t]}\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}S$.
The comparison is an actual scheme isomorphism with both full projection
identities.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The polynomial coefficient square is an actual ring pushout, by the
genuine polynomial tensor-product equivalence.  Taking Spec makes it
a pullback square.  Paste this square to the previously established
whole native polynomial-Proj coefficient-base-change square.  The
universal property of this actual pasted pullback supplies the
isomorphism and both projection identities.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A single morphism from the actual multiplicative-group product]
\label{lem:native-laurent-action-morphism}
\uses{lem:native-parameter-family-product,lem:native-parameter-family-rings}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeParameterLaurentActionMorphism,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeParameterLaurentFamily_evaluate}
\leanok
The single universal Laurent family defines an actual morphism
$\mathbb P^M_{R[t]}\times_R\operatorname{Spec}R[z,z^{-1}]
\to\mathbb P^M_{R[t]}$.
After actual Laurent evaluation at any coefficient-extended unit, the
whole original native morphism is exactly the proved unit parameter
family map.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Transport the actual universal Laurent native family through the inverse
of the preceding actual product isomorphism.  Contravariant coefficient
functoriality and the whole universal ring evaluation identity prove the
whole scheme evaluation identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The universal coordinate action diagrams]
\label{lem:native-laurent-action-coordinate-laws}
\uses{lem:native-laurent-action-morphism,lem:native-parameter-family-rings}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.parameterLaurentProductUnit,EnvelopeProperty.parameterLaurentMultiplicationHom,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeParameterLaurentFamily_one,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeParameterLaurentFamily_mul}
\leanok
Evaluation of the universal family at $z=1$ is the identity whole native
scheme morphism.  On the actual iterated Laurent coefficient scheme,
first acting by $z$ and then by $w$ equals the single action whose
parameter is $wz$.  These are equalities of whole scheme morphisms on the
universal native coordinate sources.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first identity is the whole Laurent evaluation formula at the unit
$1$, followed by the actual identity-family law.  In the iterated Laurent
ring the product of the second Laurent unit with the coefficient image
of the first is an actual unit.  Laurent evaluation at this product is
the actual multiplicative-group multiplication ring map.  Its whole
evaluation identity followed by the already proved actual two-family
composition identity proves the second scheme-morphism equality.
The actual product comparison identifies the single native source with
the whole multiplicative-group product.  The second identity is recorded
on the entire iterated Laurent native coordinate source.
\end{proof}

The coordinate action identities concern the whole schemes.  Restricting
and transporting these identities to the original variety model and
lifting the family to the constructed blowup remain separate steps.
