\section{The original invariant Cartier factors on the normalized PL model}

\begin{lemma}[Original equivariant pullback preserves whole invariant ideals]
\label{lem:pl-original-equivariant-ideal-pullback}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLOriginalModelCompatibility.invariantIdeal_comap}
\leanok
If $f:A\to B$ is an equivariant morphism of original group schemes with
actions and $K\subset O_B$ is an invariant original ideal sheaf, its entire
original ideal pullback $f^{-1}K\,O_A$ is invariant on $A$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $a_A,a_B$ for the two whole action maps and $p_A,p_B$ for the whole
second projections. Equivariance gives
$f\circ a_A=a_B\circ(1\times f)$, and the product projection square gives
$f\circ p_A=p_B\circ(1\times f)$. Functoriality of entire ideal comap and
$a_B^{-1}K=p_B^{-1}K$ therefore give
$a_A^{-1}(f^{-1}K)=p_A^{-1}(f^{-1}K)$ as original ideal sheaves, including
their nonradical scheme structure.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Canonical original normalized Cartier factor linearizations]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-cartier-factor-linearizations}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousNativeCoordinateIso_isModHom,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousNativeCartierFactor_invariant,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeCartierFactor_invariant,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousRatioNativeGmLinearization}
\leanok
\uses{lem:pl-original-equivariant-ideal-pullback,thm:pl-original-normalized-simultaneous-gm-action}
For the same original two parameter-invariant ideals $I,J$, the original
Cartier factors on the actual normalized simultaneous blowup are invariant
for its constructed actual action. Their original ideal modules carry the
canonical whole action comparisons; their tensor-dual ratio carries its
canonical whole linearization, satisfying the unit and multiplication cocycle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The whole original coordinate isomorphism compares the comap of each original
simultaneous-model Cartier factor with the constructed Cartier
factor on the homogeneous-coordinate blowup. The coordinate isomorphism is
equivariant by construction of the transported original action. Apply the
preceding lemma along its inverse to derive invariance of the same actual
original simultaneous-model factor. Apply that lemma again along the actual
equivariant normalization projection to obtain invariance of the original
normalized factor, with no flatness assumption on normalization. Its Cartier
property follows from dominance and original integrality. The group action
and the second projection are flat over the original field. Canonical flat
Cartier module pullback therefore produces the whole inclusion-preserving
factor-module comparison. Tensor pullback and duality give the ratio
comparison. Its unit identity and whole multiplication cocycle follow from
the corresponding canonical factor identities and tensor-dual coherence.
\end{proof}
