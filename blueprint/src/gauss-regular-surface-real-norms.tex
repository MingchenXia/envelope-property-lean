\section{Actual fixed-radius norms on the original surface field}

Let $k$ be a field, let $S$ be the SAME original Noetherian local domain
with its actual $k$-algebra structure, and suppose
$\mathfrak m_S=(\pi,z)$, $\pi$ is prime, and $\pi\nmid z$.
Write $K=\operatorname{Frac}S$, $T=S_{(\pi)}$, and keep the actual weighted
DVRs $V_N$ and their previously constructed embeddings into this SAME $K$.
The coefficient action on $K$ is the original action through $S$.
Fix an original $t\in S$ with $t\ne0$ and $\pi\mid t$, and a real radius
$0<r<1$. No family of norms or comparison of orders is supplied.

\begin{definition}[Actual scaled points on the same original field]
\label{def:gauss-surface-actual-scaled-field-points}
\uses{thm:gauss-regular-surface-original-dvr,lem:gauss-surface-original-vertical-fraction-identities,lem:gauss-surface-original-residual-vertical-dvrs,thm:actual-dvr-fraction-point,def:gauss-surface-original-residual-vertical-rings}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.weightedPoint,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.verticalPoint}
\leanok
\leavevmode\par
For $c>0$, let $w_{N,c}$ be the actual extended DVR point of $V_N$ with
uniformizer norm $\exp(-c)$, and let $w_{T,c}$ be the corresponding actual
point of $T$. Both are genuine elements of $\operatorname{Berk}_k K$ on
the literal original field, with the literal original coefficient action.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Give each actual DVR its $k$-action through the original map from $S$.
The previously proved identity for EVERY original coefficient says that
the composite into $K$ is its original coefficient map. Applied to the
original coefficients from $k$, this proves the actual scalar tower.
The already constructed embeddings make $K$ the fraction ring of these
same DVRs. Apply the existing actual DVR fraction-point construction with
these maps and towers. Its coefficient norm is trivial because every
nonzero original coefficient from the field $k$ is a unit.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[All original surface coefficients remain bounded]
\label{lem:gauss-surface-scaled-field-points-bounded}
\uses{def:gauss-surface-actual-scaled-field-points,thm:actual-dvr-fraction-point,lem:gauss-surface-original-vertical-fraction-identities,thm:gauss-regular-surface-original-dvr}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.weightedPoint_coefficient,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.verticalPoint_coefficient,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.weightedPoint_coefficient_le_one,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.verticalPoint_coefficient_le_one}
\leanok
\leavevmode\par
Every original $s\in S$ has its exact original extended DVR coefficient
value $\exp(-c\operatorname{ord}(s))$, in particular at most one at every $w_{N,c}$ and
every $w_{T,c}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Its literal image in the actual DVR has extended norm at most one.
The original whole-coefficient embedding identity identifies that value
with the value of the original image of $s$ in $K$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Native whole-field order determines the real logarithm]
\label{lem:gauss-surface-native-order-real-log}
\uses{def:gauss-surface-actual-scaled-field-points,def:gauss-surface-same-field-native-orders,thm:actual-dvr-fraction-point,lem:gauss-surface-original-vertical-fraction-identities,thm:gauss-regular-surface-original-dvr}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.weightedPoint_log_of_order,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.verticalPoint_log_of_order}
\leanok
\leavevmode\par
If the actual native order of $f\in K^\times$ for the indicated original
DVR is $\exp(a)$, then the corresponding actual point satisfies
$\log|f|=-ca$. In particular this uses the native integer order on the
whole original fraction field, including negative orders.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write the SAME nonzero original field element as a ratio of two actual
nonzero DVR elements, of actual finite additive orders $m,n$. The native
comparison between whole-field order and the maximal-ideal valuation gives
native orders $\exp(m)$ and $\exp(n)$ for these literal coefficients.
Thus its actual whole-field order is $\exp(m-n)$, and injectivity of the
native exponential gives $m-n=a$. The actual extended coefficient formula
gives norm $\exp(-c)^m/\exp(-c)^n$. Both factors are positive, so taking
the ordinary real logarithm gives $-c(m-n)=-ca$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual parameter has positive vertical order]
\label{lem:gauss-surface-parameter-positive-vertical-order}
\uses{thm:gauss-surface-original-eventual-orders,lem:gauss-surface-original-residual-vertical-dvrs,lem:gauss-surface-original-vertical-fraction-identities,thm:gauss-regular-surface-original-dvr,def:gauss-surface-original-residual-vertical-rings}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.exists_parameter_orders,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.verticalParameterOrder,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.weightedParameterOrder,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.verticalParameterOrder_spec,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.weightedParameterOrder_spec}
\leanok
\leavevmode\par
Internally produce integers $e,c\in\mathbf N$ with $e>0$ such that the
actual vertical additive order of $t$ is $e$, and the actual $V_N$-order
of $t$ is $eN+c$ for every $N>c$. Moreover its actual vertical
order and every actual weighted order $d_N$ for $N>0$ are finite positive
natural numbers, defined by their genuine additive orders.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine eventual-order theorem to this nonzero original $t$.
The actual image of $\pi$ is a vertical uniformizer, and divides the
actual image of $t$. The native additive-order divisibility criterion
therefore gives vertical order at least one. Thus the internally
produced $e$ is positive. The remaining assertion is exactly that
theorem's actual eventual order formula. Each actual coefficient image
of $t$ is nonzero, by its actual embedding into $K$. The image of $\pi$
divides it and has weighted order $N>0$, giving positivity for EVERY $N$.
This does not assert that
$t$ is a unit times $\pi^e$ in $S$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Internally normalized actual fixed-radius points]
\label{thm:gauss-surface-actual-fixed-radius-points}
\uses{lem:gauss-surface-parameter-positive-vertical-order,def:gauss-surface-actual-scaled-field-points,lem:gauss-surface-scaled-field-points-bounded,lem:gauss-surface-native-order-real-log,berkovich:actual-trivial-parameter-gauss-evaluation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.normalizedWeightedPoint,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.normalizedVerticalPoint,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.normalizedWeightedPoint_gauss,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.normalizedVerticalPoint_gauss}
\leanok
\leavevmode\par
For EVERY $N>0$, normalize by the actual positive order $d_N$ of $t$
in $V_N$. For the internally produced vertical order $e$, put
\[
 N_n=n+1,\qquad
 \varepsilon_n=\frac{-\log r}{d_{N_n}},\qquad
 \varepsilon_0=\frac{-\log r}{e}.
\]
Eventually $d_{N_n}=eN_n+c$ with the internally produced $c$.
These numbers are positive. The literal actual points
$w_n=w_{N_n,\varepsilon_n}$ and $w_0=w_{T,\varepsilon_0}$ satisfy
$|t|_{w_n}=|t|_{w_0}=r$. Every original surface coefficient is bounded
by one, and for EVERY original polynomial $F\in k[X]$,
\[
 |F(t)|_{w_n}=|F(t)|_{w_0}=|F|_{\mathrm{Gauss},r}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The displayed denominators are the actual positive orders just proved.
Use the actual additive orders $d_{N_n}$ and $e$ in the original
coefficient norm formula. The chosen scales give $\exp(\log r)=r$.
The whole-coefficient boundedness lemma gives the surface assertion.
Apply the existing polynomial Gauss comparison to each genuine point
and its now proved parameter value $r\in(0,1)$. This proves EVERY
polynomial identity without supplying a restriction certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole-field convergence of the genuine points]
\label{thm:gauss-surface-actual-fixed-radius-convergence}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.exists_normalized_log_profile,EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.tendsto_normalizedWeightedPoint}
\leanok
\leavevmode\par
\uses{thm:gauss-surface-actual-fixed-radius-points,thm:gauss-surface-original-fraction-eventual-orders,lem:gauss-surface-native-order-real-log,lem:gauss-surface-canonical-vertical-leading-field}
The SAME actual points $w_n$ tend to the SAME actual vertical point
$w_0$ in the genuine evaluation topology of $\operatorname{Berk}_k K$.
For every nonzero original $f\in K$, let $a,b\in\mathbf Z$ be internally
produced by its genuine eventual-order theorem. Then, eventually,
\[
 \log|f|_{w_n}=\frac{a}{e}\log r
       -\varepsilon_n\left(b-\frac{a}{e}c\right),\qquad
 \log|f|_{w_0}=\frac{a}{e}\log r.
\]
Here $a$ is the true vertical order, and $b$ is the true residual order
of its leading unit under the canonical original residue-field map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The old whole-field theorem gives the actual orders $aN+b$ eventually
for the SAME original $f$, together with its actual vertical order and
canonical leading-unit residue. Apply the native-order logarithm lemma
to these actual orders. Substitution of the displayed $\varepsilon_n$
and the eventual identity $d_{N_n}=eN_n+c$, followed by an algebraic
rearrangement, give the first identity, including the
correction term $-(a/e)c$. The vertical formula gives the second.
Since $N_n\to\infty$ and $e>0$, one has $\varepsilon_n\to0$.
Thus the actual log values converge for every nonzero original $f$;
exponentiation gives convergence of their actual positive norm values.
At $f=0$ all values are zero. The topology on genuine field-valued
Berkovich points is induced by ALL evaluations $K\to\mathbf R_{\ge0}$.
Their pointwise convergence therefore proves the asserted native
point convergence, rather than convergence of an auxiliary parameter.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The exact fixed-base corrected quotient]
\label{cor:gauss-surface-actual-fixed-radius-log-quotient}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussRegularSurfaceRealNorms.exists_normalized_log_quotient}
\leanok
\leavevmode\par
\uses{thm:gauss-surface-actual-fixed-radius-convergence,lem:gauss-surface-canonical-vertical-leading-field}
For every nonzero original $f$ the actual quotient
\[
 \frac{\log|f|_{w_n}-\log|f|_{w_0}}{\varepsilon_n}
\]
is eventually equal to $-\bigl(b-(a/e)c\bigr)$, and hence tends to that
number. Equivalently the actual negative-log quotient is eventually
$b-(a/e)c$ and tends to that number. If its true vertical order is zero,
the negative-log quotient
is exactly its true original residual order $b$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Subtract the two genuine log formulas and divide by the strictly
positive actual $\varepsilon_n$. This gives eventual equality, which
implies the limit. For $a=0$ the correction vanishes. The native
leading-residue theorem identifies this $b$ with the actual residual
order; no desired slope is an input.
\end{proof}

This construction concerns the literal original field and actual bounded
surface coefficients. It supplies neither a global surface selection nor
a representation of arbitrary harmonic functions. Transport to an
original global model and its original line is a separate geometric step.
