\section{Actual Jacobian monomial neighbourhoods in adapted SNC charts}

\begin{theorem}[A principal-open monomial identity for the actual Jacobian]\label{thm:sc0-native-snc-jacobian-neighbourhood}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.exists_principal_monomial_jacobian_neighbourhood}
\leanok
\uses{lem:sc0-native-affine-transform-ideal,lem:sc0-monomial-transform-localized-stalk,cor:sc0-jacobian-localized-stalk-monomial,lem:sc0-snc-chart-prime-germs,lem:sc0-etale-stalk-differential-basis,lem:sc0-jacobian-monomial-spreading,lem:sc0-resolution-stalk-scalar-towers}
Let $\pi:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ be a morphism over $k$, isomorphic off the support of an actual
ideal sheaf $J$, and let $Y$ be smooth of pure dimension $n$ over a field $k$.
Give $R$ actual \'etale coordinates of length $n$. On an actual affine adapted
SNC chart $U$ about $x$, suppose the actual native total transform of $J$ is a
monomial in the boundary chart functions. There is a principal open $D(s)$ of
$U$ containing $x$ on which the actual original-base Jacobian image ideal is
generated by a monomial in those same boundary chart functions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original section and germ maps give all scalar towers. The actual chart's
monomial total transform produces \'etaleness on its full boundary complement.
Pass this property to the boundary-localized stalk using the constructed
localization map. The actual chart differential basis passes to the stalk, and
the SNC data proves the boundary germs are prime there. The localized-stalk
Jacobian theorem therefore makes the actual stalk image ideal a boundary
monomial. Base change identifies this ideal with the extension of the original
actual affine image ideal. Its monomial equality spreads to some element $s$
outside the point's prime by Noetherian finite generation. Thus $x\in D(s)$ and
the asserted principal affine monomial identity holds there.
\end{proof}
