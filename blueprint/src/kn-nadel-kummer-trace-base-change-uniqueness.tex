\section{Intrinsic trace normalization after actual flat base change}
\label{sec:kn-nadel-kummer-trace-base-change-uniqueness}

All rings and maps in this section retain the original coordinate-power
action.  Put $A=R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, $B=R[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$,
$\varphi(x_j)=t_j^{d_j+1}$ and $C=S\otimes_{A}B$ for the actual map
$a:A\to S$.  Write $\ell:S\to C$ and $r:B\to C$ for its actual
tensor inclusions.  The already constructed $C$-linear dual frame and
canonical counit are used without modification.

\begin{lemma}[Native trace normalization on the whole tensor algebra]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-tensor-trace-normalization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.trace_one_tmul,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.trace_tmul,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.trace_eq_extended_coefficient,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.baseChangeTrace,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.coefficientFunctional,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.coefficientFunctional_apply,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.baseChangeTrace_tensor,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.baseChangeTrace_eq_coefficient_jacobian}
\leanok
For arbitrary $a$ and nonzero $R$, the native algebra trace satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{C/S}(s\otimes b)
   =s\,a(\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}(b)).
\]
If $\Lambda_C$ is the previously constructed extended top coefficient and
$J=\prod_j(d_j+1)t_j^{d_j}$, then for every $c\in C$,
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{C/S}(c)=\Lambda_C(r(J)c).
\]
No flatness of $S/A$ is needed for these formulas.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Multiplication by $1\otimes b$ on the actual tensor algebra is the
scalar extension of multiplication by $b$ on $B$.  Native linear trace
commutes with scalar extension of a finite free module.  The actual
power-map basis supplies finite freeness, so this gives the formula
for $1\otimes b$; $S$-linearity gives it for every pure tensor.
On $s\otimes b$, the second formula is the already proved original
identity $\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}(b)=\Lambda_B(Jb)$, followed by the
actual extended-coefficient evaluation.  Both sides are additive,
and actual tensor induction proves equality on all of $C$.
\uses{thm:kn-nadel-power-dual-arbitrary-base-change,lem:kn-nadel-kummer-power-map-basis,thm:kn-nadel-polynomial-trace-jacobian}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniqueness after flat base change]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-flat-tensor-trace-uniqueness}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.jacobian_mul_coordinateProduct_baseChange,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.branchPolynomial_baseChange_regular,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.functional_ext_of_trace,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.eq_coefficientFunctional_of_trace,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.coextensionFrame_eq_of_trace}
\leanok
Suppose $R$ is a characteristic-zero domain and $a$ is flat; $S$ need
not be connected or nonzero.  Set $D=\prod_j(d_j+1)x_j$ and
$T=\prod_jt_j$.  The element $a(D)$ acts injectively on $S$, and
\[
 r(J)r(T)=\ell(a(D)).
\]
Two actual $S$-linear functionals $\lambda_i:C\to S$ satisfying
$\lambda_i(r(J)c)=\operatorname{Tr}_{C/S}(c)$ for all $c$ agree on
the whole actual tensor algebra.  In particular each such functional
equals the constructed $\Lambda_C$; its native coextension frame is
the previously constructed actual frame.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each factor of $D$ is nonzero in the original polynomial domain, so
$D$ is regular.  The native regular-element theorem for flat scalar
extension proves that $a(D)$ acts injectively on $S$, without choosing
a component or assuming injectivity of $a$.  Apply the actual right
inclusion to $JT=\varphi(D)$ and use the proved whole tensor coefficient
square to obtain the displayed identity.

Evaluate both normalization identities at $r(T)c$.  Their right sides
are the same intrinsic trace, while their left sides are
$a(D)\lambda_i(c)$ by the displayed identity and actual $S$-linearity.
Injectivity of multiplication by $a(D)$ gives equality at every $c$.
The preceding lemma proves the normalization of the actual constructed
coefficient functional internally.  Substitution in native adjunction
proves equality of the whole final-algebra-linear frames.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-tensor-trace-normalization,thm:kn-nadel-power-dual-arbitrary-base-change,lem:kn-nadel-trace-jacobian-denominator}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Trace normalization for every actual base top form]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-etale-all-topforms-trace-normalization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalDifferentialPullback,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalDifferentialPullback_D,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalPullback,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalPullback_generator,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalCounit_pulled_generator,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalCounit_pulled_topform}
\leanok
Let $R$ be a nonzero commutative ring and suppose $a$ is formally etale.
Flatness and characteristic zero are not needed for this statement.  Let
$\omega_S=\bigwedge^n_S\Omega_{S/R}$ and
$\omega_C=\bigwedge^n_C\Omega_{C/R}$ with the actual original small
coefficient structures.  The genuine native exterior differential
map $P:\omega_S\to\omega_C$ through $\ell$ sends the actual base
wedge to $r(J)$ times the actual target wedge.  The already constructed
canonical counit $\tau:\omega_C\to\omega_S$ satisfies, for every
$c\in C$ and every $w\in\omega_S$,
\[
 \tau(c\,P(w))=\operatorname{Tr}_{C/S}(c)\,w.
\]
Thus normalization on one coordinate generator extends to all actual
base top forms by a proved whole-map identity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original small coefficient square constructs the genuine scalar
tower $R\to S\to C$.  Use its native K\"ahler morphism and the native
exterior universal property to construct $P$.  On the actual base
differential wedge its image is the wedge of $D_C(\ell(a(x_j)))$.
The whole tensor square identifies these functions with
$r(t_j^{d_j+1})$.  The original Jacobian differential identity, carried
through the genuine target exterior map, therefore gives
$P(dx_S)=r(J)dt_C$.

The actual counit coefficient formula and the first lemma now give
the assertion for $w=dx_S$.  The genuine etale top-form basis writes
every actual $w$ uniquely as $s\,dx_S$.  Semilinearity of $P$,
$S$-linearity of $\tau$, and commutativity of $S$ prove the displayed
formula for every $w$.  No coordinate-change or trace-normalization
comparison is supplied as input.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-tensor-trace-normalization,thm:kn-nadel-etale-exterior-counit-square}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniqueness of the actual canonical counit]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-etale-canonical-counit-uniqueness}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalCandidateCoefficient,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalCandidateCoefficient_apply,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalCounit_eq_of_generator,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalCounit_eq_of_topforms}
\leanok
For a flat formally etale $a$ as above, every actual $S$-linear
$\sigma:\omega_C\to\omega_S$ satisfying
$\sigma(cP(w))=\operatorname{Tr}_{C/S}(c)w$ for all actual $c,w$
equals the constructed whole canonical counit.  The same conclusion
holds if the normalization is only given on the actual base wedge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose $c\mapsto\sigma(c\,dt_C)$ with the actual coefficient
trivialization of $\omega_S$.  The result is an actual $S$-linear
functional on $C$.  The proved generator formula for $P$ converts its
normalization into the hypothesis of the flat uniqueness theorem.
It therefore equals the already constructed $\Lambda_C$.  Every
target top form has a unique coefficient in the actual target
top-form basis, so equality of these whole coefficient functionals
proves equality of $\sigma$ and the actual counit on every form.
\uses{thm:kn-nadel-flat-tensor-trace-uniqueness,thm:kn-nadel-etale-all-topforms-trace-normalization}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Invariance under actual coefficient-preserving coordinate automorphisms]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-coordinate-automorphism-counit}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.algebraDifferentialMap,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.algebraDifferentialMap_D,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.algebraExteriorMap,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.algebraExteriorMap_differential_wedge,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.coordinateExteriorMap,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.coordinateExteriorMorphism,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.baseChangeTrace_coordinate,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.coordinateExteriorMap_pulled_generator,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.coordinateExteriorMap_pulled_topform,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelKummerTraceBaseChangeUniqueness.canonicalCounit_coordinate}
\leanok
For a flat formally etale $a$ over a characteristic-zero domain, let
$e:C\simeq_S C$ be any actual $S$-algebra automorphism.  Its genuine
native K\"ahler exterior map $E:\omega_C\to\omega_C$ fixes the
genuine pullback $P(w)$ of every actual $w\in\omega_S$, and
\[
 \tau(E(v))=\tau(v)\qquad(v\in\omega_C).
\]
The coordinate map is actual algebra data; no canonical-form comparison
or counit invariance is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual small coefficient tower to restrict $e$ to an original
$R$-algebra map.  Its native K\"ahler morphism sends $D_C(c)$ to
$D_C(e(c))$, and its exterior map is semilinear through $e$.  Since
$e(\ell(s))=\ell(s)$ for every actual $s$, the actual differential
wedge of the base coordinate images is fixed.  The genuine base
top-form basis, semilinearity, and $e$ fixing the whole coefficient
ring then imply $E(P(w))=P(w)$ for every actual base form.

The same map $E$ is $S$-linear after actual restriction of scalars,
so $\tau\circ E$ is an actual candidate counit.  Its value on
$cP(w)$ is $\tau(e(c)P(w))=\operatorname{Tr}_{C/S}(e(c))w$ by the
proved all-top-form normalization.  Native algebra trace is invariant
under the actual algebra automorphism, so this is
$\operatorname{Tr}_{C/S}(c)w$.  Apply the preceding whole-counit
uniqueness theorem.  No coordinate normalization diagram was assumed.
\uses{thm:kn-nadel-etale-all-topforms-trace-normalization,thm:kn-nadel-etale-canonical-counit-uniqueness}
\end{proof}

This intrinsic normalization is a uniqueness producer, not a new
permanent hypothesis in a vanishing theorem.  To compare genuinely
different charts presenting different finite algebras, their actual
overlap identifications must still be produced.  The theorem above
proves invariance for arbitrary actual automorphisms of the same finite
algebra, without supplying a canonical comparison.  Global canonical
sheaf gluing, local vanishing, and
Nadel vanishing are not inferred from a supplied comparison record.
