\section{Every original tensor section determines the coherent tensor map}

\begin{lemma}[Native coherent tensor extensionality]
\label{lem:scheme-native-coherent-tensor-ext}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorSectionExt.tensorSection_hom_ext}
\leanok
For arbitrary modules $M,N,P$ on a scheme $X$, two morphisms
$M\otimes N\to P$ are equal whenever they agree on every actual pure
tensor section over every original open of $X$. No global generation
or affine hypothesis is required.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Precompose both maps by the inverse of the original monoidal tensor
comparison. The existing sheafification tensor extensionality reduces
equality to the original sheafification-unit images of pure tensors.
Those images followed by this inverse comparison are precisely the
original native tensor sections. The given equality therefore applies
to every required section, and the comparison is an isomorphism.
\end{proof}
