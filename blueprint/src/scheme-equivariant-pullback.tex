\section{The action on the same original scheme fibre product}
\label{sec:scheme-equivariant-pullback}

\begin{theorem}[The native fibre product with its original coefficients]
\label{thm:scheme-equivariant-pullback}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeEquivariantPullback.object, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeEquivariantPullback.firstProjection, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeEquivariantPullback.secondProjection, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeEquivariantPullback.square, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeEquivariantPullback.modObj, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeEquivariantPullback.firstProjection_isModHom, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeEquivariantPullback.secondProjection_isModHom, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeEquivariantPullback.isModHom_of_comp}
\leanok
\uses{thm:pullback-cone-monoid-action}
\source{bj22-trivval:sec:tc}
Let $X,Y,S$ be schemes over the same original coefficient scheme $T$,
and let $f:X\to S$, $g:Y\to S$ be original morphisms over $T$.
Their \emph{same native scheme fibre product} $X\times_SY$,
with its original coefficient map through $X$, is Cartesian
in schemes over $T$. If a monoid scheme $G/T$ acts on all
three original schemes and $f,g$ are equivariant, this same
native scheme fibre product has a derived genuine action.
Both original projections are equivariant; equivariance into
this product is detected by these original projections.
The action on $S$ may be nontrivial, as for the parameter line.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Give the native scheme fibre product its coefficient map
$\operatorname{pr}_XX\to T$. Its first projection is over $T$
by definition. Its second projection is over $T$ by the
original Cartesian equation and the two original coefficient
triangles for $f,g$. The original whole native Cartesian square
remains Cartesian in schemes over $T$: the forgetful functor
creates connected limits, in particular Cartesian squares.
Apply the preceding action construction to precisely this
specified original cone. It derives the genuine action and
both complete projection equations. Its projection detection
statement also proves the last assertion. No comparison with
a different chosen fibre product or a supplied product action
is required.
\end{proof}
