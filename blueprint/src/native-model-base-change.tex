\subsection{Actual native model maps across coefficient rings}

\begin{theorem}[Compatible coefficient restriction of whole native points]
\label{scheme:native-coefficient-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.map_basePoint,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.mapOverBase,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.mapOverBase_coe,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.continuous_mapOverBase,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.mapOverBase_id}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:relative-valued-point-functor,scheme:affine-valued-point-homeomorphism}
Let $\varphi:R\to S$ be a homomorphism of arbitrary commutative rings,
and let $v,w$ be real multiplicative seminorms on $R,S$ satisfying
$w(\varphi(r))=v(r)$ for every $r\in R$. For actual structure maps
$f_X:X\to\operatorname{Spec}S$ and
$f_Y:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ and an actual scheme morphism $g:X\to Y$
with
\[
 f_Y\circ g=\operatorname{Spec}(\varphi)\circ f_X,
\]
the genuine native point map restricts to a continuous map from the
whole prescribed-$w$ locus of $X$ to the whole prescribed-$v$ locus of
$Y$. For the identity coefficient map this is the original commuting
same-base native map. No boundedness, properness, field, finite-type or
point-existence hypothesis is needed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Under the actual native affine homeomorphism, the native map of
$\operatorname{Spec}(\varphi)$ is genuine seminorm pullback under
$\varphi$. Compatibility on every original-ring element therefore sends
the whole native affine base point of $w$ to that of $v$: injectivity of
the actual affine homeomorphism proves equality of these entire points.
The whole native composition law and the actual commuting scheme
diagram show that the image of a source point over $w$ lies over $v$.
Restrict the genuine native map to these subtypes; continuity follows
from its actual continuity and the subtype topology. Its underlying map
is the original native point map. Identity coefficient restriction has
the same whole native point, hence agrees with the old same-base map.
No existence or surjectivity of points under a scalar extension is
asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine proper-center comparison across bounded bases]
\label{scheme:proper-center-coefficient-change}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_overBase,

EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.isLocalHom_centerRingMap_overBase,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_fraction_overBase,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.residueBaseMap_naturality_overBase,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_base_overBase,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerLift_naturality_overBase,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_closedPoint_overBase,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.center_map_overBase}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:native-coefficient-restriction,scheme:proper-native-center}
In the preceding diagram suppose $f_X$ is proper over
$\operatorname{Spec}S$ and $f_Y$ is proper over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$. Assume explicitly
\[
 v(r)\le1\quad(r\in R),\qquad w(s)\le1\quad(s\in S).
\]
For every native point $y$ of $X$ above $w$, the actual residue-field
pullback from its native image restricts to a local homomorphism of
the genuine valuation rings, with exact maximal-ideal contraction and
with all original coefficient maps compatible. The entire valuative
lifts commute under this map. In particular the true proper-model
centers satisfy
\[
 g(c_X(y))=c_Y(g(y)).
\]
The source is only required to be proper over its own coefficient ring
$S$. No properness of $X$ over $R$, no properness of the intervening
map $g$, and no noetherian or completeness hypothesis is imposed.
These centers are distinct constructions from native kernel supports.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual native residue-field embedding preserves every value by the
native pullback definition, so it sends the target valuation ring into
the source valuation ring. The strict-value description of genuine
valuation-ring maximal ideals gives exact contraction; the ordinary
local-ring criterion therefore proves that this is an actual local
homomorphism. The whole fraction-field diagram is the original
residue-field map together with the genuine valuation-ring inclusions.

For each $r\in R$, use the actual scheme diagram on global sections,
genuine residue evaluation naturality and the original-ring
$\Gamma\operatorname{Spec}$ isomorphism naturality. They prove equality
of the actual residue coefficient elements: the target coefficient
pullback equals the source coefficient map evaluated at $\varphi(r)$.
This is equality of elements, rather than only equality of their values.
The two explicit coefficient bounds place both maps in their actual
valuation rings, and subtype injectivity proves the corresponding whole
valuation-ring coefficient diagram.

Use the source valuation ring and its actual fraction field to form a
true valuative square for $f_Y$, whose generic map is the genuine source
residue point followed by $g$, and whose base coefficient map is the
source valuation-ring map composed with $\varphi$. The source proper
lift followed by $g$ and the target proper lift pulled back under the
valuation-ring homomorphism both satisfy the two whole triangles: the
source base triangle uses the actual structure diagram, and the target
base triangle uses the proved coefficient equality. Separated valuative
uniqueness of $f_Y$ identifies these entire scheme lifts. This comparison
never uses properness of the source over $R$. Finally the proved local
homomorphism sends the actual source closed point to the actual target
closed point. Evaluating the full lift identity there proves the stated
center identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Intrinsic model ideal pullback across coefficient rings]
\label{scheme:model-ideal-coefficient-change}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:proper-center-coefficient-change,scheme:genuine-ideal-pullback-stalk,
scheme:native-model-ideal-function,lem:general-ideal-pullback}
Under the preceding properness and two bounded-base hypotheses,
every actual ideal-sheaf datum $I$ on $Y$ satisfies
\[
 \|g^*I\|_X(y)=\|I\|_Y(g(y)),\qquad
 \log\|g^*I\|_X(y)=\log\|I\|_Y(g(y)).
\]
Here $g^*I$ is Mathlib's genuine geometric ideal pullback and the model
functions are the intrinsic true-center stalk norms. No finite
generation, noetherianity, flatness or birationality is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual native residue evaluation and restriction of sections identify
the whole source section-spectrum pullback under the genuine local
section homomorphism with the whole target section-spectrum point.
This equality holds for every section, even though the two section
rings have different derived coefficient actions.

Choose a genuine affine neighborhood of the target model center. The
proved true-center comparison puts the source center in its preimage;
choose an actual affine refinement there. Both intrinsic model norms
agree with their genuine affine ideal norms on these true center
neighborhoods. The independently derived geometric pullback formula
identifies the source ideal as the actual image of the target ideal
under the local section map. The source proper center makes all its
local section values bounded by one. The proved bounded ideal-image
norm formula therefore gives the target norm at the pulled-back section
point, and the whole section-spectrum equality gives the asserted
native-image norm. Apply the actual extended logarithm, retaining zero
norms and negative infinity.

This supplies model-function compatibility for actual valuation-ring
coefficient changes and commuting model maps. It assumes no affine
image, center, stalk or norm equality as a certificate and claims no
analytic structure-sheaf identification or general scalar-extension
point surjectivity. The two coefficient bounds remain explicit;
a nontrivially valued entire field is not thereby globally bounded.
\end{proof}
