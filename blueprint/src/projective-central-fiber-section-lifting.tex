\begin{proof}

The old whole kernel/geometric-ideal comparison retains the original
inclusion, so its composite is the claimed actual multiplication map.
No flatness or regularity certificate is an input.
\end{proof}

Here is the precise canonical comparison for the right arrow. For the

\begin{theorem}[Every prescribed original central section lifts to the same line]
\label{thm:projective-central-fibre-original-section-surjective}
\uses{thm:projective-central-fibre-actual-line-sequence,lem:projective-central-fibre-hopfian-obstruction,thm:projective-central-fibre-native-hone-finite}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjectiveCentralFiberSectionLifting.restriction_surjective_of_hone_zero}
\leanok
For the actual projective integral dominant $p$, the actual zero fibre
$j:Y_0\to Y$, and the actual invertible $L$, suppose only the genuine
ordinary vanishing
\[
 H^1(Y_0,j^*L)=0.
\]
Then the ORIGINAL restriction
\[
 \Gamma(Y,L)\longrightarrow\Gamma(Y_0,j^*L)
\]
is surjective. Equivalently, EVERY prescribed actual original central
section has an actual global lift in the SAME original $L$.
The fibre is allowed to be nonreduced and of arbitrary dimension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the actual parameter sequence by the first two theorems.
The inverse-image cover on the actual fibre is affine, because $j$ is
an actual closed immersion. The existing original affine-cover Cech
comparison translates the GIVEN original sheaf $H^1$ vanishing on
$j^*L$ into exactness of its actual first base-linear Cech complex.
The genuine whole pushforward Cech comparison identifies this with
the quotient term in the actual parameter sequence, preserving the
whole original base action. Concretely, in every degree take the product
of the existing original Cech-factor pushforward isomorphisms over ALL
tuples. Their actual face naturality makes these degreewise isomorphisms
commute with every coface and hence its alternating-sum differential.
This proves the needed all-tuples complex isomorphism, rather than
assuming a comparison or replacing it with the ordered complex.
Thus its actual first homology is zero,
derived internally rather than supplied as a comparison certificate.

Apply the preceding Hopfian obstruction argument. Finally identify
global sections of the pushforward with actual sections of the SAME
$j^*L$ by the canonical preimage-of-top transport. The already proved
unit triangle says that the map being transported is exactly the
original adjunction-unit restriction, literally the existing general
\texttt{affinePullbackUnitTop} function (whose definition has no affine
hypothesis). Hence every prescribed original
central section lifts to the original line. No reducedness, integral
fibre, centre ampleness, very-ampleness bound, Nakayama lifting datum
or finite-cohomology certificate is used as an input.
\end{proof}

This is the ordinary fixed-line section-lifting step. It does not prove
nef-to-ample, central ampleness, relative ampleness near zero, a closed
projective embedding after shrinking, or whole-base same-line globalization.
Those separate geometric steps must be supplied before envelope assembly.
