\subsection{Actual open immersions and intrinsic regular-unit locality}
\begin{lemma}[Genuine unit presentations descend through an actual open immersion]
\label{scheme:native-unit-open-immersion-descent}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:native-regular-unit-local-psh,scheme:valued-open-immersion-image,scheme:relative-open-embedding}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.unitTropAffine_map_appIso_inv,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.IsConvexPLOn.of_comp_openImmersion}
Let $j:X\to Y$ be an actual open immersion over the same prescribed coefficient
ring and valuation. Let $U$ be any set of whole fixed-base native points of $Y$
contained in the actual image of the original native map. If $u\circ j$ is
intrinsically convex PL on the full inverse image of $U$, then $u$ is
intrinsically convex PL on $U$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Lift the original whole point through the genuine open-immersion native image
bijection. Its actual source presentation has an analytic open $W$, a genuine
scheme open $V$ and actual units in $\Gamma(X,V)$. The actual open native map
sends $W$ to an open neighborhood. Its image lies in $U$, and its whole support
lies in the genuine open $j(V)$. The inverse of the actual open-immersion
section isomorphism sends every original section unit, with its inverse, to a
true unit of $\Gamma(Y,j(V))$. The genuine section/residue evaluation identity
retains EVERY value of these inverse section images at every actual native
image point. The same rational family and constants therefore give exactly
the whole original function on the entire target neighborhood. Whole native
point injectivity ensures that no choice of support preimage loses valuation
data. No supplied section or point inverse certificate enters the argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Genuine sequences and local psh descend through the same actual open image]
\label{scheme:native-local-psh-open-immersion-descent}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:native-unit-open-immersion-descent,scheme:native-regular-unit-local-psh}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.IsDecrConvexPLLimitOn.of_comp_openImmersion,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.IsLocallyPSHOn.of_comp_openImmersion}
Under the same hypotheses, genuine decreasing-limit and intrinsic local-psh
presentations descend on $U$ from their full original inverse images.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a genuine source sequence, define each target term on the actual native
image by the value at its unique whole preimage, and set it to zero outside
that actual image. This is a total function, without a continuity assertion
outside the image. Whole point injectivity proves that its composite with the
original map is EXACTLY the original source sequence. The preceding true unit
presentation descent applies term by term. For each point of $U$, its true
whole inverse preserves every sequence inequality and the actual infimum, so
the resulting sequence is genuinely decreasing with the required original
limit. For intrinsic local psh, push the actual source analytic neighborhood
forward by the genuine open point map and apply this decreasing-limit result
there. Each target neighborhood remains inside $U$. No common denominator or
countable intersection is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Locality on an actual scheme open cover]
\label{scheme:native-local-psh-scheme-cover}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:native-local-psh-open-immersion-descent,scheme:relative-open-embedding}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.IsLocallyPSHOn.of_openCover}
Let an actual scheme open cover of $Y$ be given, together with ACTUAL
coefficient structure maps on its source schemes and the WHOLE commuting
triangles with the original structure map of $Y$. If the pullback of $u$ to each
of its genuine fixed-base native chart spaces is intrinsically locally psh on
the whole inverse image of $U$, then $u$ is intrinsically locally psh on $U$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual scheme chart containing the support of the original native
point. The genuine residue-field inverse for that open immersion supplies a
whole native preimage. The exact original coefficient triangle makes that
preimage lie in the same prescribed-base locus. Its actual local-presentation
neighborhood can be pushed forward by the preceding open-immersion result,
using the true open native image and original unit section isomorphisms.
This gives the required local presentation around the original WHOLE point,
not just around its support. Every such image is open and the genuine scheme
cover makes them cover all the native points. No analytic sheaf or potential
property is claimed.
\end{proof}
