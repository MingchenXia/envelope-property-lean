\section{Gluing the original whole module morphisms and isomorphisms}

\begin{theorem}[Compatible original module maps glue uniquely]
\label{thm:scheme-original-module-morphism-gluing}
\uses{thm:module-hom-original-linearity-descent}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomSections,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomRestriction,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomRestriction_app,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomTypePresheaf,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomTypePresheaf_isSheaf,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomTypeSheaf,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomTopEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomTopEquiv_restrict,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHom_existsUnique,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleGlueHom,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleGlueHom_restrict,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHom_ext,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomRestriction_id,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomRestriction_comp}
\leanok
On any actual open cover of a scheme, original morphisms between the
restrictions of two original module sheaves glue uniquely when their
literal restrictions agree on every overlap. The resulting whole
morphism has exactly the prescribed original local restrictions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The derived sheaf of original local module morphisms identifies its
sections on each member of the cover with exactly the supplied local
module maps. Their original overlap equalities are its sheaf
compatibility equations. Sheaf gluing therefore gives one unique
section on the entire scheme. A morphism on the over-site of the
largest open yields its global module morphism by evaluating on each
original subordinate open. Conversely each global morphism restricts
to the over-site of the largest open. Both operations are inverse on
every original component. Transporting the unique glued section
through these operations proves the assertion, including its exact
local restrictions. The same separatedness says that whole original
module morphisms equal on the cover are equal globally.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compatible original local isomorphisms glue]
\label{thm:scheme-original-module-isomorphism-gluing}
\uses{thm:scheme-original-module-morphism-gluing}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomRestrictionIso,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomRestrictionIso_hom,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleHomRestrictionIso_inv,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleLocalIso_inv_compatible,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleGlueIso,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleMorphismGluing.bundleGlueIso_restrict}
\leanok
Compatible actual isomorphisms between the restrictions of two original
module sheaves on an actual open cover give an actual whole module
isomorphism with those same original restrictions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Restriction preserves identities and composition, so it takes each
original local isomorphism to the isomorphism with its restricted
original homomorphism and inverse. Equality of the homomorphisms of
two isomorphisms implies equality of the isomorphisms, hence equality
of their inverses. Thus the original local inverse maps also agree on
every overlap. Glue the original local homomorphisms and glue those
original local inverses separately. Their composites restrict on each
cover member to the composites of the local isomorphisms with their
inverses, which are identities. Uniqueness of the glued morphisms
makes both whole composites the identity. The resulting whole
isomorphism has exactly the original local homomorphisms and inverses.
\end{proof}
