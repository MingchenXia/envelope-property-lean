\section{Every original local section has its native trace coefficient}

\begin{lemma}[The original local trace on every actual section]
\label{lem:kn-structure-trace-original-local-value}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.traceOnSpec_top}
\leanok
For every actual section of the restriction of the original
$\pi_*\mathcal O_Y$ to a whole free chart's base coefficient spectrum,
the constructed local trace has coefficient equal to the native algebra
trace of its image under the actual whole chart pushforward comparison.
This includes sections on that whole local inverse image; extension to
a global section of $Y$ is not required.
\uses{lem:kn-structure-trace-original-local-map,lem:kn-structure-trace-raw-top}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By construction the actual local trace is the whole chart pushforward
comparison, followed by the native affine sheaf trace, followed by the
inverse actual unit restriction isomorphism. Read its value through
the forward actual unit isomorphism. The two unit isomorphisms cancel
on every section, and the proved native affine whole-section trace
formula gives the stated coefficient.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Every original local section specializes to the actual field trace]
\label{lem:kn-structure-trace-original-local-field-value}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.traceOnSpec_fieldBaseChange_top}
\leanok
After any field specialization of the original coefficient ring of a
whole free chart, the coefficient of the actual local trace on every
original local section equals the actual finite-field sheaf trace of
its image under the actual first projection from the entire base change.
No matching identity is supplied, and the original section need not
extend to the whole original source.
\uses{lem:kn-structure-trace-original-local-value,lem:kn-structure-trace-affine-field-specialization}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The preceding lemma reads the same local trace through its whole chart
comparison. Apply the proved affine-to-field specialization formula to
that actual whole chart section. It identifies the image of the affine
trace coefficient with the actual field trace of the actual first
projection pullback of that section. Substituting the preceding local
trace coefficient equality proves the assertion for every original
local section.
\end{proof}
