\section{Kodaira vanishing over the original arbitrary affine base in characteristic zero}

\begin{theorem}[The original relative ample Kodaira theorem]
\label{thm:kn-relative-kodaira-characteristic-zero}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeKodairaCharacteristicZero.kodaira}
\uses{lem:kn-relative-arithmetic-smooth-ample-model,lem:kn-relative-arithmetic-closed-kodaira,thm:relative-canonical-produced-witt-specialization,lem:relative-original-canonical-square-cohomology}
\leanok
Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero, let $R$ be any
finite-type $k$-algebra, and let
$f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be separated and smooth of
relative dimension $n$.  Let
$g:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ be compatible with $f$, and let
$L$ be the same original $g$-ample line.  Then
\[
 H^{q+1}(X,\omega_{X/k}\otimes L)=0
 \qquad(q\geq0).
\]
The algebra $R$ may have zero divisors or nilpotents, and the
smooth scheme $X$ may be disconnected.  Projectivity over $k$
is not assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the actual smooth integer coefficient domain
$A\subset k$, the separate finitely presented algebra $B/A$,
and the smooth separated dimension-$n$ model $Y/A$, projective
over $B$, with the actual relatively ample line $M$ whose whole
original pullback is $L$.  This construction retains the
original cartesian square with $X/k$ and the original affine
base fibre $k\otimes_A B\simeq R$.

Compute the canonical-twisted model cohomology on the finite
ordered affine cover furnished by relative projectivity over
$B$.  Its complex is bounded, has terms flat over $A$ by the
smoothness of $Y/A$, and has homology finite over $B$ by
projective cohomology finiteness.  Generic freeness therefore
gives one coefficient open where all its finitely many
possibly nonzero homology modules are locally free over $A$.
Choose a closed point of this open with finite residue field
$\kappa$ of characteristic $p>n$, together with the actual
coefficient lift $A\to W_2(\kappa)$ supplied by the smoothness
of $A/\mathbb Z$.  Relative Witt Kodaira, using the actual
special-fibre comparison and the original pulled-back $M$,
annihilates the closed-fibre positive canonical-twisted
cohomology.

At this same point the localized homology modules of the
coefficient complex are free, with arbitrary rank.  Tensor
homology base change for this bounded flat complex identifies
the closed-fibre homology with the residue scalar extension
of those free modules.  A free module with zero scalar
extension to a nonzero field is zero.  Thus the localized
homology is zero and so is its extension to the injective
coefficient field $k$.  The actual affine-cover fibre
comparison identifies this with canonical-twisted cohomology
on the original-field fibre of $Y/A$.

Finally the original cartesian square identifies that fibre
with the whole original $X$.  Smooth canonical base change
identifies the pulled-back canonical model with
$\omega_{X/k}$, and the whole original-line isomorphism
identifies the other factor with $L$.  Cohomology transports
through these actual module and scheme isomorphisms, proving
the asserted vanishing.
\end{proof}
