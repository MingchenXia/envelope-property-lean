\section{Actual relative exceptional positivity}
\label{sec:kn-relative-nadel-exceptional-ample}

\begin{theorem}[An original blowup exceptional ideal becomes relatively very ample]
\label{thm:kn-relative-nadel-exceptional-very-ample}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelCoverExceptionalAmple.exists_positive_isVeryAmple_exceptional_tensor_pullback}
\leanok
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-same-global-pair-closed,lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-global-controlled-generators,thm:kn-arithmetic-original-line-generated,thm:original-segre-pulled-line-iso,lem:positive-power-projective-block}
Let $k$ be a field, let $R$ be any $k$-algebra, and retain the actual
factorization $X\xrightarrow\pi\operatorname{Spec}R\to\operatorname{Spec}k$.
Suppose $X$ is locally Noetherian.  If $C$ is an actual ample line over
the same $R$ and $D$ is any coherent ideal, then some original positive
power $C^r$ makes
\[
 \mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}_D X}(-E)\otimes\mu^*C^r
\]
very ample over the original $R$, where $\mu$ is the actual blowup and
$E$ its actual exceptional divisor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the positive power $C^a$ and actual closed embedding
$j:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^m_R$ in the original ample datum.  Serre
generation applied to the original ideal on that embedding supplies a
finite family generating $D\otimes j^*\mathcal O(N)$ for some $N$.
Pull these actual generators to the blowup and use the original image
ideal comparison.  They generate the invertible line
$\mathcal O(-E)\otimes\mu^*j^*\mathcal O(N)$ and produce an actual
map $q:\operatorname{Bl}_D X\to\mathbb P^s_k$.  Its pair with the
original blowup map is closed: on each original affine frame of the
twisting line, it is the closed projective presentation of the original
Rees algebra by its actual generators.  These affine presentations glue
with their literal generator coefficients.

The original coefficient square identifies
$X\times_R\mathbb P^s_R$ with $X\times_k\mathbb P^s_k$.
Transport the preceding actual closed pair through this isomorphism.
Its second component is the actual coefficient lift $q_R$ of $q$.
The original coefficient pullback comparison for $\mathcal O(1)$
identifies $q_R^*\mathcal O(1)$ with the same original generated line.
The closed embedding $j$ induces a closed embedding of these projective
products, so the pair $(\mu^*j,q_R)$ is closed into
$\mathbb P^m_R\times_R\mathbb P^s_R$.  Compose with the Segre closed
embedding.  Its whole $\mathcal O(1)$ pullback is
\[
 \mu^*C^a\otimes\mathcal O(-E)\otimes\mu^*C^{aN}
 \simeq\mathcal O(-E)\otimes\mu^*C^{a+aN}.
\]
The exponent $a+aN$ is positive.  This constructs the claimed actual
closed embedding and whole line comparison over the original $R$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Positive exceptional weights along the original relative sequence]
\label{lem:kn-relative-nadel-weighted-exceptional-ample}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelCoverWeightedAmple.exists_positive_weights_isAmple}
\leanok
\uses{thm:kn-relative-nadel-exceptional-very-ample}
Assume in addition that $X$ is of finite type over the original field.
For every original finite blowup sequence $\mu:Y\to X$ over the preceding
coefficient square and every original relatively ample $C$, there are
positive integer weights $w_i$ and $r>0$ such that
$\bigotimes_i\mathcal O_Y(-w_iE_i)\otimes\mu^*C^r$ is ample over the
same original $R$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual finite sequence.  For the empty sequence take
$r=1$ and the empty product.  At the first blowup the preceding theorem
gives an ample line $C_1=\mathcal O(-E_0)\otimes\mu_0^*C^a$ for $a>0$.
Apply the induction hypothesis to the remaining sequence with seed
$C_1$.  If its seed exponent is $s>0$, expand $C_1^s$ and pull it through
the remaining sequence.  The resulting first exceptional weight is
$s$, all remaining weights are positive by induction, and the original
seed exponent is $as>0$.  The actual pullback, tensor and ideal-product
comparisons identify the expanded line with precisely the stated
weighted exceptional ideal tensored by the original pulled power.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[An actual ample rounded numerator on the original relative principalization]
\label{lem:kn-relative-nadel-ample-numerator}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelCoverNumeratorAmple.exists_ample_perturbed_numerator}
\leanok
\uses{lem:kn-relative-nadel-weighted-exceptional-ample}
In the original relative Nadel setting, retain the actual identity
$M^d\simeq C\otimes B^t$, $dc=t$, $d>0$, with $C$ ample over the original
$R$, and a finite generating family of $I\otimes B$.  On the actual
original principalization $\mu:Y\to X$, write $J=I\mathcal O_Y$.
For every lower bound, there are positive exceptional weights, an
integer $N$ at least that bound and divisible by $\operatorname{den}(c)$,
and $a$ with $a/N=c$, such that
$K=J^a\prod_i\mathcal O_Y(-w_iE_i)$ is an SNC ideal,
$\lfloor K/N\rfloor=\lfloor cJ\rfloor$ as whole intrinsic ideal sheaves,
and $\mu^*M^N\otimes K$ is ample over the same original $R$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every exceptional divisor of the original principalization lies over the
support of the original ideal.  Its pulled weighted product is Cartier
and supported on the total transform.  The SNC components of that
transform therefore give a single finite component family expressing
both $J$ and the weighted exceptional product as monomials.  Choose a
multiple $N$ of the denominator of $c$ so large that every exceptional
coefficient divided by $N$ is smaller than the distance to the next
integer in the corresponding rational coefficient of $cJ$.  Explicitly,
if $c=u/v$, choose $N=vT$ with $T$ larger than every exceptional
coefficient and the prescribed lower bound, and put $a=uT$.
Then componentwise Euclidean division proves
$\lfloor(a b_i+e_i)/N\rfloor=\lfloor c b_i\rfloor$; hence the entire
intrinsic rounded ideals agree.

Choose the positive weights from the preceding lemma.  Taking the
$d$-th power of the original numerator and using the original cleared
identity gives the actual whole line comparison
\[
 (\mu^*M^N\otimes K)^d\simeq
 (E_w^d\otimes\mu^*C^N)\otimes
 (J\otimes\mu^*B)^{ad}.
\]
The first factor is ample for all sufficiently large $N$: factor off
the fixed ample power supplied by the preceding lemma, and use eventual
generation of original powers of $C$ to tensor by their actual pulled
generating families.  The second factor has a finite actual generating
family, obtained by pulling the given generators of $I\otimes B$ and
taking tensor products.  Tensoring an ample line by this finitely
generated invertible line preserves ampleness.  Thus the displayed
$d$-th power is ample.  The original numerator is invertible and
$d>0$, so its own actual positive-power ample criterion follows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original relative source produces its entire ample rounded cover]
\label{thm:kn-relative-nadel-numerator-cover}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelCoverNumeratorProduced.exists_original_numerator_cover}
\leanok
\uses{lem:kn-relative-nadel-ample-numerator,thm:kn-relative-nadel-rational-cover-ample}
The original relative Nadel inputs internally produce their actual
principalization, a perturbed SNC numerator with the same whole original
intrinsic rounded ideal, and actual original auxiliary sections.  The
entire normalization of their original root-cover product is finite over
the principalization and smooth of the same original dimension over $k$.
Its actual original rounded root line is ample over the same original $R$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding ample-numerator lemma to the original cleared power
identity and the original finite generating family.  Let $N$ and $K$
be its actual positive denominator and SNC numerator, and set $q'=1/N$.
The original numerator line gives exactly the positive cleared ample
line for $(\mu^*M,K,q')$, since $q'$ has numerator one and denominator
$N$.  Apply the actual rational SNC cover theorem to those same objects.
It produces all original auxiliary sections, their whole finite smooth
normalized cover and actual rounded root-line ampleness over the original
$R$.  Keep the earlier whole rounded-ideal equality and every original
coefficient identity alongside these actual produced objects.
\end{proof}
