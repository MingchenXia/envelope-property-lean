\subsection{The paper's actual complete algebraic closure and rational lift}
\label{subsec:paper-rigid-lifts}

\begin{theorem}[The actual constructed rigid point in the paper interface]
\label{spectrum:paper-rigid-complete-extension}
\leanok
\uses{spectrum:rigid-complete-algebraic-closure-lift,spectrum:trivial-field-bridge,
spectrum:scalar-restriction}
For every actual paper point $x$ of $\operatorname{Berk}_k(A)$, the
genuine field factory constructs $F=\widehat{\overline{H(x)}}$ with its
complete algebraically closed ultrametric norm. Its genuine $F$-rational
lift of $x$ gives an actual point of the paper space
$\operatorname{Berk}_k(F\otimes_kA)$ that restricts to the whole original
point. Every $F$-scalar retains its actual norm value, which may be
nontrivial, and the support is genuinely maximal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual old-space/relative-spectrum homeomorphism to express the
whole original point as a point with the trivial $k$ valuation prescribed.
Apply the already constructed generic rigid lift, with its genuine
completed algebraic closure field and full surjective tensor evaluation.
The proved coefficient valuation equality permits actual scalar restriction
from the entire norm of $F$ to the original trivial $k$ valuation. The
inverse old-space homeomorphism therefore gives a genuine paper point.
These homeomorphisms preserve the entire valuation, so the existing
whole right-inclusion restriction, all $F$-coefficient values and actual
maximal support transfer directly. No complete algebraically closed
extension or rigid-lift witness is assumed as an input certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual function norm at the constructed rational lift]
\label{spectrum:paper-flat-norm-rigid-lift}
\leanok
\uses{spectrum:paper-rigid-complete-extension,spectrum:paper-flat-norm-base-change}
For every finite algebra map $A\to B$, every EReal function $u$ on the
original paper points of $B$, and the genuinely constructed rational
lift $x_F$ above, the existing function norm on the literal map
$F\otimes_k A\to F\otimes_k B$ evaluated at $x_F$ equals
$\mathrm{flatNormFun}(u)(x)$, after actual restriction of $u$ through the
right tensor inclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Give the literal scalar-extended map its proved actual coefficient algebra
and module-finite structure. Apply the already proved all-function
scalar-extension comparison at the constructed point. Its whole old-space
restriction is precisely $x$, so the right side becomes the original
function norm value. The actual finite local dimensions retain nilpotent
and inseparable contributions. This is a pointwise comparison for every
EReal function with the actual conventions; no regularizability, freeness,
flatness, reducedness or curve hypothesis is required.

This makes the field and rigid-point choice used in Fang--Han--Xia
Proposition~5.22 concrete in the paper's current point-space interface.
Lines and the later analytic-curve and normalization arguments remain
separate constructions.
\end{proof}
