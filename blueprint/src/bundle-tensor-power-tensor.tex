\begin{lemma}[Actual tensor powers of a tensor product]
\label{lem:fc-original-bundle-tensor-power-tensor}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.BundleTensorPowerTensor.powerTensorIso}
\leanok
For any two original sheaves of modules $A,B$ and every natural exponent
$r$, there is a canonical whole original isomorphism
$(A\otimes B)^{\otimes r}\simeq A^{\otimes r}\otimes B^{\otimes r}$
for the actual recursively sheafified tensor powers.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At exponent zero use the inverse of the original structure-line
multiplication. For the successor exponent, convert the original
sheafified product to the genuine monoidal product and use the previous
whole power comparison on its first factor. Associativity and symmetry
move the new $A$ past the previous $B$ factors, giving the tensor of the
actual next $A$ and $B$ powers. The canonical sheafified/monoidal tensor
comparisons give precisely the original two successor powers. This
induction constructs the whole actual line isomorphism for every $r$;
no power comparison is assumed.
\end{proof}
