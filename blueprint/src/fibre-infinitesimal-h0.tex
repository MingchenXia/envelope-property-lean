\section{The original global-section image filtration and infinitesimal quotients}

\begin{lemma}[The whole original global-section image kernel]
\label{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-image-kernel}
\uses{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-whole-module-kernel}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.sectionKernel,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.mem_sectionKernel_iff,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.mem_sectionKernel_iff_exists,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.sectionKernel_eq_range,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.sectionsEquivKernel,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.sectionsEquivKernel_apply}
\leanok
For every order $n$, let $F_n$ be the kernel ideal of the literal original
projection homomorphism
$\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)\to\Gamma(X_n,\mathcal O_{X_n})$.
A global section belongs to $F_n$ exactly when it is a section of the
original geometric ideal power $(I\mathcal O_X)^n$. Consequently $F_n$
is exactly the entire image of the original ideal-power global-section
inclusion.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the proved whole-open section-kernel theorem to the top open of
$X$. A section in its kernel has, on every affine open, a restriction
in the actual original extended ideal power. This is precisely the
section condition for the original ideal module, and its subtype
inclusion retains that entire global section. Conversely, every
ideal-module section has this local membership and its whole original
projection therefore vanishes. Both maps are the original global-section
maps, so this proves the asserted equality of kernel and image.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual original coefficient-ideal filtration]
\label{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-image-filtration}
\uses{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-image-kernel,lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-quotient-transition}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.sectionKernel_antitone,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.sectionKernel_zero,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.coefficient_restriction,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.coefficient_mul_mem_sectionKernel,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.imageFiltration,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.mem_imageFiltration,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.imageFiltration_zero}
\leanok
With the genuine original $R$-module structure on
$\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$, the submodules $F_n$ form an $I$-filtration:
$F_0=\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$, $F_{n+1}\subseteq F_n$ and
$I F_n\subseteq F_{n+1}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The zeroth geometric ideal power is the whole structure sheaf, giving
$F_0=\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$. The original infinitesimal transition
$X_n\to X_m$ for $n\le m$ commutes with the original projection to $X$.
Its section map therefore carries a vanishing projection at order $m$
to a vanishing projection at order $n$, proving monotonicity.
For $r\in I$ and $s\in F_n$, restrict to any original affine open $U$.
The restriction of the original global coefficient $r$ is its actual
original coefficient image in $\mathcal O_X(U)$, so lies in
$I\mathcal O_X(U)$. The restriction of $s$ lies in its $n$th power by
the preceding lemma. Their product lies in the $(n+1)$st power.
Every such local product is the restriction of the actual original
scalar product $rs$. The whole-open kernel characterization therefore
places $rs$ in $F_{n+1}$. Additivity gives the required inclusion for
the entire submodule $I F_n$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The genuine quotient inclusion and all original transitions]
\label{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-quotient-transition}
\uses{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-image-kernel}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.transition_projection_sections,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.quotientInclusion,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.quotientInclusion_mk,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.projection_coefficient,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.quotientInclusionAlgHom,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.quotientInclusion_injective,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.quotientTransition,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0.quotientInclusion_transition}
\leanok
The actual projection induces an injective homomorphism
$\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)/F_n\to\Gamma(X_n,\mathcal O_{X_n})$
which sends every original quotient representative to its literal
original projection section. For $n\le m$, the whole original
transition map intertwines this inclusion with the canonical quotient
map modulo $F_m\subseteq F_n$. These maps preserve the whole original
$R$-coefficient structures, so the quotient inclusion is an
$R$-algebra homomorphism.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The projection vanishes exactly on $F_n$, so it descends to the quotient.
A quotient class mapping to zero has a representative in its original
kernel $F_n$, hence is zero; this proves injectivity. The defining
formula on representatives is the original projection itself. The
original scheme transition and projection compose to the order-$n$
projection. Contravariance of the global-section functor gives the
same equality of their whole section homomorphisms. Apply this equality
to each quotient representative; surjectivity of the original quotient
map proves the complete quotient-transition square. The same
composition rule identifies the original coefficient map followed by
projection with the coefficient map of the composite structure
morphism, proving the asserted $R$-algebra compatibility.
\end{proof}
