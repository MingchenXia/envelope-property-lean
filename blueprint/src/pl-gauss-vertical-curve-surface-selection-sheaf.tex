\section{Actual closed cuts through the prescribed original subscheme}
Let $R$ be a commutative algebra over an infinite field $k$. All
projective spaces, closed immersions, kernels and ideal pullbacks below
are the actual original schemes and maps. Finite families used for a
proper cut are nonempty. No homogeneous representation, ideal-sheaf
matching, desired cut or curve family is supplied as an input.

\begin{definition}[The original closed zero scheme of a homogeneous equation]
\label{def:pl-gauss-original-homogeneous-cut}
\uses{graded:projective-homogeneous-quotient-ideal-sheaf}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionSheaf.homogeneousPrincipalIdeal,EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionSheaf.projectiveHypersurfaceIdeal,EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionSheaf.originalHypersurfaceCut}
\leanok
For an actual homogeneous polynomial $F$ of degree $d$, form its actual
homogeneous principal ideal $(F)$ and the existing projective quotient
kernel ideal sheaf $\mathcal H_F$. For an actual original map
$i:Y\to\mathbf P^n_R$, define $\mathcal K_F=i^{-1}\mathcal H_F\mathcal O_Y$
by the existing geometric ideal pullback. Its actual subscheme is the
original pulled closed zero scheme of $F$.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The ideal generated by one homogeneous element is homogeneous, because
each homogeneous component of a multiple is again a multiple of that
element. The existing original graded quotient construction supplies the
actual projective quotient closed immersion and its whole kernel ideal
sheaf. The existing geometric ideal pullback is the kernel of the
actual pulled closed immersion. These are the stated original ideals
and actual subschemes, retaining their powers and nilpotents.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original equation has its actual projective zero locus]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-original-homogeneous-cut-support}
\uses{def:pl-gauss-original-homogeneous-cut,graded:projective-homogeneous-quotient-ideal-sheaf}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionSheaf.projectiveHypersurfaceIdeal_support}
\leanok
The support of $\mathcal H_F$ is exactly the original $V_+(F)$, and
the support of the pulled ideal is its actual inverse image on $Y$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The existing original quotient-kernel support theorem identifies the
support with the relevant homogeneous primes containing the actual
ideal $(F)$. Containing $(F)$ is equivalent to containing $F$, giving
the asserted $V_+(F)$. The genuine geometric ideal pullback support
identity is the inverse-image equality, including zero equations.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole original quotient ideal sheaves preserve inclusion]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-original-quotient-ideal-sheaf-monotone}
\uses{graded:native-polynomial-quotient-coordinate-ideal}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionSheaf.quotientIdealSheaf_mono}
\leanok
For actual homogeneous ideals $I\subseteq J$, their entire original
projective quotient kernel ideal sheaves satisfy
$\mathcal I_I\subseteq\mathcal I_J$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On every original coordinate open, the genuine original coordinate
section-ring isomorphism identifies these whole sheaf ideals with the
actual dehomogenization images of $I$ and $J$. Actual ideal image is
monotone, so the former coordinate ideal is contained in the latter.
The original section-ring isomorphism reflects ideal inclusion, and
the actual coordinate cover detects inclusion of whole ideal sheaves.
This proves the stated original sheaf inclusion, without passing to
radicals or assuming either quotient reduced.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Original points outside the subscheme avoid its actual representative]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-original-kernel-representative-point-avoidance}
\uses{projective:actual-homogeneous-ideal-sheaf-representation,graded:projective-homogeneous-quotient-ideal-sheaf}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionSheaf.ambientKernelRepresentative_point_notMem}
\leanok
Let $c:C\hookrightarrow Y$ and $i:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_R$ be
actual original closed immersions. If $y\notin c(C)$, its actual
ambient image $i(y)$ is outside $V_+(J_C)$, where $J_C$ is the already
constructed canonical homogeneous representative of the literal
original kernel $(i\circ c).\ker$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The canonical representation theorem identifies the WHOLE quotient
kernel ideal sheaf of $J_C$ with that literal original ambient kernel.
Its actual support is therefore $V_+(J_C)$. The support of the kernel
of the actual closed immersion $i\circ c$ is its actual closed image.
If $i(y)$ lay in it, some original $z\in C$ would satisfy
$i(c(z))=i(y)$. The original closed embedding $i$ is injective, giving
$c(z)=y$, contrary to the hypothesis. Hence $i(y)$ avoids the original
homogeneous zero locus, with no representation or point-matching input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every large degree gives an actual proper original cut through C]
\label{thm:pl-gauss-original-closed-cut-through-subscheme}
\uses{def:pl-gauss-original-homogeneous-cut,lem:pl-gauss-original-homogeneous-cut-support,lem:pl-gauss-original-quotient-ideal-sheaf-monotone,lem:pl-gauss-original-kernel-representative-point-avoidance,cor:pl-gauss-original-projective-hypersurface-avoidance,projective:actual-homogeneous-ideal-sheaf-representation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionSheaf.exists_eventually_original_closed_cut}
\leanok
Under these actual original closed immersions, let $(y_a)$ be a finite
NONEMPTY family of original points of $Y$ outside $c(C)$. There is $N$
such that for EVERY $d\ge N$ there are an original homogeneous
$F\in(J_C)_d$ and an actual original CLOSED immersion
\[
 g:C\hookrightarrow\mathcal K_F.\mathrm{subscheme}
\]
with the WHOLE original triangle $\iota_F\circ g=c$, where
$\iota_F:\mathcal K_F.\mathrm{subscheme}\hookrightarrow Y$ is the actual
kernel-subscheme inclusion. The actual cut avoids every original $y_a$,
and its support is a proper closed subset of the original $Y$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding point theorem puts all actual ambient images outside
$V_+(J_C)$. Apply the proved original homogeneous-cut producer to this
finite actual projective-point family. It internally constructs, in
every sufficiently large SAME degree, $F\in J_C$ avoiding these actual
points. Since $(F)\subseteq J_C$, the preceding WHOLE sheaf monotonicity
and the canonical representation equality give
$\mathcal H_F\subseteq(i\circ c).\ker=c.\ker.\mathrm{map}(i)$.
The actual ideal pullback/pushforward adjunction therefore gives
$\mathcal K_F\subseteq c.\ker$. The genuine closed-immersion lifting
theorem constructs the actual map $g$ and proves the whole triangle.
Because its composite with the actual closed immersion $\iota_F$ is
the original closed immersion $c$, the same closed-immersion cancellation
theorem makes $g$ a closed immersion.

The preceding exact support theorem and actual ideal-pullback support
identity identify the cut's support with the true inverse image of
$V_+(F)$. The constructed polynomial avoids every original ambient
image $i(y_a)$, so the actual cut avoids every $y_a$. Choose one index
from the NONEMPTY family: its actual original point witnesses that the
support is not all of $Y$. No desired cut, whole triangle, scheme
inclusion, norm or family is assumed in this construction.
\end{proof}

These are actual closed cuts of the prescribed original $Y$ containing
the original $C$ as a CLOSED subscheme. Selection of the actual
horizontal integral component containing $C$, transfer of the proved
local component dimensions, and the induction with finite normalization
and tracked central curves remain separate geometric conclusions.
