\section{Actual tensor frames and multiplication of original transitions}

\begin{lemma}[The original tensor frame restricts and multiplies transitions]
\label{lem:scheme-original-tensor-frame-transitions}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleTensorFrames.tensorFrame,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleTensorFrames.tensorFrame_restrict,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleTensorFrames.tensorFrame_hom_eq_comp_scalars}
\leanok
Two original module frames on an original open give the original tensor
module its coefficient-preserving tensor frame. Restricting it is the
tensor frame of the restricted original frames. If the original two
frames change by multiplication by $r$ and $s$, their tensor frame
changes by multiplication by $rs$ in the same original structure ring.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the true restriction-tensor comparison, tensor the two original
frames, and then use the structure-module multiplication isomorphism.
The original restriction/pullback coherence and the original unit
comparison identify its restriction with the same construction on the
smaller original open. For the transition formula, substitute the two
given original frame equalities. Functoriality of tensor separates the
tensor of these composites into the tensor of the original right-hand
frames followed by the tensor of the scalar multiplications. Under
structure-module multiplication the latter map sends $x\otimes y$ to
$(rx)(sy)=rs(xy)$; hence it is multiplication by $rs$. This gives the
claimed equality for the entire original tensor-frame homomorphism.
\end{proof}
