\section{Native section-homology restriction with actual scalar comparison}
\label{sec:scheme-section-homology-restriction}

\begin{definition}[Actual native homology restriction]
\label{def:scheme-original-native-section-homology-restriction}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomologyRestriction.restrictionHomologyMap}
For any original module complex $K$ and inclusion $U\subseteq V$,
there is an actual native homology map
$H^q(\Gamma(V,K))\to H^q(\Gamma(U,K))$, linear after restricting
the target scalars along the original structural ring map.
It is the native homology functor on the original entire
restriction cochain map, followed by genuine preservation
of native homology under this actual restriction of scalars.
\uses{prop:scheme-original-section-complex-restriction}
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Apply the native homology functor to the actual whole
restriction cochain map. Its target is the native homology
of the target section complex with scalars restricted along
the original structural map. The exact scalar-restriction
functor gives its genuine native comparison isomorphism
to restricted scalars of the target homology module.
Their composite is the stated whole original map.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The canonical comparison retains the original homology-sheaf restriction]
\label{prop:scheme-original-native-section-homology-restriction}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSectionHomologyRestriction.comparison_restriction}
The actual native homology restriction followed by scalar
restriction of the canonical section-homology comparison on
$U$ equals the canonical comparison on $V$ followed by the
actual original restriction of $H^q(K)$.
\uses{def:scheme-original-native-section-homology-restriction,prop:scheme-original-section-homology-restriction,lem:left-exact-original-homology-composition,lem:left-exact-original-homology-preservation-agreement}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The original section-homology restriction square is already
proved for the composite of evaluation on $U$ and actual
restriction of scalars. The canonical comparison for this
composite equals the canonical comparison for scalar
restriction followed by scalar restriction of the
comparison for evaluation. Scalar restriction genuinely
preserves native homology, so its canonical comparison is
exactly the proved native preservation isomorphism.
Substitution into that whole original square yields precisely
the asserted equality, including every original coefficient.
\end{proof}

No affine or quasicoherent hypotheses are needed for this
square. Invertibility of canonical comparisons, when needed,
is supplied by proved affine geometry rather than assumed here.
