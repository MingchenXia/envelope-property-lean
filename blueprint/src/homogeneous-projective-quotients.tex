\subsection{Actual projective schemes of homogeneous ideal quotients}
\label{subsec:homogeneous-projective-quotients}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine homogeneous-quotient projective closed immersion]
\label{graded:projective-homogeneous-quotient-immersion}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.awayMap_surjective,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.quotientSchemeMap,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.isPullback_awayMap,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.instIsClosedImmersion_quotientSchemeMap}
\leanok
\uses{graded:homogeneous-ideal-quotient-grading}
Let $A$ be a commutative algebra over an arbitrary commutative ring $R$,
with an actual internal natural-number grading, and let $J$ be a genuine
homogeneous ideal. The actual graded quotient constructed above gives
a canonical scheme morphism
\[
 \operatorname{Proj}(A/J)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Proj}(A).
\]
This genuine Mathlib projective-spectrum morphism is a closed immersion.
It retains the original ideal, including nilpotents and powers; no
radical replacement, field, domain, noetherian or finite-generation
assumption is used.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original quotient map is genuinely graded, and its image of the
actual irrelevant ideal is the quotient irrelevant ideal. These proved
facts supply the precise input to the actual Mathlib projective-spectrum
scheme morphism, rather than an assumed projective-chart certificate.

Fix any original homogeneous element $s$ of positive degree. Every
actual element of the degree-zero homogeneous localization of $A/J$
away from the image of $s$ has a genuine fraction representation with
numerator in degree $n\deg(s)$ and denominator the $n$th power of that
image. The proved same-degree quotient lift of the numerator gives an
actual original homogeneous fraction with denominator $s^n$. The true
homogeneous-localization map sends this entire fraction to the selected
quotient fraction. Consequently the entire away-ring map is surjective,
including the degenerate case in which the quotient localization is zero.

The actual projective-spectrum functoriality theorem gives the whole
commuting square between these two actual affine spectra and their
canonical open immersions into the projective schemes. Its exact
basic-open preimage formula identifies the whole source chart as the
preimage of the target chart. The actual open-immersion pullback theorem
therefore makes this a genuine scheme pullback square.

The actual spectrum map of the surjective away-ring homomorphism is a
closed immersion. The preceding true pullback identification conjugates
it to the restriction of the original projective scheme map to the
selected target affine chart. The actual homogeneous affine open cover
covers all of the target projective scheme. Since closed immersion is
local at the target, these derived closed immersions prove the assertion
for the entire canonical projective morphism. In particular actual
surjectivity on stalks and the closed topological embedding are obtained
from genuine scheme closed immersions, without independent certificates.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[True original-ideal image and homogeneous localization kernel]
\label{graded:projective-homogeneous-quotient-ideal-sheaf}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.quotientIdealSheaf,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.quotientIdealSheaf_support,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.mem_quotientIdealSheaf_awayToSection_iff,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.awayMap_mk_eq_zero_iff,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.quotientIdealSheaf_awayToSection_comap,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.quotientPoint,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.quotientSchemeMap_quotientPoint,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.range_quotientSchemeMap,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.quotientIdealSheaf_support_eq_zeroLocus,
EnvelopeProperty.GradedQuotient.mem_quotientIdealSheaf_awayToSection_mk_iff}
\leanok
\uses{graded:projective-homogeneous-quotient-immersion,graded:homogeneous-ideal-quotient-grading}
The preceding actual closed immersion has its genuine kernel ideal sheaf.
Its support and the scheme image are exactly the relevant original
homogeneous prime ideals containing $J$. On an actual positive-degree
homogeneous chart $D_+(s)$, its entire ideal pulls back under the true
homogeneous-localization section isomorphism to the kernel of the actual
away-ring quotient map. In particular, for a same-degree fraction
$a/s^n$, membership in this ideal is precisely
\[
 \text{there exists }m\geq0\text{ such that }s^m a\in J.
\]
This is the original localization annihilator criterion, not a radical
or reduced quotient criterion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a relevant homogeneous prime $p$ containing $J$, take its actual
ideal image in $A/J$. The genuine quotient prime-ideal theorem shows
this image is prime, and the actual graded ideal map makes it homogeneous.
Original quotient map/comap cancellation recovers all of $p$. If the
quotient irrelevant ideal lay in the image prime, the proved entire
irrelevant-image equality and map/comap adjunction would put the original
irrelevant ideal in $p$, contradicting relevance. Thus the actual image
prime defines a quotient projective point, whose whole scheme image is
$p$. Conversely every original element of $J$ maps to zero in $A/J$, so
it belongs to the entire contracted prime of every quotient point. This
proves the stated genuine image equality without replacing $J$ by any
radical or assuming a prime-lift certificate.

Take the actual kernel ideal sheaf of the proved closed immersion.
A closed immersion is quasi-compact, so on every actual affine open
its real sheaf ideal is exactly the kernel of the original section map.
Its support is the closure of the scheme image. The actual closed
embedding makes that image closed, so its support is the image itself.

On a positive homogeneous chart, the entire projective functoriality
identity equates the original away-to-section map followed by the actual
section pullback with the genuine away-ring quotient map followed by the
quotient away-to-section map. The latter map is an actual isomorphism,
so a section's pullback is zero exactly when its whole away-ring quotient
image is zero. This proves membership for every original away-ring
element and therefore the full pulled-back ideal equality.

For the selected fraction $a/s^n$, forget the genuine homogeneous
localization into its actual ordinary localization; this map is injective.
Its quotient image is the ordinary quotient fraction with numerator the
class of $a$ and denominator the class of $s^n$. The true localization
zero criterion says this fraction is zero precisely when a power of the
class of $s$ annihilates the class of $a$. The actual quotient kernel
criterion translates this to $s^m a\in J$. This proves the formula on
the original ideal, retaining all local nilpotent structure. It does not
assert that the associated sheaf recovers irrelevant-supported parts of
$J$ from its global sections, nor compare a coherent analytic sheaf.
\end{proof}
