\section{Linearizations of powers and tensor products}
\label{sec:native-tc-nef-fs-tensor-comparisons}

Fix a test configuration $\pi\colon\mathcal X\to\mathbf A^1$ of $X$ with its
$\mathbf G_m$-action $\mathrm{act}\colon\mathbf G_m\times\mathcal X\to\mathcal X$, the
projection $\mathrm{pr}\colon\mathbf G_m\times\mathcal X\to\mathcal X$, the inclusion
$i_1\colon\mathcal X_1\to\mathcal X$ of the fibre over $1$ and the identification
$\phi_1\colon\mathcal X_1\simeq X$.  A \emph{linearized line} is a module $M$ on
$\mathcal X$ with an isomorphism $\alpha\colon\mathrm{act}^*M\simeq\mathrm{pr}^*M$ and an
isomorphism $e\colon i_1^*M\simeq\phi_1^*L$ with a module $L$ on $X$.

\begin{lemma}[Linearizations of powers and tensor products]
\label{lem:native-tc-nef-fs-whole-tensor-comparisons}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.powerLineActionComparison,
EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.tensorLineActionComparison,
EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.powerFibreOneComparison,
EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.tensorFibreOneComparison,
EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.powerTensorLineActionComparison,
EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.powerTensorFibreOneComparison}
\uses{thm:native-test-configuration-line-trivialization}
\source{bj22-trivval:sec:tc}
Let $(M,\alpha,e)$ and $(H,\beta,f)$ be linearized lines with generic lines $L$ and $K$.
For every $n\ge0$ the power $M^{\otimes n}$ carries the linearization
$\alpha_n\colon\mathrm{act}^*M^{\otimes n}\simeq(\mathrm{act}^*M)^{\otimes n}
\xrightarrow{\alpha^{\otimes n}}(\mathrm{pr}^*M)^{\otimes n}\simeq\mathrm{pr}^*M^{\otimes n}$
and the fibre-one identification $e_n$ defined in the same way from $e$, with generic line
$L^{\otimes n}$.  The tensor product $M\otimes H$ carries the linearization
$\mathrm{act}^*(M\otimes H)\simeq\mathrm{act}^*M\otimes\mathrm{act}^*H
\xrightarrow{\alpha\otimes\beta}\mathrm{pr}^*M\otimes\mathrm{pr}^*H\simeq\mathrm{pr}^*(M\otimes H)$
and the analogous fibre-one identification with $L\otimes K$.  Combining both, for all
natural $a,b$ the line $M^{\otimes b}\otimes H^{\otimes a}$ is a linearized line with
generic line $L^{\otimes b}\otimes K^{\otimes a}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Pullback of modules along a morphism of schemes is a strong monoidal functor.  Its
comparison with tensor powers, $g^*(M^{\otimes n})\simeq(g^*M)^{\otimes n}$, is built by
induction on $n$ from the tensorator, and the tensor power of an isomorphism is an
isomorphism.  Composing the three isomorphisms displayed in the statement gives
$\alpha_n$, $e_n$, and the tensor linearization and fibre-one identification.  All of
them are isomorphisms of module sheaves on the action scheme $\mathbf G_m\times\mathcal X$
or on $\mathcal X_1$, for the given maps $\mathrm{act}$, $\mathrm{pr}$, $i_1$, $\phi_1$.
\end{proof}
