\subsection{The actual coordinate cover of the original Rees scheme}

\begin{theorem}[The original polynomial irrelevant ideal]
\label{scheme:polynomial-proj-coordinate-irrelevant}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PolynomialProj.irrelevant_eq_span_X}
\leanok
For any original commutative coefficient ring $A$ and any family of
variables, the true irrelevant ideal of the polynomial total-degree grading
is the ORIGINAL ideal generated by the variables. No radical is taken.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each original variable has degree one, hence lies in the irrelevant ideal.
Conversely, the irrelevant ideal is generated by the original positive
degree homogeneous components. A homogeneous polynomial of degree $d>0$
has every original supported monomial of total degree $d\ge1$, so the
actual support criterion for the variable ideal places the WHOLE polynomial
in the original variable ideal. The two ideal inclusions prove equality,
even for infinite or empty variable types and zero or nonreduced rings.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original Rees coordinate chart forms the true affine cover]
\label{scheme:actual-rees-coordinate-cover}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.irrelevant_eq_span_generators,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.coordinateAffineOpenCover,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.coordinateAffineOpenCover_f,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.instIsOpenImmersion_chartImmersion,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.generator_eq_zero_of_eq_zero,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.nonzeroCoordinateAffineOpenCover,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.nonzeroCoordinateAffineOpenCover_f}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:polynomial-proj-coordinate-irrelevant,scheme:actual-rees-presentation-proper,scheme:actual-rees-projection-surjective}
For any original commutative $A$ and family $g_i$, the ACTUAL charts of the
original homogeneous Rees kernel quotient at its original classes $Y_i$
are a genuine affine open cover of the SAME quotient Proj. Every chart
structure map is the same coefficient map used by the original projection.
No domain, finite generation, nonzero family or Noetherianity is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every actual positive-degree homogeneous quotient element has a true
same-degree original polynomial lift. The preceding support argument puts
this WHOLE lift in the original variable ideal. Apply the original quotient
map: the actual ideal map of the original variable span is the span of the
original quotient variables. This proves that the true quotient irrelevant
ideal is contained in that original span. The reverse inclusion follows
from actual degree-one membership of every quotient variable. Thus the
ideals themselves are equal, without radical or support replacement.

Apply the genuine Proj affine-cover construction to this ORIGINAL degree-one
family and the proved irrelevant ideal bound. Its actual component rings
are the same homogeneous degree-zero localizations, and its morphisms are
the same chart immersions used in the original projection formula. That
formula preserves every original coefficient, so the entire cover lies
over the ORIGINAL coefficient scheme. If an original coefficient $g_i$ is
zero, the presentation kills the WHOLE original variable $Y_i$, so its
quotient class is zero and its chart is empty. Removing exactly those zero
coefficient indices therefore gives another genuine affine open cover:
the original span is unchanged because each omitted generator is actually
zero. This works over every coefficient ring, not just domains. Empty
original charts and empty families cause no extra case or nonemptiness
assumption.
\end{proof}
