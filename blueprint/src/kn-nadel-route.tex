\section{The original Nadel target and its remaining proof producers}
\label{sec:kn-nadel-route}

This section gives a proof route to Nadel vanishing for the original
multiplier ideal, rather than a new vanishing hypothesis.  Every new node
below is a mathematical proof obligation: none is marked as Lean-complete.
The proved global Deligne--Illusie decomposition supplies the upstream proof of
ordinary Kodaira vanishing. Kawamata--Viehweg vanishing, local vanishing,
canonical duality, and Nadel vanishing are to be proved, not supplied as
additional inputs to a final theorem.
The ordinary Kodaira deduction in
Theorem~\ref{thm:kn-original-characteristic-zero-kodaira} uses the actual
global decomposition of the original Witt model. No decomposition premise
is required of the original characteristic-zero vanishing statement.

Throughout the algebraic part, $k$ is a characteristic-zero field, $X$ is
a smooth integral projective $k$-scheme of dimension $d$, and
$\omega_X=\bigwedge^d\Omega^1_{X/k}$.  Write $\mathcal J_X(I^c)$ for the
\emph{actual} ideal
\texttt{Char0.schemeMultiplierIdealSheaf} of the original nonzero coherent
ideal sheaf $I$, with $c\in\Q_{\geq0}$.  Its affine definition uses every
divisorial DVR of the original function field; it is not a choice of a
finite list of valuations or a newly defined rounded ideal.  Its module
is of finite presentation.  A positive-degree assertion $H^q=0$ means
\texttt{Subsingleton (Sheaf.H M.sheaf (r+1))} for every $r\in\N$, using
the actual tensor module $M$ on the original scheme.

The original source application requires a twist uniform in a multiplier
ideal's index.  It appears in the proof of Boucksom--Jonsson's
\texttt{thm:contenvlisse}, after equation \texttt{e508}, and in
Fang--Han--Xia's proof of \texttt{prop:entropy\_approximation}.  The sources are \cite{BJ22} and \cite{FHX} (revision of 2026-10-28).
The latter explicitly invokes Nadel vanishing and Castelnuovo--Mumford
regularity to choose one twist for all sufficiently divisible indices.
Ordinary coherent Serre vanishing with a bound depending on the index
does not establish this assertion.
\source{bj22-trivval:thm:contenvlisse,fhx-envelope-20261028:prop:entropy_approximation}

\begin{theorem}[The original algebraic ideal-power Nadel statement]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-original-target}
\notready
Let $A,B$ be line bundles on the original $X$.  Suppose that
$I\otimes B$ is globally generated and that the rational numerical class
$A-cB$ is nef and big.  Then
\[
 H^q\bigl(X,\omega_X\otimes A\otimes\mathcal J_X(I^c)\bigr)=0
 \qquad(q>0).
\]
The multiplier module, $A$, and the original structure morphism are
retained literally in the conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the internally produced log resolution of
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-original-resolution}.  On it, put
$Q=\mu^*A-cF$, where $I\mathcal O_Y=\mathcal O_Y(-F)$.
The evaluation map for $I\otimes B$ pulls back to a surjection onto
$\mu^*B\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-F)$, so that line is globally generated
and nef.  Consequently
\[
 Q=\mu^*(A-cB)+c(\mu^*B-F)
\]
is nef and big.  Its fractional support lies in the SNC divisor $F$,
and $\lceil Q\rceil=\mu^*A-\lfloor cF\rfloor$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:kn-nadel-snc-kv} kills all positive cohomology of
$\omega_Y\otimes\mu^*A\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-\lfloor cF\rfloor)$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-local-vanishing}, the original whole-module
formula, and the actual line projection formula identify its derived
pushforward with
$\omega_X\otimes A\otimes\mathcal J_X(I^c)$ concentrated in degree zero.
The Leray spectral sequence therefore identifies the two cohomology
groups in every degree, proving the displayed assertion.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-original-resolution,thm:kn-nadel-snc-kv,lem:kn-nadel-local-vanishing,def:sc0-original-global-multiplier-ideal-sheaf,thm:sc0-original-global-multiplier-module-finite-presentation}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Produce the original global principalization and module formula]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-original-resolution}
\notready
Starting from the original $X$ and $I$, produce a projective birational
map $\mu:Y\to X$, given by a finite sequence of blowups in smooth
centres, such that $Y$ is smooth of dimension $d$ and
$I\mathcal O_Y=\mathcal O_Y(-F)$ for an effective divisor $F$ whose
support, together with the exceptional locus, is SNC.  For every
$c\in\Q_{\geq0}$, construct on the same original $X$ an isomorphism
\[
 \mathcal J_X(I^c)\simeq
 \mu_*\bigl(\omega_Y\otimes(\mu^*\omega_X)^\vee
                         \otimes\mathcal O_Y(-\lfloor cF\rfloor)\bigr)
 \tag{N1}
\]
of the whole actual modules, preserving the original ideal inclusion
and canonical differential map.  No chosen resolution, multiplier
comparison, or canonical identification is an input to the producer.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply characteristic-zero principalization to the original coherent
ideal, including accumulated exceptional divisors in the boundary at
each step.  The centres are smooth, avoid the locus where the ideal is
already a unit, and have normal crossings with that boundary.  The
termination invariant supplies a finite sequence.  Its final total
transform is invertible and monomial in local SNC equations, giving
the stated $F$; blowups preserve projectivity and dimension.

To prove (N1), work on an original smooth affine chart with etale
coordinates.  The already proved actual BP formula computes the entire
multiplier module from its internally produced smooth-centre sequence,
not just a kernel or the image of a selected section.  Compare this
sequence and the restriction of $\mu$ on a common smooth principalizing
refinement.  For each original divisorial DVR, change of variables for
the canonical differential identifies the condition for a rational
section to be regular in the rounded relative canonical module with
\[
 c\,\operatorname{ord}_V(I)
 <\operatorname{ord}_V(a)+1+\operatorname{ord}_V(K_{V/X}).
\]
For completeness, the SNC calculation also covers divisors above $Y$:
divide the section times the canonical Jacobian by the monomial
$\prod t_i^{\lfloor cf_i\rfloor}$.  Its regularity gives nonnegative
order in every DVR centred on $Y$.  For the reduced SNC divisor,
$A_Y(V)\geq\sum_i\operatorname{ord}_V(t_i)$, while every coefficient
$cf_i-\lfloor cf_i\rfloor$ is strictly less than one.  These inequalities
give the displayed strict multiplier condition for every such DVR.
Conversely the DVRs of the divisors on $Y$ give the required rounded
regularity.  Thus no valuation is discarded.  The full all-DVR
definition gives the same module on both principalizations.
Equality on their common function
field also identifies the original inclusion and differential maps.
These affine identifications commute with restriction, so they glue to
the whole module isomorphism (N1).  This is an $H^0$ pushforward formula;
the argument asserts no vanishing of higher direct images.
\uses{sc0-bp-geometric-multiplier-module:whole-original-module,thm:rounded-snc-original-independent,def:sc0-original-global-multiplier-ideal-sheaf,thm:sc0-original-global-multiplier-ideal-affine-values}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The normalized SNC cyclic cover is not generally smooth]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-normalization-warning}
\notready
Even in characteristic zero, normalization of a cyclic cover of a
smooth scheme with SNC branch support need not be smooth.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero consider
$z^2=xy$ over $\mathbb A^2_{x,y}$.  Its branch divisor is the union of
the two coordinate axes, hence SNC.  The hypersurface is integral,
Cohen--Macaulay, and regular in codimension one: its only singular point
is the origin, of codimension two.  Serre's criterion therefore shows
that it is already normal.  At the origin the Jacobian row
$(-y,-x,2z)$ is zero, so the hypersurface is singular there.  Thus
normalization does not make this cover a smooth input for ordinary
Kodaira vanishing.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Canonical finite duality on the existing normalized-cover branch]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-normalized-canonical-duality}
\notready
For the actual normalized SNC cyclic cover $\pi:Z\to X$ already used
by the normalized eigensheaf comparison, prove its finiteness and
Cohen--Macaulay property, construct its genuine dualizing sheaf
$\omega_Z$, and construct
\[
 \pi_*\omega_Z\simeq
 \mathcal Hom_X(\pi_*\mathcal O_Z,\omega_X)
 \simeq\bigoplus_{i=0}^{n-1}\omega_X\otimes E_i^\vee.
 \tag{N2}
\]
Here $E_i$ is the same actual eigensheaf in the existing whole
normalized pushforward, and $\omega_Z$ is not declared to be a locally
free top exterior cotangent module on a singular scheme.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original finite monic cover has a finite generic algebra.  Over the
excellent smooth base its integral closure is finite, so the actual
normalization projection is finite.  The already constructed SNC local
normalization has a free rounded monomial basis over the base.  This
proves finite local freeness, and hence the Cohen--Macaulay property
over the regular base.  On an affine chart with finite locally free
algebra $C$ over the smooth algebra $R$, use
$\operatorname{Hom}_R(C,\omega_R)$ with its genuine $C$-action as the
finite dualizing module.  Its duality adjunction and its localization
maps identify these modules on overlaps, giving $\omega_Z$ and the
first isomorphism in (N2).  Dualize the existing whole eigensheaf
isomorphism.  Finite direct sums commute with duality and the $E_i$
are actual lines, giving the second isomorphism.  The already proved
dual-rounded comparison then identifies each displayed coefficient
with the same original rounded canonical tensor module.  The original
evaluation and trace maps must be retained when gluing; an unrelated
module with isomorphic ranks is insufficient.
\uses{lem:kn-normalized-projection-affine,lem:kn-normalized-weighted-cohomology,lem:kn-nadel-cyclic-dual-rounded,cor:kn-nadel-cyclic-canonical-rounded}
\end{proof}

The current normalized-cover cohomology theorem transports an
\emph{unknown} cohomology group across an actual affine projection.
It proves no cover vanishing.  Formula (N2) is its first missing
canonical producer, and still would not justify applying ordinary
Kodaira on the singular $Z$.  The proof below instead produces a
different, genuinely smooth adapted cover.  It needs no unproved
``vanishing on the normalized cover'' premise and does not require
(N2) as a hypothesis.

\begin{lemma}[Produce a smooth adapted denominator cover]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-smooth-adapted-cover}
\notready
Let $Y$ be smooth projective and let $Q$ be a rational Cartier divisor
whose fractional support is SNC.  Produce a finite surjective flat
map $\tau:W\to Y$ with the entire $W$ smooth projective of the same
dimension, such that $\tau^*Q$ is an integral Cartier divisor.  Preserve the actual pullback
line and the facts that ampleness, nefness and bigness pull back along
$\tau$.  The analogous construction is valid for smooth
quasi-projective $Y$, with projectivity over a prescribed affine base
preserved.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write the fractional support as $\bigcup D_i$, with denominator $m_i$
on $D_i$.  For each $i$ choose a sufficiently positive line $H_i$ so
that $m_iH_i-D_i$ is very ample.  Over the infinite field $k$, Bertini
and avoidance give $d+1$ divisors $A_{ij}$ in that linear system such
that their union with all $D_i$ is SNC and, for each fixed $i$, the
$A_{ij}$ have empty common intersection.  Take the cyclic root covers
of all the sections with divisor $D_i+A_{ij}\in|m_iH_i|$, form their
fibre product, and normalize its finite generic algebra.  Finiteness
is proved by the actual local finite free normalization models below;
the entire generic algebra and all its factors are retained.

Here the simultaneous roots are essential.  At a point of $D_i$, some
$A_{ij_0}$ is absent.  After an etale extension extracting roots of
units, that equation supplies a root of a parameter for $D_i$ alone.
The ratios with the other roots supply roots of the parameters of
the $A_{ij}$ through the point.  The SNC condition makes all these
parameters distinct.  The integral closure of the local fibre product
is thus, etale locally, obtained by adjoining independent roots of
regular parameters, followed by a finite etale algebra.  Its local
power bases prove that it is regular and finite free over the base.
This proves smoothness and flatness, including descent from the etale
unit-root extension.  No assertion that a single equation
$z^m=\prod x_i^{a_i}$ is smooth has been used.

Every divisor above $D_i$ now has ramification multiplicity divisible
by $m_i$, so $\tau^*Q$ is integral.  A finite pullback preserves
ampleness, nefness and bigness.  Finiteness preserves projectivity
over the original base.  On a quasi-projective source choose the
auxiliary systems from an ample line after a projective embedding;
the same finite Bertini and local computation apply.  The construction and the finite-pullback ampleness proof retain the
whole possibly disconnected cover. No assumption that $k$ contains
roots of unity is required.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual rounded canonical summand on the smooth cover]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-smooth-cover-rounded-summand}
\notready
For the cover just constructed, set
$N=\mathcal O_W(\tau^*Q)$.  Construct original inclusion and projection
maps exhibiting
\[
 \omega_Y\otimes\mathcal O_Y(\lceil Q\rceil)
 \quad\text{as a direct summand of}\quad
 \tau_*(\omega_W\otimes N).
 \tag{N3}
\]
These maps are produced from the cover, not supplied as a retraction
or as a canonical identification certificate.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $Q=M-\sum_i(a_i/m_i)D_i$, where $M=\lceil Q\rceil$ and
$0\leq a_i<m_i$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-whole-root-ideals} constructs
from all actual original row roots the invertible ideals $J_i$ with
$J_i^{m_i}=I_{D_i}\mathcal O_W$.  Put $J=\prod_iJ_i^{a_i}$, so that
$N=\tau^*\mathcal O_Y(M)\otimes J$.

The original canonical differential factors through $\omega_W\otimes J$
by Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-bounded-ramification-factor}.  The regular
canonical trace of Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-whole-canonical-trace} has
composite $D\,\mathrm{id}$ with that differential, where $D$ is the
positive degree of the whole original cover.  Adjoint the factorization,
restrict the actual trace along the ideal inclusion, and multiply the
trace by $D^{-1}$.  Tensoring by $\mathcal O_Y(M)$ and the actual line
projection formula give the required retraction, exactly as in
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-trace-rounded-retraction}.  No invariant-sheaf
identification is a premise.  Applying actual sheaf cohomology preserves
the identity composite, and the actual affine pushforward comparison
identifies the cover cohomology.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-whole-root-ideals,lem:kn-nadel-whole-canonical-trace,lem:kn-nadel-bounded-ramification-factor,lem:kn-nadel-trace-rounded-retraction,lem:kn-normalized-affine-cohomology}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Ample rational SNC vanishing from ordinary Kodaira]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-ample-q-vanishing}
\notready
If $Q$ is ample with SNC fractional support on smooth projective
$Y$, then $H^q(Y,\omega_Y\otimes\mathcal O_Y(\lceil Q\rceil))=0$
for all $q>0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The constructed smooth cover makes $N=\mathcal O_W(\tau^*Q)$ an
actual ample line.  Ordinary characteristic-zero Kodaira vanishing
on the actual whole smooth projective $W$ kills
$H^q(W,\omega_W\otimes N)$.  Finite affine pushforward and the
actual retraction (N3) then kill the desired group.  This uses the
upstream ordinary Kodaira theorem, whose proof is the DI/arithmetic
route; it uses no Kawamata--Viehweg or Nadel vanishing hypothesis.
\uses{thm:kn-original-characteristic-zero-kodaira,lem:kn-nadel-smooth-adapted-cover,lem:kn-nadel-smooth-cover-rounded-summand}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Canonical acyclicity of actual smooth-centre blowups]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-smooth-blowup-canonical}
\notready
For a blowup $\sigma:V\to Y$ of a smooth scheme in a smooth centre
containing no irreducible component of $Y$,
construct $R\sigma_*\omega_V\simeq\omega_Y$, with its actual
canonical map.  This holds for a finite composition of such blowups
and after twisting by a line pulled back from $Y$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The empty centre is defined by the unit ideal and its blowup is an
isomorphism. A codimension-one smooth centre under the stated condition
is an effective Cartier divisor, whose blowup is also an isomorphism.
Codimension zero is excluded: blowing up the zero ideal gives the empty
scheme and would make the asserted canonical comparison false.
In codimension $r\geq2$, choose actual adapted affine neighbourhoods
of the centre, etale over affine space, with the whole centre ideal
pulled back from the coordinate ideal.  The actual Rees blowup is the
flat base change of the whole coordinate blowup.  On that original
coordinate model, every tuple intersection of the original chart cover
is the spectrum of its actual polynomial ring localized at the product
of the relevant ratios.  The original restriction maps retain all
coefficients and monomials under these identifications.

Decompose each actual Cech cochain into its finite set of Laurent
weights.  The centre-coordinate weights have nonnegative total sum,
equal to the original radial degree.  Thus their negative support omits
at least one centre index.  Inserting that index gives the alternating
support contraction in every positive degree.  It respects the actual
restriction maps and differentials.  Summing the primitives over the
finite weight support proves exactness of the whole original Cech
complex in every positive degree, hence the actual structure-sheaf
cohomology vanishing.  Its degree-zero sections are the original
polynomial base ring, through the original blowup projection.

For the actual etale coefficient algebra, the genuine geometric Cech
comparison identifies the entire coefficient-changed complex with the
flat tensor extension of this model complex.  Flat tensor extension
preserves exactness; its degree-zero comparison retains the original
coefficient map.  This proves the structure-sheaf assertion on each
actual adapted affine neighbourhood, with no acyclicity hypothesis
supplied by the neighbourhood construction.

Let $E$ be the actual exceptional subscheme.  Its whole flat base-change
identification is projective space of dimension $r-1$ over the original
centre ring, and the actual line restriction is
$\mathcal O_V(jE)|_E=\mathcal O_E(-j)$.  For $1\leq j\leq r-1$ every
cohomology group of this last line, including degree zero, vanishes.
The true Cartier exact sequences
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_V((j-1)E)
 \longrightarrow\mathcal O_V(jE)
 \longrightarrow i_*\mathcal O_E(-j)\longrightarrow0
\]
therefore propagate the positive vanishing and the original degree-zero
section isomorphism from $j=0$ to $j=r-1$.

The entire coordinate Jacobian and its etale transport construct
$\sigma^*\omega_Y\simeq\omega_V\otimes I_E^{r-1}$, preserving the
actual canonical differential.  The actual Cartier ideal-power/tensor
comparison gives the equivalent positive-divisor formula.  The line
projection formula now proves the canonical pushforward identification
and all positive acyclicity on the adapted affine preimages, retaining
the original canonical map.  To extend from the adapted chart cover to
an arbitrary affine base open, its finite affine refinement has acyclic
inverse-image tuple intersections by the same flat coefficient
calculation.  The actual Cech comparison of
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-leray-acyclic-intersections} proves vanishing on
that whole affine preimage.  Thus $R\sigma_*\omega_V=\omega_Y$.
The same local comparison and canonical maps give the assertion for a
finite composition; actual line projection proves the pulled-line
version.  This derives the assertion from the explicit blowup
calculation without adding a general local-vanishing premise.
For the actual principalization sequence, the centre condition is proved
internally.  Its final scheme is nonempty by
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-original-principalization-integral}; its actual
maps imply nonemptiness at every intermediate blowup.  Inductively the
ambient stages are integral.  A centre containing their whole component
would have zero ideal and empty blowup, a contradiction.  Thus the
application supplies no new centre or acyclicity certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Integral nef-and-big Kodaira from the ample rational case]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-integral-nef-big}
\notready
If $D$ is an integral nef and big divisor on smooth projective $Y$,
then $H^q(Y,\omega_Y\otimes\mathcal O_Y(D))=0$ for $q>0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine numerical Kodaira lemma for interior-pseudoeffective
bigness, including its numerical-to-geometric positivity comparison,
gives a decomposition $D=A_0+E_0$ with $A_0$ ample rational and
$E_0$ effective rational.  Resolve $E_0$ by smooth-centre blowups.
On the resulting $\mu:V\to Y$, subtract a sufficiently small positive
exceptional divisor from $\mu^*A_0$ to make it ample, and add that
divisor to $\mu^*E_0$.  This gives
$\mu^*D=A+E$ with $A$ ample rational and $E$ effective SNC rational;
the exceptional divisor is included in that SNC boundary.  The
exceptional correction exists because a negative exceptional
combination for a composite of blowups is relatively ample; for a
small correction, its sum with the pulled-back ample class is ample.

Choose rational $\varepsilon>0$ with $\varepsilon<1$ and every
coefficient of $\varepsilon E$ less than one.  The rational divisor
\[
 Q_\varepsilon=\mu^*D-\varepsilon E
              =(1-\varepsilon)\mu^*D+\varepsilon A
\]
is ample, its fractional support is SNC, and
$\lceil Q_\varepsilon\rceil=\mu^*D$.  The ample rational result kills
the positive cohomology of $\omega_V\otimes\mu^*\mathcal O_Y(D)$.
Canonical acyclicity of the actual blowup sequence identifies this
with the requested cohomology on $Y$.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-ample-q-vanishing,lem:kn-nadel-smooth-blowup-canonical}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Prove global SNC Kawamata--Viehweg vanishing]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-snc-kv}
\notready
If $Q$ is rational nef and big with SNC fractional support on smooth
projective $Y$, then
\[
 H^q(Y,\omega_Y\otimes\mathcal O_Y(\lceil Q\rceil))=0
 \qquad(q>0).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The internally constructed smooth adapted cover makes $\tau^*Q$
an integral nef and big divisor on the smooth projective $W$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-integral-nef-big} gives vanishing there.
The actual normalized-trace retraction (N3) and affine cohomology
comparison return the desired group as a direct summand of a zero
group.  In particular, this theorem is proved from ordinary Kodaira,
covering, blowup, positivity and duality producers.  It is not an
additional final input called ``KV vanishing''.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-smooth-adapted-cover,lem:kn-nadel-smooth-cover-rounded-summand,lem:kn-nadel-integral-nef-big}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Derive local vanishing on the produced resolution]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-local-vanishing}
\notready
For the actual $\mu,F$ of
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-original-resolution},
\[
 R^j\mu_*\bigl(\omega_Y\otimes
                  \mathcal O_Y(-\lfloor cF\rfloor)\bigr)=0
 \qquad(j>0).
 \tag{N4}
\]
Equivalently the higher images of the same rounded relative canonical
module in (N1) vanish.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an ample $H$ and an integer $a$ so that $I\otimes H^a$ is
globally generated.  Thus $\mu^*(aH)-F$ is nef.  For all large $m$,
\[
 Q_m=\mu^*(mH)-cF
    =c(\mu^*(aH)-F)+\mu^*((m-ca)H)
\]
is nef and big with SNC fractional support.  Global SNC KV gives
$H^j(Y,\omega_Y\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-\lfloor cF\rfloor)
                    \otimes\mu^*H^m)=0$ for every $j>0$.
Properness gives coherence of every actual higher direct image in
(N4); there are only finitely many possibly nonzero degrees.
Serre vanishing on $X$, projection formula, and Leray then identify
\[
 H^0\bigl(X,R^j\mu_*(\omega_Y(-\lfloor cF\rfloor))\otimes H^m\bigr)
 \simeq
 H^j\bigl(Y,\omega_Y(-\lfloor cF\rfloor)\otimes\mu^*H^m\bigr)=0
\]
for $m$ sufficiently large.  The same coherent sheaf twisted by
$H^m$ is globally generated for large $m$, so zero global sections
force it to be zero.  Untwisting proves (N4).  Projection formula
with $\omega_X^\vee$ gives the relative-canonical version.  The map
$\mu$ is generally not affine; affine pushforward acyclicity cannot
replace this argument.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-original-resolution,thm:kn-nadel-snc-kv}
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The effective rational-divisor form]
\label{cor:kn-nadel-q-divisor}
\notready
Let $\Delta$ be any effective rational divisor on the original
smooth projective $X$, with no SNC requirement on $X$.  Choose a
positive integer $N$ with $N\Delta$ integral Cartier, and define
\[
 \mathcal J_X(\Delta)
   =\mathcal J_X\bigl(\mathcal O_X(-N\Delta)^{1/N}\bigr)
\]
using the actual original ideal construction.  This is independent
of $N$.  If $A-\Delta$ is nef and big, then
$H^q(X,\omega_X\otimes A\otimes\mathcal J_X(\Delta))=0$ for $q>0$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
At each original divisorial DVR, the product of the exponent $1/N$
and the order of the ideal $\mathcal O_X(-N\Delta)$ is exactly the
order of $\Delta$.  The full valuative inequalities therefore prove
independence of $N$, including at exceptional divisors.  Apply the
original ideal-power theorem with $I=\mathcal O_X(-N\Delta)$,
$B=\mathcal O_X(N\Delta)$ and $c=1/N$.  The tensor $I\otimes B$ is
the actual unit line and $A-cB=A-\Delta$, proving the claim.
\uses{thm:kn-nadel-original-target,def:sc0-original-global-multiplier-ideal-sheaf}
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Uniform generation for the original graded base ideals]
\label{cor:kn-nadel-asymptotic-uniform-generation}
\notready
Let $I_\bullet$ be an actual graded sequence of nonzero ideal sheaves
on $X$ and let $M$ be a line such that $I_p\otimes M^p$ is globally
generated for every positive $p$.  For a very ample $H$, put
$G=\omega_X\otimes H^{d+1}$.  Then
\[
 G\otimes M^m\otimes\mathcal J_X(\|I_\bullet^m\|)
 \quad\text{is globally generated for every }m\geq1.
 \tag{N5}
\]
The ideal is the existing whole
\texttt{schemeAsymptoticMultiplierIdealSheaf}; $G$ is independent
of $m$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The proved whole-sheaf stabilization gives, for this $m$, a positive
index $p$ with
$\mathcal J_X(\|I_\bullet^m\|)=\mathcal J_X(I_p^{m/p})$.
For $1\leq i\leq d$ apply the original Nadel theorem with
\[
 I=I_p,\qquad c=m/p,\qquad B=M^p,\qquad
 A=M^m\otimes H^{d+1-i}.
\]
Its positivity difference is exactly $(d+1-i)H$, which is ample;
the assumed generation is precisely that of $I_p\otimes B$.
Thus all groups in the Castelnuovo--Mumford zero-regularity test
for the sheaf in (N5) vanish.  Degrees greater than $d$ vanish by
dimension.  Castelnuovo--Mumford regularity gives global generation.
Although the stabilizing $p$ depends on $m$, the cancelling expression
for $A-cB$ proves that $G$ does not.  This is the uniform assertion
needed in the source, rather than an eventual Serre statement for
each separately chosen multiplier ideal.
\uses{thm:kn-nadel-original-target,thm:sc0-original-global-asymptotic-stabilization,def:sc0-original-global-asymptotic-multiplier-ideal}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The relative extension needed for test configurations]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-relative-source-application}
\notready
Let $f:X\to S$ be projective, with $X$ smooth integral quasi-projective over
$k$ and $S$ affine of finite type over $k$.  The original ideal-power
Nadel statement holds with ``nef and big'' interpreted relative to
$f$ and with conclusion
\[
 R^q f_*(\omega_X\otimes A\otimes\mathcal J_X(I^c))=0
 \qquad(q>0).
 \tag{N6}
\]
If $H$ is relatively very ample and $I_p\otimes M^p$ is relatively
globally generated, the analogue of (N5) is relatively globally
generated, hence globally generated over the affine base.  This is
the version to apply to the smooth resolved test configurations in
the original Boucksom--Jonsson and Fang--Han--Xia arguments.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First derive relative ordinary Kodaira from the \emph{proved absolute}
SNC KV theorem.  For an $f$-ample line $L$, choose $a>0$ giving a
closed immersion $X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^N_S$ with
$L^a=\mathcal O(1)|_X$.  Compactify $S$ by its closure in projective
space so its complement is the support of an ample Cartier divisor
$D_\infty$.  Close the same immersion over this compactification
and resolve the boundary, without changing the smooth open $X$.
After a large base twist, the ambient $\mathcal O(1)$ extends to an
ample line $P$ on the possibly singular closure.  Its pullback to
the resolution is nef and big.  A rational section of the original
$L$ identifies a divisor of $P$ whose restriction to $X$ is exactly
$a$ times the original divisor of $L$, since the base twist is
trivial on $S$.  Consequently the rational divisor $Q=P/a$ restricts
to that \emph{integral original divisor}, and
$\mathcal O(\lceil Q\rceil)|_X=L$; no replacement of $L$ by $L^a$
occurs in the conclusion.  Resolve the boundary so that the
fractional support of this actual $Q$ is SNC.

For every $t\geq0$, $Q+t f^*D_\infty$ is nef and big with the same
fractional support.  Absolute SNC KV kills positive cohomology of
its rounded canonical sheaf on the smooth compactification.
Taking the direct limit over poles along $f^*D_\infty$ gives
$H^q(X,\omega_X\otimes L)=0$ for $q>0$.  This direct limit is the
actual open-restriction cohomology, since the boundary is the
support of that effective Cartier divisor.  On the affine $S$,
Leray and coherence identify this with zero of
$R^q f_*(\omega_X\otimes L)$.  This proves relative ordinary
Kodaira while keeping the original line.

Now repeat the adapted-cover and finite-duality argument over $S$
to prove the relative ample rational case.  Relative Kodaira's
decomposition, a smooth-centre resolution, and a small effective
SNC exceptional correction give the relative integral nef-and-big
case by exactly the divisor
$(1-\varepsilon)\mu^*D+\varepsilon A$ used above.  This decomposition
comes from relative section growth and relative Serre generation;
over the affine base its effective rational divisor is an actual
global divisor.  Canonical blowup acyclicity is local on the target
and thus gives the same derived pushforward comparison.  The
adapted denominator cover then proves relative SNC KV.  All these
are producers to be implemented; no relative KV premise is added
to (N6).

For the actual principalization $\mu:Y\to X$, $-F$ is $\mu$-globally
generated because it is the invertible total transform of $I$.
Thus $-cF$ is $\mu$-nef and $\mu$-big: the generic fibre of the
birational map has dimension zero.  Relative SNC KV applied to
$\mu$, locally over affine opens in $X$, proves (N4) on this
quasi-projective source as well.  The actual multiplier formula
glues from the same affine all-DVR formula.  Finally
$\mu^*A-cF$ is nef and big over $S$ by the original positivity
and generation assumptions.  Relative SNC KV and the just derived
local vanishing identify its derived pushforward with the sheaf
in (N6), proving (N6) by Leray.  Apply it to the stabilized
constituents with $A=M^mH^{d+1-i}$ and $B=M^p$ as above.
Relative Castelnuovo--Mumford regularity gives the fixed uniform
generation statement.  A common divisible index clears the
original rational line classes in the source application; the
fixed twist can be enlarged once to a chosen ample twist,
independently of $m$: relative Serre generation makes
$H^{m_0}\otimes(\omega_X\otimes H^{d+1})^\vee$ generated for one
sufficiently large $m_0$.  Tensoring its evaluation map with (N5)
gives generation of $M^m\otimes H^{m_0}\otimes\mathcal J_X(\|I_\bullet^m\|)$
for every relevant $m$.
\uses{thm:kn-nadel-snc-kv,lem:kn-nadel-smooth-adapted-cover,lem:kn-nadel-smooth-cover-rounded-summand,lem:kn-nadel-smooth-blowup-canonical,lem:kn-nadel-original-resolution,cor:kn-nadel-asymptotic-uniform-generation}
\source{bj22-trivval:thm:contenvlisse,fhx-envelope-20261028:prop:entropy_approximation}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The additional analytic arbitrary-weight Nadel target]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-analytic-arbitrary-weight}
\notready
Let $(X,\omega)$ be a weakly pseudoconvex K\"ahler manifold, $F$ a
holomorphic line bundle, and $h$ a singular Hermitian metric, with
local norm $|v|_h=|v|e^{-\varphi}$.  Suppose
$\mathrm i\Theta_{F,h}\geq\varepsilon\omega$ for a continuous
strictly positive function $\varepsilon$.  For the actual analytic
multiplier ideal of locally integrable $|a|^2e^{-2\varphi}$,
\[
 H^q(X,K_X\otimes F\otimes\mathcal J(h))=0\qquad(q>0).
 \tag{N7}
\]
This includes weights without algebraic or rational-divisor
singularities.  It is an additional target, not a consequence of
replacing its ideal by an algebraic one.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Produce the sheaf $\mathcal L^q$ of $F$-valued measurable $(d,q)$
forms for which the form and its distributional $\bar\partial$ are
locally square integrable with weight $e^{-2\varphi}$.
Its degree-zero closed germs are exactly
$K_X\otimes F\otimes\mathcal J(h)$: a distributionally holomorphic
coefficient has a holomorphic representative and the weight
condition is the defining multiplier integrability.  The local
weighted $L^2$ solution theorem on small balls makes this complex
exact in positive degrees.  Multiplication by smooth cutoff
functions preserves the domain, so these sheaves are fine and
acyclic.  This constructs an actual resolution of the original
analytic ideal tensor sheaf.

For a closed positive-degree global form, choose a smooth
plurisubharmonic exhaustion $\psi$ and a convex increasing
$\chi$ growing fast enough that the relevant weighted integrals,
including the inverse curvature bound, converge after multiplying
the metric by $e^{-\chi\circ\psi}$.  The extra curvature is
semipositive.  The global weighted $L^2$ estimate therefore solves
$\bar\partial v=u$.  On every compact set the extra exhaustion
weight and its inverse are bounded, so this solution belongs to
the original local weighted complex.  Its positive cohomology is
zero, and the acyclic resolution proves (N7).

Thus a formal proof must produce the original weighted form sheaves,
distributional domain, fine resolution, local solution theorem,
and global exhaustion estimate.  Existing coherence or DEL
subadditivity for holomorphic germ ideals supplies none of these
global cohomology identifications by itself.  This is the actual
proof of Demailly's Nadel theorem, \cite[Theorem~5.11]{DemAM}.
It is a separate analytic proof; no $L^2$ estimate is assumed
as an extra input to the algebraic theorem above.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Compare algebraic singularities with the original analytic ideal]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-algebraic-analytic-comparison}
\notready
On a smooth complex algebraic variety, if in algebraic local
generators of the original $I$ a weight has the form
\[
 \varphi=\frac c2\log\Bigl(\sum_j|g_j|^2\Bigr)+O(1),
 \qquad c\in\Q_{\geq0},
\]
then its actual analytic multiplier ideal is the analytification of
the original $\mathcal J_X(I^c)$.  For projective $X$, GAGA identifies
the cohomology of the same canonical line tensor ideal in the two
categories.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual log resolution of $I$.  In local SNC coordinates its
pullback generators are a common monomial times generators of the
unit ideal.  Bounded changes of weight do not change local
integrability.  Change of variables multiplies the measure by the
squared absolute value of the actual holomorphic Jacobian.  Thus a
coefficient $a$ is integrable precisely when, for each component,
\[
 \operatorname{ord}(a\circ\mu)
 +\operatorname{ord}(K_{Y/X})-c\operatorname{ord}(F)>-1.
\]
The product one-variable integrals give this strict inequality,
equivalently the rounded relative canonical condition with
$-\lfloor cF\rfloor$.  Change of variables and the full original
multiplier formula identify the entire analytic ideal, respecting
restriction, not just its values away from the exceptional set.
Coherence and GAGA then give the stated projective cohomology
comparison.  This comparison has to be proved for the existing
analytic germ object; it is explicitly not supplied by
\texttt{Char0/AnalyticMultiplierSubadditivity.lean}, whose header
records it as open.  Arbitrary quasi-PSH weights need not have this
finite algebraic form.  Extending by regularization would require
additional equisingularity or strong-openness and curvature-preserving
cohomology arguments, so the general assertion (N7) remains an
independent obligation.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-original-resolution,def:sc0-original-global-multiplier-ideal-sheaf}
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Reference and implementation boundary.}
Demailly \cite[\S6.D, especially (6.23)--(6.25)]{DemAM} states the
SNC rounding, change-of-variables multiplier formula, and
Kawamata--Viehweg vanishing.  Its proof of (6.25) uses analytic Nadel;
that proof cannot be used here to derive Nadel from KV without a
cycle.  The algebraic argument above instead uses the adapted-cover
method described there as the original Kawamata--Viehweg approach
and developed in Esnault--Viehweg, \emph{Lectures on Vanishing
Theorems}, DMV Seminar~20 (1992): smooth simultaneous root covers,
the regular canonical trace, ordinary Kodaira, and the explicit blowup
calculation.  Local tame finite duality constructs the regular trace;
the actual differential and its normalized trace composite construct
the rounded retraction.
The repository currently contains Demailly's source, not a formal
implementation of those covering and duality constructions.

After the proved dual-rounded and normalized-cohomology comparisons,
the first missing producer on that existing normalized-cover branch
is (N2), including the actual canonical/dualizing object and its maps.
The first missing producer that can introduce \emph{vanishing} on the
acyclic algebraic route above is the genuinely smooth adapted cover
and its actual rounded canonical retraction (N3).  The existing single
normalized SNC cover does not satisfy its smoothness conclusion.
Global original-ideal principalization, proper higher-direct-image
machinery/local vanishing, and the positivity/relative applications
also remain obligations.  The current logarithmic Serre result and
empty-boundary comparison do not provide a logarithmic DI splitting
or a logarithmic vanishing theorem for a nonempty boundary.  In
particular this section claims neither Nadel nor the arbitrary-weight
analytic theorem as formally completed.
