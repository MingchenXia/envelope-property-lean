\label{sc0-valuative-line-regularity:every-original-section}

\end{theorem}

values in $F$, derived from genuine whole ring-homomorphism squares.

inverse belongs to $V$. Original field cancellation therefore

SAME normalized original coefficient, without supplied matches.

\end{proof}
