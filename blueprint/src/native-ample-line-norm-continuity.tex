\section{Actual geometric ample lines have continuous whole-section norms}
\label{sec:native-ample-line-norm-continuity}

\begin{theorem}[Every whole ample-line weighted datum has continuous true evaluation]
\label{thm:native-ample-line-whole-fs-continuous}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeLineFubiniStudy.FSData.continuous_eval_of_isAmple}
\leanok
\uses{thm:native-ample-model-fs-evaluation,def:gl-fs,thm:projective-fixed-native-analytic-gluing}
On an integral original scheme proper over its original field, actual
geometric ampleness of an original line implies continuity of every
whole-section weighted FS datum for that line on the entire original
native point space. Zero families, arbitrary real weights and common
zeroes are allowed. No norm, section, model or continuity comparison
certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The geometric ample criterion supplies a positive power, an actual closed
projective embedding of the same original scheme, its whole original
structure triangle and an isomorphism between the pulled original
$\mathcal O(1)$ and that same original power line. Choose a nonempty actual
projective coordinate open through the native original generic point.
This constructs the genuine full-function-field model and the whole
native homeomorphism. The actual weighted-datum transport theorem identifies
its entire true original norm evaluation with evaluation of its actual
model datum under this same homeomorphism. Every genuine model datum has
continuous evaluation, without a nonzero-family requirement. Composition
with the true native homeomorphism proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every entire original ample-line section has continuous true norm]
\label{thm:native-ample-line-global-section-norm-continuous}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalSectionNormContinuity.continuous_of_isAmple}
\leanok
\uses{thm:native-ample-line-whole-fs-continuous,thm:scheme-global-section-model-norm-isometry}
For every actual entire section of an actual geometrically ample original
line on an integral proper original scheme, its true original model norm
is continuous as a nonnegative-real-valued function on the same entire
original native space, including its zero locus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the genuine original unit-tensor isomorphism to regard the original
section as an entire section of the actual first tensor power. Give it
weight zero in the one-term FS datum of degree and denominator one.
The actual module-isomorphism norm theorem identifies the evaluation of
this literal datum with the extended logarithm of the original section
norm. The preceding theorem makes that extended logarithm continuous,
even where the norm vanishes. The extended exponential is continuous
and sends this logarithm back to the same nonnegative norm, viewed in
the extended nonnegative reals. The inclusion of nonnegative reals into
extended nonnegative reals is a topological embedding, so the original
nonnegative-real norm is continuous. No exclusion of zero sections or
zero native values is needed.
\end{proof}
