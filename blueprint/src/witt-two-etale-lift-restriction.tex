\section{Chosen Witt Frobenius lifts extend along actual etale restrictions}
\label{sec:witt-two-etale-lift-restriction}

Let $k$ be the original perfect field of characteristic $p$, and let
$A,A'$ be smooth algebras over its length-two Witt ring. Let
$\beta:A\to A'$ be an actual etale Witt algebra map. Retain an
arbitrary actual relative Frobenius lift on $A$, with its genuine
reduction equation. No lift on $A'$ or commuting lifted square is
supplied.

\begin{lemma}[Actual etaleness of the original twisted restriction]
\label{lem:witt-two-twisted-restriction-etale}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleLiftRestriction.twistMap_etale}
\leanok
The SAME original tensor restriction between the actual Witt
Frobenius twists of $A$ and $A'$ is etale.
\uses{thm:witt-two-source-native-untwist,def:witt-two-actual-twist-restriction-map}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original inverse-Witt-coefficient tensor equivalences identify
the two actual twisted rings with $A$ and $A'$. On every pure
tensor, the actual tensor restriction keeps its copied coefficient
and applies $\beta$ to its original algebra factor. The two actual
untwisting maps therefore commute with this restriction by the
Witt algebra equation for $\beta$. Tensor induction proves the
whole ring-map identity. Thus the SAME twisted restriction is
obtained from $\beta$ by composing actual ring equivalences.
Etaleness is invariant under these equivalences.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An actual restricted lift is produced]
\label{thm:witt-two-etale-restricted-lift-exists}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleLiftRestriction.exists_restrictedLift}
\leanok
There exists an actual target relative Frobenius lift whose entire
reduction is the original target relative Frobenius and whose
composite with the SAME actual twisted restriction equals
$\beta$ composed with the prescribed source lift, on EVERY
original twisted element.
\uses{lem:witt-two-twisted-restriction-etale,lem:witt-two-smooth-algebra-reduction,thm:witt-two-actual-residue-frobenius-naturality}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Give $A'$ its actual source-twist algebra structure through the
prescribed lift followed by $\beta$. Give its actual residue ring
the composite reduction algebra structure. The original target
residue Frobenius is a homomorphism of these source-twist
algebras: actual reduction naturality, the supplied source lift's
reduction equation, and the proved whole residue Frobenius
naturality give the equation on EVERY source-twist element.
The actual target reduction is surjective and its ENTIRE kernel
has square zero, as derived from smooth Witt prime exactness.
The first lemma gives formal smoothness of the actual twisted
restriction. Its infinitesimal lifting property produces a
source-twist algebra homomorphism from the actual target twist
to $A'$. Its source-twist algebra equation is precisely the
required original lifted commutation equation. Evaluating that
same equation on the copied coefficient image proves the
produced map is a homomorphism over the original copied Witt
base. The lifting equation is its required entire original
relative Frobenius reduction. Thus both equations follow from
the produced actual homomorphism; neither is an input matching
certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The produced actual restriction lift is unique]
\label{thm:witt-two-etale-restricted-lift-unique}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.WittTwoEtaleLiftRestriction.restrictedLift_unique}
\leanok
Any two actual target relative Frobenius lifts with the original
reduction equation and the same prescribed commutation along the
actual twisted restriction are equal as whole copied-Witt-base
algebra homomorphisms.
\uses{lem:witt-two-twisted-restriction-etale,lem:witt-two-smooth-algebra-reduction}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Retain the actual source-twist algebra structure on $A'$ through
the prescribed source lift and $\beta$. Each target lift is a
homomorphism of these algebras by its given actual commutation
equation. Their reductions are the SAME original target residue
Frobenius. Formal unramifiedness of the actual twisted restriction
implies injectivity on lifts across the actual square-zero
reduction kernel. The two source-twist algebra homomorphisms are
therefore equal, so their underlying ring maps and original
copied-Witt-base algebra maps are equal. No uniqueness or
restriction-matching conclusion is supplied as an input.
\end{proof}
