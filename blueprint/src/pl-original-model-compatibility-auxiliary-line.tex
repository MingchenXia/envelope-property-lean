\section{The unchanged normalized tautological product line}

\begin{lemma}[The same polynomial inclusion preserves the original parameter]
\label{lem:pl-original-polynomial-inclusion-parameter-structure}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeProductPolynomialInclusion_over_originalParameter}
\leanok
The whole original product's polynomial projective closed inclusion,
followed by the ordinary projective-space structure map, is precisely the
unchanged original product parameter map.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The ordinary projective-space structure and the native polynomial-Proj
structure have the same original degree-zero coefficient inclusion.
The inverse original polynomial-product isomorphism followed by that
structure is the actual product parameter projection. Compose with the
unchanged original closed product inclusion to obtain the assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The actual original normalized tautological product line]
\label{def:pl-original-normalized-tautological-product-line}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalProductIdeal,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalTwistLine}
\leanok
Let $\mu:B\to X\times\mathbb A^1$ be the original blowup of $IJ$ and
$\nu:N\to B$ its original normalization. Put
$K_N=(IJ)\mathcal O_N$. For an original twist $\mathcal O_X(q)$, put
$H_q=q_N^*\mathcal O_X(q)\otimes K_N$. This uses the actual product-ideal
module, namely the finite pullback of the original blowup tautological
ideal, rather than a replacement line with the same numerical class.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The actual tautological line has its whole group and fibre-one data]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-tautological-product-line-action-fibre-one}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalProductIdeal_isInvertible,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalTwistLine_isInvertible,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalProductIdeal_invariant,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalProductNativeGmComparison,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalTwistNativeGmComparison,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalProductFibreOneIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTautologicalTwistFibreOneIso}
\leanok
\uses{def:pl-original-normalized-tautological-product-line,thm:pl-original-normalized-cartier-factor-linearizations,thm:pl-original-normalized-projection-action-invariant,thm:pl-original-normalized-fibre-one-variety}
The unchanged ideal $K_N$ is Cartier, invariant under the constructed
actual normalized action, and trivial on the entire actual fibre at $1$.
Consequently $H_q$ is invertible, has an explicit whole group comparison,
and restricts on the same original fibre at $1$ to the same original
$\mathcal O_X(q)$ through its actual fibre-one isomorphism.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The blowup tautological ideal $(IJ)\mathcal O_B$ is Cartier. Dominance and
integrality of the original normalization preserve its Cartier property.
Whole ideal comap respects multiplication, so
$K_N=I_NJ_N$. The already proved entire ideal invariance of both factors
therefore proves entire invariance of $K_N$. Its canonical
inclusion-preserving Cartier group comparison applies. Both original
ideals contain parameter powers, so their entire supports miss the
noncentral open; the same holds for $K_N$. The original fibre at $1$
factors through this open. Pulling its actual ideal inclusion
trivialization back gives the stated fibre-one trivialization. Finally,
tensor this actual line with the unchanged original twist pullback.
Its whole group comparison is the tensor of the two original comparisons,
and its fibre-one comparison follows by tensoring the actual ideal
trivialization and using the exact original fibre-one projection formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The tautological twist as a linearized line on the normalized test configuration]
\label{def:pl-original-normalized-tc-tautological-line-data}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeTestConfigurationTautologicalLineComparison,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeTestConfigurationTautologicalFibreOneLineIso}
\leanok
\uses{thm:pl-original-normalized-tautological-product-line-action-fibre-one}
Let $\mathcal C$ be the normalized simultaneous model regarded as a test
configuration of $X$ (its action and fibre-one identification are the
constructed ones). The $\mathbf G_m$-linearization of $H_q$ and the
identification $H_q|_{\mathcal C_1}\simeq\mathcal O_X(q)$ of the preceding
theorem are the line-action comparison and the fibre-one identification of
$H_q$ required by the line API of test configurations.
\end{definition}
