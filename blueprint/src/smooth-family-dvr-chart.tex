\section{A produced simultaneous monomial leaf in every actual divisorial DVR}

\begin{definition}[The genuine original-source DVR Jacobian ideal]\label{def:sc0-original-source-dvr-jacobian-ideal}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.dvrOriginalSourceJacobianIdeal, EnvelopeProperty.Char0.dvrSourceJacobianIdeal_through_leaf}
\leanok
For an actual inclusion $C\subseteq V$ and actual produced DVR coordinate
partials, the original-source DVR Jacobian ideal is the ideal generated by
their determinants on every tuple of original $C$-functions. Computing this
ideal through an intermediate literal leaf retains exactly that ideal,
because the literal inclusion maps compose.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[An actual smooth-source family tree supplies every divisorial chart]\label{thm:sc0-smooth-family-dvr-chart}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.exists_smooth_family_dvr_chart}
\leanok
\uses{thm:sc0-smooth-family-rees-tree,lem:sc0-actual-dvr-valuation-subring,lem:sc0-actual-leaf-dvr-coordinate-index,thm:sc0-dvr-leaf-source-jacobian,def:sc0-original-source-dvr-jacobian-ideal}
Let $C$ be an actual smooth affine domain over a characteristic-zero field,
$K$ its actual fraction field, and $\mathfrak a_j$ a finite family of actual
nonzero ideals. For every actual divisorial DVR $V\subset K$ containing $C$,
there exist a literal finite Rees tree rooted at $C$ and a leaf
$C\subseteq E\subseteq V$, with actual \'etale coordinates, such that all
original ideals extended to $V$ are monomial in their actual images, and the
genuine all-original-$C$-functions DVR Jacobian ideal is the principal ideal
of the actual coordinate derivative determinant times the same actual leaf
Jacobian monomial. The matching DVR/leaf coordinate index equivalence is
produced internally.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Produce the literal simultaneous family tree from the smooth source. The
actual DVR determines a literal valuation subring of $K$. The already proved
valuation covering property of a finite Rees tree chooses an actual leaf
contained in that subring, hence in $V$. The tree supplies that leaf's actual
\'etale coordinates, every original factor monomial, and its genuine
original-source Jacobian monomial. The actual field of fractions remains
$K$ on the intermediate leaf. Base change of its actual differential basis
and the actual DVR field-coordinate basis therefore produce the matching
finite coordinate index equivalence. Extend every actual factor identity
through the literal inclusion into $V$. Apply the actual DVR derivative
Jacobian comparison to the actual leaf Jacobian monomial. Finally compose
the actual inclusions to identify its ideal with the ideal generated by all
original $C$-function determinants. Every geometric and differential input
has thus been produced, including containment, dimension compatibility and
the actual original-source Jacobian factorization.
\end{proof}
