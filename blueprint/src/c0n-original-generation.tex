\section{The original homogeneous obstacle family generates its whole scheme ideal}
\label{sec:c0n-original-generation}

Keep the original projective subvariety $X\subset\mathbb P^M_k$, the
original obstacle $\varphi$, original polarization integer $d$, and index
$m\geq0$.  Write $J_m$ for the actual original homogeneous obstacle ideal,
with the parameter moved only by the proved polynomial exchange to the
coefficient ring $k[t]$.  Let $Y$ be the actual coefficient-extension
projective family, $i:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^M_{k[t]}$ its actual closed
inclusion, and $I_m=i^{-1}J_m\,\mathcal O_Y$ its whole geometric comap ideal.
This section never replaces $J_m$ by an unrelated full-section base ideal.

\begin{lemma}[An original homogeneous form gives its whole principal chart ideal]
\label{lem:c0n-original-homogeneous-section-ideal}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierOriginalGenerationCore.homogeneousSection_sectionIdeal}
\leanok
For an original homogeneous $F\in R[x_0,\ldots,x_M]$ of degree $e$,
the native global section of the original $\mathcal O(e)$ has, on every
whole native coordinate chart $U_j$, the principal section ideal generated
by the actual dehomogenization $F/x_j^e$ under the native chart-ring
isomorphism.  This is equality of entire ideals.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The proved native frame-coefficient formula identifies the whole section
ideal with the principal ideal of its actual coefficient in the original
$\mathcal O(e)$ frame.  The proved native homogeneous-section coefficient
comparison identifies that coefficient exactly with the original
$F/x_j^e$, including its original chart-ring isomorphism.  Substitute
this equality in the principal ideal formula.  No radical or support
comparison is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual finite evaluation has exactly the original whole ideal image]
\label{lem:c0n-original-homogeneous-evaluation-image}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierOriginalGenerationCore.homogeneousEvaluation_imageIdeal}
\leanok
Let $J$ be a homogeneous ideal of $R[x_0,\ldots,x_M]$, and let a finite
set $G$ of actual degree-$e$ forms generate its entire ideal.  The genuine
evaluation from the direct sum of the original $\mathcal O(-e)$ factors,
using the original global sections represented by $F\in G$, has whole
image ideal equal to the original sheaf ideal associated with $J$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The native family-evaluation image ideal is the supremum of the section
ideals of the same original family.  On each native coordinate affine
chart, the preceding lemma identifies these with the principal ideals
of the actual dehomogenizations of $F\in G$.  Their supremum is exactly
the ideal spanned by those same coefficients.  Ideal-map preservation of
span and the given whole span equality identify this with the actual
coordinate ideal of the native quotient sheaf.  The native coordinate
charts cover the entire target, so equality on those whole affine ideals
proves equality of the entire original sheaf ideals.
\uses{lem:c0n-original-homogeneous-section-ideal}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The pulled original evaluation source is genuinely quasi-coherent]
\label{lem:c0n-original-pulled-evaluation-source-coherent}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierOriginalGenerationCore.pullbackFreeTensorDual_isQuasicoherent}
\leanok
For any original invertible $L$, finite index type $G$, and actual morphism $f$,
the actual pullback of $\mathcal O^{(G)}\otimes L^{-1}$ is quasi-coherent.
No flatness or quasi-coherence certificate for the pulled source is input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The native free-tensor-dual isomorphism identifies the original source
with the actual coproduct of copies of $L^{-1}$.  Pull back this whole
isomorphism and use preservation of coproducts by the actual pullback
left adjoint.  Its target is the coproduct of the actual $f^*L^{-1}$.
Reindex this finite coproduct by its actual finite ordinal.  Each summand
is genuinely invertible and hence quasi-coherent.  The proved
native coproduct closure gives quasi-coherence of that coproduct, and the
actual whole isomorphism transfers it to the original pulled source.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The pulled original evaluation has the whole geometric comap as image]
\label{lem:c0n-original-pulled-evaluation-image}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierOriginalGenerationCore.pullbackEvaluation_imageIdeal}
\leanok
For the same finite homogeneous family and any actual $f:Z\to\mathbb P^M_R$,
pull its original evaluation morphism back and compose with the actual
pullback-unit isomorphism.  The image ideal of this exact morphism is the
entire original geometric comap $J\mathcal O_Z$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The preceding whole image equality supplies the actual epimorphic factor
of the original evaluation through the actual $J$-module.  Pull back this
factor and compose it with the native epimorphism from the pulled ideal
module to its genuine geometric comap.  The native ideal-inclusion
naturality square, the original image-factor inclusion equation, and
functoriality show that this epimorphic factor composes with the original
comap ideal inclusion to exactly the pulled original evaluation followed
by the native unit isomorphism.  In the abelian category of actual scheme
modules this is a strong-epi/mono factorization of that same morphism.
Uniqueness of its whole image identifies its entire geometric image ideal
with the original geometric comap.  The inclusion of whole ideal modules
also proves equality on every original affine ideal, so this is not only
a categorical or support identification.
\uses{lem:c0n-original-homogeneous-evaluation-image,lem:c0n-original-pulled-evaluation-source-coherent}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual homogeneous finite generators survive every geometric comap]

\leanok
For any actual morphism $f:Z\to\mathbb P^M_R$, the original finite family
in the preceding lemma gives a genuine finite generating family of
$(J\mathcal O_Z)\otimes f^*\mathcal O(e)$.
No flatness, extra generators, or desired image certificate is input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The native image factorization of the actual evaluation, using its proved

Pull back this epimorphism by $f$ and compose with the native epimorphism

Tensor by the actual pulled invertible $\mathcal O(e)$ and use the native
monoidal comparison and dual evaluation to cancel $\mathcal O(-e)$.
All these functors preserve the actual epimorphisms.  Pull back the genuine
finite standard basis of the free module indexed by the same finite set
$G$, and descend its evaluation through the resulting epimorphism.
Its index remains finite, proving actual whole-module global generation.
\uses{lem:c0n-original-homogeneous-evaluation-image}
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The same original obstacle forms give the whole ambient image]
\label{prop:c0n-original-obstacle-evaluation-image}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierOriginalGeneration.exists_original_homogeneous_evaluation_image}
\leanok
For every original $X,d,\varphi,m$, there is a finite set $G_m$ consisting
of original obstacle forms, each of exact degree $md$, such that their
actual homogeneous evaluation has whole image ideal equal to the entire
original ambient obstacle sheaf $J_m$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The proved original generator theorem uses Noetherianity to select a
finite subset of the same original parameter-exchanged obstacle monomials.
It proves both whole ideal span and exact degree $md$ for every selected
form, internally from the original obstacle construction.  Apply the
second lemma to this very family and the original homogeneous ideal.
No finite family, span equality, or evaluation image equality is supplied
as an external hypothesis to this proposition.
\uses{lem:c0n-original-homogeneous-evaluation-image}
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The actual original sequence has finite polarized generators]
\label{prop:c0n-original-obstacle-finite-generation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierOriginalGeneration.exists_generatingSections}

\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Internally choose the original finite set $G_m$ just described.  Apply the
third lemma to the actual original family inclusion $i$.  Its geometric
comap is literally the already defined $I_m$; its invertible factor is
literally the pulled native pole-sheaf power $i^*\mathcal O(md)$.
The genuine native positive-twist/power isomorphism and pullback monoidality
identify this actual line with $H^{md}$, including power zero.  Transport
the finite family through that whole module isomorphism.  Every original
object is retained, and no target global-generation premise occurs.
\uses{lem:c0n-original-homogeneous-pullback-generation,prop:c0n-original-obstacle-evaluation-image}
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The same original polarization supplies every index needed by Nadel]
\label{prop:c0n-original-polarization-power-generation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierOriginalGeneration.exists_generatingSections_polarization_power}
\leanok
With the same original $H$, put $L=H^d$.  Every natural index $m$ has a

\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The preceding proposition constructs actual generators of
$I_m\otimes H^{md}$.  Commutativity of the natural-number exponents and
the native iterated tensor-power isomorphism identify $H^{md}$ with
$(H^d)^m$.  Tensor this actual isomorphism by the same ideal module and
transport the produced finite family.  This is the exact original
polarization required by the uniform multiplier theorem, for every index
and including zero; no desired generation premise is supplied.
\uses{prop:c0n-original-obstacle-finite-generation}
\end{proof}

The conclusions are algebraic finite generation for the actual original
obstacle ideals, not the uniform multiplier-ideal generation theorem or
the final analytic envelope statement.  They supply the required
input family for those separately proved conditional consumers.
