\section{Actual homogeneous models of finite original projective maps over the entire native field}
\label{sec:finite-projective-native-field-models}

\begin{theorem}[Original projective-family coordinates in the entire original function field {\cite[\S1.2]{BJ22}}]
\label{thm:finite-projective-native-field-models}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FiniteProjectiveNativeFieldModels.chartSite, EnvelopeProperty.FiniteProjectiveNativeFieldModels.pivot, EnvelopeProperty.FiniteProjectiveNativeFieldModels.coordinate, EnvelopeProperty.FiniteProjectiveNativeFieldModels.coordinate_pivot, EnvelopeProperty.FiniteProjectiveNativeFieldModels.model, EnvelopeProperty.FiniteProjectiveNativeFieldModels.model_dimension, EnvelopeProperty.FiniteProjectiveNativeFieldModels.model_coordinate}
\leanok
\uses{thm:projective-chart-function-field-generation,thm:projective-chart-whole-native-field-model}
Let $X$ be integral over its original field and let
$q_j:X\to\mathbf P^{d_j}_k$ be a nonempty finite family of
actual original maps, all over its exact original structure
morphism. Suppose the first map is an actual closed embedding.
Then their actual original normalized projective coordinate
ratios internally construct a multiprojective homogeneous
model over the entire same native $K(X)$, with exactly these
original factor dimensions and original coordinate values.
No field-generation, homogeneous-model or numerical comparison
certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For each actual map choose an original coordinate chart
containing the image of the original generic point. Its
inverse image is nonempty. Pull each original coordinate
ratio back and take its actual generic germ into the same
$K(X)$. Each selected pivot is one.

The first original map is a closed embedding. The actual
coordinate-field theorem proves that its own original
coordinate ratios generate the entire native $K(X)$ over
its original coefficient field. Its exact original structure
triangle identifies this coefficient action with the given
original structure morphism of $X$.

Take the algebra generated by all original monomials
$c_{ja}T_j$. It is finitely generated and graded because
its generators are finitely many monomials. Each original
coordinate is in it and the selected pivot coefficient is
one in every factor. Integrality over its own coordinate
algebra is immediate. For the model's field-generation
condition it suffices to use the first factor: dividing
each first-factor coordinate monomial by its selected
pivot monomial gives exactly its original coefficient.
Those coefficients generate all of the actual native
$K(X)$ by the preceding paragraph. Thus the field generated
by same-degree homogeneous quotients is the entire same
native field. This constructs the full model, and its
dimensions and coordinate values are exactly its defining
original dimensions and ratios. Scheme, analytic and
numerical-psh equivalences require further comparison.
\end{proof}
