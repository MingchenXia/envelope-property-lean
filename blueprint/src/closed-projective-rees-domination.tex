\subsection{Constructed original Rees domination of a closed projective birational witness}

The coefficient ring in the final theorem is an arbitrary commutative domain.
No Noetherian, finite-type over a field, characteristic, or valuation hypothesis is
used. A REAL closed projective embedding and REAL dense-open inverse data are
retained as geometric input. This does not construct a projective modification of
an arbitrary proper birational morphism; that Chow-type boundary remains separate.

\begin{lemma}[The actual dense-open inverse gives the whole fraction-field map]
\label{scheme:actual-partial-iso-fraction-lift}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.fractionSpec_range_subset_denseOpen,
EnvelopeProperty.PartialIso.fractionLift,
EnvelopeProperty.PartialIso.fractionLift_comp}
For any actual fraction field $K$ of a domain $A$, the entire image of
$\operatorname{Spec}K\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ lies in every genuine dense open.
Consequently actual partial-isomorphism data from $Y$ to $\operatorname{Spec}A$
construct a genuine map $\operatorname{Spec}K\to Y$. If the original partial
isomorphism is over $f:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}A$, the whole constructed map
composed with $f$ is exactly the original coefficient map on spectra.
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original fraction-field ring map is injective. The true spectrum closure
formula therefore makes its image dense. Every dense base open is nonempty,
so it meets this actual image. The spectrum of a field has only one point;
hence every original source point maps into that open. The genuine
open-immersion lifting property constructs the map into the target open of the
partial isomorphism. Compose with the inverse of its actual scheme isomorphism
and then the source-open immersion. Its true over-base equation and the actual
open-lift identity give equality of ENTIRE scheme morphisms to the coefficient
scheme, including their section maps. No generic inverse or point-lift
certificate is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual projective geometry constructs original finite coordinates]
\label{projective:actual-generic-original-coordinates}
\uses{projective:native-homogeneous-chart-isomorphism,projective:native-structure-morphism,projective:support}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.exists_coordinateEvaluation,
EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.exists_original_fraction_family,
EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.exists_original_coordinateFamily}

the true coefficient map $A\to K$, lies in a genuine coordinate chart. It is the
whole scheme map of an actual $A$-algebra homomorphism from that coordinate ring
to $K$. When $A$ is a domain and $K$ its actual fraction field, there are
original elements $g_0,\ldots,g_n\in A$ and an actual index $j$ with $g_j\ne0$
such that every chart coordinate is $g_i/g_j$. The ENTIRE original field-valued
projective map is retained, not only its support or numerical coordinate values.
The intermediate coefficient-chart assertion needs only a commutative $A$ and
an actual field $K$ with its given $A$-algebra action.
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual irrelevant ideal is the original variable ideal. Its genuine
coordinate basic opens therefore cover the same projective scheme. Choose a
chart at the image of the unique field point. All field-spectrum points agree,
so the whole image lies in that actual open. The real coordinate open immersion
lifts the original scheme map; full faithfulness of Spec gives its whole ring
homomorphism. The original projective coefficient triangle proves its actual
$A$-algebra law on every coefficient.

Set the selected coordinate equal to one and take the other genuine chart
coordinate elements in $K$. The real localization common-denominator theorem
for this finite family gives a single original non-zero-divisor $d\in A$ and
original numerators. Because the selected coordinate is one, injectivity of
$A\to K$ identifies its numerator with $d$. It is genuinely nonzero. Actual
field cancellation gives all $g_i/g_j$ identities, while the already proved
whole chart factorization retains the original projective morphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A closed projective birational witness is dominated by a constructed original Rees scheme]
\label{scheme:actual-closed-projective-rees-domination}
\uses{scheme:actual-partial-iso-fraction-lift,projective:actual-generic-original-coordinates,scheme:actual-rees-generic-projective-comparison,scheme:actual-original-rees-integral,algebra:actual-rees-chart-fraction-embedding,scheme:actual-reduced-closed-factorization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.denseRange_chartImmersion,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.denseRange_genericLift,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.range_standardImmersion_subset,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.exists_standardRees_domination}
Let $A$ be ANY domain. Let $\iota:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_A$ be a REAL
closed immersion, and let $f:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ be its actual original
coefficient projection. Assume genuine partial-isomorphism data with dense
source and target opens, over this SAME $f$ and the identity of
$\operatorname{Spec}A$. Then there are actual original $g_i\in A$, an index
$j$ with $g_j\ne0$, and a constructed whole map
\[
 \delta:\operatorname{ReesScheme}(g_i)\longrightarrow Y
\]
such that $\iota\circ\delta$ is the actual standard Rees immersion and
$f\circ\delta$ is the ENTIRE original Rees projection. The resulting
$\delta$ is itself an actual closed immersion. No source-integrality,
generic-agreement, image-containment or factorization certificate is an input.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first lemma constructs the actual generic inverse $q$ into $Y$ and retains
its full coefficient triangle. Compose it with $\iota$. The second lemma
constructs an actual chart, original finite numerators $g_i$ and a nonzero
selected denominator. The fourth lemma identifies the original generic Rees
map followed by its standard immersion with the WHOLE $\iota\circ q$.

The actual good-open lift is dense in the same Rees scheme by the original
nonzero generator. Its whole factorization through the selected chart makes
that actual chart dense. The original chart RingHom into $\operatorname{Frac}A$
is injective, so the true spectrum closure formula makes its induced map dense
in that chart. Composition gives density of the ACTUAL original generic Rees
lift in the whole original Rees scheme. This derives generic density from
actual algebra and topology, rather than assuming it.

The inverse image of the genuine closed set $\iota(Y)$ under the actual
standard Rees immersion is closed. The whole generic-map identity puts its
actual dense generic image inside this inverse image. Hence the inverse image
is the entire original source, proving genuine image containment. The SAME
Rees scheme is reduced, so the proved reduced-source closed-immersion
factorization constructs $\delta$ and its whole first triangle. The true
original coefficient triangles give its entire second triangle. Finally the
composite $\iota\circ\delta$ and $\iota$ are actual closed immersions, which
makes $\delta$ a true closed immersion as well. No target reducedness is needed.
\end{proof}
