\section{Equivariance of the unchanged original parameter projection}

\begin{lemma}[The original blowup parameter map has the standard action]
\label{lem:pl-original-blowup-parameter-equivariance}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeClosedBlowupParameterStructure,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeClosedBlowupParameterOver,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeClosedParameterFamilyMap_parameter,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.nativeClosedBlowupParameterOver_isModHom}
\leanok
The unchanged parameter map of the actual homogeneous-ideal blowup is
equivariant for its constructed whole action and the actual standard
multiplicative-group action on the ordinary affine parameter line.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual whole coefficient-family product isomorphism to pull the
required group-action square back to the Laurent coefficient family of
the original blowup. The defining projection triangle of the family lift
identifies the left composite with the original closed-family map followed
by its parameter structure. The ambient-inclusion triangle and the
coefficient-base square then identify this map with the Laurent coefficient
parameter structure followed by the spectrum of $t\mapsto zt$.
For the right composite, compare the two whole projections to the group
and to the parameter line. The first projection identity of the actual
blowup-family product isomorphism and its second coefficient projection
identity identify this composite with the same Laurent coefficient
parameter structure followed by the canonical whole group--parameter
product comparison. Under that comparison the actual standard action is
the spectrum of $t\mapsto zt$. The two entire composites agree.
Cancellation of the product isomorphism proves the required equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The unchanged normalized parameter map is equivariant]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-parameter-equivariance}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousNativeParameterOver,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousNativeParameterOver_isModHom,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeParameterOver_isModHom}
\leanok
\uses{lem:pl-original-blowup-parameter-equivariance,thm:pl-original-normalized-simultaneous-gm-action}
The same normalized simultaneous model's unchanged original projection
to the ordinary parameter line is equivariant for its constructed actual
action and the actual standard parameter-line action.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original whole coordinate isomorphism is equivariant by the exact
transport construction and preserves its original parameter projection.
Its inverse followed by the preceding equivariant parameter map is
therefore precisely the simultaneous blowup parameter map and is
equivariant. The actual normalization projection is equivariant. Its
composite with that simultaneous parameter map is the unchanged
normalized parameter map, so functoriality of equivariant composition
gives the assertion on the entire original group product.
\end{proof}
