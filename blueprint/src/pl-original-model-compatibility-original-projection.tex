\section{The normalized action fixes the original variety projection}

\begin{lemma}[The entire original parameter family fixes the original variety]
\label{lem:pl-original-parameter-family-forgets-scaling}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.parameterFamily_forgets_scaling,EnvelopeProperty.ProjBerk.closedParameterFamily_forgets_scaling}
\leanok
For any original coefficient extension and any original parameter unit,
the two whole parameter-family maps followed by the projection forgetting
the parameter agree. The same holds for the actual original lifted blowup
action and its original whole product projection.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On the original coefficient polynomial ring the scaled and unscaled maps
both send every original coefficient $c$ to its prescribed coefficient image.
Their composites with the inclusion of coefficients are therefore equal.
Functoriality of the entire native projective coefficient morphisms gives
the first equality. The original closed-family ambient-inclusion triangle
gives the same equality on the original closed family. Compose the original
blowup-family projection identity with the original closed inclusion and
then this coefficient projection. Replace the scaled closed-family map by
the unscaled one. The defining Cartesian square of the original blowup
family then gives its original product projection.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same original normalized projection is action invariant]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-projection-action-invariant}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLOriginalModelCompatibility.invariantMorphism_comp,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousNativeOriginalProjection_invariant,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeOriginalProjection_invariant}
\leanok
\uses{lem:pl-original-parameter-family-forgets-scaling,thm:pl-original-normalized-simultaneous-gm-action}
The actual constructed action on the same original normalized simultaneous
PL model fixes its original projection to $X$, as an equality of entire
original scheme morphisms on the original whole group product.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding lemma and the exact original coordinate-isomorphism projection
triangle give invariance of the original coordinate-model projection to $X$;
the original closed immersion of $X$ detects this equality. Transport through
the original equivariant coordinate isomorphism yields invariance on the same
actual simultaneous blowup. The actual normalization projection is
equivariant. Composing its whole equivariance identity with the original
blowup-to-$X$ projection and using the whole product-projection square gives
invariance on the original normalized model. All projection morphisms are
the original ones.
\end{proof}
