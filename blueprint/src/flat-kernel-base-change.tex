\begin{lemma}[Flat scalar extension of an actual commuting kernel square]
\label{lem:fc-flat-kernel-base-change}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FlatKernelBaseChange.kernelEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.FlatKernelBaseChange.kernelEquiv_tmul}
\leanok
Let $R\to S$ be an actual flat map of commutative rings, $d:M\to N$
an original $R$-linear map, and $d':M'\to N'$ an original $S$-linear
map. Suppose actual whole $S$-linear equivalences
$e_0:S\otimes_R M\simeq M'$ and $e_1:S\otimes_R N\simeq N'$
commute with these maps. Then the canonical original kernel map is an
$S$-linear equivalence $S\otimes_R\ker d\simeq\ker d'$.
Every $s\otimes x$ has underlying image $e_0(s\otimes x)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Flatness gives the actual canonical equivalence from the tensor of the
original kernel to the kernel of $1\otimes d$. The original commuting
square restricts $e_0$ to these actual kernels. Its inverse restricts
$e_0^{-1}$: the square and injectivity of $e_1$ show that the inverse
image of every original $d'$-kernel element is killed by $1\otimes d$.
The two restrictions are inverse because the original whole maps are
inverse. Compose the two genuine equivalences. The native tensor-kernel
map retains every pure tensor, which proves the stated formula.
\end{proof}
