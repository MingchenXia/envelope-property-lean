\section{The actual original multiplier cone ideals satisfy the chart discrepancy bound}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original multiplier cone discrepancy at chart DVR points]
\label{thm:c0n-original-multiplier-chart-discrepancy}
\uses{cor:c0n-original-family-chart-estimate,lem:c0n-raw-canonical-cone-generators,lem:c0n-original-native-chart-coefficients,lem:c0n-chart-generator-orders,lem:c0n-chart-obstacle-ideal-order}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierBaseIdeals.exists_originalMultiplierConeIdeal_chart_le_obstacle_all,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierBaseIdeals.exists_originalMultiplierConeIdeal_chart_le_obstacle}
\leanok
Assume characteristic zero and the original smooth-chart condition on
$X$, and let the original obstacle have a real lower bound.
For every genuine original chart DVR datum $(j,V,b)$ with $b>0$ and the
actual parameter torus law, there is $A\ge0$, independent of $m$, such that
for each $m\in\mathbf N$ some $q>0$ satisfies
\[
 \phi_{B_m}(v_{j,V,b})\le
 (m/q)\phi_{J_q(\phi)}(v_{j,V,b})+A.
\]
Here $B_m$ is exactly the actual original multiplier cone ideal obtained
from the full original scheme asymptotic multiplier ideal.  The assertion
includes index zero and infinite orders of zero polynomial images.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual original family chart estimate supplies a fixed $D\ge0$.
Put $a=1/b>0$ and $A=aD$.
For a fixed $m$, this genuine scheme estimate chooses $q>0$ working for
all original affine sections of the full multiplier ideal.
Noetherianity selects finite homogeneous members spanning the entire
canonical native representative of this same whole multiplier ideal.
Their actual inverse parameter exchanges form finite whole polynomials
$G$ generating the same original cone ideal exactly.
The actual native chart membership formula places the inverse chart
section of every dehomogenized $F\in G$ in the original multiplier ideal.
Hence the original scheme estimate bounds
$-a\operatorname{ord}_V(F^{(j)})$ by
\[
 (m/q)(-a\operatorname{ord}_V(\mathfrak a_q(U_j)\mathcal O_V))+A.
\]
The whole original chart-ideal identity identifies that sequence ideal
with the dehomogenization of the original obstacle ideal $J_q(\phi)$.
Its extension is nonzero: the original positive-index generic stalk is
nonzero, and both actual chart/DVR maps are injective.
The proved original-generator order lemma therefore bounds its negative
scaled ideal order by $\phi_{J_q(\phi)}(v_{j,V,b})$.
Since $m/q\ge0$, every $F\in G$ has the asserted common upper bound.
The exact whole-ideal chart potential formula takes the finite maximum
over these actual generators and gives the desired bound for $B_m$.
The same argument works at $m=0$; zero polynomial images retain order
$+\infty$ and logarithmic value $-\infty$ throughout.
\end{proof}
