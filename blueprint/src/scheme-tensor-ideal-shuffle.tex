\begin{lemma}\label{sc0-tensor-ideal-shuffle:inclusion}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorObjMorphisms.tensorObjIdealMap,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorObjMorphisms.tensorObjIdealMap_monoidal}

The resulting ENTIRE original map agrees, through the genuine tensor comparison, with the SAME native monoidal map induced by $i$ and the original structure-unit comparison.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual original tensor comparison is natural on ENTIRE original maps. Cancel its actual target inverse to compare the same explicit tensor map with its native monoidal counterpart. The preceding whole original right-unit identity then gives precisely the required entire inclusion formula. No ideal, monicity, flatness, invertibility or comparison premise is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{sc0-tensor-ideal-shuffle:whole-shuffle}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorObjMorphisms.tensorObjIdealShuffleIso,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorObjMorphisms.tensorObjIdealShuffleIso_inclusion}
\leanok
\uses{sc0-tensor-ideal-shuffle:inclusion}
There is an actual explicit isomorphism
\[
 (B\otimes I)\otimes D\simeq (B\otimes D)\otimes I
\]
between the SAME original sheafified tensor modules. Under it, tensoring the ENTIRE original inclusion $B\otimes I\to B$ by the identity of $D$ equals the SAME original inclusion $(B\otimes D)\otimes I\to B\otimes D$. This holds for EVERY original module-to-structure map $i:I\to\mathcal O_X$, with no invertibility or matching certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In a genuine braided monoidal category, associator naturality moves the original map $i$ across the last factor. Naturality of the SAME original braiding moves it across $D$, and associator naturality moves it across the middle factor. The remaining diagram is the genuine braiding with the unit and original monoidal unit coherence. This proves the entire native inclusion interchange for EVERY original $i$.

Construct the explicit original isomorphism from the SAME original tensor comparisons, actual associators and original braiding. Conjugating by the genuine source and target tensor comparisons identifies it with precisely that native shuffle; actual inverse comparison maps cancel. On the original left side, whole tensor-comparison naturality followed by the preceding inclusion formula gives the SAME native original inclusion tensored with $D$. On the original right side, the same inclusion formula, original tensor interchange and genuine right-unit naturality give the SAME native inclusion on $B\otimes D$. The preceding whole native interchange equates those ENTIRE original maps, and cancellation of the actual comparison isomorphisms proves the explicit original sheaf statement. No chosen section, frame, weakened image, or tensor-ideal compatibility hypothesis is used.
\end{proof}
