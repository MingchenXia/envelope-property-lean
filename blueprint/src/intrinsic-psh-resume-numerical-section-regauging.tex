\section{Actual rational sections and true support-open regauging}

\begin{lemma}[The original Cartier generic unit is one actual local section]
\label{lem:intrinsic-numerical-cartier-generic-unit-support-section}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NumericalSectionRegauging.supportResidueFactor,
EnvelopeProperty.NumericalSectionRegauging.supportResidueFactor_comp,
EnvelopeProperty.NumericalSectionRegauging.genericIdealUnitSection_eq_supportFrame}
\uses{lem:actual-ideal-module-pullback-map,scheme:native-model-ideal-zero-locus,lem:actual-local-spectrum-affine-factor,def:actual-line-frame-via-factor,lem:actual-ideal-module-off-support,def:actual-cartier-generic-inclusion-frame,lem:actual-cartier-generic-unit-section-model-norm,lem:fc-original-line-section-frame-naturality,lem:fc-original-line-section-fibre}
Let $A$ be an actual invertible Cartier ideal on a proper model over a bounded
valued coefficient ring. On every genuine open $V$ disjoint from its actual
support, the original canonical generic ideal unit section at EVERY native
point whose support lies in $V$ is the actual residue pullback of the inverse
of the original inclusion-induced frame on $V$. The residue morphism factors
through the actual open with its literal scheme triangle. No valuative-center
membership, section-matching equation or norm certificate is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Factor the actual residue-field spectrum morphism through its genuine support
neighborhood, using the original open immersion. The actual off-support
inclusion is an isomorphism of whole modules. Naturality of the native
restriction/pullback comparison identifies its whole frame with the original
pulled inclusion followed by the genuine unit comparison. The actual
pullback-composition and unit comparisons identify the transported frame with
the original pointwise generic inclusion frame. Invert that whole equality
and apply it to the original unit section. This proves the assertion for
EVERY support point, without moving the valuative center off the divisor.
\end{proof}
