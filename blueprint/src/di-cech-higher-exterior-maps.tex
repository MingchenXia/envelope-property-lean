\section{Normalized higher maps into the original Cech total}

Let $k$ be a field of prime characteristic $p$, let $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$
be smooth of relative dimension $n$, and write $Y=X^{(p)}$ with its original
structural morphism $g:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}k$. Put
$P=\Omega^{1,\mathrm{pre}}_{Y/k}$ and
$\Omega_Y^q=(\bigwedge^q P)^{\mathrm{sh}}$. Let $U$ be a family of original
opens of $Y$, let $K=F_*\Omega^\bullet_{X/k}$ with the original differential,
and let $T=\operatorname{Tot}\check C(U,K)$ be the published sheaf total.
The construction below uses the original all-tuple product and its proved
total derivation identity. No arbitrary multiplication on $T$ is supplied.

\begin{definition}[The raw degree-one map]
\label{def:di-higher-exterior-raw-one}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.rawOneUnit, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.rawOneUnit_apply, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.oneComponent, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.rawOne, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.rawOne_apply, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.rawOneUnit_restrict, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.oneComponent_closed, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.oneComponent_f_one, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.rawOne_restrict, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.rawOne_closed}
\leanok
\uses{thm:di-cech-degree-one-descent-map,def:di-cech-original-local-cochains}
Let $D$ be actual local degree-one descent data with source $\Omega_Y^1$,
and let $\tau:\Omega_Y^1[-1]\to T$ be the associated original map.
For an original open $W$, define
\[
 t_W:P(W)\longrightarrow T^1(W),\qquad
 t_W(m)=\tau^1_W(\eta_{1,W}(m)).
\]
Here $\eta_{1,W}$ is the original one-fold exterior sheafification unit.
This is linear over $\mathcal O_Y(W)$, commutes with every original
restriction, and has values in the kernel of $d_T^1$.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The one-fold exterior unit is the alternating one-variable map obtained
from the original exterior-presheaf unit. Composing it with the actual
degree-one component of $\tau$ gives the stated linear map. The exterior
unit and $\tau^1$ commute with restrictions, so their composite does too.
The source of $\tau$ is concentrated in degree one. Its chain-map equation
therefore gives $\tau^1d_T^1=0$, and evaluation on the original unit proves
closedness on every open. The construction uses the actual $\xi,h$ maps
and their proved local equations through $D$, not a supplied higher map.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Ordered positive cup powers]
\label{def:di-higher-exterior-ordered-powers}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.orderedPower, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.orderedPower_zero, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.orderedPower_succ, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.orderedPower_restrict, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.orderedPower_closed}
\leanok
\uses{def:di-higher-exterior-raw-one,def:di-cech-total-cup,lem:di-cech-total-cup-leibniz}
For $q>0$, define a multilinear map
\[
 \mu_{q,W}:P(W)^q\longrightarrow T^q(W)
\]
by $\mu_{1,W}=t_W$ and the fixed left-parenthesized recursion
\[
 \mu_{q+1,W}(m_0,\ldots,m_q)
 =\mu_{q,W}(m_0,\ldots,m_{q-1})\smile t_W(m_q).
\]
Every $\mu_q$ commutes with restriction and has closed values.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The original total product is bilinear on sections of each original open.
Compose its left input with the already constructed multilinear map,
its right input with $t_W$, and use the finite-variable currying
equivalence. This constructs the stated multilinear map without assuming
multilinearity of the desired formula. The defining currying evaluation
formula gives the displayed recursion. Induction on $q$ proves restriction
compatibility by the whole restriction identity for the original total
product and the preceding restriction identity for $t$. The same induction
proves closedness: both factors are closed, and the original total
derivation identity makes their product closed. No associativity or
commutativity of the chain-level cup product is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Normalized alternating families]
\label{def:di-higher-exterior-normalized-family}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.constantMap, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.constantMap_restrict, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap_zero}
\leanok
\uses{def:di-higher-exterior-ordered-powers,lem:di-reused-native-normalization}
For $0<q<p$, let $\beta_W:k\to\mathcal O_Y(W)$ be the actual structural
map on sections, and set
\[
 A_{q,W}=\beta_W((q!)^{-1})\operatorname{Alt}(\mu_{q,W}).
\]
These are actual alternating maps over the original rings of sections,
including empty opens, and form a restriction-compatible family.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Use the existing normalized alternatization of the genuine multilinear
map $\mu_{q,W}$. That construction is alternating also in characteristic
two. The original structural maps satisfy
$\rho_{WV}\beta_W=\beta_V$. The proved restriction identity for $\mu_q$
therefore satisfies exactly the hypotheses of normalized-alternatization
naturality, giving the entire restriction identity for $A_q$.
Since $q<p$, primality implies $p\nmid q!$, so $q!$ is nonzero in $k$.
Its inverse exists in $k$, and the existing normalization identity gives
$\beta_W((q!)^{-1})(q!)=1$ in every original section ring. This argument
uses no nontriviality or characteristic assumption on section rings;
it also applies to the zero ring on the empty open.
For $q=1$, the one-variable ordered map is the original $t_W$ and the
permutation group has one element. Since $1!=1$, normalization gives
exactly the alternating family corresponding to $\tau^1$. Injectivity
of the original exterior-sheaf universal property therefore retains
the whole original degree-one sheaf morphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual higher exterior morphism]
\label{thm:di-higher-exterior-sheaf-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap_unit, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap_closed}
\leanok
\uses{def:di-higher-exterior-normalized-family}
For $0<q<p$, the preceding original family induces a genuine sheaf map
$\phi_q:\Omega_Y^q\to T^q$. On every original raw wedge unit it is exactly
\[
 (\phi_q)_W(\eta_{q,W}(m))=A_{q,W}(m).
\]
The whole original morphism satisfies $\phi_qd_T^q=0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Apply the existing exterior-sheaf universal property to the actual
restriction-compatible alternating family $A_q$. Its inverse constructs
$\phi_q$ through the exterior presheaf and original sheafification
adjunction. The inverse-equivalence identity evaluated on each tuple is
the displayed formula. To prove whole-map closedness, use injectivity of
the same universal property with target $T^{q+1}$. The corresponding
alternating family is obtained by composing $A_q$ with $d_T^q$.
Closedness of $\mu_q$ says that this differential composed with the whole
multilinear map is zero. The existing normalized-alternatization
closedness theorem makes the corresponding alternating family zero.
Injectivity therefore gives $\phi_qd_T^q=0$. Thus equality on raw units
has been promoted to equality of the whole sheaf morphism through the
actual universal property, without assuming global generation of all
sections by such units.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine positive-degree complex maps]
\label{thm:di-higher-exterior-single-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.higherCochainMap, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.higherCochainMap_f_self, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechHigherExteriorMaps.higherCochainMap_zero}
\leanok
\uses{thm:di-higher-exterior-sheaf-map}
For every $0<q<p$, the same closed morphism gives an actual complex map
$\Phi_q:\Omega_Y^q[-q]\to T$. Its degree-$q$ component is the original
single-object identification followed by $\phi_q$, and hence retains
the preceding every-open raw-unit formula.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Use the native map-from-single construction with the original morphism
$\phi_q$. The only possible outgoing differential from the nonzero
source degree is $d_T^q$, whose composite is zero by the preceding
theorem. All other components have zero source. The native construction's
degree-$q$ evaluation identity gives the stated component exactly.
Degree zero is the already constructed original coefficient complex map
followed by the original Cech augmentation; it is not reconstructed here.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Higher maps from the original Witt geometry]
\label{thm:di-higher-exterior-witt-maps}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap, EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap_unit, EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap_zero, EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap_zero_localEvaluation, EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittHigherExteriorMaps.higherMap_closed, EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittHigherExteriorMaps.higherCochainMap, EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittHigherExteriorMaps.higherCochainMap_f_self, EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittHigherExteriorMaps.higherCochainMap_zero}
\leanok
\uses{thm:di-higher-exterior-single-map,thm:di-actual-witt-cech-one-map}
Let $k$ be perfect, and let
$\widetilde f:\widetilde X\to\operatorname{Spec}W_2(k)$ be separated
and smooth of relative dimension $n$, with the actual original special
fibre $f$ and its actual true Frobenius twist $Y$. On the canonical Witt
affine cover, the internally constructed geometric $\xi,H$ maps and
$h=-H$ determine the preceding $\Phi_q$ for every $0<q<p$.
The producer takes the original geometry and the degree bound only;
no higher map, quasi-isomorphism, or desired homology identification is
an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The canonical Witt-cover construction selects the actual local and
overlap Frobenius lifts. The original geometric maps satisfy closedness,
the pair equation with $h=-H$, and the triple equation, including actual
further restrictions. Their internally constructed local data therefore
give the original degree-one map used in the first definition.
Substitute exactly those local data into the preceding constructions.
Every subsequent operation is the actual cup recursion, existing
normalized alternatization, original exterior universal property, and
native single-degree construction. Consequently the result has the
stated original source and total target and requires no desired
higher-map certificate. Identification of its leading local homology
map with Cartier and the final global quasi-isomorphism are separate
computations, not hypotheses or conclusions of this theorem.
\end{proof}
