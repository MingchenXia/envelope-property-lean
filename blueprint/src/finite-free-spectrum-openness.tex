\subsection{Actual openness of finite free seminorm-spectrum maps}
\label{subsec:finite-free-spectrum-openness}

\begin{lemma}[Original fractions approximate completed tensors in finitely many evaluations]
\label{spectrum:actual-completed-tensor-fraction-approximation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.residueToH_eq_original_fraction,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.fractionTensor,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.fractionTensor_zero,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.fractionTensor_coefficient,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.fractionTensor_add,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.tensorPoint_val_tmul,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.exists_fractionTensor_approx}
\uses{spectrum:completed-residue-field,spectrum:fibre-tensor-evaluation}
Let $A\to B$ be any map of commutative rings, $x$ an actual real-valued
seminorm of $A$, and $E=\mathcal H(x)\otimes_A B$. For every original
tensor $e\in E$, positive $\epsilon$, and finite family of actual
seminorms of $E$ restricting to the prescribed norm on $\mathcal H(x)$,
there are actual $b\in B$ and $d\in A$, $|d|_x\ne0$, such that
\[
  \left|e-(d^{-1}\otimes b)\right|_z<\epsilon
\]
for every member $z$ of that family. Here $d^{-1}$ denotes the inverse
of the actual image of $d$ in $\mathcal H(x)$, not an inverse assumed
to exist in $A$ or $B$.
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use actual tensor induction. The zero tensor has numerator zero and
denominator one. For a true pure tensor $h\otimes b$, let $M$ be the
finite supremum of the actual values $|1\otimes b|_z$. Density of the
actual residue-field map $\kappa(x)\to\mathcal H(x)$ gives a genuine
$q\in\kappa(x)$ approximating $h$ within an error strictly less
than $\epsilon/(1+M)$. The whole prescribed coefficient identities
and multiplicativity bound the error at every family member by the
coefficient error times $M$, hence strictly below $\epsilon$.

The actual fraction-field presentation of $\kappa(x)$ writes $q$ as
$a/d$ for actual classes in $A/\operatorname{supp}(x)$ with nonzero
denominator. Lift both classes to original elements of $A$; the
nonzero denominator means exactly $|d|_x\ne0$. The true tensor
balancing law transfers the numerator to the original $B$ element
$ab$. This gives an actual original fraction, retaining the whole
coefficient element rather than just a matching value.

For a sum, approximate both summands at the same positive tolerance.
The actual common denominator is the product of their two original
denominators. Its image is nonzero by multiplicativity. The genuine
balancing identity expresses the sum with numerator
$b_1d_2+b_2d_1$ in the original algebra. Nonarchimedean subadditivity
bounds the sum error by the maximum of the two strict errors. This
proves the statement on every tensor without a tensor-density or
approximation certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A genuine original element peaks at the chosen finite fibre point]
\label{spectrum:actual-finite-fibre-peak}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ArtinianFactors.localIdempotent_map,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.exists_finite_fibre_peak}
\uses{spectrum:actual-completed-tensor-fraction-approximation,spectrum:integral-completed-fibres,algebra:artinian-local-power-decomposition}
Suppose $B$ is genuinely finite over $A$. If $y$ is an actual point
of the whole seminorm fibre over $x$, there are actual $b\in B$,
$d\in A$, $|d|_x\ne0$, such that
\[
 |b|_y=|d|_x,
 \qquad |b|_z<\tfrac12|d|_x
 \quad\text{for every other actual }z\text{ over }x.
\]
This peak is constructed; no element separating the branches is
supplied as an input.
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine finite completed fibre algebra $E$ is Artinian. The
proved whole-point fibre classification identifies its actual
maximal ideals with the original seminorm fibre. The actual
maximal-power Chinese remainder decomposition supplies a genuine
idempotent which is one in the factor of $y$ and zero in all other
factors. The full kernel identities of the actual tensor evaluation
maps show that its image in $\mathcal H(y)$ is exactly one and in
all other $\mathcal H(z)$ is exactly zero. In particular its actual
seminorm values are one and zero respectively.

Apply the preceding proved original-fraction approximation to the
actual finite family of fibre seminorms, with tolerance $1/2$.
At the selected point, nonarchimedeanness and an error less than one
force the approximating fraction to have value exactly one; at all
other points its value is strictly less than $1/2$. The actual
nonzero common denominator has the same value $|d|_x$ throughout the
whole fibre. Multiply these exact inequalities by that positive
value to obtain the original-element peak. Nilpotents and inseparable
residue extensions remain in the genuine Artinian construction.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Continuous norm detects a branch above the original threshold]
\label{spectrum:finite-free-norm-branch-detection}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.continuous_wgaussValuation_prod,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.normDisc,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.normDisc_eq_prod,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.continuous_normDisc,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.normDisc_eq_pow_of_forall,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.pow_lt_normDisc_of_exists,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.exists_branch_of_pow_lt_normDisc}
\uses{spectrum:actual-finite-fibre-peak,spectrum:finite-free-weighted-norm}
Assume additionally that the actual $A$-module $B$ is free, of finite
rank $n$. For the constructed original $b,d$ above, the condition
that the whole weighted Gauss norm of the genuine norm form of
$(b,1)$ at radii $(1,|d|_{x'}/2)$ exceeds $(|d|_{x'}/2)^n$
defines an actual open neighborhood of $x$. At every $x'$ in this
neighborhood there is an actual fibre point $z$ with
$|b|_z>|d|_{x'}/2$.
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine norm form is one fixed polynomial with original $A$
coefficients. Its weighted Gauss norm is the finite supremum of
continuous coefficient evaluations times continuous radius powers,
so it is continuous jointly with the actual base-dependent radius.
The actual finite-free weighted norm formula gives its value as
\[
 \prod_{z\mapsto x'}\max(|b|_z,|d|_{x'}/2)^{m_z},
\]
where the actual positive local-length/residue-degree multiplicities
sum to the original free rank $n$. At $x$ the constructed selected
branch has $|b|_y=|d|_x>|d|_x/2$, all other branches contribute the
threshold, and the selected multiplicity is positive. Thus the
strict product inequality holds and, by continuity, persists on an
actual open neighborhood. If no branch exceeded the threshold there,
every factor would equal it and the rank-sum identity would make the
product exactly the stated power, contradicting the strict inequality.
No branch-existence or rank certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual finite free maps of seminorm spectra are open]
\label{spectrum:actual-finite-free-open-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.Spectrum.isOpenMap_comap_of_finiteFree,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.isOpenMap_comap_of_finiteFree}
\uses{spectrum:finite-free-norm-branch-detection,spectrum:actual-finite-fibre-peak,spectrum:integral-proper-map}
For arbitrary commutative rings $A,B$ and an actual finite free
$A$-algebra $B$, the genuine restriction map of real-valued
multiplicative seminorm spaces is open. For every commutative
coefficient ring $R$, prescribed seminorm $v$ of $R$, and compatible
actual $R$-algebra actions, the genuine map of prescribed-base
relative spectra is open as well. No field, completeness, finite
presentation, separability, reducedness or coefficient bound is
assumed as additional input.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $U$ be any actual open set in the original seminorm spectrum of
$B$, and $y\in U$ over $x$. Construct the genuine original $b,d$
peaking at this whole fibre point. The actual set
\[
 C=(\operatorname{Spectrum}B\setminus U)
   \cap\{z:|b|_z\ge |d|_{f(z)}/2\}
\]
is closed: the second condition compares continuous evaluations of
original elements $b$ and the original image of $d$. Module
finiteness makes the actual ring map integral, so its already proved
proper/closed seminorm map sends this actual closed set to a closed
set. The peak shows that $x$ is outside that image: every other
branch is strictly below the threshold, and the chosen branch lies
in $U$.

Intersect the true open complement of that closed image with the
actual open neighborhood supplied by the preceding continuous norm
inequality. At every resulting base point, the genuine norm formula
provides a branch above threshold. It cannot lie outside $U$, by the
closed-image exclusion. Thus the resulting actual neighborhood of
$x$ is contained in the whole image of $U$, proving openness.

For any prescribed base, the full original coefficient square makes
the relative source exactly the inverse image of the fixed base
locus under the absolute map. Restrict the proved open map to that
actual inverse image. The elementary whole image identity for an
inverse-image restriction gives openness in the subspace topology.
No extension of a separately prescribed coefficient seminorm, fibre
bijection, analytic structure sheaf or curve-potential comparison is
inferred from this open-map theorem.
\end{proof}
