\section{Actual affine Cartier module pullback}

\begin{lemma}[Canonical affine tensor formula for the actual module pullback comparison]
\label{lem:affine-cartier-module-pullback-tensor}
\uses{lem:actual-ideal-pullback-unit-sections}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.toModulesComapPullbackMap_affineUnitTop,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.toModulesComapPullbackMap_affine_tmul}
\leanok
For a morphism of affine schemes, the true affine pullback/extension-of-scalars
isomorphism identifies the actual canonical ideal-module comparison with
$s\otimes x\mapsto s f^\#(x)$ whenever the original ideal module is quasicoherent.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine affine pullback isomorphism sends $1\otimes x$ to the actual
section supplied by the original adjunction unit. The proved
whole unit-section formula therefore computes the included comparison
image as the original scheme section image of $x$. True linearity in
$\Gamma(X)$ gives the formula for every $s\otimes x$. No compatibility
certificate for the actual affine isomorphism is assumed: its proved
unit formula is used explicitly.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Genuine affine flat pullback of a Cartier ideal module]
\label{lem:affine-cartier-module-pullback-iso}
\uses{lem:affine-cartier-module-pullback-tensor,lem:affine-ideal-tensor-sections,lem:cartier-ideal-affine-module-trivializations}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.IsInvertible.affine_toModulesComapPullbackMap_isIso}
\leanok
For an actual flat morphism of affine schemes and an actual effective
Cartier ideal, the canonical actual sheaf-module pullback comparison to
the module of its true geometric ideal comap is an isomorphism.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original Cartier ideal module is genuinely locally free, hence
quasicoherent. Its actual geometric ideal comap is again Cartier under
flatness, so its original ideal module is quasicoherent as well. Actual
affine sheaf pullback preserves quasicoherence. On genuine top-level
sections, compare the existing affine pullback/extension-of-scalars
isomorphism and the actual tensor-to-comap-ideal section isomorphism.
The preceding pure-tensor formula proves that their composite is exactly
the actual canonical comparison, on the entire tensor product by
additivity. Thus the canonical top section map is bijective. The genuine
affine quasicoherent sheaf theorem promotes this top-section isomorphism
to an isomorphism of the actual whole module sheaves. No Noetherianity,
finite-generation, reducedness, global line-bundle comparison, or
linearization premise is added. Global gluing and equivariance coherence
remain subsequent obligations.
\end{proof}
