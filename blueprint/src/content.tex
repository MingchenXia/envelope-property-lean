It covers the main theorems (Section~\ref{sec:main-fully-unconditional}), the errata certificates,
and the mathematical results used in their statements and proofs. Citations of \cite{FHX}
use the numbering of the paper in \texttt{paper/}.

Statements marked \emph{imported} are formulated as explicit hypotheses in the indicated
intermediate results. The final theorems supply these hypotheses by proved results.
The proof of the envelope property does not use Boucksom--Jonsson's Theorem~6.21 \cite{BJ22}
or their Lemma~4.6; the relevant counterexamples and replacement arguments are described below.
