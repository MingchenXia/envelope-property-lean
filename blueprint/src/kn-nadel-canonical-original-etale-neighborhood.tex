\section{The same whole original cover is etale near the original generic point}

\begin{theorem}[An internally produced whole etale generic neighborhood]
\label{thm:kn-canonical-original-etale-generic-neighborhood}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTraceGenericBranch.exists_original_etale_neighborhood}
\leanok
For the original normalized adapted cover $\pi:Y\to X$, the original
smooth integral SNC data, positive tame degrees and empty rows produce
an actual coefficient ring $S$, an open immersion
$j:\operatorname{Spec}S\to X$, and a map
$t:\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_{X,\eta}\to\operatorname{Spec}S$
such that $t j=\gamma$ is the same original generic-point morphism and
the entire actual pullback $Y\times_X\operatorname{Spec}S\to
\operatorname{Spec}S$ is etale. No generic etale or matching certificate
is supplied by a caller.
\uses{thm:kn-canonical-original-ratio-localized-etale,lem:kn-canonical-original-ratio-coefficients-nonzero,lem:kn-nadel-whole-native-ratio-model}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose a closed specialization of the original generic point and the
actual original common SNC frames there. The existing whole native
normalization comparison identifies the entire inverse image of its
actual affine base chart $U$ with the spectrum of its original ratio
algebra $B$ over $A=\mathcal O_X(U)$, preserving the original base map.
Let $a$ be the product of every original ratio coefficient and put
$S=A[1/a]$. Original SNC geometry proves $a\ne0$; the actual original
germ into the generic field is therefore a unit on $a$. The universal
property of this actual localization produces $t$, and its coefficient
triangle proves $t j=\gamma$.

The original whole ratio algebra becomes etale after this actual base
change, by the proved tame unit-root and whole tensor comparison.
The native spectrum--tensor pullback isomorphism preserves the actual
coefficient projection. The original complete normalization chart and
its base triangle give the actual cartesian comparison with
$Y\times_X\operatorname{Spec}S$, also preserving its whole projection.
Transport etaleness through these actual isomorphisms. The map $j$ is
the actual principal-open immersion followed by the original affine
chart immersion, so its image is an open neighborhood of the original
generic point. No connected component of either pullback is selected.
\end{proof}
