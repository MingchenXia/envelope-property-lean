\begin{theorem}[The original Segre embedding line is the tensor of original factor lines]
\label{thm:fc-original-segre-projective-twist}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SegreProjectiveTwist.leftLine, EnvelopeProperty.SegreProjectiveTwist.rightLine, EnvelopeProperty.SegreProjectiveTwist.segreLine, EnvelopeProperty.SegreProjectiveTwist.segreSection, EnvelopeProperty.SegreProjectiveTwist.tensorSection, EnvelopeProperty.SegreProjectiveTwist.twistIso, EnvelopeProperty.SegreProjectiveTwist.twistIso_section}
\leanok
\uses{lem:fc-original-projective-chart-section-ratios,lem:fc-original-tensor-section-homs}
On the actual original fiber product $\mathbf P^m_R\times_R\mathbf P^n_R$,
the whole pullback of the original $\mathcal O(1)$ along the native
Segre closed immersion is isomorphic to the tensor product of the whole
original factor pullbacks of $\mathcal O(1)$. Every original Segre
coordinate section corresponds to the tensor of its two original factor
coordinate sections. This holds over any original commutative ring.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Cover the actual product by the original pairs of nonvanishing
coordinate opens. The true chart is the spectrum of the tensor product
of the two original polynomial chart rings. On the chart indexed by
$(i,j)$ the original coordinate sections $X_i$, $Y_j$, and the Segre
section $Z_{ij}$ are actual original whole isomorphisms. For any pair
$(k,l)$, the ratios to these original pivots are respectively
$X_k/X_i$, $Y_l/Y_j$, and $Z_{kl}/Z_{ij}$. The native affine Segre
coordinate map sends the last ratio to the actual product of the first
two ratios. The genuine tensor-section pullback and scalar-product
identities therefore give the same original coefficient for the pulled
Segre section and for the original tensor section on this entire chart.
The inverse pulled Segre pivot followed by the original tensor pivot
is a whole local line isomorphism preserving every original coordinate
section. On every chart overlap both maps preserve the same invertible
pivot section, so cancellation makes them equal on the whole overlap.
Original sheaf descent glues these local isomorphisms to the asserted
whole original isomorphism and retains every original coordinate-section
identity. No line comparison or matching certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}
