\section{The original global regular structure trace}

\begin{theorem}[The actual original rational trace has a unique regular global factor]
\label{thm:kn-structure-trace-global-regular-factor}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.existsUnique_originalStructureTrace,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.originalStructureTrace,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.originalStructureTrace_factor,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.originalStructureTrace_unique}
\leanok
The same original normalized adapted cover $\pi:Y\to X$, under the
original smooth SNC, empty-row, positive-degree and tameness hypotheses,
has a unique actual $\mathcal O_X$-linear map
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{\pi}:\pi_*\mathcal O_Y\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X
\]
whose composite with the actual original inclusion into the generic-field
pushforward is the actual original rational structure trace. The chart
cover, local traces, local factor equations and their compatibility are
all derived internally from the original data. No global free algebra,
trace map, local-factor certificate or overlap certificate is a hypothesis.
\uses{thm:kn-structure-trace-produced-whole-free-chart,lem:kn-structure-trace-local-regular-factor,lem:kn-structure-trace-generic-unit-mono,def:kn-structure-trace-rational}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original adapted-cover construction makes $\pi$ finite. Around each
original point choose the whole free chart provided by the proved
original chart producer, whose base contains that point. The actual
coefficient spectra of these charts are open immersions covering $X$:
the point in the base open is carried to its coefficient spectrum by
the original affine-spectrum isomorphism, whose inverse followed by
the original open inclusion returns the point.

On each such coefficient spectrum the constructed actual local trace
is a regular factor of the restriction of the same original rational
trace. The native restriction functor and native pullback functor of
an open immersion are naturally isomorphic. Conjugating the local
trace by that isomorphism, and using its naturality on the original
structural unit and rational map, gives a factorization of the actual
pulled-back rational map through the actual pulled-back inclusion.

The original structural unit into the generic-field pushforward is a
monomorphism. Factorization through a monomorphism is detected on an
actual open-immersion point cover: equivalently, the composite with its
cokernel vanishes on that cover, hence vanishes globally, and exactness
of the monomorphism--cokernel sequence produces the required global
factor. Apply this criterion to the internally produced cover and
factors. The result is the asserted actual global regular trace. If
two such global maps have the same composite with the original
inclusion, right cancellation by that monomorphism makes them equal.
This also proves independence of all chart choices.
\end{proof}
