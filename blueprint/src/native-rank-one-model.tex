\section{The actual projective scheme of an original rank-one model}

\begin{definition}[The original rank-one multicone defines its native scheme]
\label{def:fc-native-rank-one-model}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneScheme, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneCoefficients, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneStructure, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneChartRing, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneCoordinateOpen}
\uses{lem:fc-native-model-total-grading}
For an original rank-one model, take the actual Proj of the original
algebra with its native total grading. The original coefficient map lands
in degree zero, hence gives its actual structure morphism to the original
coefficient field. The original coordinate elements define their actual
standard basic opens and actual degree-zero homogeneous localization rings.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Actual original rank-one projective charts and integrality]
\label{lem:fc-native-rank-one-model-charts}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneCoordinate_mem, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneHomogeneousChartIso, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneCoordinateOpen_isAffine, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneCoordinate_ne_zero, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOne_irrelevant_ne_bot, EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.nativeRankOneScheme_isIntegral}
\uses{def:fc-native-rank-one-model}
Each original coordinate is homogeneous of degree one. Its actual basic
open is the spectrum of the native degree-zero localization, without a
chart-isomorphism assumption. The actual rank-one Proj is integral.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original coordinate is exactly its original coefficient times the
single-variable monomial of degree one. The native Proj construction
therefore gives its whole standard affine scheme isomorphism. At least
one original coordinate coefficient is nonzero by the original model
axiom. Its corresponding actual nonzero degree-one element belongs to
the irrelevant ideal, so that ideal is nonzero. The original algebra is
a subalgebra of a polynomial ring over the original field and hence a
domain. The actual zero homogeneous prime gives an irreducible Proj,
while its native stalk embeddings into localizations of that same domain
give reducedness. Thus the whole actual native scheme is integral.
\end{proof}
