\section{Actual birational positivity toward the original nef-and-big target}

The target remains Theorem~\ref{thm:kn-nadel-snc-kv}, with its original
nef-and-big hypothesis. In the source numerical space, pseudoeffectivity
is the closed cone generated by effective real Cartier classes, and
bigness is its interior. Neither a desired ample--effective decomposition
nor a vanishing conclusion is a definition of bigness here. The following
birational positivity steps are also needed in the ample rational case;
that case is a separate milestone and does not replace the full target.

\begin{lemma}[An ample line absorbs every sufficiently large pulled ample power]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-ample-pulled-powers}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelCoverPulledPowers.exists_eventual_isAmple_tensor_pullback}
Let $R$ be a domain, $p:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ an original morphism,
$H$ an original ample line, and $f:W\to X$ any original morphism.
If $B$ is ample for the original composite $fp$, then
$B\otimes f^*H^n$ is ample for every sufficiently large original $n$.
The assertion retains the whole original $W$ and requires no integrality,
connectedness, finiteness or flatness of $f$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual positive $r$ such that $B^r$ is very ample. The original
ample $H$ has actual finite global generators for every power $H^t$ with
$t\geq N$. If $n\geq N$, positivity of $r$ implies $nr\geq N$.
Pull the actual finite generators of this same $H^{nr}$ back along the
original $f$. The whole-source very-ample-times-generated theorem gives
an actual closed embedding with hyperplane line $B^r\otimes f^*H^{nr}$.
The actual original tensor-power splitting, original iterated-power
isomorphism and original pullback-power comparison identify this whole
line with $(B\otimes f^*H^n)^r$. Pullback and tensor preserve original
invertibility. Thus the actual positive-power ample criterion proves
the assertion on the entire original $W$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original finite ideal ratios generate the entire Rees chart]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-rees-chart-generators}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigReesRatios.adjoin_ratios_eq_top}
For the original ideal $I=(g_1,\ldots,g_s)$ of a commutative ring $A$
and original pivot $a\in I$, the entire original chart algebra $A[I/a]$
is generated over $A$ by the original ratios $g_1/a,\ldots,g_s/a$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By its actual Rees-chart construction, $A[I/a]$ is the subalgebra of
$A[1/a]$ generated by all $b/a$, $b\in I$. Write an original $b$ as a
finite sum $\sum c_i g_i$. The original localization identities give
$b/a=\sum c_i(g_i/a)$. Therefore every original defining chart generator
lies in the algebra generated by the displayed finite ratios. Conversely
each displayed ratio is an original defining chart element. These two
inclusions give equality of the whole algebras, including their nilpotents.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The same original ideal generators generate its whole exceptional line]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-exceptional-generators}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigExceptionalGenerators.generatingMap,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigExceptionalGenerators.generatingMap_ι,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigExceptionalGenerators.generatingMap_epi,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigExceptionalGenerators.generatingSections,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigExceptionalGenerators.generatingSections_isFiniteType,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigExceptionalGenerators.generatorHom_generatingSections}
For an original ideal $I$ of any commutative ring and an original family
$g_i\in I$ generating that entire ideal, the actual exceptional sections
of these same $g_i$ genuinely generate the entire original exceptional
ideal module on the blowup. A finite original family gives a finite
actual generating family with exactly the same original index set and
whole section homomorphisms.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Evaluate the original free structure module at the same original
exceptional section at each original basis index. Its composite with
that basis inclusion is the whole original exceptional section. The
original charts at the actual $g_i$ cover the blowup because the $g_i$
generate the original ideal. On the original $g_i$ chart, its own
exceptional section is an isomorphism: under the original ideal
inclusion it is the regular principal generator of that entire ideal.
Its pulled free basis inclusion therefore factors an isomorphism through
the pulled whole evaluation map, making that evaluation an epimorphism.

To check the original evaluation is epic, take two whole maps out of its
target whose composites with it agree. Pulling to each actual chart and
cancelling the proved local epimorphism makes the two maps equal there.
The actual open-chart point cover detects equality of the entire original
maps, so they agree globally. Thus the same original free evaluation is
epic. Its recorded actual sections form the claimed generating family;
its actual index set is unchanged, so finiteness is retained.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual exceptional pivot frame retains every original ratio]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-exceptional-coefficients}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigExceptionalFrames.chartFrame,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelNefBigExceptionalFrames.coefficient_chartFrame}
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-exceptional-generators}
On the actual original chart $A[I/a]$, use the inverse of the genuine
original exceptional pivot section as the whole frame of the original
exceptional ideal module. Each original generator $g_i$ has coefficient
exactly its original Rees ratio $g_i/a$ under this frame, with the original
spectrum's whole section-ring identification.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original pivot section is an isomorphism by its proved entire regular
ideal generator formula. Its inverse is therefore the stated genuine
whole frame. The actual pullback of the constructed free generator is
the actual exceptional section of the same $g_i$. The original Rees
chart section identity writes this section as the original scalar
$g_i/a$ times the original pivot. Composing with the same original
pivot inverse cancels the entire pivot map, giving exactly scalar
multiplication by $g_i/a$. Evaluating this whole structure-module map
on its original unit gives the stated entire original coefficient.
No frame or coordinate matching certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual affine Rees exceptional line is relatively very ample]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-affine-rees-positive}
\notready
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-rees-chart-generators,lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-exceptional-generators,lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-exceptional-coefficients}
For an original finitely generated ideal $I$ of an original domain $A$,
let $\pi:V=\operatorname{Bl}_I(\operatorname{Spec}A)\to\operatorname{Spec}A$
be the actual blowup and $J=I\mathcal O_V$ its actual invertible ideal.
The same original $J\mathcal O_V=\mathcal O_V(-E)$ is very ample over
$\operatorname{Spec}A$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take finite original generators $g_i$ of $I$. Pull their actual original
ideal sections through the canonical ideal-comap epimorphism to sections
of the same $J$. On the actual chart $A[I/g_i]$, the pivot section is an
isomorphism, because its original inclusion is the regular generator of
the entire exceptional ideal. These original generator charts cover the
whole blowup. Consequently the finite original sections genuinely generate
$J$; no generating-family certificate is supplied.

Their actual projective morphism pulls the original hyperplane line back
to the same $J$. In the original pivot frame its entire coefficient tuple
consists of the original ratios $g_j/g_i$, with pivot one. The preceding
whole-chart generation lemma proves that these actual coefficients
generate the entire original chart algebra over $A$. Pad the original finite generator list with an actual zero generator.
The original graded polynomial map onto the Rees algebra, sending each
variable to its same original degree-one generator, is surjective, so its
actual Proj map is a closed immersion. On every original pivot chart,
this native closed map and the generated-line map both have precisely
the same entire padded tuple of original ratios. Equality of these actual
affine projective maps glues on the original generator-chart cover.
Therefore the generated-line map is this same native Rees closed immersion.
Its actual hyperplane comparison proves very ampleness of the
original exceptional ideal module. The zero ideal yields the empty blowup
and is included in the same chart argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original global Rees line becomes very ample after a pulled twist]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-global-rees-positive}
\notready
For an original integral projective scheme $X$ over a field, original ample $H$
and original coherent ideal $I$, the actual blowup $\pi:V\to X$ has,
for some original positive $a$, an actual very ample line
$\pi^*H^a\otimes(I\mathcal O_V)$ over the original field.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual positive-power projective embedding for $H$. Actual
closed-projective generation supplies finitely many original sections
of $I\otimes H^d$ for sufficiently large $d$. Their actual pullback,
followed by the original ideal-comap epimorphism, generates the whole
original $(I\mathcal O_V)\otimes\pi^*H^d$. The actual projective map
of this finite family has this same whole hyperplane line. Over every
original affine frame of $H^d$, its actual coefficient ratios are those
of the original ideal generators. The preceding whole Rees-chart argument
identifies the pair with $\pi$, on the inverse image of each such
original base affine, with the original Rees polynomial closed immersion
into relative projective space over that affine. The whole blowup of that
affine is the actual cartesian base change of the original blowup. These
are therefore exact base charts of the original target product, so
closed-immersion locality on the target proves that the entire original
pair is a closed immersion into $X\times\mathbf P^s$. Pair it with an actual
positive-power embedding of $X$, then use the actual Segre closed
immersion. Its hyperplane line is the original exceptional ideal module
tensored with an actual positive power of the original pulled $H$.
Original symmetry and power-addition identify the displayed actual line.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A finite original blowup sequence has a weighted effective correction]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-weighted-correction}
\notready
For a finite original sequence of coherent ideal blowups, whose actual
stages are integral, over an original projective scheme with ample $H$, there are an original positive integer
$a$ and an effective Cartier correction $E$ on its actual last scheme
such that $\mu^*H^a\otimes\mathcal O(-E)$ is ample. The correction is
an actual integral nonnegative weighted sum of the whole pulled centre
divisors. For an SNC boundary-compatible smooth-centre sequence these
centre divisors lie in its actual accumulated SNC boundary.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct through the original sequence. For the identity use the zero
effective correction and the original ample line. Suppose the original
intermediate line is $\mu^*H^a\otimes\mathcal O(-E)$. Apply the global
Rees positivity lemma to this actual ample line at the next blowup
$\sigma$, obtaining a positive $b$ and an ample line equal to
$\sigma^*(\mu^*H^a\otimes\mathcal O(-E))^b\otimes\mathcal O(-F)$,
where $F$ is the actual new centre divisor. Original tensor and pullback
identities give $\mu_{\mathrm{new}}^*H^{ab}\otimes
\mathcal O(-b\sigma^*E-F)$. The new correction is effective and its
actual integral weights are obtained by multiplying the old weights by
$b$ and adding the new weight one. This proves the assertion for the
finite original sequence. Accumulated normal-crossing boundary data
retain exactly these total centre divisors. A Cartier centre can give
an isomorphism blowup; its centre divisor is not asserted to be a
nontrivial exceptional locus.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Arbitrarily small actual ample corrections on the original blowup]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-small-ample-correction}
\notready
For the actual weighted correction above and an original ample rational
Cartier divisor $Q$, there are arbitrarily small positive rational
$\varepsilon$ such that the actual $\mu^*Q-\varepsilon E$ is ample.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take a positive original denominator $r$ for which $H=\mathcal O(rQ)$
is an actual ample line. The weighted construction gives an original
positive $a$ and an actual ample $B=\mu^*H^a\otimes\mathcal O(-E)$.
The first lemma makes $B\otimes\mu^*H^n$ ample for all sufficiently
large original $n$. Its actual divisor is $(a+n)r\mu^*Q-E$.
Divide by the positive original integer $r(a+n)$. Thus
$\varepsilon=1/(r(a+n))$ gives the claimed actual ample rational divisor.
These positive rationals tend to zero as the original $n$ tends to infinity.
The argument uses actual global generators of original pulled ample powers;
it does not replace them by an unproved general nef-plus-ample criterion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original rational coefficients have stable ceilings]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-nef-big-stable-ceiling}
\notready
For a rational Cartier divisor $P$ and an effective rational Cartier
divisor $E$ with finite support, the original whole divisor satisfies
$\lceil P-\varepsilon E\rceil=\lceil P\rceil$ for all sufficiently
small positive rational $\varepsilon$. If the fractional support of $P$
and support of $E$ are contained in one original SNC family, the perturbed
fractional support remains in this same family.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $e_i\geq0$ for the finitely many original nonzero coefficients
of $E$ and $p_i$ for the coefficients of the same original $P$ there.
For each such original index,
$\delta_i=p_i-(\lceil p_i\rceil-1)>0$. Choose an original positive
rational $\eta$ smaller than every $\delta_i/(e_i+1)$. If
$0<\varepsilon<\eta$, then
$\lceil p_i\rceil-1<p_i-\varepsilon e_i\leq\lceil p_i\rceil$.
Thus its ceiling is the same original integer $\lceil p_i\rceil$.
Every original coefficient outside this finite support is unchanged,
so the entire divisors agree, not just their supports. The only possible
new fractional components lie in the original support of $E$, proving
the stated SNC support assertion. An empty support imposes no constraint.
\end{proof}

The full nef-and-big reduction still requires the genuine numerical
Kodaira lemma for the source's interior-pseudoeffective bigness, its
connection to actual sections or effective rational divisors, and the
actual numerical nef-plus-ample ampleness criterion. None of these
statements follows merely from positive combinations of already ample
lines or from openness on a fixed finite family. They remain separate
positivity obligations for Theorem~\ref{thm:kn-nadel-snc-kv}.
