\begin{lemma}[Actual affine module sections under scalar base change]
\label{lem:fc-affine-module-flat-base-change}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.AffineModuleFlatBaseChange.sections,EnvelopeProperty.AffineModuleFlatBaseChange.tensorEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.AffineModuleFlatBaseChange.tensorEquiv_tmul}
\leanok
Let $p:U\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ have actual affine source, let $M$ be
an original quasicoherent module, and let $S$ be an actual $R$-algebra.
For the original cartesian base change $g:U_S\to U$, its actual whole
module of sections is canonically $S\otimes_R\Gamma(U,M)$.
The image of $s\otimes m$ is the original coefficient $s$ times the
original pullback-unit section of $m$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original affine cartesian square is the actual pushout of its
whole rings of functions. The genuine affine module pullback theorem
identifies sections with extension of scalars from $\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)$.
The ring pushout and tensor associativity identify that extension with
$S\otimes_R\Gamma(U,M)$. On $1\otimes m$ the native affine theorem gives
the actual pullback unit. Linearity supplies every $s\otimes m$.
No local section comparison or ring-pushout certificate is an input
to this lemma. Its use on original affine opens will be combined with
the genuine restriction/pullback unit square.
\end{proof}
