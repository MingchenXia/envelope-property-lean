\begin{definition}[The original product graph on the original all-affine blowup]
\label{def:whole-original-native-product-line-comparison}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.BlowupHomogeneousTautologicalLine.originalNativeProductWholeLineIso}
\leanok
\uses{def:whole-original-native-global-line-comparison,prop:whole-original-native-global-line-sections}
Fix the same $p,j,J,d,F_k$ and set $D=j^{-1}J$ and
$M=j^*\mathcal O(d)$. Write $Z$ for the original small-site Rees blowup,
$B=D.\mathrm{blowUp}$ for the original all-affine Rees blowup, and
$\epsilon:Z\simeq B$ for the genuine original identity-of-Rees comparison.
Let $q:Z\to\mathbf P^m_R$ be the original homogeneous-form map, and let
$a:Z\to X\times_R\mathbf P^m_R$ be its original product graph. Put
$b=\epsilon^{-1}\mathbin{\gg}a$ and $\pi:B\to X$.
There is an actual whole isomorphism
\[
 b^*\operatorname{pr}_2^*\mathcal O(1)
 \simeq \pi^*M\otimes(D\mathcal O_B).
\]
Here $b\mathbin{\gg}\operatorname{pr}_1=\pi$ and
$b\mathbin{\gg}\operatorname{pr}_2=\epsilon^{-1}\mathbin{\gg}q$ are exact
identities of the original morphisms. This concerns the original product graph
under the genuine Rees comparison. It does not identify it with a different
projective-bundle morphism.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The two original product-graph projection identities give the second equality
and identify its first projection with the original small-site blowup map.
The projection triangle for $\epsilon$ then gives the first equality.
Pull the whole original comparison and all of its section identities back by
$\epsilon^{-1}$. The latter is an isomorphism, hence an open immersion.
The canonical open tensor and ideal-comap comparison identifies the target
with $\pi^*M\otimes(D\mathcal O_B)$; the original homogeneous lifted-section
naturality theorem preserves each same old lift under this comparison.
The native composition comparison for $b$ and $\operatorname{pr}_2$ identifies
the source with the pullback along $\epsilon^{-1}\mathbin{\gg}q$.
Thus the actual isomorphism and all prescribed section identities exist
simultaneously. Choose one with these identities. All projection equalities,
ideal comparisons and section relations used here have just been derived;
none is an input certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[All prescribed sections on the original all-affine blowup]
\label{prop:whole-original-native-product-line-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.BlowupHomogeneousTautologicalLine.originalNativeProductWholeLineIso_section}
\leanok
\uses{def:whole-original-native-product-line-comparison}
The whole comparison sends $b^*\operatorname{pr}_2^*\sigma_k$ to the original
ideal-valued lift $\beta_{F_k}$ formed using the actual original projection
$\pi$ for every prescribed row $k$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The chosen isomorphism comes with all these identities in the same existence
statement. Its defining property gives each original row. In particular the
section family is the original family on the actual all-affine blowup, not a
replacement selected after transport.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The original finite-form closed-product witness retains the whole line]
\label{prop:whole-original-native-product-witness-line-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.BlowupHomogeneousTautologicalLine.exists_originalNativeProductWholeLine}
\leanok
\uses{def:whole-original-native-product-line-comparison,prop:whole-original-native-product-line-sections}
Let $R$ be Noetherian, let $j:X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_R$ be the original
closed embedding of integral $X$, and let $D_0$ be the original ideal sheaf.
The original finite same-degree representation of $D_0$ supplies actual
$m,d,J,F_k$ with $j^{-1}J=D_0$. Its original closed product embedding, carried
to the all-affine blowup by the identity of Rees algebras, retains the whole
line comparison above and all the same prescribed sections. Its first
projection is the actual original blowup projection and its second projection
is the same original form map under the same Rees identity.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the existing finite same-degree representation of the original ideal
through the given closed embedding. It supplies $J,d,F_k$, homogeneity,
generation of $J$ and the exact whole equality $j^{-1}J=D_0$ internally.
For this same family the original forms product map is a closed immersion;
precomposition by the genuine whole Rees isomorphism preserves closedness.
Its two projection identities were proved above. Apply the preceding whole
comparison and its complete section law to these same witnesses. This
strengthens the existing original closed-product construction while retaining
its literal ideal and its original homogeneous-form coordinates.
\end{proof}
