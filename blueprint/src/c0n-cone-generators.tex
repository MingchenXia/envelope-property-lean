\section{Actual bounded polynomial generators of the original multiplier cone ideal}
\label{sec:c0n-cone-generators}

Keep the original smooth projective subvariety $X$, its actual parameter
family $i:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^M_{k[t]}$, original lower-bounded
obstacle $\varphi$, and the entire original scheme multiplier ideal
$K_m$.  Its cone ideal is the already defined original $B_m$, obtained
from the native homogeneous representative of $(i_*K_m)$ and the original
parameter exchange and cone quotient.  The only vanishing assumption is
the named relative ample Nadel proposition; polynomial representatives,
evaluation images, bounded generators and Fubini--Study conclusions are
not inputs.

\begin{lemma}[Actual tensor-ideal inclusion and original included sections]
\label{lem:c0n-cone-generators-included-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsTensor.idealTensorInclusion,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsEvaluation.includedSection,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsEvaluation.includedSection_sectionHom}
\leanok
For a whole original ideal $K$, invertible module $L$, and genuine
generating family $G$ of $K\otimes L$, compose its original evaluation
with $K\otimes L\to L$ induced by the actual ideal inclusion.  Its
original basis morphisms give actual global sections $s_a$ of the same
$L$, indexed by the same $G.I$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Tensor the original $K\hookrightarrow\mathcal O_Y$ by $L$ and compose
with the native left-unit comparison.  Compose each original free basis
inclusion and the original epimorphic evaluation with this morphism.
The actual unit-Hom/global-section equivalence, evaluated at the original
unit section on the whole scheme, gives $s_a$.  Applying the same native
section-Hom constructor recovers exactly those original composites.
No independent coefficient module or chosen scalar section intervenes.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The included original generators have exactly the whole ideal image]
\label{lem:c0n-cone-generators-whole-image}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsTensor.cancellationIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsTensor.cancellationIso_inclusion,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsTensor.coefficientEpimorphism,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsTensor.coefficientEpimorphism_epi,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsTensor.coefficientEpimorphism_inclusion,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsEvaluation.familyEvaluation_eq_tensorEvaluation,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsEvaluation.includedEvaluation_imageIdeal}
\leanok
The genuine family evaluation of the preceding same sections against
$L^{-1}$ has whole geometric image exactly $K$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Tensor the original epimorphism
$\mathcal O_Y^{(G.I)}\twoheadrightarrow K\otimes L$ by $L^{-1}$.
The actual invertible tensor functor preserves that epimorphism.  Its
native inverse evaluation cancels $L\otimes L^{-1}$, producing an actual
epimorphism from the original free-tensor-dual source onto $K$.
Naturality of this cancellation and the native unit comparisons show
that its composite with the original ideal inclusion is precisely the
family evaluation of the same included sections.  Verify this equality
on the original coproduct basis; preservation of the coproduct by the
invertible tensor functor and the genuine free-tensor-dual isomorphism
retain the same index and section morphisms.  Thus this is a genuine
epi--mono image factorization of the actual original evaluation map.
The uniqueness of the categorical image, read on whole ideal-module
sections on every affine open, identifies its whole ideal image with
$K$.  Neither an expected image equality nor generation of an unrelated
full-section base ideal is assumed.
\uses{lem:c0n-cone-generators-included-sections}
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The actual fixed lift range.}
From the named Nadel proposition and original smooth charts, choose one
$c_0$ before all $d,\varphi,m$ such that
$i^*\mathcal O(md+c_0)\otimes K_m$ has finite genuine generators.
There is one further bound $b$, depending only on the original inclusion,
such that for $c=c_0+b$ every original module
$i^*\mathcal O(md+c)\otimes K_m$ has finite genuine generators and every
included original section has an actual homogeneous polynomial lift of
exact degree $md+c$ over $k[t]$.

Use the proved original uniform multiplier-generation producer for $c_0$.
The proved fixed closed-embedding restriction bound supplies $b$ before
all varying data.  The actual $H^b$ has finite generators pulled from the
native ambient monomial family.  Tensor these with the actual original
multiplier generating family, and use native symmetry, pullback
monoidality and projective twist addition to identify the result with
$i^*\mathcal O(md+c_0+b)\otimes K_m$.  Swap the two factors if necessary
before applying the preceding included-section construction.  Because
$md+c_0+b\geq b$, the proved actual restriction theorem lifts each section
to an original ambient $\mathcal O(md+c)$ section.  The independently
proved ambient section--homogeneous-polynomial theorem represents that
same whole section by a polynomial in $k[t][x_0,\ldots,x_M]$ of exact
degree $md+c$.  Both lifts are deductions from the actual geometry.


\begin{lemma}[The actual lifted finite polynomials recover the whole original scheme ideal]
\label{lem:c0n-cone-generators-polynomial-whole-ideal}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsSheaf.homogeneousFamilyIdeal,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsSheaf.homogeneousFamilyIdeal_sheaf_eq_iSup,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsSheaf.homogeneousFamilyIdeal_comap_eq}
\leanok
Let $F_a$ be the actual polynomial lifts just constructed, and let $J$
be their homogeneous span.  On the actual original parameter family,
$(\widetilde J)\mathcal O_Y=K_m$ as whole ideal sheaves.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The same actual polynomial lifts pull back to the included original
sections.  The proved native ideal-of-a-section pullback theorem
identifies the entire section ideal of each lift with the corresponding
section ideal on the original family.  The native finite family
image theorem, and preservation of suprema by geometric ideal comap,
therefore identify the whole comap of $\widetilde J$ with the image of
that same original family evaluation.  The second lemma identifies that
whole image with $K_m$.  The finite homogeneous-evaluation theorem uses
exactly the span of these lifted forms, with no saturation replacement
before this equality of actual scheme ideals is established.
\uses{lem:c0n-cone-generators-whole-image,lem:c0n-original-homogeneous-section-ideal,thm:c0n-original-uniform-buffer-growth,lem:c0n-relative-homogeneous-polynomial-lift}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual finite polynomial representatives give both original cone bounds]
\label{lem:c0n-cone-generators-actual-cone-bounds}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGeneratorsAlgebra.hasBoundedPolynomialConeGenerators_of_lifts}
\leanok
For the original family, original whole ideal $K$, and a finite genuine
generating family of $K\otimes i^*\mathcal O(e)$, suppose actual homogeneous
forms $F_a$ of degree $e$ pull back to the same included sections.
Then the original $\operatorname{coneIdeal}(K)$ has bounded polynomial
generators of degree $e$.  Its desired whole scheme image or cone
saturation comparison is not an input to this lemma.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The preceding whole ideal lemma proves that the homogeneous span $J$ of
the same forms has whole comap exactly $K$.  The native ideal-sheaf
adjunction gives $\widetilde J\subset i_*K$, so the native homogeneous
representative adjunction gives $J\subset\operatorname{Rep}(i_*K)$.
Mapping the actual original cone quotient gives the lower cone bound.
The canonical representative has whole comap $K$ as well; the proved
same-family saturation theorem gives the opposite saturated bound.
The inverse parameter exchange takes the finite image of $a\mapsto F_a$
to a finite set of original polynomials, and the actual cone quotient
identity preserves both spans.  Every original parameter coefficient is
homogeneous of degree $e$ by the proved coefficient-swap theorem.  These
are the exact three clauses of the bounded-polynomial-generator predicate.
\uses{lem:c0n-cone-generators-polynomial-whole-ideal,lem:c0n-canonical-scheme-cone-representative,lem:c0n-scheme-cone-saturation-product,lem:c0n-relative-homogeneous-parameter-coefficients}
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Actual uniformly bounded polynomial generators of the original cone ideals]
\label{prop:c0n-original-bounded-polynomial-cone-generators}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierConeGenerators.exists_uniform_bounded_polynomial_generators}
\leanok
Assume the named Nadel proposition and the original smooth charts of $X$.
There is one $c\geq0$, chosen before all $d,\varphi$, their lower bounds,
and $m$, such that the original $B_m$ satisfies
\nolinkurl{HasBoundedPolynomialConeGenerators X B_m (m*d+c)}.
No polynomial lifting, evaluation-image, saturation, or desired FS
certificate is supplied to this final conclusion.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choose the finite actual generator index and actual homogeneous lifts
internally as above, and take the finite set of their original polynomials.
By the whole scheme-ideal equality, their homogeneous ideal maps into the
native representative of the entire original extended multiplier ideal;
the original cone quotient therefore sends their span into $B_m$.
Conversely the canonical native representative has whole comap $K_m$.
The proved original coordinate-localization/saturation theorem, applied
to the actual equality in the previous lemma, gives containment of $B_m$
in the saturation of the span of those same cone classes.  All annihilator
powers are constructed by that theorem.  Finally apply the inverse of
the original parameter exchange to each entire polynomial.  Its proved
coefficient theorem shows that every original $t$-coefficient is
homogeneous of the same $x$-degree $md+c$, hence of degree at most that
bound.  The actual parameter-exchange/cone-quotient identity preserves
the whole span and both ideal containments.  These are exactly the three
clauses of the stated bounded-polynomial-generator property.  Individual
$t$-components are never asserted to lie in $B_m$.
\uses{lem:c0n-cone-generators-actual-cone-bounds,thm:c0n-original-uniform-buffer-growth,lem:c0n-relative-homogeneous-polynomial-lift}
\end{proof}
