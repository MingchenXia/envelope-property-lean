\begin{lemma}[Actual open restriction of the original pullback and its unit]
\label{lem:fc-module-pullback-open-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModulePullbackOpenSections.pushforwardSquareIso,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModulePullbackOpenSections.pullbackRestrictIso,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModulePullbackOpenSections.unit_square}
\leanok
For any original morphism $g:Y\to X$, original module $M$, and original
open $U\subseteq X$, put $V=g^{-1}U$ and retain the actual restricted
morphism $g_U:V\to U$. There is a canonical whole module isomorphism
$g_U^*(M|_U)\simeq(g^*M)|_V$. The actual two composite adjunction units
commute with this same isomorphism and the actual pushforward square.
No affine, flatness or quasicoherence hypothesis is needed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual restricted morphism satisfies $g_U\,i_U=i_V\,g$.
Native pushforward composition and that literal equality identify the
two entire composite right adjoints. Compose the genuine restriction
adjunction with pullback along $g_U$ and transport its right adjoint
through that original identification. Compose pullback along $g$ with
the genuine restriction adjunction to $V$. These are actual left
adjoints to the same right adjoint; uniqueness of adjoints supplies the
whole original isomorphism. The actual uniqueness theorem for the unit
and the genuine formula for the unit of a composite adjunction give the
stated whole unit square. Evaluation on original opens therefore keeps
the original section pullback and original restriction maps.
\end{proof}
