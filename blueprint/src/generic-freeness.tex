\section{Actual generic freeness}

The four source modules in this section are unchanged Apache-licensed sources from
\texttt{TauCetiProject/TauCeti}, commit
\texttt{15198c5caca3d2ccadc8d5b4e98cbef1a50b4a4c}. They compile under the existing
Lean and Mathlib pins. The upstream provenance is recorded in
\texttt{EnvelopeProperty/deps/tauceti-generic-ep33}.

\begin{lemma}[Free exhaustive filtrations]\label{lem:actual-free-filtration}
\leanok
\lean{Module.Free.of_filtration}
Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ an $R$-module. Suppose
$0=N_0\subseteq N_1\subseteq\cdots$ is an exhaustive filtration by actual
submodules and every $N_{j+1}/N_j$ is free. Then $M$ is free.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Projectivity splits the actual quotient map at each step. The resulting map
from the direct sum of the subquotients to $M$ is injective by induction on the
largest nonzero summand, and surjective by exhaustiveness. Transport its actual
direct-sum basis along this linear equivalence. No finiteness is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Local generic freeness]\label{thm:actual-local-generic-freeness}
\leanok
\lean{Module.freeLocus_mem_nhds_of_mem_minimalPrimes, Module.dense_interior_freeLocus_of_finiteType}
\uses{lem:actual-free-filtration}
Let $A$ be reduced and noetherian, $B$ a finite-type $A$-algebra, and $M$ a finite
$B$-module with its compatible $A$-module structure. Define the actual free locus
$\{\mathfrak p\in\Spec A:M_{\mathfrak p}\text{ is free over }A_{\mathfrak p}\}$.
This locus is a neighbourhood of each minimal prime of $A$, and its interior
is dense in $\Spec A$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Induct on a finite algebra generating set. For a polynomial extension, filter
$M$ by powers of the new variable applied to a finite module generating set.
The actual submodules of coefficient relations increase and stabilize by
noetherianity. Thus only finitely many subquotients need to be made free near
the prescribed minimal prime. Intersect their actual open neighbourhoods and
apply Lemma~\ref{lem:actual-free-filtration} at every prime of that intersection.
Surjective algebra maps transfer the statement to the given finite-type algebra.
Every nonempty open of the actual spectrum contains a minimal prime, which
gives density of the interior. This is local freeness at the stated primes;
a global basis after a single localization is a further statement.
\end{proof}
