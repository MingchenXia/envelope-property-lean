\section{Characteristic-free simultaneous Cartier ideal models}

\begin{lemma}[Simultaneous principalization on the actual product]
\label{lem:pl-product-blowup-cartier}
\uses{lem:pl-native-variety-ideal-log}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousIdealBlowup,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousIdealBlowupProjection,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousIdealBlowupProjection_isProper,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousIdealBlowup_isInvertible,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousIdealBlowupProjection_isIso_off_centre}
\leanok
Let $I,J$ be genuine ideal sheaves on the actual $X\times\mathbf A^1$ model.
Blowing up their product gives a proper morphism $\pi:Z\to X\times\mathbf A^1$
such that both $I\mathcal O_Z$ and $J\mathcal O_Z$ are invertible ideals,
hence effective Cartier ideals.  The morphism is an isomorphism over the
complement of the support of $IJ$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take $Z=\operatorname{Bl}_{IJ}(X\times\mathbf A^1)$ with its actual blowup
projection.  The base is locally Noetherian because it is proper of finite
type over the Noetherian ring $k[t]$, so the blowup projection is proper.
The inverse image of the centre ideal $IJ$ is invertible by the blowup
construction.  Geometric ideal pullback commutes with multiplication;
therefore $(I\mathcal O_Z)(J\mathcal O_Z)$ is invertible.  The factors of
an invertible ideal sheaf are invertible: affine locally this is the
regular-generator argument for factors of a product, and the affine
criterion glues the result.  Over the complement of the centre the centre
ideal is the unit ideal, whose blowup is an isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Cartier equations compute the intrinsic model logarithm]
\label{lem:pl-cartier-model-log}
\uses{lem:pl-product-blowup-cartier}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.exists_regular_generator_modelIdealLog}
\leanok
On a proper model over a bounded coefficient valuation, the intrinsic
model logarithm of an invertible ideal is the logarithm of the value of
an actual regular local defining equation at every valued point.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At the proper centre of the valued point, invertibility supplies an
affine neighbourhood $U$ and a nonzerodivisor $g\in\Gamma(U,\mathcal O)$
with $I(U)=(g)$.  The intrinsic stalk ideal norm equals the ideal norm on
this affine centre neighbourhood.  Boundedness identifies the norm of
the principal ideal $(g)$ with $|g|$.  Taking logarithms gives the claimed
identity, including the possible zero value of $g$ at the support.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Gauss lifting through the constructed blowup]
\label{lem:pl-blowup-gauss-lift}
\uses{lem:pl-product-blowup-cartier,lem:pl-native-variety-gauss-lift}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeProductGaussPoint_notMem_productSupport,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousIdealBlowupGaussPoint,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.map_simultaneousIdealBlowupGaussPoint,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.modelIdealLog_simultaneousIdealBlowup_comap,EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.liftThroughOpenIso,EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.map_liftThroughOpenIso}
\leanok
For ideals $I,J$ whose intrinsic logarithms at the actual variety Gauss
points are finite, those Gauss points lift through the constructed
blowup, preserving the entire valued point.  The intrinsic logarithms
of the two pulled-back Cartier ideals equal the original logarithms.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The ideal logarithm is negative infinity exactly when the native support
belongs to the ideal's closed support.  Finiteness therefore puts each
Gauss point outside both closed supports, hence outside the support of
$IJ$, which is their union.  Restrict the point to this open complement,
apply the inverse of the actual blowup isomorphism there, and include it
in the whole blowup.  Its full image is the original valued point by the
two inverse identities.  Geometric ideal pullback preserves the whole
intrinsic model norm at this point, and therefore preserves its logarithm.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Cartier ideal functions for every PL function]
\label{prop:pl-native-cartier-ideal-models}
\uses{lem:pl-native-variety-ideal-log,lem:pl-product-blowup-cartier,lem:pl-cartier-model-log,lem:pl-blowup-gauss-lift}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.IsPL.exists_simultaneousCartierIdeal_models}
\leanok
Every rational PL function on $X^{\rm an}$ is, after multiplication by
an integer $r>0$, the difference of the intrinsic functions of two
constructed effective Cartier ideals on a constructed proper blowup of
the actual $X\times\mathbf A^1$ model, up to an integral constant.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Construct the two actual ideal sheaves from the PL function on the actual
variety product.  Their logarithms are finite at the actual Gauss points.
Blow up their product and pull back the two ideals; both pulled-back
ideals are invertible.  Lift each Gauss point through the constructed
off-centre isomorphism.  The entire logarithms are unchanged under
geometric ideal pullback, so the original exact difference formula holds
on the blowup.  The preceding regular-equation lemma identifies each
intrinsic ideal function with the function of its actual local Cartier
equation.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Remaining test-configuration geometry]
\label{rmk:pl-cartier-remaining-testconfig}
\uses{lem:pl-blowup-gauss-lift,thm:wu-bj-testconfig,thm:wu-cld-nef,thm:wu-bj-nef-psh}
The blowup and the two Cartier ideals are constructed in arbitrary
characteristic.  To obtain the full test-configuration statement one
must retain the central-support and equivariance data, identify the
isomorphism over the whole nonzero-parameter locus, normalize the
projective modification, and construct its polarized model line bundle.
The curve-degree and relative-nef approximation steps of local positivity
remain separate mathematical obligations.
\end{remark}
