\section{A single finite actual divisorial family for all multiplier exponents}

\begin{theorem}[Finite actual divisorial determination for all rational exponents]\label{thm:sc0-finite-divisorial-multiplier-all-exponents}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.exists_finite_divisorialDVRs_forall_multiplier_exponents}
\leanok
\uses{thm:sc0-fixed-boundary-multiplier-detection,thm:sc0-finite-etale-boundary-divisorial-dvrs,thm:sc0-leaf-monomial-multiplier-membership,thm:sc0-valuative-multiplier-membership}
Let $C\subset K$ be an arbitrary smooth affine domain over a
characteristic-zero field $k$, with original fraction field $K$, and let
$0\ne\mathfrak a\subset C$. There exists a finite set $S$ of actual
original-source essentially finite-type divisorial DVR subalgebras of
$K$ such that, for every $q\in\mathbb Q$ and every $f\in C$,
\[
 f\in\mathcal J_C(\mathfrak a^q)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 f\text{ satisfies its intrinsic original-source multiplier condition
 at every }V\in S.
\]
The same internally produced finite family works for every rational $q$.
No finite testing hypothesis, supplied resolution, coordinate family,
canonical-image comparison or discrepancy estimate is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Produce the actual simultaneous monomial Rees tree from the original
smooth source and the actual nonzero ideal. Its actual leaf occurrences
form a finite set. Each leaf has actual etale coordinates and actual
monomial expressions for the extended original ideal and the genuine
original-source relative Jacobian ideal. Its selected boundary
coordinates are nonzero. Take every prime minimal over their principal
ideals and its literal prime-localization image in $K$.

The fixed boundary-family construction derives that each such image is
an actual original-source divisorial DVR, with the same original
fraction field and actual essential finite type. On each leaf there
are only finitely many such primes, and the tree has only finitely many
leaves. Their combined images therefore form a finite set $S$. None
of these choices depends on $q$.

Full multiplier membership implies its condition at every $V\in S$
because its definition tests every actual original-source divisorial
DVR. Conversely suppose all those selected conditions hold. On each
actual leaf, the fixed-boundary multiplier detection theorem converts
the corresponding selected conditions into membership of the actual
rounded monomial ideal for the given $q$.

Now take any actual divisorial DVR $W$ containing $C$. Actual valuation
coverage of the Rees tree gives a leaf contained in $W$. Choose the
finite DVR coordinates internally supplied by $W$ and identify their
rank with the actual etale leaf rank using the original common field.
Membership in that leaf's rounded monomial ideal gives the genuine
original-source multiplier inequality at $W$ by the monomial multiplier
bound, including its actual original-source Jacobian. The intrinsic
contribution equals this coordinate computation. Since $W$ was arbitrary,
$f$ belongs to the full multiplier ideal. The argument applies to every
rational $q$ with the same set $S$, as required.
\end{proof}
