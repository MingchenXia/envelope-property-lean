\section{Uniform generation of the actual whole asymptotic multiplier family}

Fix the original characteristic-zero field, original integral smooth
scheme, original whole graded ideal sheaves and original projective
embedding over a finite-type affine base. The only analytic vanishing
input is the explicit relative ample Nadel proposition. Every multiplier
ideal below is the original all-divisorial-DVR scheme ideal. In particular,
no maximal constituent, stabilization index, global generator family for
the asymptotic ideal, or envelope conclusion is supplied.
\source{bj22-trivval:thm:contenvlisse,fhx-envelope-20261028:thm:smooth-EP}

\begin{definition}[The original whole asymptotic adjoint module]
\label{def:c0n-asymptotic-adjoint-module}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGeneration.twistedModule}
\leanok
For the original field structure $p:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, original
integer $n$, original sequence $\mathfrak a_\bullet$ generically nonzero
at each positive index, $m\in\mathbb N$ and original module $A$, set
\[
 \mathcal M_m(A)=
 (\omega_{Y/k}\otimes A)\otimes
 \mathcal J_Y(\|\mathfrak a_\bullet^m\|),
 \qquad \omega_{Y/k}=\bigwedge^n\Omega^1_{Y/k}.
\]
The expression uses the actual module of the whole original asymptotic
ideal sheaf, with this displayed parenthesization.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Whole asymptotic adjoint modules are attained internally at divisible original indices]
\label{lem:c0n-asymptotic-adjoint-attainment}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGeneration.exists_divisible_twistedModule_eq,
EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGeneration.exists_constituent_twistedModule_eq,
EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGeneration.exists_constituent_twistedModule_eq_of_projective}
\leanok
\uses{def:c0n-asymptotic-adjoint-module,thm:sc0-original-global-asymptotic-stabilization}
Assume the original smooth integral scheme is quasi-compact and the
original sequence is graded. For each $m\geq0$, there is a positive
original index $q_0$ such that at every positive original multiple $q$
of $q_0$, the entire original module $\mathcal M_m(A)$ equals
\[
 (\omega_{Y/k}\otimes A)\otimes
 \mathcal J_Y(\mathfrak a_q^{m/q}).
\]
In particular one such positive original constituent is produced. The
index is allowed to depend on $m$; no eventual divisibility or desired
module comparison is an input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the proved whole global divisible-index stabilization theorem to
the original sequence and its actual gradedness. Its proof constructs
an attained member of the directed family in the Noetherian ideal-sheaf
lattice; that is the entire original asymptotic ideal. Apply the actual
ideal-to-module construction and tensor by the displayed fixed original
module to this proved equality. For the last assertion take $q=q_0$,
whose positivity and divisibility are part of that internally produced
index. This works unchanged at $m=0$ because the original stabilization
theorem quantifies over all natural $m$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One original canonical-hyperplane twist generates the whole asymptotic family]
\label{thm:c0n-asymptotic-uniform-canonical-generation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGeneration.exists_generatingSections}
\leanok
\uses{lem:c0n-asymptotic-adjoint-attainment,prop:c0n-uniform-canonical-generators,def:c0n-relative-ample-nadel-input}
Assume relative ample Nadel vanishing over $k$. Let the original integral
$Y$ have a closed embedding $j:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_R^N$ over a
finite-type $k$-algebra $R$, with original $n$-smooth field structure $p$
equal to its actual base composite. Let $H$ be its actual hyperplane
pullback and $L$ an original invertible line. Suppose the original graded
family satisfies that each actual
$\mathfrak a_q\otimes L^q$ is finitely globally generated, and is
nonzero at the generic point for every $q>0$. Then for every $m\geq0$
\[
 (\omega_{Y/k}\otimes(H^{N+1}\otimes L^m))\otimes
      \mathcal J_Y(\|\mathfrak a_\bullet^m\|)
\]
has actual finite global generating sections. The fixed twist
$\omega_{Y/k}\otimes H^{N+1}$ is chosen before $m$, and neither its
exponent nor its line depends on the internally selected index $q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original closed projective embedding makes its original structure
map proper over the affine base, hence its entire original scheme is
quasi-compact. Apply the preceding lemma to construct a positive original
$q$ realizing the whole asymptotic ideal for this $m$. Apply the proved
ordinary uniform-generation theorem to this same original
$\mathfrak a_q$, original $L$, positive index $q$, natural $m$ and its
actual finite generating sections after tensoring by $L^q$. That theorem
uses precisely the rational coefficient $m/q$ and produces finite
generators with the same fixed hyperplane exponent $N+1$. Transport those
actual sections along the proved equality of the whole original ideals,
which identifies the entire displayed original modules. The chosen
constituent may vary with $m$, while the ordinary theorem's fixed
canonical-hyperplane twist does not. No fixed-family Serre threshold is
mistaken for uniformity in $m$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A fixed pure hyperplane twist generates all whole asymptotic ideals]
\label{cor:c0n-asymptotic-uniform-hyperplane-generation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGeneration.exists_uniform_pure_power_generatingSections}
\leanok
\uses{lem:c0n-asymptotic-adjoint-attainment,prop:c0n-uniform-pure-hyperplane-generators}
Under the same original geometry, there is a fixed integer $c$, independent
of $m$, such that every actual
$H^c\otimes L^m\otimes\mathcal J_Y(\|\mathfrak a_\bullet^m\|)$
has finite global generating sections.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The proved ordinary theorem chooses its genuine canonical-inverse Serre
buffer once, before all original varying ideals, lines and multiplier
indices. Let its fixed exponent be $b$. After receiving an original graded
family, line and index $m$, internally choose a positive original $q$
realizing the entire original asymptotic ideal as in the attainment lemma.
The ordinary endpoint constructs actual finite generators of
$H^{N+1+b}\otimes L^m\otimes\mathcal J_Y(\mathfrak a_q^{m/q})$.
The actual equality of the entire original ideals transports this family
to the entire displayed original asymptotic module. Thus the same
$c=N+1+b$, chosen before the original family, line and $m$, works for all
of them. The ordinary endpoint's proof constructs the fixed inverse
canonical finite family by Serre generation, tensors it with its actual
canonical family, cancels the original canonical evaluation, and uses
the actual tensor-power addition isomorphism. No uniform Serre bound for
the varying multiplier family is supplied or inferred.
\end{proof}

The actual direct-obstacle sequence must still supply its own genuine
polarized homogeneous-section generators and graded nonzero ideals. A
full-sections base ideal is not substituted for that original ideal.
Those separate producers instantiate the results above; the only
vanishing proposition is the displayed Nadel input.
