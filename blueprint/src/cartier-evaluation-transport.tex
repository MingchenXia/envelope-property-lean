\section{Actual dual transport determined by original evaluation}

\begin{lemma}[Canonical transport of actual pulled duals]
\label{lem:cartier-actual-evaluation-dual-transport}
\uses{lem:cartier-actual-line-tensor-fully-faithful}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.pulledEvaluationIso}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.evaluationDualTransportIso}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.evaluationDualTransportIso_pairing}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.evaluationDualTransportIso_balanced}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.evaluationDualTransportIso_unique}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.evaluationDualTransportIso_refl}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.evaluationDualTransportIso_trans}
\leanok
Let $\mathcal L$ be an invertible module sheaf on an arbitrary scheme
$Y$, let $f,g:X\to Y$ be actual morphisms, and let
$e:f^*\mathcal L\simeq g^*\mathcal L$ be an actual isomorphism.
The original pulled canonical evaluations, composed with the genuine
pullback tensorator and monoidal unit comparison, give actual pairings
$E_f:f^*\mathcal L\otimes f^*(\mathcal L^\vee)\simeq\mathcal O_X$
and $E_g$.
There is a canonical actual isomorphism
$T_e:f^*(\mathcal L^\vee)\simeq g^*(\mathcal L^\vee)$, uniquely
characterized among all actual morphisms by
\[
 (1\otimes T_e)E_g=(e^{-1}\otimes1)E_f.
\]
Equivalently, the actual simultaneous tensor map preserves the original
evaluation: $(e\otimes T_e)E_g=E_f$.
It is the identity for the actual identity of $f^*\mathcal L$, and
$T_eT_{e'}=T_{ee'}$ for every composable pair of original isomorphisms
between three actual pullbacks of $\mathcal L$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Construct $E_f$ by the actual tensorator of $f^*$, pullback of the
original canonical evaluation isomorphism, and the inverse actual
unit comparison. Use the same construction for $g$ and for any third
original morphism $h$. Tensor by the genuine invertible $g^*\mathcal L$
is fully faithful, by original invertibility and the actual braiding.
Lift the actual isomorphism
$(e^{-1}\otimes1)E_fE_g^{-1}$ uniquely through that functor. This is
$T_e$. Its actual image is that composite, which proves the displayed
identity. Conversely that identity determines the image by cancelling
the original isomorphism $E_g$, and faithfulness determines the map.
Composing the characterizing equation with $e\otimes1$ and cancelling
$e\otimes1$ against $e^{-1}\otimes1$ proves the balanced identity.
For the original identity, the identity on its pulled dual satisfies
the characterization, giving the unit formula. For composable $e,e'$,
the tensor interchange identity moves $e'^{-1}$ past $T_e$.
The two characterizing equations then identify the pairing of
$T_eT_{e'}$ with
$(e'^{-1}\otimes1)(e^{-1}\otimes1)E_f=((ee')^{-1}\otimes1)E_f$.
The same uniqueness proves the composition formula.
\end{proof}
