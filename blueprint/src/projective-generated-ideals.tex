\begin{lemma}[Original global sections of a proper projective ideal]
\label{lem:fc-original-projective-ideal-global-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjectiveGeneratedIdeals.eq_top_of_nonzero_globalSection, EnvelopeProperty.ProjectiveGeneratedIdeals.globalSection_eq_zero_of_ne_top}
\leanok
\uses{lem:fc-original-projective-global-function-units}
Let $X$ be the original integral projective scheme over $k$ and $I$ any
original ideal sheaf.  An actual nonzero section of
$\Gamma(X,I)$ forces $I=\mathcal O_X$.  Consequently every original
global section of a proper $I$ is zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original ideal inclusion identifies its whole section with a nonzero
original global function.  By projective integrality that function is a
unit in the same original global ring.  Its restriction to every affine
open is a unit lying in the original affine ideal, so every affine ideal
is the unit ideal.  This gives equality of the whole original ideal
sheaves.  The second assertion is the contrapositive.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual generated projective ideals are zero or unit]
\label{thm:fc-original-generated-projective-ideal-zero-or-unit}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjectiveGeneratedIdeals.eq_bot_or_top_of_generatingSections}
\leanok
\uses{lem:fc-original-projective-ideal-global-sections}
Every genuinely globally generated original ideal on $X$ is either the
zero ideal or the unit ideal.  No finite generating family is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If the ideal is proper, all its original global generators vanish by the
preceding lemma.  Compose the original free evaluation epimorphism with
the actual original ideal inclusion.  Every coproduct component is zero,
so the composite is zero.  Epimorphic cancellation makes the entire
original ideal inclusion zero.  On every actual affine open the original
ideal-module section comparison then makes every original ideal element
zero.  Hence the whole original ideal sheaf is zero.
\end{proof}
