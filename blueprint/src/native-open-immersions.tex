\subsection{Native open restrictions of valued scheme points}
\label{subsec:native-open-immersions}

The following statements concern genuine scheme open immersions and the native
point topology. No analytic structure sheaf or coherent-sheaf gluing is used.

\begin{theorem}[Actual open-immersion image and point injectivity]
\label{scheme:valued-open-immersion-image}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.map_injective_of_isOpenImmersion,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.exists_map_eq_of_point_mem_range,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.range_map_of_isOpenImmersion,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.image_point_preimage}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:valued-point-pullback}
For an actual scheme open immersion $f:X\to Y$, its native valued-point map
is injective and its image is exactly the inverse image of the scheme image
under point projection. No valuation extension assumption is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The scheme point map is injective, and the induced map of the actual stalk
residue fields at each source point is an isomorphism. Thus equality of mapped
native points first identifies their scheme points; equality after pullback
along this field isomorphism then identifies their entire valuations. For any
native target point whose scheme point is $f(x)$, pull its valuation back along
the inverse residue-field isomorphism. Actual inverse-isomorphism identities
show that the resulting native source point maps to the original target point.
This proves the exact image statement. The same construction, and point
injectivity, show that every support-open inverse image maps onto the inverse
image of its scheme open image.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Open immersion of the native point spaces]
\label{scheme:valued-open-embedding}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.eval_map_appIso_inv,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.image_sectionOpen,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.isOpenMap_map_of_isOpenImmersion,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.isOpenEmbedding_map_of_isOpenImmersion,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.openImmersionHomeomorph,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.openImmersionHomeomorph_apply_coe,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.restrictionHomeomorph,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.restrictionHomeomorph_apply_coe,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.isoHomeomorph,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.isoHomeomorph_apply}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:valued-open-immersion-image,scheme:valued-point-topology}
An actual scheme open immersion induces an open embedding of native valued
point spaces, and a homeomorphism onto the exact support-open subspace of its
image. In particular an open subscheme $U\subset X$ gives precisely the native
points whose underlying scheme support lies in $U$. Actual scheme isomorphisms
induce canonical homeomorphisms of their native valued-point spaces.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The scheme's actual section isomorphism transports a section on a source open
to a genuine section on its open image. Evaluation naturality and the inverse
section-map identity prove equality of all their values at mapped native
points. Together with the preceding lifting theorem, each section-evaluation
generator maps exactly onto the corresponding target section-evaluation
generator. Support generators likewise map to support generators. Their images
are therefore open, as is the image of the whole source space. Induction on
opens generated by these sets proves the map open: injectivity preserves image
intersections and images commute with unions. The already proved continuity
and injectivity give an open embedding. Its standard homeomorphism with its
image, combined with the exact image equality, gives the support-open
homeomorphism and open-subscheme specialization. Applying the genuine
topological functor to a scheme isomorphism gives an isomorphism of
topological spaces, hence the last canonical homeomorphism.
\end{proof}
