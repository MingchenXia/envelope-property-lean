\section{The actual mixed candidate on the native whole affine pushforward}

\begin{lemma}[The same original mixed candidate is a regular sheaf morphism]
\label{lem:kn-canonical-mixed-native-sheaf-candidate}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.mixedCanonicalSheafCandidate,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.mixedCanonicalSheafCandidate_sections}
\leanok
Let the original characteristic-zero field, etale coordinates, positive tame
degrees, active set, injection and units define the entire original mixed
algebra $B$ over $A$. Write $\beta:\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$
for its original algebra map. The same original $A$-linear mixed canonical
candidate lifts to an actual regular map
$\beta_*\omega_B\to\omega_A$ on the native affine module sheaves.
Its entire affine-section map is the original mixed candidate following
the existing native affine-pushforward section comparison.
This construction asserts neither differential normalization nor a
factorization of the original rational canonical trace.
\uses{lem:kn-global-trace-mixed-canonical-candidate,lem:cartier-spec-pushforward-sections}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original etale coordinates produce smoothness of the base, and the
original mixed smoothness theorem produces smoothness of the entire
original source. Their canonical sheaves are invertible and hence
quasicoherent. The native affine pushforward preserves quasicoherence.
Compose the existing native pushforward/global-section isomorphism with
the same original mixed candidate. Lift that map through the existing
affine quasicoherent equivalence, using its established Hom preimage.
The equivalence's checked map--preimage identity proves the entire native
section readback. No new scalar action, affine section isomorphism,
quasicoherence certificate or desired section equation is supplied.
\end{proof}
