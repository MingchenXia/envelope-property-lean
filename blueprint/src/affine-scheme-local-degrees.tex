\section{Residue-weighted local degrees at the original scheme points}

\begin{definition}[The actual stalk and residue coefficient actions]
\label{def:fc-affine-scheme-local-coefficients}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.stalkAlgebra, EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.stalkLocalizationAlgEquiv, EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.residueAlgebra, EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.residueAlgebra_algebraMap, EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.residueLocalizationAlgEquiv, EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.pointPrimeEquiv, EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.pointPrimeEquiv_eq}
The original structure morphism $D\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ acts on the same
original stalk $\mathcal O_{D,x}$ by the original global-section map followed
by its actual germ. The same original residue field $\kappa(x)$ receives
the quotient action; its coefficient map is exactly evaluation of the
original scalar. For an affine $D$, its actual stalk is identified with
the localization of its same entire section algebra at its actual point
prime, linearly over the same original field, and the induced residue-field
identification is also linear over that field. The canonical whole affine
scheme isomorphism supplies the bijection of original points and primes.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The true local degree sum on the original points]
\label{thm:fc-affine-scheme-local-degrees}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.local_degree_eq, EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.finrank_sections_eq_sum_points, EnvelopeProperty.AffineSchemeLocalDegrees.normalizedEulerCharacteristic_idealModule_eq_neg_sum_points}
\uses{def:fc-affine-scheme-local-coefficients, thm:fc-finite-closed-ideal-local-degrees}
For an actual affine scheme whose original entire section algebra is finite
over the original field, its section dimension is the sum over its original
points of the true local length multiplied by the actual residue-field degree.
Consequently, for any actual finite closed subscheme $D\hookrightarrow X$ of
an actual projective scheme over the field, the normalized full Euler
characteristic of its same original native ideal is minus that sum over
its original points, with their actual stalks and original residue fields.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual affine germ is a prime localization. Its universal property gives
the whole stalk algebra isomorphism over the original section algebra, then
over the original coefficient field using its actual scalar tower.
The actual local algebra isomorphism induces the genuine residue algebra
isomorphism. Its submodule lattice isomorphism preserves the actual local
length, and its residue linear isomorphism preserves the original residue
dimension. Transport the finite-algebra local-degree sum through the actual
canonical affine scheme point--prime bijection. For the original finite
closed subscheme, affineness follows from its actual finite structure map;
finiteness of its original section algebra follows from original closed
projective geometry. The already proved full ideal Euler formula now gives
the claimed same-point weighted sum without a length, residue, affine-cover,
or degree-preservation certificate.
\end{proof}
