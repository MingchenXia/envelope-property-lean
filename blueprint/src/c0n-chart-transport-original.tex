\section{The actual original family in its original chart coordinates}

\begin{corollary}[The same actual original family supplies the chart estimate]
\label{cor:c0n-original-family-chart-estimate}
\uses{thm:c0n-scheme-estimate-chart-coordinates,lem:c0n-original-family-affine-sections,thm:c0n-actual-original-family-smooth,prop:c0n-original-asymptotic-multiplier-cone}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.MultiplierBaseIdeals.exists_originalMultiplier_chart_order_bound}
\leanok
For the actual original parameter family of $X$, assume only the original
smooth-chart condition and a real lower bound on the original obstacle.
For each original relevant chart and each genuine birational DVR of the
original $A_j[t]$, the preceding fixed-discrepancy estimate holds for all
sections of the same actual original multiplier sequence, in every
multiplier index including zero.  Its sequence ideal orders are taken
under the actual original family chart section isomorphism.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The actual original family is integral, and the original smooth-chart
condition proves smoothness of its absolute structure map.
The original obstacle ideal-sheaf sequence is genuinely graded, and the
obstacle lower bound proves all its positive-index generic stalks nonzero.
The original relevant coordinate supplies the actual nonempty affine
family chart and its actual algebra isomorphism to $A_j[t]$ for the same
absolute structure map.  Apply the preceding theorem to precisely this
scheme, morphism, sequence, chart isomorphism, and valuation.
The whole asymptotic ideal is, by its original definition, the same
original multiplier sequence in the assertion.
\end{proof}
