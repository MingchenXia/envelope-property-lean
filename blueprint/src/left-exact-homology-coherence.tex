\section{Coherence of the canonical homology comparison}
\label{sec:left-exact-homology-coherence}

\begin{lemma}[Agreement with actual native homology preservation]
\label{lem:left-exact-original-homology-preservation-agreement}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LeftExactHomologyCoherence.map_eq_mapHomologyIso}
If a finite-limit-preserving zero-preserving functor actually
preserves left homology of a short complex $S$, the canonical
comparison $H(FS)\to F(H(S))$ is exactly the forward map of
the native homology-preservation isomorphism.
\uses{prop:left-exact-original-homology-comparison}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The native preserved-homology data have cycle object $F(ZS)$
and homology projection equal to $F$ of the original
projection. Their cycle comparison has the same composite
with the monomorphism $F(ZS\to S_2)$ as the canonical
preserved-kernel comparison. Uniqueness at that kernel makes
the cycle comparisons equal. The native and canonical
homology comparisons therefore agree after precomposition
with the original cycle projection. That projection is epic,
so the whole homology maps are equal.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual comparison for a composite functor]
\label{lem:left-exact-original-homology-composition}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LeftExactHomologyCoherence.map_comp}
For finite-limit-preserving zero-preserving functors $F,G$,
the canonical comparison for $G\circ F$ is the canonical
comparison for $G$ on $FS$, followed by $G$ of the canonical
comparison for $F$ on $S$.
\uses{prop:left-exact-original-homology-comparison}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The direct cycle comparison and the composite of the two
cycle comparisons have identical composites with the actual
image under $G\circ F$ of the original cycle inclusion. This
inclusion remains monic because the functors preserve its
kernel fork. Thus the whole cycle comparisons agree. On
cycles, the proposed composite homology map is the first
cycle comparison followed by $G$ of the second cycle
comparison and then $G(F(ZS\to HS))$. The cycle equality
identifies it with the direct canonical cycle formula.
Cancellation of the native epic cycle projection proves
the equality of the entire homology maps.
\end{proof}

These identities retain the native maps and all coefficients.
They permit the actual affine-open section functor to be
factored into original open restriction and affine sections,
without a new matching or exactness hypothesis.
