\section{The original obstacle family has a uniform asymptotic generating twist}

Keep the original smooth projective subvariety, original homogeneous
embedding, original coefficient family over $k[t]$, and original function
$\varphi$. The only vanishing proposition supplied is relative ample
Nadel vanishing. All native smoothness, total relative dimension,
gradedness, nonzero generic stalks, and original polarized-ideal generator
families are proved constructions.

\begin{theorem}[The original family has one total canonical generating buffer]
\label{thm:c0n-original-asymptotic-canonical-generators}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGenerationOriginal.exists_uniform_canonical_generatingSections}
\leanok
\uses{thm:c0n-actual-original-family-smooth,thm:c0n-asymptotic-uniform-canonical-generation,prop:c0n-original-obstacle-scheme-sequence,prop:c0n-original-obstacle-generic-stalk,lem:c0n-original-family-integral,lem:c0n-smooth-integral-pure-dimension,prop:c0n-original-polarization-power-generation}
Assume the stated Nadel input over the original characteristic-zero field.
For the original $X$ with smooth charts, let $Y$ be its actual projective
parameter-family quotient, $p:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ its actual total
field map, and $H$ its actual original hyperplane. Internally there is a
true total smooth dimension $n$, depending only on $X$, such that for every
original $d\geq0$, every original $\varphi$ with a real lower bound, and
all $m\geq0$ the whole original module
\[
 (\omega_{Y/k}\otimes(H^{M+1}\otimes(H^d)^m))\otimes
     \mathcal J_Y(\|\mathfrak a_\bullet(d,\varphi)^m\|)
\]
has finite generating sections. No total dimension, native smoothness,
ideal stabilization or target generators are supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The proved same-family smoothness follows from the original algebraic
smooth-chart condition. Its actual original homogeneous quotient is
integral. Apply the proved smooth integral pure-dimension theorem once
to this same original total field map, before $d,\varphi,m$ are given.
The original family closed immersion gives the literal original
polynomial-base square. For each received $d,\varphi$, original obstacle
multiplication proves gradedness; the original lower-bound monomial
witness proves every positive generic stalk nonzero. The actual
homogeneous finite evaluation producer constructs finite generators of
$\mathfrak a_q\otimes(H^d)^q$ for every $q$. Apply the proved whole
asymptotic canonical uniform-generation theorem with those internally
produced data. Its constituent index is chosen internally and does not
change the original fixed canonical-hyperplane buffer. Its entire ideal
is literally the original scheme multiplier sequence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One original pure hyperplane exponent works before all obstacle indices]
\label{thm:c0n-original-asymptotic-pure-generators}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGenerationOriginal.exists_uniform_pure_power_generatingSections}
\leanok
\uses{thm:c0n-original-asymptotic-canonical-generators,cor:c0n-asymptotic-uniform-hyperplane-generation}
There is a natural $s$, chosen before the original $d,\varphi,m$, such
that every entire original module
\[
 (H^{M+1+s}\otimes(H^d)^m)\otimes
       \mathcal J_Y(\|\mathfrak a_\bullet(d,\varphi)^m\|)
\]
has finite global generating sections. All natural $d,m$, including zero,
are covered. The only function hypothesis is its original real lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual total smooth dimension as above. Apply the proved pure
asymptotic theorem once to the original total and polynomial-base maps
and the same original closed embedding. It chooses $s$ before receiving
any varying line or graded ideal family. Only afterwards receive the
original $d,\varphi$, lower bound and $m$. Supply the actual line $H^d$
and its proved invertibility, and internally prove all original family
properties exactly as in the preceding proof. The resulting finite family
therefore generates precisely the displayed whole original module.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal original projective twist and the same original cone ideal]
\label{thm:c0n-original-asymptotic-literal-twist-generators}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGenerationOriginal.literalTwistIso,
EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGenerationOriginal.exists_uniform_literal_twist_generatingSections,
EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGenerationOriginal.originalMultiplierConeIdeal_eq_coneIdeal}
\leanok
\uses{thm:c0n-original-asymptotic-pure-generators,cor:c0n-original-multiplier-cone-smooth-charts}
A fixed natural $c$, depending only on the original geometry and the Nadel
input, gives actual finite generators of
\[
 j^*\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^M_{k[t]}}(md+c)\otimes
       \mathcal J_Y(\|\mathfrak a_\bullet(d,\varphi)^m\|)
\]
for every original $d,\varphi,m$. The ideal here is the same whole scheme
ideal used by the constructed original multiplier cone ideal; no cone
comparison or expected module isomorphism is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take $c=M+1+s$. The native iterated-power isomorphism identifies
$(H^d)^m$ with $H^{dm}$. The actual tensor-power addition isomorphism
then identifies $H^c\otimes H^{dm}$ with $H^{md+c}$, using the ordinary
natural-number commutativity identities. The proved original hyperplane
power comparison identifies this exact line with the literal original
$j^*\mathcal O(md+c)$. Tensor the actual isomorphism with the identity of
the entire original multiplier ideal module and transport the finite
generating family. Finally unfold the original multiplier cone ideal:
its internally produced smoothness proof is propositionally irrelevant,
and its entire scheme ideal is definitionally the same original scheme
multiplier sequence used here. This proves the actual cone-ideal identity
without a comparison premise. No ambient polynomial lift is claimed.
\end{proof}
