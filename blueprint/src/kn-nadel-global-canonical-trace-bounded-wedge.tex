\section{Every original branch factor in the same mixed top differential}

\begin{lemma}[The entire original coordinate top form has all root factors]
\label{lem:kn-global-trace-native-mixed-bounded-wedge}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.mixedCoordinateTopDifferential_factor}
\leanok
In the same entire original mixed quotient, the original pulled coordinate
top form is divisible by
\[
 \prod_{i\in I} t_i^{N_i-1}.
\]
Thus there is an actual original top form $\eta$ with
\[
 d\tau^{\mathrm{ord}}(dx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_n)
       =\left(\prod_{i\in I}t_i^{N_i-1}\right)\eta.
\]
All original active factors occur simultaneously.  The distinct positions
are those selected by the original SNC injection $\sigma:I\hookrightarrow
\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and every original unit derivative is retained in the
produced factors.  No desired bounded-image assertion is a hypothesis.
\uses{lem:kn-global-trace-native-root-differential-factor}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The native ordinary exterior pullback takes the original coordinate wedge
to the wedge of the differentials of the original coefficient images.
For each active $i$ the preceding root-equation identity expresses the
entry in position $\sigma(i)$ as $t_i^{N_i-1}$ times an actual original
differential vector.  Distinct active rows have distinct positions by
injectivity of $\sigma$.  In each selected position choose this internally
produced factor vector; keep every unselected vector and attach coefficient
one to its position.  The alternating-map scalar formula pulls the product
of all position coefficients outside the same original wedge.  The
selected-position coefficient function is the original root-power function
on the image of the injection and is one off that image.  Restrict its
finite product to that image, reindex through the actual injective map,
and obtain exactly the displayed product over all original active rows.
The resulting wedge of the produced vectors is $\eta$.
\end{proof}
