\section{Producing every divisorial coordinate input internally}

\begin{lemma}[Actual finite DVR coordinates from essential finite type]\label{lem:sc0-actual-finite-dvr-coordinates}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.exists_finite_dvr_coordinates}
\leanok
An actual DVR essentially of finite type over a field has an actual
uniformizer and a finite family of actual elements whose residues form a
transcendence basis of its residue field.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual uniformizer of the DVR. Its residue field is essentially
of finite type over the coefficient field and thus has finite transcendence
degree. Choose a finite transcendence basis in that actual residue field
and lift each of its finitely many elements through the actual surjective
residue map. Index the finite basis by its actual finite cardinal. The
residues of these lifts are exactly the chosen basis, transported by that
finite index bijection, hence form the required transcendence basis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The smooth-family divisorial chart has no coordinate assumptions]\label{thm:sc0-actual-smooth-family-divisorial-chart}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.exists_smooth_family_divisorial_chart}
\leanok
\uses{lem:sc0-actual-finite-dvr-coordinates,thm:sc0-smooth-family-dvr-chart}
For an actual smooth affine domain over a characteristic-zero field,
a finite family of nonzero original ideals, and any actual divisorial DVR
in its actual function field containing the source, the actual uniformizer,
finite residue coordinates, matching coordinate indices, contained literal
Rees leaf, simultaneous original-family monomials and actual original-source
DVR Jacobian factorization are all produced internally.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding coordinate constructor to the actual divisorial DVR.
Use its actual uniformizer and finite lifted residue-transcendence basis in
the actual smooth-family DVR-chart theorem. That theorem produces every
remaining tree, containment, dimension, coordinate and original-source
Jacobian datum, retaining its exact simultaneous monomial identities.
No coordinate choice or determinant ideal is supplied as an additional
mathematical assumption.
\end{proof}
