\section{Internally produced whole free charts for the structure trace}

\begin{definition}[A whole free chart of the original projection]
\label{def:kn-structure-trace-whole-free-chart}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart}
\leanok
A whole free chart of $\pi:Y\to X$ of degree $D$ consists of an
original affine base open $U$, an algebra $B$ over $\mathcal O_X(U)$,
an actual basis indexed by $\{0,\ldots,D-1\}$, and an isomorphism
$\operatorname{Spec}B\simeq\pi^{-1}U$ over the original base.
It contains no trace map or overlap agreement.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The original adapted cover produces its whole free atlas]
\label{thm:kn-structure-trace-produced-whole-free-chart}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.exists_whole_free_chart_at_point,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.producedWholeFreeChart,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.mem_producedWholeFreeChart}
\leanok
Under the original adapted-cover assumptions, put
$D=\prod_a N_a$, retaining all original row/copy factors. Every
original point $x$ has an internally produced whole free chart with
$x\in U$. Choosing these proved witnesses gives a whole free atlas
indexed by the original points of $X$. No chart, basis, trace, or
compatibility certificate is an incoming assumption.
\uses{def:kn-structure-trace-whole-free-chart,lem:kn-nadel-global-original-raw-producer,lem:kn-nadel-whole-native-ratio-model,lem:kn-nadel-whole-cover-degree}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose a closed specialization of $x$ by the original Jacobson
geometry. The original SNC producer supplies a common affine open,
all original line frames, the original local coordinates, and every
unit coefficient there. The empty-row property supplies the absent
copy in each original row. The canonical ratio-chart producer then
gives the actual isomorphism from the whole ratio spectrum onto the
entire inverse image, including its original projection triangle.
The original varying-root monic basis has cardinality $D$. Reindex
this actual basis by the corresponding finite-set equivalence to
obtain the asserted basis indexed by $D$. The produced open contains
the closed specialization and hence $x$. All data and proofs in the
chart are therefore supplied by the original construction. Choose
one witness for each original $x$; its proved membership gives the
covering property of this derived atlas.
\end{proof}
