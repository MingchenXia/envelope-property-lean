\section{Nonnegative mixed intersections from the actual curve-nef condition}

\begin{lemma}[Closed restriction preserves original curve-nefness {\cite[\S1.2]{BJ22}}]
\label{lem:native-curve-nef-closed-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SeshadriAmplenessCriterionNefAmpleMixedIntersections.lineClass_pullback_mem_nefCone_of_closed}
\leanok
\uses{thm:native-numerical-nef-cone}
Let $p:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be projective, and let $N$ be an
invertible line whose original numerical class is nef. For any original
closed immersion $i:Y\hookrightarrow X$, the original $i^*N$ is nef
on $Y$ for its actual structure morphism $p\circ i$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An original closed integral curve $j:C\hookrightarrow Y$ gives the
original closed integral curve $i\circ j:C\hookrightarrow X$.
The coefficient morphism agrees by associativity, and the canonical
whole pullback-composition isomorphism identifies $j^*i^*N$ with
$(i\circ j)^*N$. Invariance of full Euler degree under this isomorphism
identifies the required degree with a nonnegative original nef test.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One nef entry and repeated original ample entries {\cite[\S1.2]{BJ22}}]
\label{thm:native-nef-ample-mixed-euler-nonnegative}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SeshadriAmplenessCriterionNefAmpleMixedIntersections.mixedEulerDifference_nef_ample_nonneg}
\leanok
\uses{lem:native-curve-nef-closed-restriction,thm:native-numerical-nef-cone,thm:line-section-zero-ideal-cartier,lem:seshadri-mixed-euler-coherent-additivity,thm:seshadri-mixed-euler-dimension-vanishing,lem:seshadri-mixed-euler-section-cut,lem:intersection-power-entry,lem:intersection-permutation,thm:seshadri-ample-top-intersection-positive,thm:projective-original-ample-section-prescribed-closed-set,lem:seshadri-original-section-dimension-drop}
Let $p:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be projective and separated, with
$\dim X\leq d+1$. Let $N$ be invertible and nef for the actual
original closed-curve degree tests, let $A$ be ample, and let $F$ be
coherent. The original top mixed Euler difference satisfies
\[
  \Delta_N\Delta_A^d\chi(X,F)\geq0.
\]
The scheme $X$ need not be reduced or integral, and no mixed
intersection positivity is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We prove the assertion by induction on $d$, together with its
integral version with any invertible coefficient $T$.

In every dimension, a finite original coherent prime filtration
reduces the assertion for $F$ to the integral version: the successive
quotients are pushforwards of invertible lines on literal reduced
point closures. Additivity in coherent short exact sequences and the
whole closed-immersion projection formula identify each quotient's
mixed value with that original integral value. Closed immersions
preserve the dimension bound, preserve ampleness by actual closed
pullback, and preserve curve-nefness by the preceding lemma.
Consequently the induction may pass freely from all integral strata
with invertible coefficients to all coherent coefficients on the
original ambient scheme.

For $d=0$, on an integral scheme of dimension at most one, the
onefold difference is independent of the invertible coefficient.
If the scheme has dimension zero it vanishes by the dimension bound
for mixed Euler differences. Otherwise it is an original integral
curve, and its value is the full Euler degree of $N$. This is
nonnegative by the actual curve-nef condition, applied to the
identity closed immersion.

Suppose the assertion holds with $d$ ample entries. On an integral
$X$ of dimension at most $d+2$, choose an actual nonzero section of
a positive power $A^r$; such a section is produced from ampleness,
with the empty prescribed closed subset. Its original zero scheme
$i:D\hookrightarrow X$ is a Cartier cut and has dimension at most
$d+1$, including when $D$ is empty, reducible or nonreduced.
The actual section exact sequence and projection formula give
\[
 r\,\Delta_A\Delta_N\Delta_A^d\chi(X,T)
 =\Delta_{i^*N}\Delta_{i^*A}^d
     \chi\bigl(D,i^*(T\otimes A^r)\bigr).
\]
Here the factor $r$ follows from top-degree tensor-power additivity,
and the order of the first two entries may be interchanged. The
coefficient on $D$ is an actual invertible line, hence coherent.
Restriction preserves the actual nef and ample hypotheses. The
induction hypothesis makes the right side nonnegative. Since
$r>0$, the desired integral mixed value is nonnegative. The prime
filtration argument then gives the original assertion for every
coherent $F$, completing the induction.
\end{proof}
