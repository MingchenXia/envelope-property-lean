\section{The original affine-base coefficient action}

\begin{lemma}[Canonical ring-equivalence transport of the actual complex]
\label{lem:kn-derived-original-scalar-coefficients}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedScalarCoefficients.restrictComplex_X_flat,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedScalarCoefficients.restrictComplex_homology_finite,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedScalarCoefficients.restrictComplex_homology_isZero_iff}
\leanok
Restriction along an actual ring isomorphism preserves flat modules,
finite modules, and finite native homology of an actual cochain
complex.  In particular, restricting the native ordered base-section
complex along the inverse of the actual canonical
$\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}R,\mathcal O)\simeq R$ identifies its
coefficients with the literal original $R$ action.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The new coefficient ring is a free rank-one module over the old
ring, via the actual restriction-of-scalars isomorphism.  Flatness
and finiteness are transitive.  Restriction of scalars along a ring
isomorphism is an actual category equivalence and preserves native
homology.  Its actual homology isomorphism therefore transports
the independently finite native homology.
\end{proof}
