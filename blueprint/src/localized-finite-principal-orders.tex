\section{True native principal orders over the entire original local finite inverse}

\begin{definition}[Actual original field structure of the whole local inverse]
\label{def:fc-local-finite-original-field-structure}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LocalizedFinitePrincipalOrders.fieldStructure}
The actual local inverse $Y_S$ retains its literal original structure
$Y_S\to\operatorname{Spec}S\to\operatorname{Spec}k$.  Every original field
coefficient is the original $S$-coefficient followed by the original
structure-section map, internally derived by native
$\Gamma\operatorname{Spec}$ naturality.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The literal original composite section map and native
$\Gamma\operatorname{Spec}$ coefficient triangle derive the actual scalar
tower; no field action or matching datum is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All native original local inverse principal orders]
\label{thm:fc-local-finite-all-native-principal-orders}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LocalizedFinitePrincipalOrders.principalQuotient_finite, EnvelopeProperty.LocalizedFinitePrincipalOrders.finsum_principal_orders}
For the original finite dominant integral curve over the original domain
$A$, an actual coefficient localization $S$ that is a local Noetherian
one-dimensional $k$-algebra with actual finite residue degree, and any
original $a\ne0$, its entire original inverse principal quotient is
internally finite over this actual original field structure, and
\[
 \sum_{z\in Y_S}\operatorname{ord}_z(a)[\kappa(z):k]
 =\operatorname{rank}_A\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y)
   \operatorname{ord}_S(a)[\kappa(S):k].
\]
\uses{def:fc-local-finite-original-field-structure,thm:fc-localized-finite-whole-integrality-rank,thm:fc-affine-principal-all-native-orders,thm:fc-nonflat-local-principal-field-degree}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The entire actual local inverse is internally integral, affine and locally
Noetherian, and its actual preimmersion into the original curve bounds its
whole dimension.  Actual finite dominance gives surjectivity and hence
injectivity of its original coefficient map.  The genuine nonflat local
principal-length theorem derives finite quotient dimension and its true
rank--order--residue-degree formula with the actual original scalar tower.
The genuine whole affine principal quotient theorem identifies that same
original dimension with the sum of true native weighted scheme orders over
all original inverse points.  The actual original localization preserves
the original generic rank.  No flatness or normality of the original finite
map, finite quotient, local point, residue match or desired degree formula
is assumed.  Actual projective geometry must still produce the local-base
residue finiteness and the global finite-curve degree consumer; this generic
local theorem does not add them as paper hypotheses.
\end{proof}
