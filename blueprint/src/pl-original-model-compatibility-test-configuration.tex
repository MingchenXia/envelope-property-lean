\section{The original normalized PL model as an actual test configuration}

\begin{theorem}[The same normalized simultaneous model is a test configuration]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-native-test-configuration}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeTestConfiguration}
\leanok
\uses{def:native-test-configuration-data,thm:pl-original-normalized-simultaneous-gm-action,thm:pl-original-normalized-fibre-one-variety,thm:pl-original-normalized-parameter-equivariance,local:original-normalized-pl-model-flat-any-field,thm:pl-normalized-polynomial-projectivity}
Let $X$ be the original projectively normal variety, and let $I,J$ be the
original coefficient-unit-invariant homogeneous ideals containing powers
of the parameter and having finite Gauss ideal logarithms. The actual
normalization $N$ of their original simultaneous blowup, with its unchanged
original projection $\pi:N\to\mathbb A^1$, is a test configuration of $X$.
Its action is the constructed actual normalized action, and its fibre-one
isomorphism is the actual original fibre inclusion followed by the same
original projection to $X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original simultaneous blowup is integral because it contains a lift of
the original finite-log Gauss point, and its normalization is integral.
The normalized projection is dominant over the original parameter line:
the original positive-radius Gauss point gives dominance of the blowup
projection, and the original normalization projection is surjective.
An integral scheme dominant over the spectrum of the Dedekind domain $k[t]$
is flat there. The original variety product is projective over $k[t]$,
the simultaneous blowup is projective, and normalization is finite, so the
same composite is projective. The constructed categorical normalized
multiplicative-group action has its full unit and associativity laws.
The original coefficient-family projection triangle identifies the
parameter morphism with scaling by the universal Laurent unit, so the
original blowup parameter map is equivariant for the actual standard
parameter action. Whole equivariance of normalization gives the same
property for $\pi$. Finally, the preceding fibre-one theorem gives its
actual isomorphism over $k$ with $X$. These are precisely the defining
test-configuration data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual test-configuration open projection is the original one]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-native-test-configuration-original-projection}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.unitOpenOriginalProjection_eq_of_invariant,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeTestConfiguration_originalProjection}
\leanok
\uses{thm:pl-original-normalized-native-test-configuration,thm:pl-original-normalized-projection-action-invariant}
The whole product trivialization of the noncentral open derived from this
actual test configuration has original-variety projection equal to the
noncentral-open inclusion followed by the unchanged original normalized
model projection $q:N\to X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $\ell:U\to G\times N$ for the actual action lift of the open inclusion,
and $v:U\to N_1$ for its actual fibre-one projection. The action-fibre
diagrams give $a\ell=i_U$ and $p\ell=i_1v$. The actual fibre-one
identification is $qi_1$. Hence the original-variety projection of the
derived trivialization is $qi_1v=qp\ell=qa\ell=qi_U$, where the middle
equality is the proved invariance $qa=qp$ on the entire group product.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The original polarized line supplies the actual native line inputs]
\label{cor:pl-original-normalized-native-test-configuration-line-inputs}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTwistedRatioLine_isInvertible,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeTestConfigurationLineComparison,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeTestConfigurationFibreOneLineIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeTestConfigurationUnitOpenLineIso}
\leanok
\uses{thm:pl-original-normalized-native-test-configuration,thm:pl-original-normalized-twisted-ratio-group-comparison,thm:pl-original-normalized-fibre-one-line}
For this same test configuration the original line
$q^*\mathcal O_X(n)\otimes(I_N\otimes J_N^\vee)$ is invertible and has an
explicit comparison of its pullbacks along the entire original action and
projection, and an explicit fibre-one identification with the original
$\mathcal O_X(n)$. Thus the original native line constructions accept this
same model and this same line without an additional assumed comparison.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Both actual normalized ideal factors are Cartier, so their tensor-dual
ratio and its tensor product with the original twist pullback are
invertible. The whole group comparison is the preceding tensor comparison.
The actual fibre inclusion lies in the entire noncentral open, where
both parameter-power ideals are the unit ideal. Pulling back the constructed
ratio trivialization therefore gives the original fibre-one line
identification. Its target projection is the actual fibre-one isomorphism
by the defining projection formula of that isomorphism.
\end{proof}
