\begin{lemma}[The original lifted component has its actual Cartier kernel]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-paired-open-component-kernel-cartier}
\uses{def:alteration-output,def:snc,lem:actual-cartier-component-dimension,thm:snc-regular-component-cartier,thm:cartier-dominant-pullback,def:variety}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussPairedSurfaceParameters.closed_component_kernel_isEffectiveCartierDivisor}
\leanok
Let $d$ be any actual altered pair over a field, with original open
$e:R\hookrightarrow\overline R$ and original SNC datum $D$. For any
native support component $E$ put $A=V(I_E)$, where
$I_E=\mathcal I_{\overline R}(E)$ is its literal vanishing ideal,
and let $\iota_E:A\hookrightarrow\overline R$ be the original inclusion.
For any actual closed $j:A\hookrightarrow R$ with
$j\gg e=\iota_E$, the actual original ideal sheaf $\ker j$ is
an effective Cartier divisor on $R$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original square with arrows $j,\operatorname{id}_A,e,\iota_E$
is a pullback. Indeed the right triangle commutes, the identity is an
isomorphism, and $e$ is a monomorphism: equality after $e$ uniquely
identifies any two candidate lifts. Equivalently its actual comparison
to $R\times_{\overline R}A$ is an isomorphism, and its composition
with the original first projection is the same $j$.
The original pullback-kernel theorem and invariance of the kernel
under precomposition by this actual isomorphism give the whole ideal
identity
\[
 \ker j=(\ker\iota_E).\operatorname{comap}(e)
       =I_E.\operatorname{comap}(e).
\]
This is the entire original ideal, not merely its radical or support.
The actual compactification is a field variety, hence locally
Noetherian. The SNC component Cartier theorem applies to this same
$I_E$. The original source and compactification are integral, and
the nonempty open immersion $e$ is dominant: its open image is a
nonempty open of an irreducible space, hence dense. The proved
Cartier-preservation theorem along dominant integral morphisms
therefore makes the actual pullback $I_E.\operatorname{comap}(e)$
Cartier. The whole ideal identity proves the assertion. No generator,
Cartier pullback certificate or field embedding was supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Original regular curve stalks are DVRs and have an actual closed point]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-paired-curve-stalks-dvr}
\uses{def:regular-scheme,thm:fc-curve-proper-support-points}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussPairedSurfaceParameters.regular_curve_stalks_and_closed_point}
\leanok
Let $A$ be an actual integral regular scheme with $\dim A=1$.
There is an actual nongeneric closed point $x_0$ of $A$.
At every actual nongeneric $x\in A$, the SAME original local ring
$Q=\mathcal O_{A,x}$ is a discrete valuation ring.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $A$ had only its generic point, all its points would be equal.
The order of irreducible closed subsets is the specialization order
of the points of a sober space. That one-point order has dimension
at most zero, contradicting $\dim A=1$. Thus an actual point different
from the generic point exists. The original integral-curve topology
makes every such point closed and gives its actual coheight one.
The scheme stalk dimension formula consequently gives
$\dim\mathcal O_{A,x}=1$. Regularity applies to this literal stalk,
so it is a Noetherian local ring. It is a domain because $A$ is
integral. Regularity identifies its cotangent-space dimension over
its OWN residue field with its ring dimension, hence with one.
The Noetherian-local-domain DVR equivalence therefore makes this
same ring a DVR. In particular it is not a field and its actual
maximal ideal has a nonzero uniformizer. No equality of its residue
field with the coefficient field is required.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Cartier equations and lifted original uniformizers give the actual parameters]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-paired-original-cartier-parameters}
\uses{lem:pl-gauss-paired-curve-stalks-dvr,def:fc-closed-immersion-principal-stalk-quotient,thm:fc-closed-immersion-stalk-order-length,alggeom:original-stalk-coefficient-map,alggeom:original-stalk-coefficient-triangle,alggeom:original-stalk-coefficient-injective,lem:scheme-stalk-coefficients-naturality}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussPairedSurfaceParameters.exists_original_cartier_parameters}
\leanok
Let $R,A$ be actual integral schemes, let $A$ be regular of dimension
one, and let $j:A\hookrightarrow R$ be an actual closed immersion
whose actual kernel ideal is Cartier. Suppose the ORIGINAL proper
dominant $b_R:R\to B$ and actual $q_A:A\to\operatorname{Spec}k$
satisfy the whole central triangle
$j\gg b_R=q_A\gg\epsilon$, with $\epsilon$ the literal evaluation
at zero morphism. For every nongeneric actual $x\in A$, set
\[
 S=\mathcal O_{R,j(x)},\quad Q=\mathcal O_{A,x},\quad
 \psi=j_x^*:S\to Q,\quad
 \rho=\operatorname{coefficientMap}(b_R,j(x)),\quad t_S=\rho(t).
\]
There are actual $\pi,z\in S$ with
\[
 \ker\psi=(\pi),\qquad \mathfrak m_S=(\pi,z),\qquad
 \operatorname{Prime}(\pi),\qquad \pi\nmid z,\qquad
 t_S\ne0,\qquad \pi\mid t_S.
\]
The actual $Q$ is a DVR, and an actual ring isomorphism
$S/(\pi)\simeq Q$ sends EVERY original quotient class $[a]$ to
$\psi(a)$. In this intermediate general lemma the Cartier hypothesis
is an ordinary geometric premise; the final paired theorem derives
it internally by the preceding whole-kernel producer.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At the actual point $j(x)$, choose an actual affine Cartier
neighborhood $U$ and its actual regular equation $f$ for
$(\ker j)(U)$. The open $U$ is nonempty, its ring is a domain, and
$f\ne0$. Define $\pi$ to be its ORIGINAL germ in $S$.
The actual germ of an integral scheme is injective, so $\pi\ne0$.
The proved original closed-immersion stalk-kernel theorem computes
\[
 \ker\psi=(\ker j)(U)S=(f)S=(\pi).
\]
The literal original $\psi$ is surjective because $j$ is closed.
The target $Q$ is a domain, so this kernel is prime. Since
$\pi\ne0$, the principal-ideal prime criterion makes $\pi$ a prime
ELEMENT of the SAME $S$. The existing original principal-stalk-
quotient isomorphism gives $S/(\pi)\simeq Q$ and its stated formula
on every representative; no comparison map is supplied.

The preceding curve-stalk result gives the actual DVR $Q$. Choose
an actual irreducible uniformizer $\bar z$ of $Q$ with
$\mathfrak m_Q=(\bar z)$ and $\bar z\ne0$. Surjectivity of the
actual $\psi$ lifts it to an actual $z\in S$. Locality of the same
stalk map gives $\psi^{-1}(\mathfrak m_Q)=\mathfrak m_S$.
The original surjective ideal map/comap identity therefore gives
\[
 \mathfrak m_S=\psi^{-1}(\psi((z)))=(z)+\ker\psi=(\pi,z).
\]
If $\pi$ divided $z$, then $z\in\ker\psi$, contradicting
$\psi(z)=\bar z\ne0$. This proves the required nondivisibility
without choosing or replacing either local ring.

The original coefficient map into every stalk is injective for
an integral source, a reduced affine base and a dominant
quasi-compact morphism. These hypotheses hold for the SAME $b_R$
(properness gives quasi-compactness). Since $t\ne0$ in $k[t]$,
its actual germ $t_S$ is nonzero. The whole original stalk
coefficient triangle gives
$\psi\circ\rho=\operatorname{coefficientMap}(j\gg b_R,x)$.
The given whole central triangle identifies this map with the
composition of evaluation at zero and the original coefficient
map of $q_A$. To see this without any desired scalar certificate,
compose the two original maps from the spectrum of $Q$ to their
base schemes; the actual fromSpecStalk structure triangles and
functoriality of Spec identify them, and faithfulness of Spec
identifies the whole ring maps. Evaluating at $t$ gives
$\psi(t_S)=0$. Thus $t_S\in(\pi)$, equivalently $\pi\mid t_S$.
The quotient and all coefficient formulas concern the original
maps. No assertion $t_S=u\pi^e$ is made, so crossings and
multiplicities are retained.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original paired surface internally supplies all actual local parameters]
\label{thm:pl-gauss-paired-surface-actual-parameters}

\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussPairedSurfaceGeometry.exists_regular_paired_surface_parameters}
\leanok

dimension two, its original closed central integral projective curve
of dimension one, and the actual entire altered pair, there exist
internally selected $E,A,j,g$ as in the proved paired-geometry

$A$ the entire preceding original parameter and quotient conclusion
holds. No selected component, local curve, Cartier certificate,
parameter or field/norm comparison is an input to this theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the frozen original paired-geometry producer to the same
original data and actual pair. It internally constructs the
original support component, the actual closed $j$, the true finite
dominant $g$, the regular source of dimension two, and the regular
projective curve of dimension one. Retain every actual whole

the Cartier property of the ACTUAL $\ker j$ internally. The curve
lemma supplies an actual nongeneric closed point and the original

original parameter producer with $q_A=g\gg q_1$ and the supplied
whole central triangle. Its ordinary hypotheses follow from the
constructed geometric properties, and its outputs are the literal

such $x$ proves the assertion. The full pair itself is ordinary
geometric output of the proved unconditional theorem used by the
later original-model wrapper, and no existence theorem is assumed
in place of that proved result.
\end{proof}
