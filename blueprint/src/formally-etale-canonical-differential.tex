\label{sc0-formally-etale-canonical:entire-map}

original $\Omega_{R/k}$, the SAME ENTIRE original scalar-extended
canonical differential map is a genuine isomorphism in EVERY
exterior degree. No field, finite-presentation, target basis,
matching formula or desired-inverse hypothesis is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine original source basis gives the actual full exterior
scalar-extension equivalence. Formal etaleness gives the actual
Kaehler differential base-change equivalence for the SAME original
algebra map. Apply the whole exterior equivalence to that genuine
differential equivalence and compose the two true equivalences.
On every coefficient-one tensor of an original decomposable
exterior form the composite is the wedge of the SAME original
Kaehler differential images, by the actual original base-change
formulas. The pre-existing genuine canonical differential map has
exactly this same all-wedge value. Original decomposable forms span
the ENTIRE source exterior module; genuine linear extensionality
therefore proves equality on every coefficient-one tensor. Actual
scalar linearity and whole tensor extensionality prove equality of
the ENTIRE original maps. The constructed genuine inverse hence
proves the asserted bijectivity of the SAME original differential,
rather than of an unconnected basis comparison.
\end{proof}

Original localizations are formally etale, so this is the algebraic
producer for the full original localization differential. Retaining
that same map through the actual native global-section and sheaf
comparisons is the next geometric obligation. The whole rounded
