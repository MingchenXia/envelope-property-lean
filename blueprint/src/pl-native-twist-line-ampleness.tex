\section{Ampleness of the projective twists of a projective subvariety}

Let $\iota:X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^M_k$ be a projective subvariety and write
$\mathcal O_X(n)=\iota^*\mathcal O_j(n)$ for the restriction of the $n$-th twist
attached to the coordinate $x_j$. These lines are the fibre-one lines of the
twisted ratio line and of the tautological twist on the normalized
simultaneous PL model; their ampleness is the polarization input of the
nef-to-FS approximation.

\begin{theorem}[Positive twists of a projective subvariety are ample]
\label{thm:pl-native-twist-line-ample}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeTwistLineSheaf_succ_isVeryAmple,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeTwistLineSheaf_isAmple,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeTwistLineSheaf_tensorPower_isAmple}
\leanok
\uses{thm:central-embedding-original-mixed-positive-twist-very-ample,lem:fc-original-ample-closed-restriction,lem:fc-original-very-ample-is-ample,thm:fc-original-ample-line-positive-powers,thm:pl-auxiliary-native-ampleness-base-change}
For every coordinate $j$ and every $n>0$, the line $\mathcal O_X(n)$ is very
ample over $k$, hence ample, and every positive tensor power
$\mathcal O_X(n)^{\otimes b}$, $b>0$, is ample over $k$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
On $\mathbf P^M_k$ the twist $\mathcal O_j(d+1)$ is very ample for every
$d\geq0$: its global sections of degree $d+1$ define a closed immersion into a
projective space whose $\mathcal O(1)$ pulls back to $\mathcal O_j(d+1)$ (the
Veronese embedding). Very ampleness is preserved
by pullback along the closed immersion $\iota$, the structure morphism of
$\mathbf P^M_k$ being the native one, so $\mathcal O_X(d+1)$ is very ample over
$k$. A very ample line is ample. Finally $X$ is integral, and positive tensor
powers of an ample line on an integral scheme are ample.
\end{proof}
