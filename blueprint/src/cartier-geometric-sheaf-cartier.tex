\section{The original global geometric Cartier sheaf isomorphism}

Let $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be smooth of relative dimension $n$
in prime characteristic $p$, let $Y=X^{(p)}$, and let $K$ be the
original geometric relative Frobenius de Rham complex. All source
sheaves below are the original exterior cotangent sheaves on $Y$.

\begin{definition}[The Cartier isomorphism on each actual principal basis open]
\label{def:cartier-geometric-principal-basis-isomorphism}
\uses{thm:cartier-geometric-local-global-homology-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.basisIso}
\leanok
For every actual principal basis open $W\subset Y$, there is an
isomorphism between actual exterior cotangent-sheaf sections on
$W$ and actual sections of the same original global $H^q(K)$ on
$W$. It is the original local Cartier isomorphism on the actual
relative inverse-image affine open, transported by the proved
equality of its twisted open with $W$.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The existing original basis supplies affineness of
$U=F_{X/k}^{-1}W$ and an honest etale tuple of length $n$ for its
original structural section algebra. The proved original open
identity gives $U^{(p)}=W$. Apply the established local Cartier
isomorphism to this very affine open and tuple, then transport the
entire original module isomorphism along that equality of opens.
The transport preserves the actual structural section-ring action.
No chosen comparison or Cartier matching data are supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entire principal-basis Cartier maps agree under restriction]
\label{thm:cartier-geometric-principal-basis-naturality}
\uses{def:cartier-geometric-principal-basis-isomorphism,thm:cartier-geometric-global-homology-cartier-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.basisIso_restrict}
\leanok
Every inclusion between original principal basis opens commutes
with the entire original Cartier isomorphisms, on every actual
source section and in every degree.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pull back the original inclusion by relative Frobenius. The two
resulting source opens are the very affine opens used to define
the two local Cartier maps. Their honest tuples are supplied by
the original basis construction. Apply the established entire
local Cartier restriction theorem to this inclusion. Transport
both endpoint maps along their proved twisted-open equalities.
After these equalities are eliminated, the transported inclusion
and the original one are parallel arrows in the category of
opens; they are equal because those hom sets are subsingletons.
The transported theorem is therefore the asserted entire square.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Original global Cartier maps in both directions]
\label{def:cartier-geometric-global-sheaf-maps}
\uses{thm:cartier-geometric-principal-basis-naturality}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.inverseCartierHom,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.cartierHom,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.inverseCartierHom_app_basis,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.cartierHom_app_basis}
\leanok
There are actual original module-sheaf morphisms in both directions
between $\Omega^q_{Y/k}$ and $H^q(K)$. On every original principal
basis open their components are respectively the original basis
Cartier isomorphism and its inverse.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The preceding theorem makes the original forward basis components
an additive natural transformation. Each is linear over the actual
original section ring, since it is the forward map of the derived
module isomorphism. For inverse components, apply the smaller
inverse to the forward restriction identity evaluated at the
larger inverse image of an arbitrary target section. The two
inverse identities reduce this to inverse restriction naturality.
These inverse components are likewise actual linear maps. Apply
the existing genuine module-sheaf basis extension separately to
the two natural transformations. Its component identity retains
exactly the original basis maps on every section.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual global geometric Cartier isomorphism]
\label{thm:cartier-geometric-global-sheaf-isomorphism}
\uses{def:cartier-geometric-global-sheaf-maps}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.inverseCartierIso}
\leanok
In every degree there is an actual original module-sheaf
isomorphism
\[\Omega^q_{Y/k}\simeq H^q(K).\]
Its forward and inverse maps are the original two extended maps.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Evaluate either composite on an arbitrary original basis open and
section. The two proved extension component formulas identify it
with the corresponding composite of the original basis
isomorphism and its inverse. That composite fixes every section.
The existing basis determination theorem for actual module-sheaf
morphisms therefore identifies each global composite with the
identity morphism. These two identities give the asserted global
isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original affine-chart and unit formulas for global Cartier]
\label{thm:cartier-geometric-global-original-chart-formula}
\uses{thm:cartier-geometric-global-sheaf-isomorphism,thm:cartier-geometric-local-global-homology-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.inverseCartierIso_app_twistedOpen,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricSheafCartier.inverseCartierIso_unit}
\leanok
On every original affine coordinate open, the entire global
Cartier component is exactly the original local Cartier map into
sections of the same global homology sheaf. Every original source
unit form therefore retains its exact original native Cartier,
whole-complex transfer, and affine homology-section composition.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The twisted coordinate open is itself in the original principal
basis, using the unit principal section. Its relative inverse
image is the original affine open. Transport of the selected
basis tuple to that same open gives an honest tuple there. The
proved entire honest-tuple independence theorem identifies its
local Cartier map with the map for any original honest tuple on
that open. The basis extension formula now identifies the entire
global component with this very local map. Apply the established
original-unit formula for the local map to obtain the exact
asserted unit image under the global isomorphism.
\end{proof}
