\section{The original canonical differential on the whole generic fiber}

\begin{theorem}[Invertibility of the same original generic differential]
\label{thm:kn-canonical-original-generic-differential-iso}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTraceGenericBranch.original_generic_differential_isIso}
\leanok
For the same original normalized adapted cover $\pi:Y\to X$, write
$\gamma:\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_{X,\eta}\to X$ for the actual
original generic point and $p:Y\times_X\operatorname{Spec}
\mathcal O_{X,\eta}\to Y$ for the entire actual generic fiber projection.
The pullback along $p$ of the original top exterior differential
$\delta:\pi^*\omega_X\to\omega_Y$ is an isomorphism.
\uses{thm:kn-canonical-original-etale-generic-neighborhood,thm:kn-canonical-smooth-etale}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Internally produce the actual open immersion $j:\operatorname{Spec}S\to X$
and the actual factorization $t j=\gamma$ on which the entire original
cover is etale. The restricted original base map $j f$ remains smooth of
the original dimension. The native top exterior differentials of $j$
and the whole etale projection $q:Y\times_X\operatorname{Spec}S\to
\operatorname{Spec}S$ are isomorphisms. Native differential composition,
the actual cartesian triangle, and the open immersion of this entire
inverse image into $Y$ prove that the pullback of the original $\delta$
to this entire inverse image is an isomorphism.

The actual original generic-point factorization produces the native
map from the whole generic fiber to this entire inverse image. Its
projection is exactly $p$. Pullback preserves the proved isomorphism;
native pullback composition and transport along this exact projection
identity give the claimed original generic differential. At no point
is the function field assumed smooth of finite presentation over the
ground field, and no component of the whole generic fiber is chosen.
\end{proof}
