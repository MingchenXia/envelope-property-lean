\begin{lemma}[Actual tensor products of original whole section morphisms]
\label{lem:fc-original-tensor-section-homs}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorSectionHoms.tensorSectionHom, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorSectionHoms.tensorSectionHom_sectionHom, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorSectionHoms.tensorSectionHom_comp, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorSectionHoms.tensorSectionHom_isIso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorSectionHoms.tensorSectionHom_precomp_scalars, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeTensorSectionHoms.pullback_tensorSectionHom}
\leanok
Let $M,N$ be original sheaves of modules on a scheme $X$. The actual
whole section morphisms $a:\mathcal O_X\to M$ and
$b:\mathcal O_X\to N$ have a tensor section obtained by the canonical
inverse multiplication $\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal O_X\otimes\mathcal O_X$
followed by $a\otimes b$. It agrees with the actual pure tensor of their
whole global sections, is an isomorphism when both original section
morphisms are isomorphisms, and is natural under postcomposition and
arbitrary original native pullback. Multiplying the two original
sections by original whole scalars $r,s$ multiplies their tensor section
by the actual original product $rs$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All constructions use the actual monoidal tensor and the canonical
structure-line multiplication. Tensor functoriality gives the
postcomposition identity. The inverse structure multiplication and the
tensor of two isomorphisms are isomorphisms. Evaluation on the original
unit global section gives the actual pure tensor section, because
structure-line multiplication sends $1\otimes1$ to $1$ and the actual
tensor morphism maps pure tensors to the tensors of their images.
Tensoring multiplication by $r$ and by $s$ and applying the canonical
structure multiplication gives multiplication by $rs$; conjugating
through this same multiplication proves the scalar formula.
For pullback, the genuine strong monoidal comparison is natural on both
original sections and preserves the monoidal unit and its multiplication.
The proved equality between the native structure-line pullback and
this monoidal unit comparison identifies the pulled structure
multiplication with the original one on the source. Its inverse and
monoidal tensor naturality then give the asserted whole section identity.
\end{proof}
