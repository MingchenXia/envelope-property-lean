\begin{proposition}[Original global form ratios]
\label{prop:whole-original-global-form-fraction-ratios}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.BlowupHomogeneousTautologicalLine.liftedFormSection_pullback_ratio_on_originalGlobalFractionChart}
\leanok
\uses{prop:whole-original-form-scalar-transport,prop:whole-original-form-fraction-ratios,prop:whole-original-global-blowup-fraction-basis}
Let $F,G$ be original homogeneous forms of the same degree in the original
ideal. On a subordinate affine open with domain ring $A$, suppose its original
coefficient $g$ for $G$ is nonzero, and put $S=A[I/g]$. Let $v:\operatorname{Spec}S\to Z$
be the original native fraction inclusion followed by the original native affine
chart inclusion into the same global blowup. On the entire fraction spectrum,
the old global lifts satisfy $v^*\beta_F=(f/g)v^*\beta_G$ with the original native ratio.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The native fraction structure triangle and the native global chart structure
triangle identify $v\mu$ with the original spectrum map followed by the affine
map to $X$. The same original homogeneous frame identifies the two included
form sections with their original coefficients in $S$. The native identity
$\varphi(f)=(f/g)\varphi(g)$ gives their scalar relation. Tensoring the ideal
inclusion with the original invertible homogeneous line is monic, so cancellation
gives this relation for the composite ideal-valued lifts. The composite map $v$
is the composite of the two actual open immersions. Canonical open transport
therefore gives the asserted relation for the old global lifts themselves.
All memberships and both triangles have been derived from the original data.
\end{proof}
