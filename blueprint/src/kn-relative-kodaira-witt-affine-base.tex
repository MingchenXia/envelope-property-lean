\section{Relative Kodaira over an affine base from the proved Witt decomposition}

The relative Kodaira results use projectivity over the original affine base
and smoothness over the original field. Projectivity over that field is not
assumed. The absolute canonical module and the actual relatively ample line
are retained throughout.

\begin{lemma}[Native Frobenius cohomology over the original affine base]
\label{lem:kn-relative-frobenius-native-cohomology}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeFrobeniusCohomology.relativePushforward_subsingleton_H_iff, EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeFrobeniusCohomology.twistPullback_subsingleton_H_iff}
\uses{lem:kn-frobenius-twist-module-unit,lem:fc-projective-finite-ordered-affine-cover,thm:affine-pushforward-cohomology-vanishing}
\leanok
Let $X/k$ be a scheme over a perfect field of characteristic $p$ and let
$X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ be actually projective, for any ring $R$.
For every original quasicoherent module, the actual relative Frobenius
pushforward preserves and reflects vanishing of positive sheaf cohomology.
The corresponding native pullback under the perfect-field twist has the same
property. No properness over $k$ or cohomology comparison is an input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Projectivity over the original affine base supplies separatedness and the
actual finite ordered affine cover. The twist projection is a scheme
isomorphism because the field is perfect. Its native adjunction unit compares
the original module with the pushforward of its actual pullback. Relative
Frobenius is affine. Apply the proved affine pushforward Cech comparison on
this finite cover and its pullback to the twist.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Relative ample Kodaira on an actual Witt special fibre]
\label{thm:kn-relative-ample-kodaira-witt-special-fibre}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativePositiveHodgeVanishing.kodaira_of_wittLift}
\uses{lem:kn-relative-frobenius-native-cohomology,cor:kn-finite-family-serre,lem:kn-exterior-finite-type,lem:kn-frobenius-diagonal-iteration,lem:kn-proved-global-witt-di,thm:kn-hypercohomology-zero-diagonal,lem:kn-amplified-cartier-bounded,lem:kn-di-amplified-target}
\leanok
For a smooth separated dimension-$n$ Witt-two lift over a perfect field of
characteristic $p>n$, let $X$ be its actual special fibre. For any Noetherian
ring $R$, actual projective map $X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$, and actual relatively
ample line $L$, every positive cohomology group of $\omega_{X/k}\otimes L$
vanishes. The global DI decomposition is supplied by its proved construction,
with no caller DI or vanishing certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The already proved coherent Serre theorem over arbitrary Noetherian affine
bases gives eventual vanishing for all absolute exterior cotangent modules
twisted by sufficiently high powers of $L$. The actual native Frobenius
cohomology comparisons above replace every previous appeal to projectivity
over the field. The original connection-power complex, global proved DI
isomorphism, and bounded hypercohomology diagonal give the same descent from
$p m$ to $m$ on every total degree above $n$. Iterate to the eventual Serre
endpoint and take top exterior degree and first power.
\end{proof}

For characteristic zero over a finite-type affine base, the arithmetic model
retains the projective map to the modeled affine base, the smooth total space
over its coefficient domain,
and the original line. Canonical cohomology specialization applies the
following comparison to its actual Witt point and special-fibre isomorphism.
Together these give relative Kodaira vanishing over the original affine base.

\begin{theorem}[The original relative line under an actual Witt special-fibre isomorphism]
\label{thm:kn-relative-kodaira-original-witt-comparison}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeWittKodairaComparison.kodaira_of_specialFibreIso}
\uses{thm:kn-relative-ample-kodaira-witt-special-fibre,def:kn-inverse-pullback-line,lem:kn-scheme-iso-positive-cohomology,lem:kn-scheme-iso-ample-pullback}
\leanok
Let $k$ be a perfect field of prime characteristic $p$, let
$\widetilde X\to\operatorname{Spec}W_2(k)$ be smooth of relative dimension $n$
and separated, and assume $n<p$. Let $R$ be any Noetherian commutative ring,
let $f_Y:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ and $g_Y:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}R$, and suppose
there is an isomorphism $e:Y\simeq\widetilde X_k$ whose composite with the
special fibre's field structure is $f_Y$. For every line $L$ relatively ample
for $g_Y$, the positive cohomology of $\omega_{Y/k}\otimes L$ vanishes.
The original map $g_Y$ and original line are retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pull the original line and affine-base map through the inverse isomorphism.
Its actual positive-power embedding gives relative ampleness there. Apply the
certificate-free relative Witt theorem. Quasicoherence and the native finite
affine cover compare cohomology under the actual scheme isomorphism using the
projective affine-base map. The original field triangle gives the native
canonical pullback isomorphism, and the actual inverse line pullbacks cancel.
\end{proof}
