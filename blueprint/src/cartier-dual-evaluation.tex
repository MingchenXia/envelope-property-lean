\section{The actual canonical dual pairing and invertible tensor cancellation}

\begin{lemma}[The original evaluation pairing is an isomorphism]
\label{lem:cartier-actual-canonical-dual-evaluation-isomorphism}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.ev_isIso}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.evaluationIso}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.evaluationIso_hom}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.monoidalEvaluationIso}
\leanok
For every genuine invertible module sheaf $\mathcal L$ on an arbitrary
original scheme $X$, its ACTUAL canonical evaluation morphism
$\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal L^\vee\to\mathcal O_X$ is an isomorphism.
The resulting isomorphism has this very original evaluation as its
homomorphism. It also identifies the genuine coherent tensor with
its actual monoidal unit.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take the actual original open trivializing cover of $\mathcal L$.
On every original open contained in one of its members, the original
trivialization identifies a section $m$ with its coefficient $E(m)$.
If $\psi$ is its coefficient functional, the actual evaluation on the
presheaf tensor has inverse $c\mapsto E^{-1}(c)\otimes\psi$.
For a pure tensor $m\otimes\varphi$, writing
$\varphi=s\psi$ gives $E^{-1}(\varphi(m))=sm$ and hence the same
pure tensor after applying this inverse. Additivity extends the
identity to every tensor; evaluation of the displayed inverse is
$c$ by the actual trivialization identities. Thus the genuine
presheaf evaluation is bijective on every original open in the
trivializing cover. Sheafification inverts that actual locally
bijective morphism. Its composite with the genuine sheafification
counit is exactly the ORIGINAL sheaf evaluation. Both factors are
isomorphisms, proving the assertion for that actual morphism.
Finally compose with the true coherent-to-explicit tensor comparison
and the genuine structure-module-to-monoidal-unit comparison.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Tensor by the actual original line sheaf is fully faithful]
\label{lem:cartier-actual-line-tensor-fully-faithful}
\uses{lem:cartier-actual-canonical-dual-evaluation-isomorphism}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.IsInvertible.tensorRightFullyFaithful}
\leanok
For every original invertible module sheaf $\mathcal L$, the genuine
functor $\mathcal M\mapsto\mathcal M\otimes\mathcal L$ on ALL original
module sheaves is fully faithful. This supplies actual unique
preimages of morphisms and isomorphisms under that functor.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By the actual evaluation isomorphism, tensoring successively with
$\mathcal L$ and its actual dual is naturally isomorphic to the
identity functor: use the original tensor associator, tensor the
actual inverse evaluation with each original object, and use its
right unitor. This natural isomorphism makes tensor by
$\mathcal L$ faithful, since equality after its subsequent dual tensor
is equality under a functor naturally isomorphic to the identity.
The same argument applies to the actual invertible dual and gives
faithfulness of tensor by that dual. The composite of the two tensor
functors is full because it is naturally isomorphic to the identity.
To lift a morphism between two $\mathcal L$ tensors, tensor it with
the dual and lift that image through the full composite functor.
Faithfulness of dual tensor shows that the image of this lift under
$\mathcal L$ tensor is the original morphism. This proves fullness;
faithfulness makes the lift unique. Applying this unique lifting to
an isomorphism and its actual inverse gives inverse original maps,
since faithfulness detects both inverse identities.
\end{proof}
