\section{Every original chart has the same whole generic fiber}

\begin{lemma}[The original chart coefficient field map retains the original generic point]
\label{lem:kn-structure-trace-chart-generic-base}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.genericBaseMap,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.genericBaseMap_over_original}
\leanok
For a nonempty whole free chart $U$ on integral $X$, the actual germ
map $\mathcal O_X(U)\to K$ gives a spectrum map into its base coefficient
spectrum whose composite with the original base chart map is precisely
the original $\gamma:\operatorname{Spec}K\to X$.
\uses{def:kn-structure-trace-whole-free-chart}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The generic point lies in every nonempty open of an integral scheme.
The morphism to $X$ obtained by taking the spectrum of its germ on the
given affine chart and then its actual affine chart inclusion is the
definition of the generic-stalk morphism computed in that neighborhood.
The independence of the original stalk morphism from the affine
neighborhood therefore identifies this whole composite with $\gamma$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual whole chart base change is the same original generic fiber]
\label{lem:kn-structure-trace-chart-generic-iso}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.genericFiberIso,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.genericFiberIso_fst,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.genericFiberIso_snd}
\leanok
Base-changing the actual entire chart spectrum over its original base
coefficient spectrum to $K$ is canonically isomorphic to the entire
original fiber $Y\times_X\operatorname{Spec}K$. The isomorphism preserves
both actual projections, including the original map into $Y$ and the
original $K$-structure map. No component or comparison identity is an
incoming hypothesis.
\uses{lem:kn-structure-trace-chart-generic-base,lem:kn-structure-trace-chart-cartesian}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Paste the actual coefficient-field pullback square with the proved
original cartesian square of the whole chart. The bottom composite is
the same original $\gamma$ by the preceding lemma, so the pasted square
is a pullback of the original projection and $\gamma$. Its universal
isomorphism with their actual chosen pullback gives the asserted whole
fiber isomorphism. The two projection identities are the defining
projection identities of this universal pullback isomorphism.
\end{proof}
