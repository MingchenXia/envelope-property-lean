\section{Actual coordinate wedges in the original affine canonical map}

\begin{lemma}[Native canonical pullback on every original coordinate wedge]
\label{lem:kn-global-trace-native-coordinate-wedge}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.affineAlgebraCanonicalPullbackMap,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.affineCanonicalCoordinateSection,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.affineAlgebraCanonicalPullbackMap_coordinate_wedge}
\leanok
Let $A,B$ be original algebras over the field $k$, let $f_A$ be smooth
of pure relative dimension $n$, and let $\varphi:A\to B$ be an original
$k$-algebra map.  The native original canonical differential, transported
through its produced field-structure square, defines
\[
 d_\varphi:\Gamma(\omega_A)\to
       \operatorname{Res}_{\varphi}\Gamma(\omega_B).
\]
For every original tuple $a_1,\ldots,a_n\in A$, its value on the original
sheafification unit of $da_1\wedge\cdots\wedge da_n$ is the original unit
of $d\varphi(a_1)\wedge\cdots\wedge d\varphi(a_n)$.
No etaleness, chosen differential comparison, or desired wedge formula
is an input.
\uses{lem:kn-global-trace-native-branch-differential,
lem:kn-global-trace-native-ordinary-unit}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Compose the actual native canonical pullback with the transport along the
proved structural square for $\varphi$.  The actual affine exterior map carries each wedge of ordinary
differentials to the wedge of its native presheaf differentials.  Its
sheafification unit is the named native coordinate section.  The proved
actual sheaf exterior differential sends this section to the sheaf wedge
of the actual cotangent images, so no comparison of a target frame is
needed.  Each cotangent image is the differential of its actual
regular-function image.  Naturality of the
original spectrum--sections isomorphism identifies that image with the
original coefficient $\varphi(a)$.  Finally, transport through the
structural square preserves the differential of each unchanged coefficient;
eliminating the equality of the structural maps and using the global
section functor's identity law proves this statement.  The internally
cached transport is proved equal to the literal original global-section
map of that same field-structure equality.  Taking wedges and their same sheafification units gives
the assertion on the original tuple.
\end{proof}
