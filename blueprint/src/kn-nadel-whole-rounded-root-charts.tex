\section{The whole rounded ideal on the actual normalized charts}

\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:kn-nadel-whole-rounded-root-chart-generator}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalAdaptedRoots.canonicalRoundedMonomial,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalAdaptedRoots.canonicalRoundedIdeal_top,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalAdaptedRoots.canonicalRoundedIdeal_comap_eq_principal}
\leanok
\uses{def:kn-nadel-whole-rounded-root-line}
Let $C:\operatorname{Spec}B\simeq\pi^{-1}U$ be the same actual
canonical normalized ratio chart, with original absent auxiliary heads
$j_0(i)$. Write $t_i$ for the original distinguished root indexed by
$(i,j_0(i))$ in the entire ratio algebra $B$. Then the inverse image of
the entire original ideal $J(a)=\prod_iJ_i^{a_i}$ is exactly the
principal ideal sheaf generated by $\prod_i t_i^{a_i}$. Its ideal of
global sections is generated by that same element under the canonical
identification $B=\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}B,\mathcal O)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The whole row-ideal chart theorem identifies the inverse image of each
original $J_i$, including all original row roots, with the principal
ideal generated by its distinguished head root $t_i$. Ideal inverse
image preserves products and powers, and on an affine scheme the ideal
of global sections preserves those same operations. Thus the ideal of
global sections of the inverse image of $J(a)$ is
$\prod_i(t_i)^{a_i}=(\prod_i t_i^{a_i})$.
For the last equality, the empty product is $(1)$; adjoining one factor
uses $(s)(t)=(st)$ and $(s)^a=(s^a)$, proving the finite product identity
by induction. Affine ideal sheaves are determined by their global
section ideals, which proves the equality of whole ideal sheaves.
Every component of the original ratio algebra is retained throughout.
\end{proof}
