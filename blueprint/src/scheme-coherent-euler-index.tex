\section{Intrinsic coherent Euler index on the actual projective scheme}

\begin{definition}[Actual field cohomology and Euler index]
\label{def:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-index}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.baseScalar, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldSections, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldCech, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldSectionsIso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldCechIso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldCechCoverIso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.finiteAffineCover, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.finiteAffineOpens, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldHOne, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.eulerIndex, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.normalizedEulerIndex, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldHOneSheafIso}
For an actual quasi-compact separated scheme $C$ over a field $k$, use
its finite affine subcover and the actual base-linear Cech complex of
an original module $M$. Restrict the scalar action along the canonical
isomorphism $k\simeq\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}k,\mathcal O)$.
Write $H^1_k(C,M)$ for its degree-one homology and $H^0_k(C,M)$ for
its actual original global sections, with this original scalar action.
The coherent Euler index is
$e(M)=\dim_k H^0_k(C,M)-\dim_k H^1_k(C,M)$, and its normalized index
is $e(M)-e(\mathcal O_C)$. On a proper curve this is the usual
cohomological definition of the degree of a line bundle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finiteness and intrinsic comparison]
\label{thm:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-index}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldSections_finite, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldCech_succ_finite, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.finiteAffineCover_finite, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.finiteAffineOpens_cover, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.finiteAffineOpens_isAffine, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldHOne_finite, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.fieldHOne_finrank_eq, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.eulerIndex_eq_of_iso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.normalizedEulerIndex_eq_of_iso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.normalizedEulerIndex_unit, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeCoherentEulerIndex.eulerIndex_eq_on_cover}
\uses{def:fc-scheme-coherent-euler-index}
If the actual structure morphism admits a projective factorization and
$M$ is finite type and quasicoherent, both vector spaces above are
finite dimensional. The degree-one space compares to the whole
original sheaf cohomology group $H^1(C,M)$. Every other finite affine
cover gives the same dimensions. Isomorphic original modules have
the same Euler index and normalized index; the normalized index of
the original structure line is zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true finite affine cover is obtained from quasi-compactness. Its
actual affine intersections are acyclic for quasicoherent coefficients.
The native two-cover double complex supplies an isomorphism of
base-linear positive cohomology groups between any two such covers;
its exact rows and columns follow from the actual affine intersection
acyclicity. Restricting these isomorphisms along the actual base-field
isomorphism preserves the entire coefficient action. The genuine
affine-Cech comparison identifies the underlying additive group with
the whole original sheaf $H^1$. Genuine projective coherent cohomology
finiteness applies to the original global sections and original
Cech homology. The canonical base-field isomorphism then makes both
spaces finite dimensional over $k$. Original module isomorphisms
induce linear isomorphisms on sections and Cech homology, hence preserve
their dimensions and both indices. The structure-line assertion is
integer subtraction of the same index.
\end{proof}
