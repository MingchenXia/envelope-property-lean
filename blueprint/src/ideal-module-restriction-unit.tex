\section{Recovering the whole geometric unit ideal from actual restriction}

\begin{lemma}[A genuinely invertible restricted inclusion gives the whole unit ideal]
\label{lem:ideal-module-restriction-unit}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.IdealModuleRestrictionUnit.comap_eq_top_of_isIso_restrict}
Let $I$ be an actual ideal sheaf on any scheme $X$, and let $U$
be any original open. If the actual original ideal-module inclusion
$I\hookrightarrow\mathcal O_X$ becomes an isomorphism after
native restriction to $U$, then the whole geometric ideal
$I\mathcal O_U$ is the unit ideal.
\uses{scheme:existing-ideal-module-affine-sections}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On every original affine open $V$ of $U$, the genuinely invertible
restricted inclusion is surjective on sections. Lift the original
unit section through this very inclusion. Its preimage is an actual
section of the original ideal module on the original image of $V$,
and its value is exactly one. The genuine affine ideal-module
membership criterion therefore places one in the original affine
ideal, making it the whole ring. The actual open-immersion formula
for geometric ideal pullback identifies the ideal on $V$ with
the inverse image of this whole ideal under the original section
ring isomorphism. It too is the whole ring. This proves the entire
ideal-sheaf equality, with no finiteness, nonempty or affine-$U$
assumption.
\end{proof}
