\section{Characteristic-zero envelopes with Nadel vanishing as input}
\label{sec:c0n-char0-after-nadel}

Keep the original characteristic-zero field, original smooth scheme,
original whole multiplier ideals and original projective polarization.
The temporary assumption in this section is Nadel vanishing.  Uniform
generation, the original graded base-ideal comparisons and the envelope
property are deductions from that input, culminating in
Theorem~\ref{thm:c0n-original-smooth-envelope-from-nadel}.  In particular the existing
\nolinkurl{SmoothEnvelopeCharZero} conclusion is not an input.
The source approximation uses ideals on $X\times\mathbb A^1$; its
vanishing input therefore has the relative form over an affine base.
See \cite[Theorem~5.20]{BJ22} and \cite[Theorem~3.1]{FHX}.

\begin{definition}[The original canonical multiplier module]
\label{def:c0n-original-nadel-module}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.nadelTwistedModule}
\leanok
For the original $n$-smooth integral $k$-scheme $Y$, original ideal $I$
nonzero at the generic point, coefficient $c\in\mathbb Q$ and line $A$,
put
\[
 M(Y,I,c,A)=\bigl(\omega_{Y/k}\otimes A\bigr)
                 \otimes\mathcal J_Y(I^c).
\]
Here $\omega_{Y/k}=\bigwedge^n\Omega^1_{Y/k}$ and
$\mathcal J_Y(I^c)$ is the original whole all-divisorial-DVR multiplier
ideal, with its original ideal module.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The explicit relative ample Nadel input]
\label{def:c0n-relative-ample-nadel-input}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.RelativeAmpleNadelVanishingInput}
\leanok
For a characteristic-zero field $k$, the input asserts the following
vanishing statement.  Let $R$ be a finite-type $k$-algebra,
$\pi:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ projective, and let its original composite
to $\operatorname{Spec}k$ be $n$-smooth and integral.  Suppose $A,B$ are
invertible, $I$ is generically nonzero, $I\otimes B$ is finitely globally
generated, and $c\geq0$ is rational.  If $d>0$, $t\in\mathbb N$,
$dc=t$, and there is an actual $\pi$-ample line $C$ with
\[
 A^{\otimes d}\simeq C\otimes B^{\otimes t},
\]
then $H^q(Y,M(Y,I,c,A))=0$ for every $q>0$.
The displayed identity expresses that the actual rational line $A-cB$
is relatively ample.  This definition specifies a proposition; no
instance or proof of that proposition is declared here.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[One twist generates every original ordinary multiplier ideal]
\label{prop:c0n-ordinary-uniform-generation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.MultiplierUniformGeneration.exists_generatingSections}
\leanok
\uses{prop:c0n-uniform-canonical-generators}
Assume the preceding Nadel input.  Let the original $Y$ have a closed
embedding in $\mathbb P^N_R$ with actual hyperplane line $H$.  Let $L$
be invertible, $p>0$, and let the original generically nonzero $I$ satisfy
that $I\otimes L^p$ is finitely globally generated.  For every $m\geq0$,
\[
 \omega_{Y/k}\otimes H^{N+1}\otimes L^m
          \otimes\mathcal J_Y(I^{m/p})
\]
is finitely globally generated.  The same fixed canonical-hyperplane
twist works for all $m$ and all such $I,p$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Call the displayed actual coherent module $M_m$.  For each
$1\leq j\leq N$, its original negative hyperplane twist is
\[
 M_m\otimes H^{-j}
  \simeq\omega_{Y/k}\otimes
       (H^{N+1-j}\otimes L^m)\otimes\mathcal J_Y(I^{m/p}).
\]
Use the Nadel input with $A=H^{N+1-j}\otimes L^m$, $B=L^p$,
$c=m/p$, $d=p$ and $t=m$.  Positivity of $p$ gives $dc=t$ exactly.
The actual tensor-power laws construct
\[
 A^p\simeq H^{p(N+1-j)}\otimes (L^p)^m.
\]
Its first factor is genuinely relatively ample: $H$ is the original
very ample hyperplane pullback, and $p(N+1-j)>0$.  The input therefore
kills the actual $j$th cohomology of the original negative twist.
These are exactly the finitely many regularity groups for the original
closed projective embedding.  Its proved regularity-to-generation
theorem constructs a finite original generating family of $M_m$.
For $N=0$ the regularity family is empty and that same theorem applies.
The original tensor comparisons, finite presentation and all multiplier
ideals are retained; no generation or vanishing conclusion is supplied
as a hypothesis beyond the specified Nadel input.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The same twist generates the whole asymptotic multiplier ideal]
\label{prop:c0n-asymptotic-uniform-generation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.AsymptoticMultiplierUniformGeneration.exists_generatingSections}
\leanok
\uses{thm:c0n-asymptotic-uniform-canonical-generation}
Let $I_\bullet$ be the original graded ideal sequence, generically
nonzero at every positive index, and suppose every actual
$I_p\otimes L^p$ is finitely globally generated.  Under the same Nadel
input, for every $m\geq0$ the entire original module
\[
 \omega_{Y/k}\otimes H^{N+1}\otimes L^m
        \otimes\mathcal J_Y(\|I_\bullet^m\|)
\]
is finitely globally generated, with twist independent of $m$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The actual projective source is quasi-compact and locally Noetherian.
The proved original divisible-index stabilization theorem, using the
actual gradedness, produces a positive index $p$ at which the entire
original asymptotic multiplier ideal is exactly
$\mathcal J_Y(I_p^{m/p})$.  Apply the preceding proposition to this
same original $I_p,L,p,m$ and its genuine generating family.  The
produced equality of whole ideals identifies their actual modules and
transports that finite family to the displayed original module.
The selected index may depend on $m$; the twist $\omega_{Y/k}H^{N+1}$
does not.  Nonvanishing and gradedness for the paper's actual base-ideal sequence
are established in the original-family construction.
\uses{prop:c0n-ordinary-uniform-generation}
\end{proof}

The final characteristic-zero deduction uses the actual
scheme-to-homogeneous-cone ideal and section comparisons, the original
nonzero Veronese sequence, and the original divisorial envelope
approximation.  The already proved subadditivity and asymptotic valuative
estimates are reused.  These geometric and analytic producers are proved in the following
sections and assembled in Theorem~\ref{thm:c0n-original-smooth-envelope-from-nadel};
they are not part of the vanishing input above.
