\section{The rounded summand from the actual canonical differential and trace}
\label{sec:kn-nadel-trace-retraction}

Let $Y$ be the original smooth integral projective scheme of dimension $n$
over a characteristic-zero field.  Use its actual simultaneous root cover
$\tau:W\to Y$, with all factors retained, as in
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-nadel-global-normalized-geometric-properties}.
For the original SNC components $D_i$, the prescribed positive integers
$m_i$ are the root degrees.  The $n+1$ original auxiliary divisors in row
$i$ have empty common intersection.

\begin{lemma}[Whole root ideals and their exact powers]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-whole-root-ideals}
\notready
For each row, let $s_{ij}$ be the actual normalized pullback of the original
raw root section, a section of the same actual pulled-back line $\tau^*H_i$.
The whole ideal
\[
 J_i=\sum_{j=0}^{n} I(s_{ij})
\]
is invertible and satisfies the equality of entire original ideals
$J_i^{m_i}=I_{D_i}\mathcal O_W$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On an original adapted affine neighbourhood choose a row index $j_0$
whose auxiliary divisor is absent.  In the actual whole ratio normalization
model, put $s=s_{ij_0}$ in the chosen original line frame.  Its equation is
$s^{m_i}=d_i u_0$, where $d_i$ generates the original $D_i$ ideal and
$u_0$ is a unit.  Every other original root coefficient is $s v_j$, and
the distinguished coefficient is exactly $s$.  Thus the entire ideal sum
is $(s)$, including every unit-root factor of the normalization algebra.
Since the cover is flat and $d_i$ is a nonzerodivisor, $s^{m_i}$ and hence
$s$ are nonzerodivisors.  This proves invertibility on the actual open
cover.  Raising $(s)$ to its $m_i$-th power gives $(d_i)$, since $u_0$ is
a unit.  The original section zero ideals and ideal powers commute with
restriction, so both assertions glue to the stated whole ideals.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-global-normalized-geometric-properties,thm:kn-nadel-ample-original-component-branch-family,lem:kn-adapted-whole-normalization}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Regular canonical trace on the entire original cover]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-whole-canonical-trace}
\notready
Construct the actual $\mathcal O_Y$-linear map
\[
 T_\tau:\tau_*\omega_W\longrightarrow\omega_Y
\]
such that, on every original affine open, for every original section $b$
of $\tau_*\mathcal O_W$ and every original top form $w$,
\[
 T_\tau\bigl(b\,d\tau(w)\bigr)=\operatorname{Tr}_{\tau_*\mathcal O_W/\mathcal O_Y}(b)w.
 \tag{T}
\]
In particular $T_\tau d\tau=D\,\mathrm{id}$, where the constant positive
degree $D$ counts all the original root factors.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Work on an actual adapted affine base $\operatorname{Spec}A$, with whole
finite free algebra $B$.  Adjoin roots of the original unit factors by a
finite free faithfully flat etale algebra $S/A$.  On the whole algebra
$C=S\otimes_A B$, the actual root rescaling identifies the ramified
factors with independent powers of the actual coordinate parameters;
all remaining unit-root factors are retained as entire etale algebras.
The monic power-basis canonical counits and their actual compositions
give a regular $S$-linear map on the top forms of this whole $C$, with
formula (T) for $C/S$.

Use the actual canonical sheaves in this comparison.  Along an etale map
the entire canonical pullback is an isomorphism: this follows on an
actual standard-smooth affine chart from its internally produced
differential basis, the etale cotangent comparison, and the wedge basis;
the actual source chart cover detects the whole isomorphism.  Affine
quasicoherent pullback on global sections therefore identifies the
source and target of the map over $S$ with the actual scalar extensions
of $\Gamma(\omega_B)$ and $\Gamma(\omega_A)$.  No global basis of
$\Omega^1_{B/k}$ is needed.

On both literal $S\otimes_A S$ pullbacks, the two maps satisfy (T).
After inverting the nonzero original branch product, the original
finite map is etale and its differential is invertible, so (T)
determines the entire map there.  The branch product remains a
nonzerodivisor after the flat extensions, and the target canonical
module is invertible.  The two regular maps are consequently equal
before localization.  Faithfully flat descent now produces the actual
map over $A$.  The same uniqueness on actual base-chart overlaps makes
these maps glue on $Y$.

The trace used over $S$ is the trace of $C/S$.  Its rank is the original
rank $D$ of $B/A$, so no degree of the auxiliary faithfully flat unit
cover enters the formula.  Taking $b=1$ in (T) proves the asserted
normalization.  All components of $B$ and all original etale factors
are included in its ordinary algebra trace.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-global-normalized-geometric-properties,thm:kn-canonical-smooth-etale,thm:kn-nadel-canonical-unit-root-counit,thm:kn-nadel-canonical-power-unit-composition,thm:kn-nadel-cross-chart-canonical-counit,thm:kn-nadel-flat-tensor-trace-uniqueness}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual bounded ramification factorization]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-bounded-ramification-factor}
\notready
Let $0\leq a_i<m_i$ be original integers and put $J=\prod_i J_i^{a_i}$.
Produce a map
\[
 d\tau_J:\tau^*\omega_Y\longrightarrow\omega_W\otimes J
\]
whose composite with the entire actual ideal inclusion into $\omega_W$
is the original canonical differential $d\tau$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The preceding root-ideal lemma makes $J$ an invertible ideal.  On the
actual faithfully flat etale unit cover of each adapted chart, the
original $J_i$ becomes the whole principal ideal generated by its
distinguished parameter root $t_i$.  In the actual pure power and unit
model the entire top differential has Jacobian
\[
 \prod_{\ell\ {
m ramified}} N_\ell t_\ell^{N_\ell-1}
\]
in the actual top-form frames, with the etale canonical identifications
on the unit layers.  For the original row $i$, this contains
$t_i^{m_i-1}$, hence $t_i^{a_i}$.  It is therefore divisible by the
entire pulled-back ideal $J$.  The actual differential functoriality
under the root rescaling and the etale base change retains the
derivatives of every original variable unit.

The cokernel of the original inclusion $\omega_W\otimes J\to\omega_W$
thus kills the original differential after this faithfully flat
pullback.  Faithfulness forces that cokernel composite to vanish
already on the original chart.  The kernel factorization supplies
$d\tau_J$.  The inclusion is monic, so these factorizations are unique
and agree on all actual chart overlaps.  They glue to the stated
factorization of the original whole morphism.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-whole-root-ideals,thm:kn-canonical-smooth-etale}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual rounded trace retraction]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-trace-rounded-retraction}
\notready
Write the original rational divisor as
$Q=M-\sum_i(a_i/m_i)D_i$, where $M=\lceil Q\rceil$ and
$0\leq a_i<m_i$.  The actual line
$N=\tau^*\mathcal O_Y(M)\otimes J$ is $\mathcal O_W(\tau^*Q)$.
Construct actual inclusion and projection maps exhibiting
$\omega_Y\otimes\mathcal O_Y(M)$ as a retract of
$\tau_*(\omega_W\otimes N)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Adjoint $d\tau_J$ to obtain
$u:\omega_Y\to\tau_*(\omega_W\otimes J)$.  Compose the pushed whole
ideal inclusion with $T_\tau$ to obtain
$v:\tau_*(\omega_W\otimes J)\to\omega_Y$.
The original adjunction triangle, the proved factorization of $d\tau$,
and (T) with $b=1$ give $vu=D\,\mathrm{id}$.  The positive integer $D$
is invertible over the original characteristic-zero field.  Thus $u$
and $D^{-1}v$ have identity composite on the actual original canonical
module.

Tensor these two maps by the actual line $\mathcal O_Y(M)$.  The genuine
line projection formula and the native tensor associativity and symmetry
identify their target with
$\tau_*(\omega_W\otimes\tau^*\mathcal O_Y(M)\otimes J)$.
This is the stated module because $J_i^{m_i}=I_{D_i}\mathcal O_W$.
Functoriality of tensoring and the actual projection formula preserve
the identity composite.  These are the required original retraction
maps; their construction introduces no group action or retraction
premise.
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-whole-root-ideals,lem:kn-nadel-whole-canonical-trace,lem:kn-nadel-bounded-ramification-factor}
\end{proof}
