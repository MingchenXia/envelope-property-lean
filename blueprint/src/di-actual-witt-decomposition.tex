\section{The actual Witt-lift Deligne--Illusie decomposition}
\label{sec:di-actual-witt-decomposition}

This chapter constructs the actual finite-source map and its truncated
and bounded Deligne--Illusie decompositions. The native good-truncation,
finite-source homology, affine Cartier, and local quasi-isomorphism
criteria are applied throughout to the original geometric objects.

\begin{definition}[The actual finite-source cochain morphism]
\label{def:di-actual-witt-finite-source-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.sourceNat,EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.componentMap,EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.finiteCochainMap,EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.componentMap_zero,EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.componentMap_succ,EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.summand_finiteCochainMap}
\leanok
\uses{thm:di-higher-exterior-witt-maps,def:di-actual-zero-summand}
Under the actual Witt-lift hypotheses of the theorem below, let
$S_p=\bigoplus_{q<p}\Omega^q_{Y/k}[-q]$ in natural degrees and let
$T$ be the original total \v Cech complex of the original $K$ on the
internally constructed cover. There is the actual cochain map
$\Phi:S_p\to T$ whose original summand at zero is the constructed zero
Cartier map and whose original summand at $q=r+1$ is the internally
constructed higher cochain map. Its composite with every original
coproduct inclusion is exactly that component map.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Base change of the given original smooth pure-dimensional lift gives the
same dimension on its actual special fibre. Use the original finite
source of the existing dimension-source API. Case analysis on its
original natural degree selects the degree-zero map or the actual positive map.
Each is already a cochain map, so the original coproduct universal
property gives $\Phi$ with the stated entire component identities.
There is no caller-supplied map family or compatibility certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The actual good-truncated candidate]
\label{def:di-actual-witt-truncated-source-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.truncatedCochainMap,EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.truncatedCochainMap_inclusion}
\leanok
\uses{def:di-actual-witt-finite-source-map,lem:di-finite-source-good-truncation}
The same map canonically gives
$\overline\Phi:S_p\to\tau_{<p}T$ after extension by zero to integer
degrees. Composing with the original good-truncation inclusion is exactly
the extension of the entire original $\Phi$.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Apply the already constructed finite-source truncation factorization to
the actual map of the preceding definition. Its original source
truncation isomorphism and the native truncation naturality square give
the claimed equality. This constructs the actual outgoing-kernel map;
it does not yet assert its quasi-isomorphism property.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The zero-degree range condition and the original summand criterion]
\label{lem:di-actual-witt-zero-range}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.componentMap_zero_quasiIsoAt,EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.finiteCochainMap_quasiIsoAt_iff,EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.finiteCochainMap_quasiIsoAt_zero}
\leanok
\uses{def:di-actual-witt-finite-source-map,lem:di-actual-zero-cartier-class}
The actual $\Phi$ is a quasi-isomorphism in degree zero. For every
original $q<p$, its quasi-isomorphism property in degree $q$ is
equivalent to that of its actual single-degree component.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The degree-zero component before augmentation induces the entire global
inverse Cartier map, hence an isomorphism on zero homology. The original
total augmentation is a quasi-isomorphism on the actual cover, so their
actual composite is a quasi-isomorphism in degree zero. The existing
finite distinct-degree summand criterion, applied to the original
inclusion of each summand, gives the stated equivalence. Its zero case
proves the assertion for the entire finite-source map. This reuses the
existing graded-source criterion and does not prove any positive-degree
Cartier comparison by assumption.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual truncated DI decomposition]
\label{thm:di-actual-witt-truncated-decomposition}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.truncatedDerivedIso}
\leanok
\uses{thm:di-actual-witt-truncated-map-quasi-isomorphism,thm:di-actual-witt-higher-sheaf-cartier,lem:di-actual-zero-cartier-class}
Let $k$ be a perfect field of characteristic $p>0$. Let
$\widetilde f:\widetilde X\to\operatorname{Spec}W_2(k)$ be an actual
smooth separated lift of pure relative dimension $n$, and let $X$ be its
original special fibre. Put $Y=X^{(p)}$, with its original structure map
to $\operatorname{Spec}k$, and let $F:X\to Y$ be relative Frobenius.
There is an isomorphism in the derived category of $\mathcal O_Y$-modules
\[
 \bigoplus_{0\le q<p}\Omega^q_{Y/k}[-q]
   \;\simeq\; \tau_{<p}F_*\Omega^\bullet_{X/k}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Use the affine cover and actual Frobenius lifts produced from
$\widetilde f$. The genuine divided differences give $\xi_i$ and
$H_{ij}$ on the original sheaves. Their proved closedness, difference
and triple equations make $(\xi,-H)$ a closed degree-one element of the
original total \v Cech complex $T$ of $K=F_*\Omega^\bullet_{X/k}$.
The original restriction identities are used here before applying the
product universal properties; no compatibility of arbitrary selected
maps is assumed.

For $1\le q<p$, iterate the actual signed total cup product on this
degree-one map, apply normalized antisymmetrization with the invertible
scalar $q!$, and descend through the original exterior-power universal
property. The actual total Leibniz formula proves the resulting map
$\varphi_q:\Omega^q_{Y/k}[-q]\to T$ is a cochain map. In degree zero
use the map of Definition~\ref{def:di-actual-zero-summand}. The original
finite coproduct gives a cochain map $\Phi:S_p\to T$, where
$S_p=\bigoplus_{q<p}\Omega^q_{Y/k}[-q]$.

Extend the natural-degree complexes by zero to integer degrees. The
original source is already strictly supported below $p$. Consequently
Lemma~\ref{lem:di-finite-source-good-truncation} gives the canonical
factorization $\overline\Phi:S_p\to\tau_{<p}T$, preserving the entire
original map after composition with the truncation inclusion.

On a member of the actual cover, compose with the original local
evaluation $T\to K$. Its zero-row formula sends $\varphi_q$ to the
normalized product of the same local $\xi_i$ representatives. The
whole-map leading-Cartier comparison identifies its class with the
existing native inverse Cartier map, and the canonical affine homology
comparison identifies this with the restriction of the actual global
Cartier sheaf isomorphism. For $q=0$ this is
Lemma~\ref{lem:di-actual-zero-cartier-class}. Restriction preserves the
original homology, and the local evaluation is already a
quasi-isomorphism. Therefore every summand induces an isomorphism in its
own degree. The existing finite graded-source criterion and the genuine
good-truncation vanishing outside $0\le q<p$ show that
$\overline\Phi$ is a quasi-isomorphism. Equivalently one can apply the
existing local-to-global quasi-isomorphism criterion on this same cover.

The original total augmentation $K\to T$ and its good truncation are
already quasi-isomorphisms. Inverting the latter in the derived category
and composing with the image of $\overline\Phi$ gives the asserted
isomorphism. The local Cartier classes used to prove the
quasi-isomorphism are the whole original sheaf-map identities of
Theorem~\ref{thm:di-actual-witt-higher-sheaf-cartier}, with degree zero
given by Lemma~\ref{lem:di-actual-zero-cartier-class}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Removing the cutoff below the characteristic]
\label{cor:di-actual-witt-full-decomposition}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.ActualWittDecomposition.fullDerivedIso}
\leanok
\uses{thm:di-actual-witt-truncated-decomposition,lem:di-reused-native-dimension-source,lem:di-reused-native-dimension-support}
Under the same hypotheses, if $n<p$, the original finite source and
untruncated original pushed de Rham complex satisfy
\[
 \bigoplus_{q=0}^{n}\Omega^q_{Y/k}[-q]
   \;\simeq\;F_*\Omega^\bullet_{X/k}
\]
in the same derived category.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The existing geometric dimension theorem makes both original form
sheaves zero in degrees above $n$. Its original source-inclusion
isomorphism identifies the source through degree $n$ with $S_p$.
Its original good-truncation inclusion is an isomorphism on $K$ because
$n<p$. Compose these two existing isomorphisms with the truncated
decomposition just constructed. This step uses the declared smooth
pure-dimension hypothesis, and does not replace an arbitrary smooth
arithmetic model by an unproved constant-dimension model.
\end{proof}
