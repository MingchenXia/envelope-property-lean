\begin{theorem}[Actual geometric ideal pullback on sections and stalks]
\label{scheme:genuine-ideal-pullback-stalk}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.comap_ideal_top_of_isAffine_native,
AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.comap_open_ideal_top_map,
AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.comap_ideal_of_affine_opens,
AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.paperStalkIdeal_comap}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:ideal-sheaf-data-stalk}
For arbitrary actual schemes and a genuine morphism $g:X\to Y$, use
Mathlib's actual ideal-sheaf-data pullback, defined by the true pullback
of its associated closed subscheme. On any compatible actual affine
opens $U\subset X$, $V\subset Y$ with $U\subset g^{-1}(V)$, its ideal is
\[
 (g^*I)(U)=I(V)\,\Gamma(X,U)
\]
under the actual local section homomorphism. At every actual point $x$,
its intrinsic stalk ideal is the genuine image
\[
 (g^*I)_x=\operatorname{Ideal.map}(g_x^\#)(I_{g(x)}).
\]
Neither equality is assumed as a certificate. No noetherian, finite
generation, separatedness or properness hypothesis is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First take actual affine source and target schemes. The true geometric
pullback is left adjoint to the true geometric ideal pushforward, and the
latter has the independently proved affine section formula given by
ideal contraction under the actual global section homomorphism. For the
candidate section ideal obtained by actual ideal extension, its ordinary
extension/contraction adjunction shows the original target ideal lies in
its geometric pushforward. The geometric adjunction therefore bounds the
actual pullback ideal above by this candidate. Conversely its genuine
adjunction unit bounds the target ideal by contraction of the actual
pullback ideal; ordinary ideal extension gives the reverse inequality.
Thus the genuine geometric pullback has exactly the asserted affine
section-ring ideal image.

For actual affine opens in arbitrary schemes, real open-immersion
pullback and the true global-section isomorphisms of restricted schemes
transport the original affine ideals. Geometric pullback composition and
the whole restricted-morphism inclusion triangle identify the two
restricted pullback data. Apply the affine result. The actual formula
for the restricted morphism's global-section map is the original local
section homomorphism conjugated by the two genuine open section
isomorphisms. Cancelling these isomorphisms gives the asserted formula
on the original section rings.

Finally choose an actual affine neighborhood of $g(x)$ and an actual
affine refinement of its preimage containing $x$. The previously proved
intrinsic stalk ideals equal these true affine germ images. Apply the
section formula and actual ideal-image composition. Genuine section
restriction, germ naturality and the whole scheme stalk-map identity
identify the two resulting homomorphisms, proving the full stalk formula.
The proof starts with Mathlib's geometric closed-subscheme pullback;
it never replaces it by a newly stipulated affine ideal map.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual model ideal norms and logarithms commute with pullback]
\label{scheme:proper-model-ideal-pullback}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.sectionSpectrum_comap_appLE,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelIdealNorm_comap,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelIdealLog_comap}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:genuine-ideal-pullback-stalk,scheme:native-model-ideal-function,
scheme:proper-center-map,lem:general-ideal-pullback}
Let $X,Y$ be actual proper models over $\operatorname{Spec}R$, with a
prescribed real seminorm $v$ bounded on every base coefficient. For every
genuine commuting model morphism $g:X\to Y$, every genuine ideal-sheaf
datum $I$ on $Y$, and every actual prescribed-base native point $y$ of
$X$, its intrinsic model functions satisfy
\[
 \|g^*I\|(y)=\|I\|(g(y)),\qquad
 \log\|g^*I\|(y)=\log\|I\|(g(y)).
\]
No finite generation, noetherian condition, flatness, birationality or
additional property of the intervening morphism $g$ is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Genuine native residue-field pullback and actual restriction of sections
prove that the whole section-spectrum point on a source open restricts
under the original local section homomorphism to the corresponding
whole target section-spectrum point. This compares all actual sections,
including every coefficient, without a generator-value argument.

Choose an actual affine neighborhood of the true target center. Actual
proper-center functoriality puts the true source center in its preimage;
choose an actual affine refinement there. The intrinsic model norm agrees
with its real affine ideal norm on both genuine center neighborhoods.
The preceding derived geometric pullback formula identifies the source
ideal with the actual image of the target ideal under the true local
section map. Proper center pullback gives genuine boundedness on every
source section. The independently proved bounded ideal-image norm
formula therefore converts the source norm to the target ideal norm at
the actual restricted spectrum point. The whole section-spectrum identity
identifies this point with the native image, giving the asserted norm
equality. Apply the actual extended logarithm for the second identity,
including zero norms and negative infinity.

This supplies genuine model-function consistency under model refinement
and birational model maps, as a specialization of the stronger commuting
morphism theorem. It uses the actual geometric ideal pullback and actual
model centers, not assumed stalk or center certificates. No analytic
structure-sheaf or coherent analytic ideal identification is claimed.
\end{proof}
