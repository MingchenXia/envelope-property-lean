\section{Actual Frobenius scalar restriction on the entire original scheme}
\label{sec:scheme-frobenius-scalar-restriction}

Let $k$ be the original field of prime characteristic $p$ and
$f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ any original scheme structure morphism.
No smoothness, nonemptiness or openwise characteristic or coordinate
certificates are assumed here.

\begin{theorem}[Entire original structure-sheaf power endomorphism]
\label{lem:cartier-entire-scheme-frobenius-coefficients}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.SchemeFrobeniusScalarRestriction.presheafMap,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.SchemeFrobeniusScalarRestriction.presheafMap_app,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.SchemeFrobeniusScalarRestriction.ringPresheafMap,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.SchemeFrobeniusScalarRestriction.sheafMap}
\leanok
The actual $p$th-power endomorphism on every original section ring
forms a genuine natural endomorphism of the entire original
structure presheaf, hence of its actual ring-valued structure sheaf.
Every original regular function on every original open is sent to
its same $p$th power. Empty opens and their zero section rings are
included internally; no characteristic-$p$ premise on each section
ring is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual original structural morphism induces the genuine original
$k$-algebra structure on every section ring, using the same actual
constant-section morphism. Apply the preceding zero-algebra-compatible
power endomorphism to each such original section ring. Its entire
naturality for every original ring homomorphism gives the naturality
square for every actual original structure restriction. Thus these
actual maps form a whole natural transformation of the original
structure presheaf, not merely a family with supplied compatibility.
Its original all-element power formula gives the stated value on
every regular function. Forgetting only commutativity gives the
same natural transformation on the actual ring-valued structure
presheaf, hence an actual structure-sheaf morphism.
\uses{lem:cartier-frobenius-original-zero-algebra-naturality}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine original sheaf-module scalar restriction]
\label{thm:cartier-entire-scheme-frobenius-modules}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.SchemeFrobeniusScalarRestriction.modulesFunctor,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.SchemeFrobeniusScalarRestriction.underlyingIso,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.SchemeFrobeniusScalarRestriction.modulesFunctor_smul,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.SchemeFrobeniusScalarRestriction.modulesFunctor_restrict}
\leanok
Restricting scalars by this actual whole structure-sheaf endomorphism
gives a genuine native endofunctor of the actual category of original
sheaves of modules on $X$. Its entire underlying abelian presheaf is
the same original abelian presheaf. On every original open its exact
scalar action is $a\cdot_F s=a^p s$, and every original restriction
map has its same whole original underlying section image. These
statements include empty opens without a new assumption.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the genuine native sheaf-of-modules restriction-of-scalars
functor for the preceding actual structure-sheaf morphism. Its
underlying module presheaf at every open is the actual original
module with coefficient action restricted by the same original
endomorphism. Its original additive presheaf and sheaf condition
are unchanged, giving the actual whole underlying-presheaf identity
isomorphism. Its scalar action is therefore exactly the preceding
whole power endomorphism applied to $a$, hence $a^p$, acting on the
same original section. Native scalar restriction retains each whole
original additive restriction map, proving the final assertion.
No line, section, scalar or presheaf comparison certificate enters
the construction.
\uses{lem:cartier-entire-scheme-frobenius-coefficients}
\end{proof}

This constructs genuine Frobenius scalar restriction on the entire
original module-sheaf category and includes zero rings internally.
It does not yet construct the original global de Rham differential,
the sheaf Cartier isomorphism, a scheme Frobenius morphism or a
Deligne--Illusie splitting. These and the later genuine geometric
vanishing arguments remain separate obligations.
