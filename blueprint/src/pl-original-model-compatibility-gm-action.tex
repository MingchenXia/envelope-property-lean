\section{The genuine action on the original normalized PL model}

\begin{theorem}[The original normalized simultaneous model carries the original action]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-simultaneous-gm-action}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLOriginalModelCompatibility.isModHom_ofIso_inv_comp,EnvelopeProperty.PLOriginalModelCompatibility.isModHom_ofIso_hom,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeOverProjection,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.simultaneousIdealBlowupNativeGmModObj,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeGmModObj,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousNativeOverProjection_isModHom}
\leanok
Let $I,J$ be the two original parameter-invariant homogeneous vertical ideals.
The actual simultaneous blowup $B$ carries its original multiplicative-group
action. Its same original generic-field normalization $\nu:N\to B$ carries a
genuine multiplicative-group action over the original field, and $\nu$ is
equivariant for these two actions. The action satisfies the whole original
unit and associativity identities.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the whole original coordinate isomorphism from the homogeneous-ideal
blowup to the original simultaneous blowup. Transport its actual categorical
action, which is constructed from the universal invariance of both original
ideals. The original finite ideal logarithms supply integrality of this same
simultaneous blowup. Apply the original normalization action construction:
the group product with the normalization is integral and has normal stalks,
as follows on affine opens from Laurent polynomial extension and localization
of the original normal affine rings. The original action composed with the
normalization projection is dominant, since its restriction along the group
unit is that original dominant normalization projection. The unique dominant
normalization lift therefore constructs the action on the normalization.
Uniqueness on the original normalization proves the unit identity. Uniqueness
on the entire original double-group product proves associativity after
composition with $\nu$. Its defining lift identity is precisely equivariance
of $\nu$. Finally, the identity on the original normalized scheme identifies
its two canonically associated coefficient structures, so transport to the
literal total object preserves the same scheme projection $\nu$.
\end{proof}
