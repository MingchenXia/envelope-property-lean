\subsection{Actual Rees birational data over a regular original generator}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine regular original generator constructs the full birational record]
\label{scheme:actual-rees-regular-birational-data}
\uses{scheme:regular-principal-open-dense,scheme:actual-rees-good-open-density,scheme:actual-rees-good-open-isomorphism}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.generatorPartialIso,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.generatorPartialIso_isOver,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.birationalOver_projection}
Let $A$ be ANY commutative ring, $(g_i)$ ANY actual family, and suppose the
ORIGINAL selected $g_j$ is a non-zero-divisor. Then the SAME original Rees
scheme and $\operatorname{Spec}A$ have genuinely constructed partial-isomorphism
data, with ACTUAL dense source and target opens. The partial isomorphism is
over the ENTIRE original projection and the identity of $\operatorname{Spec}A$.
Consequently those whole original structure maps satisfy Mathlib's genuine
$\mathrm{BirationalOver}$ predicate. No domain, reducedness, finite family,
nonzero ring or supplied birational record is required. For a finite family,
the ALREADY proved properness gives an actual proper birational projection.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original good-open lift $\operatorname{Spec}A[1/g_j]\to\operatorname{ReesScheme}(g)$
is a genuine open immersion. Its exact range is the WHOLE original projection
preimage of $D(g_j)$. The proved regular-generator density theorem makes this
actual source open dense. The genuine original localization spectrum map
$\operatorname{Spec}A[1/g_j]\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is a true open immersion
with exact range $D(g_j)$. Original regularity makes its ring map injective,
so the actual prime-spectrum closure formula proves this real target open dense.

Each of these genuine open immersions has its ACTUAL scheme isomorphism onto
its actual open range. Compose the inverse of the first with the second to
construct the whole isomorphism from the real source open to the real target
open. These opens and their proved density construct the genuine partial iso.
The true open-range isomorphism inclusion identities, together with the whole
original good-open coefficient triangle, prove its ENTIRE $\mathrm{IsOver}$
equation. Its actual witness supplies $\mathrm{BirationalOver}$, rather than
an assumed generic-point, field, lift or inverse certificate. Every original
nilpotent and coefficient action is retained; zero-ring and empty-open cases
are allowed exactly by the genuine dense-open definition.
\end{proof}
