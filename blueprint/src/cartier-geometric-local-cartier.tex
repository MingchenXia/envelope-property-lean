\section{Cartier on the evaluated geometric chart complex}

\begin{definition}[The native twist source on the geometric chart]
\label{def:cartier-geometric-local-native-domain}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.nativeDomain,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.homologyTransferIso}
\leanok
\uses{def:cartier-geometric-chart-section-complex,lem:native-module-complex-every-cycle-class}
Retain an original affine coordinate open $U$ and its original etale
coordinates $v$ in the smooth scheme $X/k$. Write $W=U^{(p)}$, $A=\Gamma(Y,W)$,
$T=k\otimes_{F_k,k}\Gamma(X,U)$ and $\sigma:A\simeq T$ for the actual
geometric chart isomorphism. In degree $q$, let $D_W^q$ be the original
native module $\bigwedge_T^q\Omega_{T/k}$ restricted along $\sigma$.
Let $H_W^q=H^q(\Gamma(W,F_*\Omega^\bullet_{X/k}))$ be homology of the
actual evaluated global complex. Scalar restriction preserves native
homology; compose its inverse comparison with homology of the whole
geometric complex exchange to define
\[
 E_v^q:\operatorname{Res}_{\sigma}H^q(K_v)\simeq H_W^q.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[The original cotangent source on the geometric chart]
\label{lem:cartier-geometric-local-cotangent-source}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.rawDomainIso,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.rawDomainIso_wedge,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.twistedUnitIso,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.twistedUnitIso_apply,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.domainIso}
\leanok
\uses{def:cartier-geometric-local-native-domain,lem:cartier-original-affine-section-unit,sc0-open-immersion-sheaf-units:original-chart}
Let $g:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be the original second projection of
the geometric twist. The coefficient-preserving isomorphism $\sigma$
induces the original universal exterior map
\[
 \bigwedge_A^q\Omega_{A/k}\simeq D_W^q.
\]
The actual exterior sheafification unit on $W$ is an isomorphism.
Consequently these same maps give an $A$-linear isomorphism
$\Gamma(W,\Omega^q_{Y/k})\simeq D_W^q$ in every degree.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The proved coefficient triangle says that $\sigma$ carries the
original structural coefficient map of $W$ to the native coefficient
inclusion in $T$. The universal cotangent map of this ring square
and its inverse induce the stated exterior isomorphism, including
the empty wedge. The original honest etale tuple on $U$ gives the
proved native cotangent basis over $T$. Transport its scalar
coefficients along $\sigma^{-1}$ and its vectors along the inverse
universal cotangent map. This is a basis of the original cotangent
module over $A$, since both transports are invertible. The actual
open $W$ is affine. The affine exterior-unit theorem applied to this
basis proves the original sheafification unit bijective. Its inverse
followed by the universal exterior map is the required isomorphism
on the original sheaf sections.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Local Cartier with the evaluated global target]
\label{thm:cartier-geometric-local-native-cartier}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.nativeInverseCartierIso,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.nativeInverseCartierIso_apply,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.inverseCartierIso,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.inverseCartierIso_unit}
\leanok
\uses{def:cartier-geometric-local-native-domain,lem:cartier-geometric-local-cotangent-source,thm:cartier-geometric-chart-complex-exchange}
For every $q\ge0$, the original native Cartier isomorphism followed by
$E_v^q$ gives a genuine $A$-linear isomorphism
\[
 C_{v,W}^{-1}:\Gamma(W,\Omega^q_{Y/k})\simeq H_W^q.
\]
On the original sheafification unit of every original raw exterior form,
its value is exactly $E_v^q$ applied to native Cartier of that form's
original universal $\sigma$-image. The empty open and zero section ring are
included. No Cartier isomorphism or comparison map is supplied as input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original honest etale tuple supplies the proved native Cartier
isomorphism over $T$, including its zero-algebra branch. Restriction
along the actual ring isomorphism $\sigma$ preserves this isomorphism.
Compose with $E_v^q$ and precompose with the preceding source isomorphism.
Each factor is defined on the original native modules and original
geometric sheaves, so the composite has the stated source, target
and $A$-linearity. On an original unit section the inverse unit cancels
the same unit, giving precisely the stated every-form formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Original cycles retain their geometric unit sections]
\label{lem:cartier-geometric-local-cycle-unit}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.imageCycle,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.imageCycle_val,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.homologyTransferIso_cycleClass}
\leanok
\uses{def:cartier-geometric-local-native-domain,lem:cartier-geometric-chart-form-exchange}
For every original native cycle $z$ in degree $q$, the image cycle in
the evaluated geometric complex is exactly the original exterior
sheafification unit of $z$, transported along $F^{-1}W=U$.
The map $E_v^q$ sends the actual native class of $z$ to the actual
homology class of this exact image cycle.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Regard $z$ as the same cycle after scalar restriction. The actual
whole geometric complex isomorphism carries it to a cycle, since it
commutes with the consecutive differentials. Its degree component is
the original unit-and-open-identification map by the whole exchange
formula, proving the asserted value. The proved native homology
comparison for scalar restriction preserves the class of this same
cycle. The native homology functor applied to the actual complex map
also preserves its cycle class. The two identities compose to the
claimed class formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Independence of the original honest tuple]
\label{thm:cartier-geometric-local-native-coordinate-independence}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.twistedUnitIso_chartChange,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.homologyTransferIso_chartChange,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.nativeInverseCartierIso_chartChange,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.GeometricLocalCartier.inverseCartierIso_chartChange}
\leanok
\uses{thm:cartier-geometric-local-native-cartier,thm:cartier-geometric-chart-complex-transition}
For any two original honest etale tuples $v,w$ of length $n$ on the
same original affine open, the original source units agree and the
homology transfer satisfies $\operatorname{Res}_{\sigma}H^q(c_{v,w})E_w^q=E_v^q$.
Both the native-source Cartier maps and the maps on original geometric
cotangent sections are independent of the tuple. In particular the
entire local maps satisfy $C_{v,W}^{-1}=C_{w,W}^{-1}$ in every degree.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $c_{v,w}:K_v\simeq K_w$ be the proved identity-component native
comparison. The original native Cartier maps satisfy
$C_v^{-1}H^q(c_{v,w})=C_w^{-1}$. Naturality of the native
scalar-restriction homology comparison identifies the two ways of
passing between the restricted native homology and homology of the
restricted complexes. The whole geometric chart-change square gives
$H^q(\operatorname{Res}_{\sigma}c_{v,w})\Phi_w=\Phi_v$.
Compose these three actual squares. Both native composites start at the same $D_W^q$ and end at the same
$H_W^q$, and the intermediate comparison isomorphisms cancel. The
source universal exterior map depends only on $\sigma$, and the
forward source unit is always the same original sheafification map.
Thus the source isomorphism is also independent of the honest tuple.
Precomposing the native equality gives the stated identity on all
original geometric cotangent sections.
\end{proof}

The target here is homology of sections of the actual global complex.
Restriction compatibility between different opens and passage from
homology of sections to sections of the global homology sheaf require
the corresponding localization, affine comparison and descent results.
