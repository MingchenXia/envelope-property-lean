\section{Finite type of tensoring by the original invertible sheaf}

\begin{lemma}[Actual affine frames of an original invertible sheaf]
\label{lem:scheme-original-invertible-affine-frames}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeInvertibleTensorQuasicoherent.exists_affine_trivializing_cover}
\leanok
Let $X$ be any original scheme and $L$ an original invertible
$\mathcal O_X$-module. There exist a family of actual affine opens
$(U_a)_{a\in A}$ covering the same $X$ and whole original
$\mathcal O_{U_a}$-module isomorphisms
\[
 e_a:L|_{U_a}\simeq\mathcal O_{U_a}.
\]
No separatedness, quasi-compactness, Noetherianity, or finite global
cover hypothesis is required. The restrictions and structure sheaves
are those of the original open subschemes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Invertibility of $L$ gives an actual open cover $(V_b)_{b\in B}$
and original trivializations of the pullbacks of $L$ to these open
subschemes. The canonical restriction--pullback comparison turns
each into a whole restriction isomorphism
$L|_{V_b}\simeq\mathcal O_{V_b}$.

Index a new family by all pairs $(b,U)$ where $U$ is an actual affine
open of $X$ contained in $V_b$, and assign to the pair this same $U$.
For any original point $x\in X$, the first cover supplies $b$ with
$x\in V_b$. The affine-open basis supplies an actual affine $U$
with $x\in U\subseteq V_b$. Thus the new family covers the same
original $X$.

Restrict the chosen original isomorphism on $V_b$ along $U\subseteq V_b$.
The actual composite-restriction and restricted-structure-sheaf
isomorphisms identify its source with $L|_U$ and its target with
$\mathcal O_U$. Their composite is $e_{(b,U)}$.
Every map remains linear for the actual original $\mathcal O_U$
action and is defined on the whole restricted module.
The construction also applies when $X$ is empty: the same open-cover
identity and the pointwise covering argument remain valid.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Tensoring by the original line preserves finite type]
\label{lem:scheme-original-invertible-tensor-finite-type}
\uses{lem:scheme-original-invertible-affine-frames,lem:scheme-original-invertible-tensor-quasicoherent}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeInvertibleTensorFiniteType.isFiniteType_tensorRight}
\leanok
Let $X$ be any original scheme, let $M$ be an original quasicoherent
module sheaf of finite type, and let $L$ be an original invertible
module sheaf. Then the same original tensor $M\otimes L$ is of
finite type. In particular its finite local generating families are
derived from the original hypotheses, rather than supplied for the
tensor or for any family of twists. No Noetherian, separatedness,
properness, or quasi-compactness hypothesis on $X$ is imposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual affine frame cover and isomorphisms $e_a$ from
\ref{lem:scheme-original-invertible-affine-frames}. On each actual
affine $U_a$, the affine section theorem for the original finite-type
quasicoherent $M$ gives a finite family of global sections
\[
 (s_{a,j})_{j\in J_a}\quad\text{of }M|_{U_a}
\]
whose actual free evaluation map onto $M|_{U_a}$ is an epimorphism.
Here the affine theorem uses the original module of sections:
quasicoherence identifies the restriction with the associated
module on $U_a$, finite type makes that module finitely generated,
and a finite module-generating family gives precisely these
original generating sections and their free evaluation map.

The whole restriction-tensor comparison, the original line frame
$e_a$, and the actual tensor unit give the module isomorphism
\[
 \rho_a:(M\otimes L)|_{U_a}
       \simeq M|_{U_a}\otimes L|_{U_a}
       \simeq M|_{U_a}\otimes\mathcal O_{U_a}
       \simeq M|_{U_a}.
\]
This is the original frame comparison of
\ref{lem:scheme-original-invertible-tensor-quasicoherent}.
Define $t_{a,j}=\rho_a^{-1}(s_{a,j})$ as an actual section of
$(M\otimes L)|_{U_a}$. The free evaluation map for this family is
the original free evaluation for $(s_{a,j})$, followed by the
isomorphism $\rho_a^{-1}$. It is therefore an epimorphism.
Its index set is the same finite $J_a$, including the case when
that generating family is empty.

The canonical equivalence between modules on the open subscheme
$U_a$ and modules over the original open-site restriction transports
these entire generating families to the open-site restriction of
the same $M\otimes L$. This equivalence preserves the original
structure-sheaf module actions and free evaluation maps. Consequently
the transported families remain epimorphic and have the same finite
index sets $J_a$.

As $U_a$ cover $X$, these families are actual local-generator data
for the whole original $M\otimes L$. If an open occurs more than
once, retain one of its original occurrences and its finite family;
the resulting open family still covers, and no generating family
or original module is changed. This gives local-generator data
indexed by actual opens of the original site, with a finite family
at every index. By the definition of finite type for sheaves of
modules, it proves that this same $M\otimes L$ is of finite type.
No finiteness premise for the target tensor was used.
\end{proof}
