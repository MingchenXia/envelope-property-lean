\subsection{Actual original Rees domination of an arbitrary proper birational affine-domain witness}

\begin{theorem}[An arbitrary proper birational affine-domain witness is dominated by a constructed original Rees scheme]
\label{scheme:actual-proper-birational-original-rees-domination}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:actual-finite-nonempty-affine-cover,scheme:actual-birational-original-product-chart-cover,algebra:actual-localized-original-product-chart-map,scheme:actual-rees-original-generic-map-unique,scheme:actual-original-rees-integral,scheme:actual-closed-projective-rees-domination,scheme:actual-rees-coordinate-cover}
Let $A$ be ANY commutative DOMAIN.  Let $f:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ be
ACTUALLY proper, and give genuine dense-open partial-isomorphism data over
the WHOLE original $f$ and identity of $\operatorname{Spec}A$.  Construct a
finite index type $J$, an ORIGINAL family $G:J\to A$, an actual member
$G_j\ne0$, and a genuine scheme morphism
\[
 \delta:\operatorname{ReesScheme}(G)\longrightarrow Y
\]
such that $f\circ\delta$ is the ENTIRE original Rees projection and the
WHOLE original generic lift followed by $\delta$ equals the SAME actual
generic inverse of the given partial isomorphism.  $Y$ need NOT be reduced,
normal, integral or Noetherian; $A$ need NOT be Noetherian.  No projective
embedding, source-integrality, finite affine cover, denominator family,
principalization, valuation ring, generic density/comparison or domination
certificate is supplied as input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual properness over the compact affine base makes the ORIGINAL $Y$
compact.  The genuine affine basis gives a finite cover by NONEMPTY original
affine opens, discarding empty members without changing the cover.  The
checked original product-chart construction applies to this real cover and
the ACTUAL partial isomorphism.  It derives every original affine coefficient
action and finite-type fraction map, then uses finite generators and true
common denominators to construct original finite families $G_i$ with actual
selected nonzero members.  Actual image algebras, not the possibly nonreduced
original affine rings themselves, give the selected-chart maps into $Y$ and
BOTH their whole original base and generic triangles.  All original affine
kernels are retained.

Use the SAME original finite product family
$G_J=\prod_iG_i(J_i)$.  Its all-selected member is genuinely nonzero.  On
EVERY nonzero original product chart, the checked proper/local-valuation
argument derives a REAL principal-open cover from the original replacement
coordinates.  For every original prime it constructs a local valuation ring
in the SAME fraction field and a true proper lift; an actual closed-point
affine factor places the reverse original fraction in that ring.  The two
original fractions multiply to one as ELEMENTS, and exact prime contraction
proves the actual cover.  No cover of a Rees chart is an input.

For each of these REAL principal opens, the checked original localization
producer constructs the selected-chart RingHom and genuine injective whole
map to the SAME fraction field.  Composing its actual Spec map with the
selected-chart map into $Y$ gives a genuine local scheme morphism.  Original
algebra-map identities prove its ENTIRE base triangle; the true fraction-map
square proves its ENTIRE generic restriction is the original inverse $q$.
The original good-open PULLBACK, whose first projection is the identity,
proves that each whole generic map into the original Rees source is the
SAME original generic lift.  No generic comparison is assumed.

Bind the actual nonzero-coordinate affine cover to these actual principal
localization covers.  The resulting genuine scheme open cover carries all
these original local maps.  On an overlap, the two actual field maps have
equal whole composites into the Rees source, hence give a REAL map to their
scheme pullback.  The overlap inclusion is an actual open immersion; the
original generic Rees map is genuinely dense, as proved from its original
injective fraction chart and dense original good open.  Inducingness of the
overlap inclusion therefore derives dominance of this SAME overlap field
map.  The original Rees source is genuinely integral from its nonzero original
member, so its actual open overlaps are reduced.  Since the ORIGINAL $f$ is
separated, genuine dominant-generic equality over the base proves equality
of the ENTIRE two maps on each overlap; it does NOT require reducedness of
$Y$.  Actual scheme open-cover gluing constructs $\delta$.  Genuine cover
extensionality proves its whole base triangle, and any true cover member
and its whole generic factor prove the original generic triangle.

The dependency on the earlier closed-projective node consumes ONLY its
general original-chart and generic-density producers, whose hypotheses have
no projective embedding.  The closed-projective domination endpoint is not
an input to this unrestricted proper-birational construction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same actual original domination is proper and scheme-surjective]
\label{scheme:actual-proper-surjective-original-rees-domination}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:actual-proper-birational-original-rees-domination,scheme:actual-rees-presentation-proper,scheme:actual-partial-iso-fraction-lift-dense}
Under EXACTLY the preceding actual hypotheses, the constructed finite
original Rees domination can be chosen ACTUALLY proper and scheme-surjective,
with BOTH whole original coefficient and generic triangles retained.
Neither properness nor surjectivity of the domination is supplied as input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Retain the SAME original finite family, nonzero member and whole $\delta$ from
the preceding theorem.  Its ENTIRE composite with $f$ is the true original
finite-family Rees projection, which is genuinely proper.  Separatedness
of the actual proper $f$ and the genuine proper-composite cancellation theorem
therefore derive properness of this SAME $\delta$.

The ACTUAL generic inverse of the original dense-open partial isomorphism has
dense image in the ORIGINAL $Y$, even when $Y$ is nonreduced.  The whole
generic triangle identifies this dense map with a genuine composite through
$\delta$, so the SAME $\delta$ has dense image.  Its genuine properness makes
that exact image closed.  The closed dense image is the entire original
space, proving actual scheme-surjectivity without a chosen lift or a
source-support replacement.
\end{proof}
