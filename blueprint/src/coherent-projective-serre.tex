\section{Ordinary coherent Serre vanishing and global generation on projective space}

\begin{lemma}[Eventual acyclicity of the original finite negative-twist source]
\label{lem:coherent-projective-negative-presentation-acyclic}
\uses{lem:coherent-projective-original-twist-addition}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.CoherentProjectiveSerreVanishing.exists_negativeSource_bound}
\leanok
A finite coproduct of original negative coordinate twists becomes acyclic
in every positive degree after tensoring with every sufficiently large
original nonnegative twist.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write its finitely many summands as $\mathcal O_{j_i}(-a_i)$ and take
$A=\max_i a_i$, with $A=0$ for the empty source. For $d\ge A$, the preceding
isomorphism identifies the $i$th tensor summand with the same original
$\mathcal O(d-a_i)$, whose degree is nonnegative. The actual standard-cover
Laurent weight contraction makes the original ordered Cech complex of
every such summand exact in each positive degree. Finite coproducts of
module sheaves and of these Cech complexes commute with their original
maps, and finite coproducts of exact module complexes remain exact.
Consequently their original positive cohomology modules vanish.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Ordinary coherent Serre vanishing]
\label{thm:coherent-projective-serre-vanishing}
\uses{lem:coherent-projective-negative-presentation-acyclic,thm:scheme-original-invertible-tensor-exact,lem:scheme-original-invertible-tensor-quasicoherent}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.CoherentProjectiveSerreVanishing.exists_orderedCech_bound,EnvelopeProperty.CoherentProjectiveSerreVanishing.exists_sheafCohomology_bound}
\leanok
Let $R$ be Noetherian and $M$ an original finite-type quasicoherent module
on the genuine polynomial projective space $\mathbb P_R^n$. There exists
$A$ such that, for every $d\ge A$ and every $q>0$, the whole original
$H^q(\mathbb P_R^n,M\otimes\mathcal O(d))$ is zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the original finite ordered standard affine cover, of cardinality
$c=n+1$. Every ordered Cech term in degree at least $c$ is zero, so the
same holds for cohomology. We prove by induction on $s$ the stronger
statement that every finite-type quasicoherent $M$ has one bound $A$
working simultaneously in all positive degrees $q$ with $c\le q+s$.
For $s=0$ use the just-proved zero Cech terms, and take $A=0$.

For the successor step choose the actual finite negative-twist
surjection $Q\twoheadrightarrow M$. Its kernel $K$ is quasicoherent and
finite type because projective space over the Noetherian base is
locally Noetherian. The induction hypothesis supplies a bound for $K$,
and the preceding lemma supplies a bound for $Q$. Take their maximum.
Tensor the original short exact sequence by the actual invertible
$\mathcal O(d)$; exactness is preserved by the genuine tensor equivalence.
Its standard affine Cech complexes give a short exact sequence. In the
original long exact sequence, for $q>0$ and $c\le q+s+1$, the group
$H^q(Q(d))$ is zero by the source bound and $H^{q+1}(K(d))$ is zero by
the induction hypothesis, since $c\le(q+1)+s$. Exactness forces the
entire group $H^q(M(d))$ to be zero. This proves the induction step.
Finally take $s=c$, when its inequality holds for every positive $q$.
\end{proof}


The comparison to the whole original sheaf cohomology uses the actual
finite standard affine cover. All of its nonempty finite intersections are
affine on this original separated projective scheme. Quasicoherence of each
original tensor twist, derived above, gives genuine acyclicity on these
intersections. The native full/ordered Cech homotopy equivalence retains
all original homology, and the actual acyclic-cover comparison identifies
positive exactness with vanishing of the original sheaf cohomology. For
the final statement an order on the finite coordinate index is constructed
internally; no order or cover certificate is an extra hypothesis.
