\section{The regular candidate on the actual whole original ratio chart}

\begin{definition}[Actual whole-ratio native canonical sheaf candidate]
\label{def:kn-canonical-whole-ratio-native-sheaf-candidate}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.ratioCanonicalSheafCandidate}
\leanok
For the original joined-family ratio data, etale coordinates, injective
active-coordinate assignment and positive tame row degrees, the entire
original ratio projection $p:\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$
has an internally constructed regular candidate
$p_*\omega_{p\circ g\circ f}\to\omega_{g\circ f}$, where $g$ is the
actual original base-chart open immersion. Its source and target are the
native original canonical objects over the original field.
No normalization or rational-factor assertion is included in this definition.
\uses{lem:kn-canonical-mixed-native-sheaf-candidate,lem:kn-adapted-original-coefficients}
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The existing original coefficient identities construct the actual active
set, coordinate injection, units and equality of the entire original ratio
coefficient family with its mixed formula. Equality elimination carries
the same mixed sheaf candidate to that same entire ratio algebra, preserving
every original root. The actual original base structural triangle and
algebra-map structural triangle then give the two canonical-object transports.
Push the source transport through the same original projection, apply the
same candidate, and apply the inverse base transport. No coefficient
identity, map, Gamma comparison or desired factor equation is a caller input.
\end{proof}
