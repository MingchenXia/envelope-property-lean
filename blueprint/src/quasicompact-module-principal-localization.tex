\section{Whole module sections and principal-open localization}

\begin{lemma}[The actual kernel of a commuting localization square]
\label{lem:localized-module-kernel-square}
Let $a:M\to M_T$ and $b:N\to N_T$ be actual localizations of
modules at a multiplicative subset $T$ of a commutative ring.
Let $d:M\to N$ and $d_T:M_T\to N_T$ be the actual linear maps
in a commuting square $d_Ta=bd$. Then the actual restriction of
$a$ to $\ker d\to\ker d_T$ is a localization. This is the same
kernel map of the square, rather than an abstractly chosen
isomorphism of the resulting kernels.
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LocalizedKernelSquares.kernelMap,EnvelopeProperty.LocalizedKernelSquares.kernelMap_apply,EnvelopeProperty.LocalizedKernelSquares.differential_eq_localizedMap,EnvelopeProperty.LocalizedKernelSquares.kernelMap_isLocalizedModule}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The universal property of $a$ and localization of $b$ identify the
whole actual map $d_T$ with the canonical localized map of $d$.
Native localization commutes with kernels for that canonical map.
After this whole map equality, the actual kernel restriction is
literally the native kernel localization map; proof irrelevance
identifies the witnesses that its values lie in the kernel.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Whole sections on the actual principal open]
\label{prop:quasicompact-module-principal-localization}
Let $Y$ be a quasicompact separated scheme, $M$ an actual
quasicoherent module, and $t\in\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y)$. The
actual whole restriction
\[
 \Gamma(Y,M)\longrightarrow\Gamma(D(t),M)
\]
is localization at the powers of $t$.
\uses{lem:localized-module-kernel-square,lem:finite-product-module-localization,prop:scheme-module-open-sections-kernel,prop:scheme-module-cech-localization-criterion}
\notready
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual finite affine open cover of $Y$. Separatedness
makes its pairwise intersections affine. The native affine
quasicoherent-section theorem identifies the restriction to the
intersection with the SAME principal open $D(t)$ as localization
on every cover term and every overlap term. Restrict scalars
through the actual coefficient maps and take finite products.
The actual Cech differential restriction square commutes by
presheaf functoriality. Apply the preceding kernel result to
these SAME two original differentials. The actual sheaf
global-section kernel isomorphisms, with their augmentation
identities, identify the resulting map with the original whole
restriction, proving the proposition.

The actual kernel-square, common-denominator finite-product localization,
and ANY-open original module-section Cech kernel with its WHOLE actual
augmentation have now been proved. The SAME actual section restriction
is identified with the actual kernel restriction; the whole section
localization follows from the SAME two original term localizations.
The geometric finite affine cover,
actual localizations of every affine cover and overlap term with
the SAME coefficient action, and the true Cech restriction square
still have to be joined. They are not supplied as a replacement
certificate for the proposition. The test-configuration consumer still needs its
actual generic-fibre line isomorphism and finite Laurent
expansion. Neither whole localization nor the literal envelope
formula is claimed proved here.
\end{proof}
