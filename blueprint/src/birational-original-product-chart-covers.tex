\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

and an original chart isomorphism onto the ACTUAL $\phi_i$ image, retaining
every original element and its kernel.

coefficient identity proves its ENTIRE base triangle; the genuine image-chart
composite identity proves its ENTIRE generic restriction is the original $q$.

Fix any original product chart with $P_J\ne0$.  Its actual original embedding

The reverse original ratio $G_i(J_i)/G_i(*)$ lies in the TRUE $\phi_i$ image:
apply the actual image-chart isomorphism to the original $J_i$ coordinate
ratio of the selected $*$ chart.  Thus choose an ORIGINAL section $s_i$ with
exactly this value in the SAME field.  Nonzero product and selected original
denominator give both genuine nonzero denominators, and the two original
fractions multiply to one as ELEMENTS of that same field.

Apply the checked proper affine reciprocal-cover theorem to the ACTUAL
product chart, its original embedding, the whole $q$, the original affine

domination, whole proper lifts and exact prime contraction now prove the
stated WHOLE chart cover.  No source reduction, principalization, center,
valuation-ring, unit or Spec-chart cover is supplied.  Actual compactness of
proper $Y$ and the separately proved finite nonempty-affine cover theorem
provide the finite $U_i$ in the final global domination construction.
\end{proof}
