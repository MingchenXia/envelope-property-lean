\subsection{Actual Rees integrality and reduced-source closed factorization}

\begin{lemma}[Reduced graded rings have genuinely reduced original Proj schemes]
\label{scheme:actual-reduced-graded-proj}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.proj_isReduced}
\leanok
For ANY reduced commutative graded ring, its actual Proj scheme is reduced.
No domain, nonempty, finite generation or grading-dimension condition is imposed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At every original relevant homogeneous prime, the actual homogeneous localization
embeds by its genuine underlying ring homomorphism in the ordinary prime
localization. Ordinary localization preserves reducedness and the embedding
reflects it. The actual Proj stalk isomorphism transports this reducedness to
EVERY original stalk. Genuine stalkwise reducedness proves actual reducedness
of the SAME original scheme, including empty Proj schemes.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original Rees scheme is genuinely integral]
\label{scheme:actual-original-rees-integral}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.quotient_isDomain,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.scheme_isReduced,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.denseRange_goodOpenLift,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesPresentation.scheme_isIntegral}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:actual-reduced-graded-proj,algebra:actual-rees-image-and-quotient,scheme:actual-rees-good-open-density}
Let $A$ be a commutative domain and $(g_i)$ ANY actual family with one
actual $g_j\ne0$. The SAME original Rees scheme is integral. Neither
finiteness nor a supplied source-integrality property is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual whole original homogeneous kernel quotient is algebra-isomorphic
to the literal Rees subalgebra of $A[T]$. Since $A[T]$ is a domain, this
actual quotient is a domain, without replacing its kernel by a radical.
For any reduced graded ring, every genuine homogeneous stalk embeds in
its ordinary prime localization, which is reduced; the actual Proj stalk
isomorphism therefore makes its original scheme reduced.

The original good-open map from $\operatorname{Spec}A[1/g_j]$ is a true
open immersion. The original nonzero $g_j$ is regular, so the proved
whole inverse image of $D(g_j)$ is dense in the SAME original Rees scheme.
The actual good-open range equality makes the original good-open map have
dense range. Its source is the spectrum of a genuine domain localization,
so is nonempty and irreducible. Its continuous image and closure are
irreducible, giving irreducibility of the whole original Rees scheme.
Reducedness and irreducibility give its actual integrality. No generic
scheme point, irreducibility or chart certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A reduced source factors through a genuine closed target from actual range containment]
\label{scheme:actual-reduced-closed-factorization}
\leanok
For actual scheme morphisms $j:Z\hookrightarrow P$ and $h:Y\to P$ with
$j$ a closed immersion and $Y$ reduced, actual containment of the whole
scheme image of $h$ in that of $j$ constructs a unique whole factor
$Y\to Z$ whose composite with $j$ is exactly $h$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual ideal sheaf kernel of $j$ has support exactly its closed image.
Its genuine pullback along $h$ has support all of $Y$, by the real range
containment. On a reduced scheme an actual ideal sheaf with full zero
locus is zero, since every local ideal is contained in the genuine zero
nilradical. The true ideal map/comap adjunction therefore puts the entire
kernel of $j$ inside that of $h$. Mathlib's real closed-immersion lift
constructs the whole factor, and the closed immersion's actual monomorphism
proves uniqueness. This is not a supplied lift or ideal-kernel certificate.
\end{proof}


The source-integrality result permits arbitrary index families. No actual closed
projective domination theorem is attached here: construction of the original
field-valued generic inverse, actual common-denominator projective coordinates
and the whole closed-target range comparison are the next producers. Arbitrary
proper-witness Chow domination and analytic potential theory remain separate.
