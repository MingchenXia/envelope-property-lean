\begin{theorem}[Every actual clopen scheme subset constructs a unique original global idempotent]
\label{scheme:actual-global-idempotent-clopen-correspondence}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalIdempotents.isClopen_basicOpen,
EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalIdempotents.exists_idempotent_basicOpen_eq,
EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalIdempotents.eq_of_basicOpen_eq,
EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalIdempotents.idempotentEquivClopens}
\uses{scheme:actual-original-section-equalizer}

Noetherianity or nonempty assumptions. For EVERY actual clopen subset $U$,
construct an ORIGINAL $e\in\Gamma(X,\top)$ such that $e^2=e$ and whose
actual basic open is EXACTLY $U$. Its genuine construction glues the original sections one on $U$
and zero on its actual open complement. It is the unique original
global idempotent with that basic open. Conversely EVERY whole original
idempotent has a genuine clopen basic open. All scalar and restriction
identities concern the ORIGINAL whole sections, not selected values.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both $U$ and its actual complement are original opens, their intersection
is empty, and their union is the whole scheme. Put the ORIGINAL section
one on $U$ and zero on its complement. Their entire restrictions to the
empty intersection agree because the genuine structure-sheaf section ring

whole section $e$. The same genuine sheaf separation compares $e^2$ and
$e$ on BOTH original opens, proving actual idempotence. Actual basic-open
restriction identities and the true one/zero formulas show that its whole
basic open is precisely $U$.

makes $D(e)$ disjoint from $D(1-e)$, because $e(1-e)=0$. The actual
basic-open addition inequality and $e+(1-e)=1$ make their union the whole
scheme. Thus $D(e)$ is genuinely clopen with complement $D(1-e)$.
At any original stalk, the image of an idempotent is zero or one because
that ACTUAL stalk is a local ring. It is one exactly on $D(e)$ and zero
on its complement. Two original idempotents with the same basic open
therefore have equal germs at EVERY original point, so genuine sheaf germ
extensionality identifies the WHOLE original sections. Empty schemes are
retained by the actual empty section ring.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual scheme connectedness is detected by its original global idempotents]
\label{scheme:actual-global-idempotents-connectedness}

EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalIdempotents.connectedSpace_of_globalSections_isDomain}
\uses{scheme:actual-global-idempotent-clopen-correspondence}

idempotent is zero or one. ACTUAL nontriviality of the original whole
section ring derives scheme nonemptiness. In particular an original scheme

certificate is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual topological connectedness criterion for clopen subsets says that
a nonempty space is connected precisely when every clopen subset is empty
or the whole space. Use the preceding genuine correspondence. The true
basic opens of the ORIGINAL zero and one sections are empty and the whole

For an actual integral domain, the genuine
algebraic idempotent equation gives $e=0$ or $e=1$, proving the last claim.
\end{proof}
