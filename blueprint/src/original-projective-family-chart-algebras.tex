\section{Every original simultaneous projective chart has its entire genuine model algebra}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:original-finite-segre-chart-preimage}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.OriginalProjectiveFamilyChartAlgebras.morphism_preimage_coordinateOpen}
\leanok
\uses{def:finite-original-segre-morphism}
For every nonempty finite family of original projective maps over the same base and every original coordinate tuple, the preimage of its actual finite Segre coordinate open is exactly the intersection of all original factor coordinate opens.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For one factor the actual finite Segre map and its coordinate index are the original map and original index. For a family with one more factor, the actual map is the original pair map followed by the genuine binary Segre map. The binary Segre preimage formula identifies the selected coordinate open with the intersection of the first factor coordinate open and the selected coordinate open of the remaining finite Segre map. Apply the induction hypothesis to that remaining map. The finite intersection formula separates its first term from its remaining terms and gives exactly the intersection for all original factors.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:actual-original-family-chart-algebra}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.OriginalProjectiveFamilyChartAlgebras.actualChartAlgebra_eq}
\leanok
\uses{thm:actual-original-positive-diagonal-segre-algebra,lem:gl-charts,lem:gl-veronese}
Let the original scheme be integral over an arbitrary field and the first original projective map a closed immersion. On every actual nonempty original simultaneous coordinate chart, the chart subalgebra of the original finite Segre whole-field model equals the entire chart subalgebra of the reconstructed multigraded family model, inside the SAME original function field and over the SAME original derived base.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine native Segre coefficient for the selected tuple is the product of all original factor coefficients. The entire original finite Segre algebra equals the strictly positive all-one diagonal algebra of the original family model. Equality of the actual algebras identifies every homogeneous piece and hence their degree-zero localizations at this same actual coefficient. The degree-zero localization of the diagonal in degree one is exactly the degree-zero localization of the original family algebra in the all-one degree. The latter is the entire multigraded chart algebra, since this degree is strictly positive in every factor and its denominator is the product of the same factor coordinates. Nonemptiness of every original chart gives nonzero actual factor coefficients and their product, so all displayed genuine localizations and chart identifications apply.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Compose this actual isomorphism with the preceding equality of subalgebras inside the same function field. The preceding preimage formula identifies the original domain as the simultaneous factor chart.
\end{proof}
