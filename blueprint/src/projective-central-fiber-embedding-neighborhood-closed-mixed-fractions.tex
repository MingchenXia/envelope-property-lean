\section{Entire actual mixed coordinate ratios}
\begin{lemma}[Entire mixed coefficients are products of original ratios]
\label{lem:central-embedding-entire-mixed-coefficient-product}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjectiveCentralFiberEmbeddingNeighborhoodClosedMixedFractions.coefficient_mixed}
\leanok
Over any commutative ring, the entire homogeneous coefficient
$X_a^dX_b/X_i^{d+1}$ equals $(X_a/X_i)^d(X_b/X_i)$ as actual sections on the
entire original $i$-th coordinate open.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
First prove the equality in the native homogeneous localization. After passing
to its actual underlying localization, the native product and power formulas
and equality criterion reduce this to polynomial cross multiplication.
The actual multiplicative witness is $1$, and the identity follows from the
commutative ring laws. Apply the original away-to-section ring morphism,
which preserves the actual product and power. No domain, reducedness,
pointwise equality or cancellation assumption is used.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[The entire actual diagonal is the same positive power]
\label{lem:central-embedding-entire-mixed-diagonal-positive-power}
\uses{lem:central-embedding-entire-mixed-coefficient-product}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjectiveCentralFiberEmbeddingNeighborhoodClosedMixedFractions.coefficient_diagonal}
\leanok
The entire actual diagonal coefficient $X_a^dX_a/X_i^{d+1}$ is exactly the
$(d+1)$-st power of the actual original coordinate ratio $X_a/X_i$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the proved entire mixed coefficient identity with the same index in
both factors, then use the native successor-power identity.
\end{proof}
These whole section identities are inputs to the subsequent internally proved
actual mixed-map chart formula and entire diagonal preimage computation.
They do not assume or conclude that the mixed projective map is closed or
that its original whole line is very ample.
