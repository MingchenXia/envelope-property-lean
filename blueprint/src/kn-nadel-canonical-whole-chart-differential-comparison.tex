\section{The native pushed canonical differential on the actual whole chart}

\begin{lemma}[Actual whole-chart comparison of the original differential]
\label{lem:kn-canonical-whole-chart-pushed-differential-square}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.exists_pushedCanonicalDifferential_chart_comparison}
\leanok
Let $C$ be an actual whole chart of the original morphism $\pi:Y\to X$,
with scalar map $\beta:B\to A$, base open immersion $a:A\to X$ and
whole source open immersion $v:B\to Y$. For every original structural
map $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ and exterior degree $n$, there are
internally produced isomorphisms
$e_A:a^*\pi_*\pi^*\omega_X\simeq\beta_*\beta^*\omega_A$ and
$e_B:a^*\pi_*\omega_Y\simeq\beta_*\omega_B$
under which the restriction of the original pushed differential satisfies
$a^*(\pi_*\delta)e_B=e_A(\beta_*\delta_\beta)$.
Here the displayed restrictions are the native open-restriction functors;
the canonical objects are the original exterior cotangent sheaves.
\uses{lem:kn-blowup-canonical-etale-square,lem:kn-structure-trace-chart-cartesian}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Both horizontal open-immersion canonical differentials are isomorphisms.
Apply the already proved native compatible-lines theorem to the chart's
actual original square. The native whole-module open base-change
isomorphism, followed by the native restriction--pullback isomorphism
on the actual whole source chart, supplies a natural comparison of the
two whole-pushforward functors. Its naturality at the original differential
and the image of the proved compatible-lines square give the asserted
square after composing the original isomorphisms. No comparison isomorphism
or desired commuting square is an incoming hypothesis.
\end{proof}
