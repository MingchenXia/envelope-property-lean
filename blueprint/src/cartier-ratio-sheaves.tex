\section{The genuine ratio line sheaf of actual Cartier ideals}

\begin{lemma}[Actual tensor-dual ratio of effective Cartier ideals]
\label{lem:actual-cartier-ratio-line-sheaf}
\uses{lem:cartier-actual-invertible-module-sheaf}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.ratioModule,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.IsInvertible.isInvertible_ratioModule}
\leanok
For two actual effective Cartier ideals $I,J$ on any scheme, the genuine
sheaf $I\otimes_{\mathcal O}\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O}(J,\mathcal O)$
is invertible in the existing rank-one sheaf interface.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual module sheaves of the original actual ideals. The preceding
regular-equation proof gives actual open-cover/pullback rank-one
trivializations. The genuine sheaf dual is the sheaf of original
$\mathcal O$-linear morphisms, not an assigned formal inverse. Its
actual local trivializations prove invertibility of the dual.
The true sheafified tensor product of these two actual sheaves is
invertible: on the actual common refinement of both original trivializing
covers, the genuine open pullback-tensor isomorphism reduces it to
$\mathcal O\otimes\mathcal O\simeq\mathcal O$. This constructs the
original ratio line sheaf. It does not yet identify a metric on it,
add the polarization $\mathcal O(r)$, normalize the model or prove nefness.
\end{proof}
