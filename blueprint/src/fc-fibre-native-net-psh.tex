required directed presentation on the whole completed chart fibre.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole original completed fibre directed PL restriction]

local directed native PL class on the entire literal completed-residue-field
scheme fibre, with no countability assumption on the original field.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

preceding native chart statement. The proved whole-fibre homeomorphism agrees

whole fibre. No point gluing, affine chart, integrality, topology, approximation
or field-countability certificate is supplied to this whole-fibre conclusion.
\end{proof}
