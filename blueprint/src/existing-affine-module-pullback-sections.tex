\subsection{Affine module pullback sections}

\begin{theorem}[Affine pullback sections and the original unit]
\label{scheme:existing-affine-module-pullback-sections}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.affinePullbackUnitTop,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.affinePullbackΓIso,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.affinePullbackΓIso_hom_one_tmul,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.affinePullbackΓIso_naturality}
\leanok
For a morphism $g:Y\to X$ of schemes, the original pullback-adjunction
unit gives a map $\eta_{g,M}:\Gamma(X,M)\to\Gamma(Y,g^*M)$,
where the inverse image of the top open is identified with the top open.
If $X,Y$ are affine and $M$ is quasicoherent, the original section
extension functor gives an isomorphism
\[
 \Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y)\otimes_{\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)}\Gamma(X,M)
       \simeq\Gamma(Y,g^*M).
\]
It sends $1\otimes m$ to $\eta_{g,M}(m)$ and is natural in every
morphism between quasicoherent modules on $X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the affine sheafification--global-sections adjunctions on $X$
and $Y$. The existing pullback comparison identifies sheafification
after extension of scalars with pullback after sheafification.
Conjugating the literal affine section identifications with this
comparison gives the displayed isomorphism. The unit identity for
this conjugate of adjunctions identifies its value on $1\otimes m$
with the original pullback unit, including the restriction from the
inverse image of the top open. Naturality of the same conjugate
natural transformation gives the asserted equality for every module
morphism. These constructions use the original structure-ring map
$g^*$ throughout.
\end{proof}
