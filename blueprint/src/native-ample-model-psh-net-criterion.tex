\section{Actual original finite FS decreasing nets and genuine model psh}
\label{sec:native-ample-model-psh-net-criterion}

\begin{definition}[Independent actual original finite FS net criterion]
\label{def:native-line-finite-fs-net-limit}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeLineFubiniStudy.IsFiniteFSNetLimit}
\leanok
\uses{def:native-line-whole-section-fs}
For an actual original line $L$ and real coefficient $\theta$, the finite FS net
criterion requires a nonidentically negative-infinite upper semicontinuous
function that is the actual pointwise infimum of a nonempty directed decreasing
net of actual original finite rational FS functions. Their original rational
coefficients converge to $\theta$. Every term uses genuine whole sections of
the original tensor powers of $L$ and their true original model norms.
No model-psh or comparison predicate occurs in this definition.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original finite FS continuity and net transport]
\label{thm:native-ample-model-finite-fs-net-transport}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeAmpleModelPSHNetCriterion.isFinFS_continuous,EnvelopeProperty.NativeAmpleModelPSHNetCriterion.isPSH_of_finiteFSNetLimit}
\leanok
\uses{def:native-line-finite-fs-net-limit,thm:native-ample-model-finite-fs-transport,thm:native-ample-model-finite-fs-iff,def:gl-psh}
Assume throughout this statement that the original scheme $X$ is integral.
Fix the actual original embedding with $L^{\otimes r}$ the first pulled twist
and $r>0$. Actual original finite rational FS functions are continuous on the
entire original native point space. Every original finite FS decreasing-net
limit of real class coefficient $\theta$ gives genuine model psh of coefficient
$\theta/r$ under the same entire native analytic homeomorphism.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine model finite FS transport and the actual entire homeomorphism
give continuity. Transport every actual term of the original directed net.
Its rational class is divided by the same nonzero integer $r$, so these real
classes converge to $\theta/r$. The same homeomorphism retains the actual
antitone inequalities, nontriviality and every original pointwise infimum.
Apply the proved genuine model FS decreasing-net criterion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact genuine model psh criterion by actual original section nets]
\label{thm:native-ample-model-psh-finite-fs-net-criterion}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeAmpleModelPSHNetCriterion.isPSH_iff_finiteFSNetLimit}
\leanok
\uses{thm:native-ample-model-finite-fs-net-transport,thm:native-ample-model-finite-fs-iff,def:gl-psh}
Assume throughout this statement that the original scheme $X$ is integral.
For every nonnegative real original coefficient $\theta$, the preceding
independent finite FS net criterion is equivalent to genuine model psh of
coefficient $\theta/r$ under the same entire original homeomorphism.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The forward direction is the preceding actual net transport. Conversely,
$\theta/r$ is a genuine nonnegative model-nef coefficient. Genuine model
regularization gives a nonempty directed decreasing net of finite rational FS
functions with rational coefficients strictly above it and converging to it.
Multiply these rational coefficients by $r$. They are nonnegative and converge
to the original $\theta$. The exact whole original finite FS equivalence lifts
each term to true original tensor-power sections with the same function on the
entire original point space. The original infimum and antitone inequalities
are retained through the actual homeomorphism. Continuity of these genuine
original terms makes their actual infimum upper semicontinuous; model
nontriviality gives original nontriviality.

This theorem proves the explicit original finite-section-net criterion on the
fixed actual ample-line ray. It does not identify arbitrary numerical
representatives, prove global numerical finite dimensionality, or replace the
separate full nonfinite-original-FS comparison or global envelope theorem.
\end{proof}
