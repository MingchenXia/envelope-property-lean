\section{The original exterior differential in actual etale coordinates}
\label{sec:etale-coordinate-de-rham}

Let $R$ and $B$ be arbitrary actual commutative rings with their original
$R$-algebra structure. Let $v_1,\ldots,v_n\in B$ be actual coordinates whose
original polynomial evaluation map $R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\to B$ is etale.
The original cotangent basis is the genuine native etale base change of the
original polynomial cotangent basis; its vectors are exactly $d v_i$.

\begin{theorem}[Derived original coordinate derivatives]
\label{lem:cartier-etale-original-partials}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.partials_commute,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.partials_aeval}
\leanok
The actual original coordinate partials $\partial_i$, defined by the dual
cotangent basis applied to the original universal derivation, commute on every
original function of $B$. For every original polynomial $f$ they satisfy
\[
 \partial_i(f(v))=(\partial_i f)(v).
\]
No commutation or derivative-matching premise is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual commutator of the two original partials is itself a derivation.
It vanishes on all original coordinate functions, since their actual partials
are the Kronecker constants. A derivation is determined by those original
coordinates through the genuine original cotangent basis; hence the entire
commutator vanishes. The genuine polynomial chain rule, proved by original
polynomial induction over arbitrary commutative rings, and the same actual
Kronecker coordinate values give the full original polynomial formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole original etale coordinate exterior complex]
\label{lem:cartier-etale-original-exterior-complex}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.tensorDifferential,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.tensorDifferential_tmul,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.tensorDifferential_square,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.formEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.formEquiv_one_tmul_basis,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.formEquiv_tmul_basis,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.differential,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.differential_formEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.differential_square,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.differential_coordinate_wedge,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.formEquiv_zero,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.differential_zero,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.differential_base_smul,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.complex}
\leanok
On the entire original exterior cotangent modules $\Omega^q_{B/R}$ construct
the actual differential with coordinate formula
\[
 d(f\,d v_S)=\sum_i\partial_i(f)\,d v_i\wedge d v_S.
\]
It squares to zero in every degree, retains the original coefficient action
and is $R$-linear. Under the genuine native zero and one exterior-power
identifications its degree-zero map is the SAME original universal K\"ahler
differential of $B/R$. Thus it gives an actual native cochain complex on the
whole original exterior cotangent modules.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First construct the actual tensor differential on
$B\otimes_R\bigwedge^q_R R^n$ using the original derived partials and true
left exterior multiplication. The actual commuting derivatives and original
exterior anticommutation cancel every off-diagonal pair; repeated factors kill
every diagonal, including characteristic two. Genuine tensor induction proves
square-zero on the entire module.
The actual original etale cotangent basis gives a derived $B$-linear
isomorphism to the SAME $\Omega^q_{B/R}$, carrying every original coefficient
and every original coordinate wedge exactly. Transport the actual differential
through it. Genuine zero/one exterior identifications and the actual original
cotangent-basis expansion of the universal derivation prove the stated exact
degree-zero identity. The original tensor differential's $R$-linearity and the
same actual coefficient action give the original coefficient-ring linearity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entire original polynomial differential compatibility]
\label{lem:cartier-etale-polynomial-differential-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.polynomialTensorMap,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.polynomialTensorMap_tmul,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.tensorDifferential_polynomialTensorMap,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.polynomialFormMap,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.polynomialFormMap_formEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.polynomialFormMap_smul_basis,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.differential_polynomialFormMap}
\leanok
The actual original polynomial coefficient evaluation, with every original
coordinate wedge retained, induces an entire additive map from the original
polynomial exterior cotangent modules to the SAME original etale exterior
cotangent modules. On every original basis form its formula is
$f\,dx_S\mapsto f(v)\,d v_S$.
This whole original map commutes with the two constructed original differentials
on EVERY original input, not only on selected representatives.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the actual original polynomial coefficient map tensored with the identity
of the same original exterior coordinate module. The first theorem gives its
true pure-tensor differential compatibility. Actual tensor induction extends
this to the whole original tensor module. Compose with the two genuine original
cotangent basis identifications. Their exact coefficient/basis formulas and
whole differential transports give both the stated original formula and the
entire original differential compatibility.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual Frobenius-linear original etale complex]
\label{lem:cartier-etale-frobenius-complex}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.tensorDifferential_pow_smul,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.differential_pow_smul,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.frobeniusModule,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.frobeniusDifferential,EnvelopeProperty.Cartier.EtaleDeRham.frobeniusComplex}
\leanok
If $B$ has prime characteristic $p$, the same original differential is linear
for the actual Frobenius coefficient action. It therefore gives an actual native
module-category cochain complex on the SAME original exterior cotangent modules
with scalars genuinely restricted along the original Frobenius of $B$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original derivation power rule gives $\partial_i(a^p)=0$ in the SAME ring.
Actual Leibniz and tensor induction prove the entire original tensor differential
commutes with multiplication by $a^p$. Transport through the same genuine
$B$-linear exterior identifications gives the whole original differential's
Frobenius linearity. The actual native restriction-of-scalars functor then gives
the stated complex, with the same differential and already proved square-zero.
\end{proof}

No field, Noetherian, reducedness or characteristic assumption is used before the
last theorem. Coordinate-change independence, the complete Frobenius base-change
isomorphism, whole sheaf comparisons, Cartier descent and global
Deligne--Illusie/vanishing remain separate obligations.
