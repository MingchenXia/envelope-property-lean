\subsection{Original translations and actual centred strict fibre discs}
\begin{lemma}[Whole polynomial translations are actual homeomorphisms]
\label{analytic:actual-polynomial-translation-homeomorphisms}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.polynomialTranslation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.polynomialTranslation_val_apply,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.polynomialTranslation_symm_apply,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.proj_polynomialTranslation,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.polynomialTranslation_val_X_sub_C}
\leanok
\uses{spectrum:topology,spectrum:centered-gauss-whole-ray,spectrum:pullback,spectrum:relative}
For arbitrary original commutative rings $R,A$, their actual algebra map and
prescribed valuation $v$ on $R$, every original $a\in A$ gives a genuine
homeomorphism $\tau_a$ of $\operatorname{Sp}_v(A[T])$. It evaluates EVERY
original polynomial by
\[
 |p(\tau_a(x))|=|p(T+a)(x)|.
\]
Its actual inverse is $\tau_{-a}$, it retains the WHOLE coefficient point,
and $|T-a|_{\tau_a(x)}=|T|_x$ on every original point. No field, reducedness,
faithfulness, completeness or radius bound is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual polynomial algebra automorphism $p(T)\mapsto p(T+a)$ and its
inverse $p(T)\mapsto p(T-a)$, restricted along the original $R$ coefficient
action. Restriction of whole valuations gives the two actual point maps.
The algebra automorphisms are inverse on EVERY polynomial, so whole-point
extensionality gives both inverse identities. Each original polynomial
evaluation after restriction is evaluation of the single fixed original
translated polynomial; the original evaluation topology therefore makes
both maps continuous. The automorphisms fix every constant in $A$, which
proves the whole coefficient identity, not only the $R$ restriction.
Finally the forward automorphism sends $T-a$ to $T$ exactly, proving the
coordinate formula. This constructs the homeomorphism on the original
polynomial spectrum itself.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual centred strict discs retain the original contraction]
\label{analytic:actual-centred-strict-fibre-discs}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.CenteredFibreOpenDisc,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.FibreOpenDisc.centeredHomeomorph,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.CenteredFibreOpenDisc.contractibleSpace}
\leanok
\uses{analytic:actual-polynomial-translation-homeomorphisms,analytic:actual-strict-fibre-disc-contraction}
For an actual whole coefficient point $y$, original $a\in A$ and radius $r$,
translation constructs a genuine homeomorphism
\[
 \mathcal D_y(r)\ \simeq\
 \mathcal D_y(a,r):=\{x\in\operatorname{Sp}_v(A[T]):
       p(x)=y,\ |T-a|_x<r\}.
\]
For $r>0$ the ORIGINAL centred strict disc is contractible. No condition
on $|a(y)|$ and no ambient closed-disc constraint is imposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The forward map is the restriction of $\tau_a$ to the actual strict fibre
disc. Its whole coefficient identity retains $y$ and its exact coordinate
formula turns the original bound $|T|<r$ into $|T-a|<r$. The inverse is the
restriction of $\tau_{-a}$: its original coordinate value is exactly
$|T-a|$, and its whole coefficient point is again unchanged. The inverse
identities and continuity restrict to the actual original subspace topologies.
For $r>0$, transport the CONSTRUCTED strict-disc contraction
through this actual homeomorphism. Its terminal point is the image of
$\eta_0(y)$, namely the whole original centred Gauss point
$\eta_{a,0}(y)$. The transported identity-nullhomotopy proves actual
contractibility. All original polynomial values and coefficient actions
remain those of the displayed original maps, with no chosen chart or
contractibility certificate.
\end{proof}
