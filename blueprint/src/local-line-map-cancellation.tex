\begin{theorem}[All original structure-line endomorphisms commute]
\label{sc0-local-line-cancel:unit-commutativity}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LocalLineMapCancellation.unitEndomorphisms_commute}
\leanok
For any original scheme, every two ENTIRE endomorphisms of its
original structure module commute. No affine, reduced, nonempty,
local-ring or coefficient-comparison hypothesis is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each actual whole module morphism on every original open is
multiplication by the restriction of its actual global value at
one, as already proved from genuine original module naturality.
Thus their two composites at EVERY original section are its product
with those SAME two restricted original coefficients, in opposite
orders. Original commutativity proves equality on every section;
actual whole module-morphism extensionality gives the result.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole original line factors of an isomorphism]
\label{sc0-local-line-cancel:entire-first-factor}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LocalLineMapCancellation.isIso_of_composite_of_frames,EnvelopeProperty.LocalLineMapCancellation.isIso_of_composite_on_localSpec}
\leanok
Given three actual globally framed original line modules and two
composable original maps whose actual composite is an isomorphism,
the SAME entire first map is an isomorphism. On the spectrum of ANY
original local ring, actual cover-local invertibility of the three
original modules derives the necessary global frames internally.
No supplied first inverse, unit determinant or frame-matching
certificate is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Conjugate the SAME maps by the actual whole frames to two original
structure-module endomorphisms. Their composite remains an actual
isomorphism. The preceding commutativity shows that their reverse
composite is also an isomorphism. The first original endomorphism
is therefore both mono and epi, hence is an isomorphism in the
actual abelian module-sheaf category. Conjugating by the SAME actual
frames proves the assertion for the entire first original map.

For a spectrum of an original local ring, choose in an actual
invertibility cover an original open containing its actual closed
point. That open is the entire local spectrum, so the existing
genuine local-spectrum triviality theorem constructs a whole
original frame. Apply this construction to all three original
modules, then the preceding argument.
\uses{sc0-local-line-cancel:unit-commutativity}
\end{proof}

This is the actual cancellation prerequisite for the full canonical
differential along an original valuative lift: original localization
makes the composite differential an isomorphism. Establishing that
actual composite identification and the full rounded module
pushforward remain separate obligations. No vanishing is claimed.
