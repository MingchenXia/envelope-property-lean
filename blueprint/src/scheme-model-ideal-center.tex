\section{Entire ideal images on the actual proper valuative center}

\begin{definition}[Original spectrum of center global sections]
\label{def:actual-center-top-spectrum}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerTopSpectrum}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.isBounded_centerTopSpectrum}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:proper-native-center}
For an actual relative scheme-valued point $y$ of the original proper
model, let $V$ be its residue-field valuation ring. The actual ring
isomorphism $\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}V)\simeq V$, followed by the
actual inclusion into the original residue field, pulls its valuation
back to a spectrum point $z_V$ on the entire original global section
ring. Every such section has value at most one, since its actual
image belongs to the original valuation ring.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Actual center-section restriction]
\label{lem:actual-center-top-spectrum-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerTopSpectrum_comap_centerLift}
\leanok
\uses{def:actual-center-top-spectrum,scheme:proper-center-local-bounds}
Pulling $z_V$ back by the actual section map of the genuine proper
valuative lift, on any original open neighborhood of its actual
closed-point image, gives exactly the original residue-field section
spectrum of $y$ on that neighborhood.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The inverse image of this neighborhood is the entire original local
spectrum. The actual valuative lift generic triangle identifies the
inclusion of its pulled section into the original residue field with
the original section evaluation at $y$. Apply the actual original
residue-field valuation to this equality for every section. Spectrum
extensionality gives the entire asserted equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Intrinsic ideal norm on the entire actual center pullback]
\label{lem:actual-model-ideal-center-pullback-norm}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelIdealNorm_eq_centerPullback}
\leanok
\uses{lem:actual-center-top-spectrum-restriction,scheme:native-model-ideal-function,scheme:genuine-ideal-pullback-stalk}
For every original ideal sheaf $I$ on an original proper model, its
intrinsic original model ideal norm at $y$ equals the ideal norm at
$z_V$ of the entire actual geometric pullback ideal on
$\operatorname{Spec}V$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual affine neighborhood of the actual proper center.
The entire local spectrum maps into this neighborhood. The native
geometric pullback ideal on its actual top affine chart is exactly
the ring-map image of the entire ideal on the chosen neighborhood.
The bounded ideal-image norm identity identifies its norm with that
of the original affine ideal at the pulled spectrum. The preceding
whole spectrum equality identifies this spectrum with the original
section spectrum. The intrinsic model ideal norm equals that affine
ideal norm, by the original intrinsic stalk-ideal calculation. No
Cartier, finite generation, flatness, or affine-target premise is
needed.
\end{proof}
