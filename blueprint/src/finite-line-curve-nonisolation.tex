\subsection{Genuine finite free line parameters and rigid nonisolation on integral curves over any field}

\begin{theorem}[An actual finite free polynomial parameter from genuine curve dimension]
\label{algebra:actual-finite-free-integral-curve-line-parameter}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.exists_finiteFree_injective_polynomial_of_dimension_le_one}
\uses{algebra:integral-normal-height,algebra:affine-domain-maximal-height}
Let $C$ be an actual nonfield finite-type domain over ANY field $F$,
of genuine Krull dimension at most one. There is an actual injective
$F$-algebra homomorphism $F[T]\to_F C$ for which the SAME original
coefficient action makes $C$ a genuine finite free module. No
perfectness, smoothness, normality, etale chart, line map, dimension-one
identification, free cover or freeness certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply actual Noether normalization to the original finite-type
domain. It constructs a genuine finite injective map
$g:F[X_1,\dots,X_n]\to_F C$. Its actual original coefficient law
proves the true scalar tower. The polynomial base is genuinely
Noetherian and normal; $C$ is Noetherian by actual finite type.
Actual integral normal-base going down identifies the height of
every maximal ideal of $C$ with that of its genuine contraction,
and the latter is maximal by actual integral finiteness. The proved
polynomial maximal-height theorem gives this height exactly $n$.

Choose a genuine maximal ideal of the original nontrivial domain.
Since $C$ is not a field, this ideal is nonzero. Its actual height
is therefore nonzero by the domain height-zero criterion. The
original dimension-at-most-one bound makes its height at most one.
Thus the constructed variable count is truly $n=1$, rather than
a supplied coordinate-count or dimension certificate.

The actual one-variable multivariate polynomial equivalence now
transports $g$ to a genuine ORIGINAL map $F[T]\to_F C$, retaining
all coefficient elements. Whole ring-map composition transfers
finiteness and actual injectivity. For that precise polynomial
coefficient action, the actual domain injection makes $C$
torsion-free. The base is the genuine polynomial principal ideal
domain, so its actual finite torsion-free module is free. This
constructs finite freeness over the actual line without perfectness
or any unramified/etale condition, including inseparable cases.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual affine closed curve point is native nonisolated over any valued field]
\label{scheme:actual-integral-curve-closed-nonisolation}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.not_isOpen_singleton_affine_closed_of_dimension_le_one,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.neBot_nhdsNE_affine_closed_of_dimension_le_one,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.not_isOpen_singleton_affine_closed_of_dedekind}
\uses{algebra:actual-finite-free-integral-curve-line-parameter,spectrum:actual-finite-free-open-map,spectrum:actual-rigid-line-nonisolation,scheme:relative-affine-valued-point-homeomorphism}
For EVERY prescribed real valuation on ANY field $F$, every native
point of the actual affine curve $\operatorname{Spec}C$ whose
original scheme support is maximal is nonisolated. Here $C$ is
any genuine nonfield finite-type domain of dimension at most one.
In particular the assertion applies to EVERY actual nonfield
finite-type Dedekind domain, with its dimension bound derived from
the genuine Dedekind structure. No completeness, perfectness,
algebraic closure, normality of a scalar extension, etale chart,
finite residue-degree or nonisolation certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the ACTUAL finite free polynomial parameter constructed above.
The true affine native homeomorphism identifies the original whole
point with its actual coefficient-prescribed seminorm. The real
finite free restriction is open by the proved whole seminorm
producer; exact coefficient identities give its relative openness.

The original support is maximal. Under the genuine integral finite
coefficient map its actual contraction in $F[T]$ is maximal.
The original polynomial ring is not a field, so this maximal ideal
cannot be zero. Thus the ENTIRE image point has actual nonzero
line support. Its nonisolation was proved by the genuine finite raw
residue-field rationalization of ONLY the line, including inseparable
residue degrees, with no completeness or perfectness requirement.
An open source singleton would map to an open image singleton,
contradicting this actual line nonisolation. The genuine affine
native homeomorphism preserves the ENTIRE original point, so the
original native singleton is not open. The standard punctured-
neighborhood identity yields nontriviality of its punctured filter.

For the actual Dedekind specialization, every nonzero prime is
maximal by the genuine Dedekind property, giving its real dimension
bound at most one. Apply the preceding constructed producer; no
dimension or line-chart certificate is inserted. These are genuine
rigid topological assertions, independent of analytic potentials,
subharmonic finite traces or maximum-principle comparisons.
\end{proof}
