\section{Actual global stabilization of the original asymptotic ideals}

\begin{theorem}[Original divisibility increases the whole global constituents]\label{thm:sc0-original-global-multiplier-divisibility}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.schemeMultiplierIdealSheaf_le_of_dvd}
\leanok
\uses{def:sc0-original-global-multiplier-ideal-sheaf}
For the original graded ideal-sheaf sequence $J_\bullet$, with
nonzero actual generic stalk in every positive degree, divisibility
of positive original indices $q\mid q'$ gives
$\mathcal J_X(J_q^{m/q})\subseteq\mathcal J_X(J_{q'}^{m/q'})$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Check the actual global inclusion on every original affine open.
For a nonempty affine open, its actual generic-germ equivalence
maps the original graded ideal sequence to an actual graded
sequence of ideals in the literal section subalgebra: ideal maps
preserve products and inclusions. Its nonzero ideals follow from
the original generic stalks. The proved affine full multiplier
divisibility theorem applies and taking comap gives precisely the
claimed original affine inclusion. For an empty affine open both
actual values are the unit ideal. Thus the inclusion holds on all
actual original affine components.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole global original asymptotic ideal stabilizes]\label{thm:sc0-original-global-asymptotic-stabilization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.exists_schemeAsymptoticMultiplierIdealSheaf_eq}
\leanok
\uses{thm:sc0-original-global-multiplier-divisibility,thm:sc0-directed-original-ideal-sheaf-attainment,def:sc0-original-global-asymptotic-multiplier-ideal}
If the original smooth integral scheme is quasi-compact, then for
each $m$ there is an actual positive original index $q_0$ such that
the whole actual $\mathfrak b_m$ equals
$\mathcal J_X(J_q^{m/q})$ for every positive original $q$ divisible
by $q_0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Index the same original constituents by positive natural numbers.
For two such indices, their product is a positive common multiple;
the preceding actual divisibility inclusions prove this is a
directed family of the original ideal sheaves. Original smoothness
proves local Noetherianity, so the actual global directed-attainment
theorem on the original quasi-compact scheme gives an actual
constituent at $q_0$ equal to the whole supremum. The positive-index
supremum is exactly the original defining supremum: every original
positive natural index appears, and no new constituent is inserted.

For any positive multiple $q$ of $q_0$, divisibility puts the
$q_0$-constituent inside the $q$-constituent; the latter always lies
in the defining supremum. Since the $q_0$-constituent is already
that whole supremum, these inclusions prove the required equality
of actual global ideal sheaves.
\end{proof}
