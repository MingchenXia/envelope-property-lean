\section{Actual adapted coordinates from one regular residual stalk}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:pointwise-regular-divisor-etale-coordinates}
Let $F$ be a perfect field and $Y$ a scheme smooth of relative dimension two over $F$. Let $J$ be an actual ideal sheaf on $Y$, and let $z$ be a closed point such that the actual quotient $\mathcal O_{Y,z}/J_z$ is a regular local ring of dimension one. Inside every prescribed affine neighborhood of $z$, there is an actual affine neighborhood with two sections whose map to $\mathbb A_F^2$ is etale and whose first coordinate cuts out precisely the restriction of $J$ as an ideal sheaf.
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemePointwiseRegularDivisorCoordinates.exists_etale_chart_within}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual local ring $R=\mathcal O_{Y,z}$ is regular, since $Y$ is smooth. At the closed point $z$ its dimension is two. A regular quotient of a regular local ring is generated by an initial segment of a regular system of parameters. Choose such a system, of length $m$, with $c$ initial members generating $J_z$. Its length equals $\dim R=2$, so $m=2$. The quotient dimension formula gives $\dim(R/J_z)+c=\dim R$. The assumed actual quotient dimension is one, hence $c=1$. Thus a two-member regular system of parameters has first member generating exactly $J_z$.

Lift both actual local parameters simultaneously to sections on an affine neighborhood inside the prescribed one. Their differentials at $z$ form a basis of the cotangent space, because the residue extension of a closed point over a perfect field is separable and $Y$ is smooth. The differential criterion supplies a smaller affine neighborhood on which their coordinate map is etale. Restriction preserves their original germs. The actual ideal $J$ and the ideal of the first restricted coordinate therefore have equal stalks at $z$. Both ideals are finitely generated on this Noetherian affine scheme. Clearing the finitely many localization denominators gives a still smaller affine neighborhood on which these entire ideals agree. The coordinate map stays etale under this open restriction. Equality of these affine ideals gives equality of the restricted ideal sheaves, which is the asserted pullback of the coordinate hyperplane. Only the one actual residual stalk was used; no smoothness assumption on a whole original parameter fibre is required.
\end{proof}
