\section{Whole original pullback-power frames and genuine factor triangles}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:fc-actual-power-frame-iso-naturality}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.tensorFrame_naturality,EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.explicitTensorIso_naturality,EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.powerFrame_naturality}
\leanok
\uses{def:fc-actual-entire-power-frame,lem:fc-original-tensor-section-homs,lem:bundle-tensor-power-isomorphism,lem:cartier-actual-monoidal-explicit-tensor-naturality}
For every actual original module isomorphism $a:P\simeq Q$ and
actual whole frame $e:Q\simeq\mathcal O_X$, the actual explicit
power isomorphism followed by $e^{[n]}$ equals the whole power
frame of $ae$. The corresponding tensor-frame identity holds in
both factors, including the genuine explicit tensor comparison.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The tensor frame identity follows by composing the genuine tensor
of the two preceding original isomorphisms with the tensor of the
two frames: functoriality combines the two maps in each factor,
while the final original structure-unit multiplication is the same.
Naturality of the genuine explicit tensor comparison transports
this whole identity to explicit original tensor sheaves. Induct
on $n$. At zero both original maps are the identity structure
frame. At a successor use the actual explicit tensor comparison,
the preceding whole tensor identity, and the induction hypothesis
in the first factor. This proves the actual entire power-frame
identity without a supplied compatibility equation.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}\label{def:fc-actual-pulled-power-frame}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.pullbackPowerFrame,EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.pullbackPowerFrame_zero,EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.pullbackPowerFrame_succ}
\leanok
\uses{def:fc-actual-entire-power-frame,lem:bundle-tensor-power-pullback,lem:fc-actual-power-frame-iso-naturality}
For any actual $f:Y\to X$ and actual entire frame $e:f^*M\simeq
\mathcal O_Y$, the true native power pullback isomorphism followed
by $e^{[n]}$ gives an entire frame of the same original $f^*(M^{\otimes n})$.
At zero this is the actual native unit comparison. At each successor,
it is the actual pulled explicit tensor comparison, followed by
the inverse native tensorator and the genuine tensor frame of the
preceding pulled power frame and $e$.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The stated composition consists of actual original module
isomorphisms and therefore is an entire frame. Expand the native
power pullback isomorphism at a successor. The true explicit tensor
comparison on $f^*M$ cancels its inverse in the original power-frame
recursion. Genuine tensor-frame naturality then combines the
preceding pullback-power comparison with the previous original
power frame in the first factor, giving exactly the asserted
recursive whole frame. At zero the native pullback power
comparison is the actual structure-unit comparison and the
original zeroth power frame is the identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:fc-actual-factor-frame-tensor-naturality}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.frameViaFactor_naturality,EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.frameViaFactor_unit,EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.frameViaFactor_tensor,EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.frameViaFactor_explicitTensor}
\leanok
\uses{def:actual-line-frame-via-factor,lem:actual-line-frame-via-factor-composition,lem:fc-actual-power-frame-iso-naturality,lem:actual-center-generic-tensor-frame}
For any actual factor triangle $ib=g$, transporting actual entire
frames through $i$ commutes with any original module isomorphism,
the native structure-unit frame and the genuine pulled tensor
frame, including its original explicit tensor comparison.
All native comparisons come from the actual triangle.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine factor comparison is the native pullback-composition
isomorphism followed by the native comparison of equal scheme
maps. Its naturality gives the whole module-isomorphism identity
by canceling this comparison with its inverse. For the structure
frame, genuine unit compatibility with composition and equal-map
comparison gives the native unit frame on $g$. For the tensor
frame, actual native pullback composition is monoidal, and the
comparison of equal maps is monoidal by substitution. Their
composite therefore preserves the genuine entire pulled tensor
frame. The native monoidal comparison theorem gives the whole
frame identity, with no frame-preservation hypothesis. Finally
apply module-isomorphism naturality to the actual explicit tensor
comparison and the preceding tensor identity. This proves the
explicit-tensor statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:fc-actual-pulled-power-frame-factor-coherence}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.pullbackPowerFrame_factor,EnvelopeProperty.SchemePulledPowerFrames.pullbackPowerFrame_generic}
\leanok
\uses{def:fc-actual-pulled-power-frame,lem:fc-actual-factor-frame-tensor-naturality,thm:fc-generic-power-frame-true-norm}
For every actual original triangle $ib=g$ and frame $e:b^*M\simeq
\mathcal O$, transporting the genuine pulled power frame through
$i$ equals the genuine pulled power frame through $g$ of the
transported degree-one frame. These are equal entire original
isomorphisms for all $n\geq0$. For the actual native residue map,
the resulting whole pulled power frames are exactly the genuine
native generic power frames. No factor, pullback, frame or power
compatibility identity is an additional assumption.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on $n$. The structure-frame statement of the preceding
lemma proves the zero case. At a successor, expand the actual
pulled power-frame recursion. The preceding explicit-tensor
factor identity moves the genuine factor transport past its
native explicit comparison and tensor frame. The induction
hypothesis identifies the previous original power frame; the
other factor is the actual transported degree-one frame. This
is exactly the successor recursion on $g$. For an actual residue
map the same zero and successor recursions are precisely those
of the native generic power frames. Induction therefore proves
that entire frame identity as well.
\end{proof}
