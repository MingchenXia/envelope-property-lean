\section{Every original invertible line has continuous true whole-section norms}
\label{sec:scheme-global-section-norm-continuity}

\begin{lemma}[True global norms are bounded and actual generators attain one]
\label{lem:scheme-original-global-generator-norms}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalSectionNormContinuity.globalSectionNorm_le_one,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalSectionNormContinuity.exists_generator_norm_one}
\leanok
\uses{lem:generated-line-top-sections,thm:actual-model-line-generator-norm-one,thm:scheme-global-section-model-norm-isometry}
On an actual proper original model over a bounded valued base, every
whole section of an actual invertible line has true norm at most one.
For every genuine global generating family of such a line, one of its
same actual whole sections has norm exactly one at each original native
point. Finiteness and integrality are not needed for these assertions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A whole section is an actual homomorphism from the original structure
line. Its true fibre norm equals the true model homomorphism norm. The
original valuative-center coefficients are integral, so this norm is at
most one. For a generating family, its actual top sections give exactly
its original free-basis generating homomorphisms. The genuine model-line
generator theorem gives a member of norm one at the same native point.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[True original section norms are finite tensor-generator maxima]
\label{thm:scheme-original-section-tensor-generator-norm}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalSectionNormContinuity.globalSectionNorm_tensor_generators_sup}
\leanok
\uses{lem:scheme-original-global-generator-norms,thm:scheme-global-section-tensor-norm}
Let $M,T$ be actual original invertible lines and let $(t_i)$ be any
actual finite global generating family of $T$. Every original whole
section $s$ of $M$ has true norm
\[
 \|s\|=\max_i\|s\otimes t_i\|
\]
at every actual original native point, using the actual sheafified tensor
sections and the true proper-model norms. No norm formula or matching
certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine tensor-section norm theorem gives
$\|s\otimes t_i\|=\|s\|\,\|t_i\|$. Each generator norm is at most one,
so every tensor norm is at most $\|s\|$. At this same native point some
actual generator has norm one, making its tensor norm exactly $\|s\|$.
The finite supremum therefore equals the original norm, including if
$s$ vanishes or the norm is zero at the given point.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Continuity for every whole section of every actual original invertible line]
\label{thm:scheme-original-global-section-norm-continuous}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeGlobalSectionNormContinuity.continuous_globalSectionNorm}
\leanok
\uses{thm:scheme-original-section-tensor-generator-norm,thm:native-ample-line-global-section-norm-continuous,thm:fc-original-ample-line-twist-very-ample,lem:scheme-ample-line-eventual-generation,thm:fc-affine-projectivity-polynomial-factorization}
On every integral original scheme projective over its original field,
the true original proper-model norm of every whole section of every
actual original invertible line is continuous on the entire original
native analytic space. No ampleness, global generation, norm continuity,
model comparison or finite tensor-family certificate for the given line
is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An actual projective factorization of the original scheme supplies a
whole original pulled $\mathcal O(1)$, which is genuinely very ample and
hence geometrically ample. Let it be $A$. Actual geometric ample-twist
generation makes $A^{\otimes m}\otimes M$ very ample for every sufficiently
large $m$. Actual eventual global generation also gives a genuine finite
generating family of $T=A^{\otimes m}$ for all sufficiently large $m$.
Choose one common $m$. The actual tensor symmetry makes $M\otimes T$
geometrically ample as well. Every true whole-section norm on this
numerator line is therefore continuous by the genuine ample-line theorem.
Apply the preceding exact tensor-generator maximum to the given original
$s$ and this internally constructed finite generating family. Each tensor
section $s\otimes t_i$ is an actual whole section of that ample numerator,
so each of its true norms is continuous. Their finite maximum is
continuous and equals the same original norm of $s$ at every actual
native point. This proves the full assertion, retaining zeros throughout.
\end{proof}
