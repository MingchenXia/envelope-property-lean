\section{Original relative projective sections lift to original polynomials}
\label{sec:c0n-section-polynomial-lift}

\begin{lemma}[One fixed bound gives all original homogeneous polynomial lifts]
\label{lem:c0n-relative-homogeneous-polynomial-lift}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Char0.RelativeHomogeneousSectionLift.exists_polynomial_lift_bound,EnvelopeProperty.Char0.RelativeHomogeneousSectionLift.exists_power_polynomial_lift_bound}
Let $R$ be the original Noetherian commutative coefficient ring and
$i:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_R^N$ the original closed immersion. Write
$H=i^*\mathcal O(1)$ for the SAME native coordinate-zero hyperplane line.
There is one $c_{\rm lift}\in\mathbb N$, depending only on this fixed
immersion, with the following property. For every $e\ge c_{\rm lift}$ and
every original whole section $s\in\Gamma(Y,H^{\otimes e})$, there is an
original homogeneous polynomial
\[
 F\in R[x_0,\ldots,x_N]_e
\]
whose original native $\mathcal O(e)$ section, pulled back by the original
$i$ and transported by the genuine native hyperplane-power comparison, is
exactly $s$. In particular this applies to the original ring $R=k[t]$,
and includes $N=0$ and zero or disconnected closed schemes. No polynomial
lifting, projective normality, expected section comparison, or desired
vanishing is an additional premise.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take the fixed bound already produced from the ENTIRE original defining
kernel and native Serre vanishing in
Lemma~\ref{lem:c0n-eventual-relative-section-restriction}. Apply that theorem
to the original section $s$, using its proved hyperplane-power comparison.
It produces an actual ambient section
$t\in\Gamma(\mathbb P_R^N,\mathcal O(e))$ whose actual original native
pullback is $s$ after that comparison.

The separate ambient whole-section polynomial theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:c0n-ambient-section-homogeneous-lift}), over the SAME
original commutative ring $R$, produces $F$ of degree exactly $e$ with
original native homogeneous section equal to $t$. This theorem uses the
actual native coordinate fractions and their whole overlap identities;
the Laurent support intersection and actual empty-intersection
reconstruction recover the original nonnegative homogeneous polynomial
from all chart coefficients. It does not replace
$R$ by a field or discard coordinate factors. Substitute its proved section
equality into the preceding actual pullback equality. This gives precisely
the asserted equality for $s$ and the SAME $F$.

Every quantifier is over the original objects. The bound is independent
of $e$, multiplier-ideal indices, and finite generating families. Choosing
the resulting polynomial for each member of any finite family therefore
gives a finite family of actual original homogeneous polynomials, all with
the SAME prescribed degree. No additional surjectivity premise is needed
for this last choice.
\uses{lem:c0n-eventual-relative-section-restriction,thm:c0n-ambient-section-homogeneous-lift}
\end{proof}
