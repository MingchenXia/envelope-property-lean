\subsection{Actual Rees charts and principal centers over arbitrary rings}

Let $A$ be ANY original commutative ring and $g:\iota\to A$ any family.
Retain the SAME original homogeneous kernel quotient $Q$ and its actual
chart $C_j=(Q[1/Y_j])_0$. Let $S_j=A[1/g_j]$ be its genuine original
principal localization. No coefficient is assumed nonzero or regular;
$S_j$ and the chart may be the zero ring.

\begin{theorem}[The actual chart map into the original coefficient localization]
\label{algebra:actual-rees-chart-away-map}
\uses{algebra:actual-rees-quotient-evaluation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.localizationToAway,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.localizationToAway_algebraMap,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.toAway,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.toAway_mk}
\leanok
There is a genuinely constructed $A$-algebra homomorphism
$C_j\to S_j$ preserving EVERY original coefficient. Every actual
homogeneous fraction with numerator $a$ of degree $d$ maps to the
ORIGINAL fraction $e(a)/g_j^d$ in $S_j$, where $e:Q\to A$ is the
actual quotient evaluation at the SAME original family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose the ACTUAL quotient evaluation $Q\to A$ with the original
$A\to S_j$. The image of $Y_j$ is the genuine unit $g_j$ in this
actual localization, including the zero-ring case. The true ungraded
localization universal property therefore extends the whole map through
$Q[1/Y_j]$. Restrict it along the actual degree-zero inclusion.
Every original coefficient follows the genuine coefficient tower.
Applying the whole localization map to the actual numerator-denominator
identity gives the stated fraction formula by the localization unit law.
No domain or fraction field is used or selected.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole chart injection through actual torsion annihilation]
\label{algebra:actual-rees-chart-away-injective}
\uses{algebra:actual-rees-chart-away-map,algebra:actual-rees-presentation}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.toAway_injective}
\leanok
The constructed $C_j\to S_j$ is injective on ENTIRE chart elements
for EVERY commutative $A$ and original family, even when $g_j$ is
zero, nilpotent or a zero divisor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Represent any chart element in the kernel by an ACTUAL homogeneous
fraction and lift its numerator to an ORIGINAL same-degree homogeneous
polynomial $F_d$. Since the image fraction vanishes, its actual unit
denominator gives $F_d(g)=0$ in $A[1/g_j]$. The TRUE localization
kernel criterion supplies an original integer $m$ with
$g_j^mF_d(g)=0$ in the ORIGINAL $A$.

The polynomial $Y_j^mF_d$ is genuinely homogeneous of degree $m+d$.
Its ORIGINAL presentation is $g_j^mF_d(g)T^{m+d}=0$, so it lies in the
SAME original presentation kernel. Thus the whole original quotient
numerator $a$ satisfies $Y_j^m a=0$ in $Q$. This actual annihilator is
an allowed denominator in $Q[1/Y_j]$, so the original homogeneous
fraction is zero there. The genuine degree-zero inclusion is injective,
so the original chart element is zero. Subtraction now proves whole
map injectivity. This uses real localization torsion, not cancellation
of an originally regular coefficient or an assumed chart embedding.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original whole chart is its actual localization-adjoin image]
\label{algebra:actual-rees-chart-away-equivalence}
\uses{algebra:actual-rees-chart-away-injective}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.toAway_isLocalization,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.awayRatios,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.away_mk_pow,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.toAway_coordinateRatio,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.coordinateRatio_self,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.aeval_awayRatios,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.awayAlgebra,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.range_toAway,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.awayEquiv,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.awayEquiv_coe,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.awayEquiv_algebraMap}
\leanok
The WHOLE image is the actual original $A$-subalgebra
\[
 A[g_i/g_j:i\in\iota]\subseteq A[1/g_j],
\]
where each ratio is its true numerator times the genuine inverse of
$g_j$ in this SAME localization. Hence $C_j$ is genuinely equivalent
to that entire algebra, preserving all original coefficients and all
actual homogeneous fractions. The selected coordinate fraction is genuinely one, including the zero chart.
Moreover the SAME $S_j$, with its coefficient action supplied by this
actual chart map, is genuinely the localization of $C_j$ at its ORIGINAL
coefficient $g_j$. No scalar-tower compatibility is assumed as a certificate.
No finite-family hypothesis is needed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every original ratio is the image of its genuine degree-zero variable
fraction. Thus the entire adjoin is contained in the true image.
For the reverse inclusion, use an actual homogeneous fraction and its
original same-degree lift $F_d$. The full original homogeneous Rees
identity, evaluated at the TRUE inverse of $g_j$ in $A[1/g_j]$, gives
\[
 F_d(g)/g_j^d=F_d((g_i/g_j)_i).
\]
This is equality of whole polynomial ring evaluations in the actual
coefficient localization, proved from the entire homomorphism and
unit identities. The right side belongs to the actual adjoin.
Therefore EVERY original chart value lies in that adjoin. The proved
whole injection and actual image equality give a genuine algebra
equivalence. No choice of a fraction field, domain or target chart
isomorphism is an input, and zero/empty chart cases remain included.

For the localization assertion, install EXACTLY the chart-algebra action
defined by the constructed whole map $C_j\to S_j$. Its selected coefficient
is a genuine unit, so every power is a unit. Every element of $S_j$ has an
ORIGINAL $A$-numerator and power-of-$g_j$ denominator; mapping those original
coefficients through $A\to C_j$ proves actual chart fraction surjectivity.
Finally, equality of two chart elements after mapping into $S_j$ already
implies their original equality by the proved whole injection, so the
localization kernel axiom holds with annihilator one. These verify all
three localization axioms directly, with the exact original coefficient
action and without an additional scalar tower.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original center becomes a genuine principal regular ideal]
\label{algebra:actual-rees-center-principalization}
\uses{algebra:actual-rees-chart-away-equivalence}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.coefficient_mul_coordinateRatio,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.centerIdeal,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.centerIdeal_eq_span,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.coefficient_mem_nonZeroDivisors,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.centerMultiplication,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.centerLinearEquiv,
EnvelopeProperty.ReesChart.centerLinearEquiv_coe}
\leanok
For the ORIGINAL center $I=(g_i)$, its actual ideal extension in
$C_j$ is exactly the principal ideal generated by the original
coefficient $g_j$. That genuine generator is a non-zero-divisor
in $C_j$. Multiplication by it gives an actual linear equivalence
from $C_j$ onto the extended original center ideal, preserving its
entire original multiplication formula. No numerical rank-one or
nontriviality assertion is made for a zero chart.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
After the proved WHOLE chart injection into $A[1/g_j]$, each relation
$g_i=g_j(Y_i/Y_j)$ is the actual numerator-inverse identity. Injection
therefore proves this ENTIRE ring relation already in $C_j$.
Every original center generator lies in the true principal ideal
$(g_j)$, while the original selected $g_j$ is one of those center
generators. Actual ideal map/span identities give both inclusions,
without a radical or support replacement.

The image of $g_j$ under the actual injective chart map is a genuine
unit, hence a non-zero-divisor. Injectivity reflects that actual
regularity to the original chart, including zero rings. Multiplication
by this SAME original generator is consequently injective. Its image
is the proved principal center ideal by the actual principal ideal
membership criterion; the whole multiplication map is linear.
These real injection and surjection laws construct the stated
linear equivalence, rather than assuming invertibility or a Cartier
principalization certificate. Global blowup universal properties and
birational scheme assembly remain separate consumers of these exact
original chart maps.
\end{proof}
