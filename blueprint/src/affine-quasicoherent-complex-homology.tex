\section{Native homology of the same entire affine quasicoherent complex}
\label{sec:affine-quasicoherent-complex-homology}

Let $R$ be ANY original commutative ring, including zero, and
let $K$ be ANY original native cochain complex on
$\operatorname{Spec}R$ whose actual original terms are
quasicoherent. No exactness, replacement resolution or
homology comparison is supplied.

\begin{lemma}[The actual whole counit complex isomorphism]
\label{lem:affine-quasicoherent-whole-counit-complex}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.AffineQuasicoherentComplexHomology.sectionComplex,EnvelopeProperty.AffineQuasicoherentComplexHomology.complexIso}
Apply the actual native $R$-module global-section functor to
$K$. The actual associated-sheaf image of this SAME whole
section complex is isomorphic to $K$. Every term comparison
is its original tilde--global-section adjunction counit.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual native global-section functor is additive and
preserves zero maps, so it gives a genuine native section
complex. Quasicoherence of each original term makes its
actual adjunction counit an isomorphism. The native counit
naturality for EACH original differential gives the whole
complex comparison; no chosen differential compatibility
or replacement sheaf map occurs.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The original native homology is associated]
\label{prop:affine-quasicoherent-complex-original-homology}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.AffineQuasicoherentComplexHomology.homologyIso,EnvelopeProperty.AffineQuasicoherentComplexHomology.homology_isQuasicoherent}
For every $q\geq0$, the actual original native homology sheaf
of $K$ is isomorphic to the associated sheaf of the actual
native homology module of its SAME original section complex.
In particular the original homology sheaf is quasicoherent.
\uses{lem:affine-quasicoherent-whole-counit-complex,thm:cartier-tilde-original-exact-homology}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The genuine associated-sheaf functor preserves native
homology by its already proved exactness. Compose that
actual native comparison, in the appropriate direction,
with the native homology functor applied to the preceding
whole counit complex isomorphism. Both actual complexes,
original differential rows and native projections are
retained. The resulting homology object is associated and
thus quasicoherent. This derives the comparison from actual
term quasicoherence; it does not assume that arbitrary
module-sheaf global sections preserve homology.
\end{proof}

The geometric smooth de Rham consumer internally supplies
these term hypotheses. Canonical Cartier descent and
Deligne--Illusie still require their actual whole maps.
