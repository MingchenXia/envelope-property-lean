\section{The genuine infinitesimal short exact sequence of module sheaves}

\begin{lemma}[The whole original ideal-power module is the actual section kernel]
\label{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-whole-module-kernel}
\uses{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-projection-kernel}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sectionMap,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sectionMap_app,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.projection_isLocallyMem_iff,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.inclusion_comp_sectionMap,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sequence,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.section_exact,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.section_injective}
\leanok
For the literal original $q_n:X_n\to X$, the genuine canonical module
map $\mathcal O_X\to q_{n*}\mathcal O_{X_n}$ has on every original
open the actual section map $q_n^\sharp$. The same geometric ideal-power
module $(I\mathcal O_X)^n$ includes into $\mathcal O_X$, and its whole
section space on every open is exactly the kernel of this map. The
inclusion is injective on every original open.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The canonical module map to the pushforward of the structure module is
the genuine original scheme section map. Membership in the actual
kernel ideal sheaf implies that this section vanishes, by locality of
sections. Conversely, if an entire section vanishes, its restriction to
every affine open vanishes by naturality. The actual affine kernel
calculation therefore puts every such restriction in the same
geometric ideal power. This is exactly the defining membership condition
for its genuine ideal module on the original open. Consequently its
subtype inclusion is injective, its image is the entire section kernel,
and its composite with the canonical section map is zero on every
open. These maps form the stated actual complex of sheaves of modules.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original ideal module is the categorical kernel]
\label{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-categorical-kernel}
\uses{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-whole-module-kernel}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.kernelLift,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.kernelLift_inclusion,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.kernelIsLimit,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sequence_exact}
\leanok
The actual inclusion of $(I\mathcal O_X)^n$ is a categorical kernel
of $\mathcal O_X\to q_{n*}\mathcal O_{X_n}$; in particular the same
original complex is exact.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let a morphism of module sheaves into $\mathcal O_X$ have zero composite
with the genuine section map. On every original open its entire image
lies in the kernel just calculated, so regard each value as a section
of the same ideal module. The original morphism is additive, linear and
commutes with restriction; the resulting subtype-valued map has all
three properties because its underlying functions are unchanged. It
therefore gives a genuine morphism into the original ideal module.
Composing with inclusion recovers the original morphism on every open.
Uniqueness follows from the injective subtype inclusion. This is the
categorical kernel universal property and proves exactness.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The original infinitesimal module sequence is short exact]
\label{prop:fibre-infinitesimal-module-short-exact}
\uses{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-categorical-kernel,lem:fibre-infinitesimal-projection-kernel}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sectionMap_isLocallySurjective,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sectionMap_epi,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sequence_shortExact}
\leanok
For every order $n\ge0$ there is a genuine short exact sequence on the
same original scheme $X$,
\[
 0\longrightarrow (I\mathcal O_X)^n\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X
 \longrightarrow q_{n*}\mathcal O_{X_n}\longrightarrow0,
\]
using the actual ideal-module inclusion and actual canonical scheme
section map. No affine, Noetherian, flatness, properness or
positive-order hypothesis is required.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The first map is a monomorphism and is the categorical kernel by the
preceding lemma. The second map is locally surjective: around every
point of any original open, choose an original affine open contained
in it. Every restricted section on its actual inverse image lifts to
an original section there, by the proved closed-immersion affine
surjectivity theorem. Thus its image sieve covers every original open.
The corresponding morphism of sheaves of abelian groups is an
epimorphism; the faithful forgetful functor from sheaves of modules
reflects epimorphisms. Together with the proved kernel this gives the
asserted actual short exact sequence.
\end{proof}


\begin{corollary}[The original sequence on every affine intersection]
\label{cor:fibre-infinitesimal-affine-module-short-exact}
\uses{prop:fibre-infinitesimal-module-short-exact,lem:fibre-infinitesimal-projection-kernel}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sectionSequence,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.sectionSequence_exact,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalModules.affine_sectionSequence_shortExact}
\leanok
Evaluation of the original sequence on any open $W\subset X$ is exact
at its middle module, with its actual $\mathcal O_X(W)$-module
structures. On every affine open $U$, it is a short exact sequence of
$\mathcal O_X(U)$-modules. Thus the same assertion holds on every affine
intersection of any original open cover.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Evaluation retains the two original section maps and their zero
composite. Their section kernel on every open was identified above
with the image of the original ideal module, proving exactness there.
On an affine open the inclusion is injective and the second map is
surjective by the closed-immersion affine section theorem. These three
properties are precisely short exactness of modules. An intersection
that is affine is itself an affine open, so it has the stated
sequence with its unchanged original section maps and scalars.
\end{proof}
