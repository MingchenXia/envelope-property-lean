\begin{lemma}[Existing multigraded torus extraction]
\label{lem:actual-multigraded-torus-component-extraction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.scaleVar,EnvelopeProperty.scaleVar_monomial,EnvelopeProperty.coeff_scaleVar,EnvelopeProperty.varPart_mem_span_scaleVar,EnvelopeProperty.monomial_coeff_mem_of_scaleVar,EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.scaleVar_mem_imageS,EnvelopeProperty.MultiModel.imageS_self}
\leanok
\uses{def:gl-model}
Let $k$ be ANY field, $A$ ANY commutative $k$-algebra, and
$\tau$ ANY variable type. Retain the EXISTING original map
$s_{c,u}$ of $A[Z_i\mid i\in\tau]$ given by $Z_c\mapsto uZ_c$,
fixing every other variable and every original $A$ coefficient.
Its exact monomial and coefficient formulas are
\[
 s_{c,u}(bZ^m)=u^{m(c)}bZ^m,\qquad
 (s_{c,u}p)_m=u^{m(c)}p_m.
\]
If $k$ is infinite, the part of $p$ of degree $n$ in $Z_c$ belongs to the original
$k$-linear span of $\{s_{c,u}p:u\in k^\times\}$.
If in addition $\tau$ is finite and an original $k$-submodule $N$ is stable under all these nonzero
scalings, then every original monomial component $p_aZ^a$ of every
$p\in N$ belongs to $N$.
For an ORIGINAL model $X$ with coefficient field $K$ and any field
extension $E/K$ with its original $k$ scalar tower, let $S_E'$ be the
literal coefficientwise image of $X.S$ in $E[Z_1,\ldots,Z_r]$.
For ANY field $k$, this original image is stable under every
$s_{c,u}$, including $u=0$. For $E=K$ it is exactly $X.S$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The existing scaling map is the original polynomial evaluation at
the displayed variables; multiplication of its monomials gives both
formulas. Let $D$ be the largest degree in the chosen variable among
the finite support of $p$. For $n>D$ the part is zero. Otherwise take
$D+1$ actual distinct units of the infinite field. Their Vandermonde
matrix has nonzero determinant and is invertible. The $n$-th row of
its inverse expresses the chosen degree part as the indicated
$k$-linear combination, by the exact original coefficient formula.
For the final assertion, apply this one-variable projection
successively to the finite set of variables. The coefficient at
$m$ after a set of projections is $p_m$ precisely when each already
selected coordinate of $m$ agrees with $a$, and zero otherwise.
After all variables it is exactly $p_aZ^a$. These are the existing
original proofs, with no completeness, field norm or reducedness
assumption on $A$.
For the model image, every original component is still in that image,
by its original grading and the coefficientwise map. Expand the finite
original polynomial support. The exact scaling formula multiplies
each such component by an original $k$ scalar, so their sum is still
in the same image. For the identity extension the coefficient map is
the identity on every original coefficient, giving the exact image
equality. No normalization is needed for this image assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Existing original DVR and parameter components]
\label{lem:actual-original-rees-component-inputs}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.addVal_ringEquiv_apply,EnvelopeProperty.algebraMap_dvd_algebraMap_C_coeff_mul_X_pow}
\leanok
\uses{def:bj-rees,algebra:rootable-action-fixes-minimal-primes}
Every ring automorphism of ANY discrete valuation ring preserves its
normalized additive valuation, including its value at zero.
Let $k$ be algebraically closed, $A$ ANY domain and original
$k$-algebra, and $g_i=a_it^{n_i}\in A[t]$. Suppose the normalized
chart of an actual original Rees index $x$ is Noetherian, and retain
its entire original valuation ring $V_x$ and inclusion $\eta_x$.
Then for every original $F\in A[t]$ and $n\in\mathbb N$,
\[
 \eta_x(F)\mid\eta_x(t^nF_n).
\]
No finite-type hypothesis on $A$ or membership of a power of $t$ in
the generated ideal is needed for this existing component assertion.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In the first assertion, write a nonzero original element as a unit
times a power of an irreducible. A ring automorphism sends the unit
to a unit and the irreducible to an irreducible, preserving the same
power and thus its normalized value. Zero is fixed.
For the second, the original parameter action $t\mapsto ut$ preserves
each original chart, integral closure and selected principal ideal.
The rootable-action minimal-prime argument fixes the actual prime,
so it restricts to a ring automorphism of the SAME $V_x$. Its
normalized order is unchanged by the first assertion. Distinct
nonzero scalars and the existing one-variable Vandermonde identity
express $t^nF_n$ as a $k$-linear combination of the scaled original
$F$'s. Each is divisible by $\eta_x(F)$, so the entire sum is as well.
These are the existing original parameter and valuation assertions;
the shared minimal-prime argument preserves their original types.
\end{proof}
