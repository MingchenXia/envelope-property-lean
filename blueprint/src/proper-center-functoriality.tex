\subsection{Genuine functoriality of proper model centers}
\label{subsec:proper-center-functoriality}

The prescribed seminorm $v$ on the arbitrary commutative ring $R$ is
bounded on every coefficient. Both actual structure maps to
$\operatorname{Spec}R$ are proper. The intervening scheme morphism need
only commute with those structure maps; no properness assumption on it,
Noetherian hypothesis or field base is imposed.

\begin{lemma}[Actual valuation-ring pullback]
\label{scheme:proper-center-valuation-ring-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_val,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_maximalIdeal_comap,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.isLocalHom_centerRingMap,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_fraction,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.residueBaseMap_naturality,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_base}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:bounded-native-valuative-square,scheme:relative-valued-point-functor,
scheme:valued-point-pullback}
For a commuting scheme morphism $g:X\to Y$ and a native prescribed-base
point $y$ of $X$, the genuine residue-field map
$\kappa(g(x))\to\kappa(x)$ restricts to a ring homomorphism
$V_{g(y)}\to V_y$ preserving every valuation value. It is local, contracts
the maximal ideal exactly, and commutes with the actual generic inclusions
and the genuine bounded coefficient maps.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The native image valuation is the actual pullback through the residue-field
map. Each element of $V_{g(y)}$ therefore maps into $V_y$, proving the
codomain-restricted ring map and its entire value preservation. The maximal
ideals of these valuation rings consist exactly of elements of value
smaller than one, so their contraction is exact. This is precisely the
local-homomorphism criterion for two actual local rings. The generic
inclusion square follows on every ring element by construction. Actual
global residue evaluation naturality and the commuting structure-map
section diagram prove coefficient equality in the residue fields, and
valuation-ring subtype injectivity upgrades it to actual coefficient equality
inside the valuation rings. Equality of values alone is not used to infer
coefficient equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole proper lifts commute with model morphisms]
\label{scheme:proper-center-lift-naturality}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerLift_naturality}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:proper-center-valuation-ring-map,scheme:proper-native-center}
The constructed proper lifts satisfy the whole scheme equality
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}V_y\longrightarrow X\xrightarrow{g}Y
 =\operatorname{Spec}V_y\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}V_{g(y)}
   \longrightarrow Y.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Form the actual valuative square for $Y$ with valuation ring $V_y$,
fraction field $\kappa(x)$ and top map the genuine residue point of $X$
followed by $g$. The first candidate lift is the actual proper lift for
$X$ followed by $g$; its two triangles commute by the original triangles
and the actual base diagram. The second candidate is the actual valuation-ring
Spec map followed by the proper lift for $Y$. Its generic triangle follows
from the genuine ring inclusion square, the proper generic triangle, and
actual residue-field map naturality. Its base triangle follows from the
actual coefficient ring equality above. Separatedness of the proper map
for $Y$ makes the two genuine whole lift structures equal, hence the two
scheme morphisms equal. No generic extension or lift identity is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual centers commute with proper model morphisms]
\label{scheme:proper-center-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerRingMap_closedPoint,
EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.center_map}
\leanok
\uses{scheme:proper-center-lift-naturality,scheme:proper-center-valuation-ring-map}
The true model centers obey $g(c(y))=c(g(y))$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The valuation-ring map is genuinely local, so its Spec map takes the
closed point of $V_y$ to the closed point of $V_{g(y)}$. Evaluate the
whole lift naturality equality at that actual closed point. By the
center definition the two resulting scheme points are exactly the
asserted centers. This concerns the constructed model centers, distinct
from the already functorial kernel supports.
\end{proof}
