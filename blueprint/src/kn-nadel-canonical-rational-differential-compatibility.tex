\section{The original rational canonical trace preserves the original differential}

\begin{theorem}[Compatibility on the same entire original generic fiber]
\label{thm:kn-canonical-original-rational-differential-compatibility}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.differential_comp_genericRationalCanonicalTrace_captured}
\uses{def:kn-canonical-original-rational-trace,def:kn-canonical-original-generic-comparison,thm:kn-canonical-original-generic-differential-iso}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ be the original entire normalized adapted cover, with
its original smooth integral SNC and tame data. Write
$\delta:\pi^*\omega_X\to\omega_Y$ for its original canonical differential.
Let $T_{\mathrm{rat}}:\pi_*\omega_Y\to\gamma_*\gamma^*\omega_X$ be the
original rational canonical trace. Independently, let
$R_{\mathrm{rat}}:\pi_*\pi^*\omega_X\to\gamma_*\gamma^*\omega_X$ be
native restriction to the entire original generic fiber, followed by the
original cartesian source comparison and the entire generic algebra
trace with line coefficients. Then
\[
  (\pi_*\delta)\,T_{\mathrm{rat}}=R_{\mathrm{rat}}.
\]
No inverse differential, finite-cover certificate, trace map or desired
compatibility equation is supplied by the caller. This is an identity
of rational maps; it asserts no regular canonical trace.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original generic differential is invertible on the same entire
generic fiber. Its original inverse followed by the native cartesian
comparison therefore carries that differential to the cartesian
comparison itself. Naturality of the native module restriction square
moves the pushed differential through restriction. Apply the entire
generic pushforward, then the same whole field trace and original line
projection formula. The resulting composite is exactly the independent
pulled-line rational trace.

The formal proof retains the compiled original maps and their actual
instance arguments. Three supporting definitional readbacks inspect
only existing constant bodies. Ordinary equality reflexivity and
equality transport give the final proof, which is checked by the normal
Lean kernel. These adapters add no separate mathematical assertion,
axiom, replacement instance or result certificate.
\end{proof}
