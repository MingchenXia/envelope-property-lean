\subsection{Actual closed immersions of native valued scheme points}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine closed immersion and exact support-closed image]
\label{scheme:native-closed-immersion}

  EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeValuedPoint.closedImmersionResidueIso_hom,

\uses{scheme:affine-chart-section-values,spectrum:surjective-closed-embedding,scheme:valued-open-embedding,scheme:canonical-native-affine-atlas}
An actual closed immersion $f:X\to Y$ of arbitrary schemes induces a
closed embedding of native valued-point spaces. Its image consists
exactly of native points whose true scheme support lies in the genuine
closed scheme image. The resulting map is a homeomorphism onto this
support-closed locus. No Hausdorff, field, noetherian or finite-type
hypothesis is required. The induced native map is also a proper
topological map. For actual scheme ideal-sheaf data $I$, its
actual closed subscheme therefore identifies with the native points
supported on the genuine scheme support of $I$. On every actual affine
open $U$ its native points are genuinely parametrized by the seminorm
spectrum of $\Gamma(Y,U)/I(U)$, preserving every original section value.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual closed immersions have injective scheme-point maps and surjective
stalk maps. A genuine residue class on the source comes from a source
stalk element and hence from a target stalk element. Residue naturality
therefore makes the actual residue-field map surjective; as a field map
it is injective. The genuine inverse residue-field isomorphism lifts
every valued point over the closed scheme image, and whole residue
values give injectivity of the resulting native point map. This proves
the exact image statement, not merely inclusion in that image.

For every actual affine open $U$ of $Y$, its genuine preimage is affine
and the actual section homomorphism
$\Gamma(Y,U)\to\Gamma(X,f^{-1}U)$ is surjective. The independently proved
native affine-open homeomorphisms identify the entire restricted native
map with seminorm pullback under this actual section map: residue and
section evaluation naturality agree on every section. The proved
surjective-ring-map spectrum theorem makes that pullback a closed
embedding. Conjugate by the genuine affine-open homeomorphisms.

All genuine affine support opens cover the target native space. The true
topological locality theorem for closed embeddings at an open cover of
the target therefore gives the global closed embedding. Its canonical
homeomorphism onto its image, together with the exact support-image
equality, gives the stated support-closed homeomorphism. The construction
uses actual scheme maps, stalks, residues and all section values; it
assumes no native embedding or chart compatibility certificate. A
closed embedding is proper, since it is a closed continuous injection
with fibers empty or singletons; this gives the proper-map conclusion.
Mathlib constructs the actual closed immersion of the scheme defined
by ideal-sheaf data and proves its scheme image is precisely the support
of that datum. Specializing the proved homeomorphism gives its stated
native support interpretation. The actual closed-subscheme structure
sheaf has its proved section-ring isomorphism with $\Gamma(Y,U)/I(U)$
on each affine open. Its whole inclusion section map is precisely the
quotient map followed by this isomorphism. Compose the genuine native
affine homeomorphism with that actual ring isomorphism; the inclusion
section formula and true evaluation naturality give every quotient
polynomial value, without an assumed quotient-chart certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Prescribed-base closed immersion]
\label{scheme:relative-native-closed-immersion}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.relativeLocus_preimage_eq,

  EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.isProperMap_map_of_isClosedImmersion,
  EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.closedImmersionHomeomorph,
  EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.closedImmersionHomeomorph_apply_coe,
  EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.subschemeHomeomorph,

\leanok
\uses{scheme:native-closed-immersion,scheme:relative-valued-point-functor}
For every actual commuting diagram over $\operatorname{Spec}R$ and every
prescribed real seminorm $v$ on $R$, an actual closed immersion gives a
closed embedding of the native spaces above the entire base point $v$.
Its image is again exactly the native points supported in the actual
closed scheme image, and it is homeomorphic to that true closed locus. The relative map is also proper.
In particular this applies to the actual
closed subscheme of any scheme ideal-sheaf datum, using its genuine
composite structure map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The whole native composition identity and the genuine commuting diagram
show that an absolute lift of a target point above $v$ is itself above
$v$. Restrict the already proved absolute closed embedding to these
actual fixed-base subtypes. This is the original native relative map,
not a new map with a stipulated base norm. The absolute support-image
argument and the whole base identity give the exact relative image.
Restricting the genuine embedding homeomorphism gives the claimed
relative homeomorphism, without a bound on any coefficient values. Its
proved closed embedding
also makes it a genuine proper topological map. Applying this to the
actual closed-subscheme inclusion and its true composite base structure
map gives the ideal-sheaf-data specialization.
\end{proof}
