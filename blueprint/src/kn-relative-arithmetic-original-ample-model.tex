\section{The original relative ample power identity is produced on the model}

\begin{lemma}[An ample arithmetic model of the original line]
\label{lem:kn-relative-arithmetic-original-ample-model}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeArithmeticOriginalAmpleModel.exists_original_ample_model}
\uses{lem:kn-relative-arithmetic-original-line-model,lem:kn-relative-arithmetic-scalar-transition,thm:kn-arithmetic-original-line-power-iso}
\leanok
Let $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ be separated and smooth of
relative dimension $n$ over a characteristic-zero field, and let
$g:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ be the original compatible map to
an arbitrary finite-type $k$-algebra.  Every actual $g$-ample
line $L$ has an internally produced arithmetic model $M$ on a
smooth separated dimension-$n$ arithmetic family, relatively
ample over a separate finite-presentation affine algebra whose
original field fibre is the same $R$.  The actual whole original
pullback of $M$ is $L$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original ampleness datum supplies a positive integer $r$,
the actual closed embedding $j:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_R^d$,
and the original isomorphism $j^*\mathcal O(1)\simeq L^{\otimes r}$.
Apply the original-line model lemma to this same $j$ and $L$.
It constructs $M$ and a relatively very ample $T$ on the same
arithmetic model, together with actual original pullback
isomorphisms to $L$ and $j^*\mathcal O(1)$.  Tensor-power
compatibility combines them into an isomorphism between the
original-field pullbacks of $M^{\otimes r}$ and $T$.

The coefficient model is smooth, hence flat, and is
quasi-compact and separated.  Consequently flat scalar
extension of global sections holds for its quasicoherent
Hom-lines.  Express the given isomorphism and its inverse as
finite tensors of original sections with coefficients in $k$.
Adjoin those finitely many coefficients to the arithmetic
domain.  These tensors construct actual whole maps between
the corresponding model lines.  Their original pullbacks are
the given inverse maps.  Flatness of the model and injectivity
of the coefficient inclusion in $k$ detect both inverse
identities, so the constructed maps are an actual model
isomorphism $M_S^{\otimes r}\simeq T_S$.

The coefficient-transition lemma preserves the actual very
ampleness of $T_S$, so this same positive power $r$ proves
relative ampleness of $M_S$.  Every other required geometric
property and the entire original pullback of $M_S$ is retained
under that scalar extension.  This argument uses only
quasi-compactness and separatedness for global sections;
properness over the arithmetic coefficient domain is unnecessary.
\end{proof}
