\section{Multiplication of the whole original base ideals}

\begin{lemma}[Actual tensor-dual contraction]
\label{lem:actual-invertible-tensor-dual-contraction}
For original invertible sheaves $L,M$, the canonical original
evaluations construct an isomorphism
$L^\vee\otimes M^\vee\simeq(L\otimes M)^\vee$.
On every open $U$, it sends original local dual sections $d,e$ to a
functional satisfying
\[
 (d\otimes e)(s\otimes t)=d(s)e(t)
\]
for every original $s\in L(U)$ and $t\in M(U)$.
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.InvertibleSheafBaseIdealMultiplication.dualTensorIso,EnvelopeProperty.InvertibleSheafBaseIdealMultiplication.dualTensorIso_pairing,EnvelopeProperty.InvertibleSheafBaseIdealMultiplication.dualTensorIso_eval_tensorSection}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Interchange the two middle factors of
$(L\otimes M)\otimes(L^\vee\otimes M^\vee)$, then apply the two
original canonical evaluations and multiply in the structure sheaf.
All these maps are isomorphisms. Tensoring by $L\otimes M$ is fully
faithful, so comparison with its original canonical dual evaluation
gives the asserted tensor-dual isomorphism and its entire pairing
identity. Apply this identity to the four original pure sections.
The actual tensor interchange, tensor-map naturality and unit
multiplication evaluate it to the product of the two original
contractions. This works on every open and uses no local frame.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Whole original base-ideal multiplication]
\label{prop:actual-invertible-base-ideal-multiplication}
For any two original invertible sheaves on any original scheme,
\[
 \mathfrak b(L)\mathfrak b(M)\subseteq\mathfrak b(L\otimes M).
\]
The same original global pure-tensor section realizes the product of
every pair of original local evaluation coefficients.
\uses{lem:actual-invertible-tensor-dual-contraction,prop:actual-line-global-section-evaluation,prop:actual-invertible-sheaf-base-ideal-affine}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.InvertibleSheafBaseIdealMultiplication.sectionEvaluation_tensorSection,EnvelopeProperty.InvertibleSheafBaseIdealMultiplication.baseIdeal_mul_le}
\leanok
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Fix global sections $s,t$ and local dual sections $d,e$ on an affine
open $U$. Restriction of the original global section $s\otimes t$
is the pure tensor of their original restrictions. The preceding
contraction identity identifies its evaluation against the actual
tensor dual with $d(s|_U)e(t|_U)$, which therefore belongs to the
whole original base ideal of $L\otimes M$.

On $U$, the two original base ideals are the sums of the entire
evaluation images over all original global sections. Ideal
multiplication distributes over these arbitrary sums, and each
product of coefficients belongs to the target ideal by the first
paragraph. This proves inclusion on every original affine open,
hence inclusion of the original ideal sheaves.
\end{proof}
