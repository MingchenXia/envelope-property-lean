\section{Actual auxiliary root sections and their whole original branch products}

Let $D$ be the original invertible ideal of an original boundary component.
Its actual module is $D\mathcal O_X=\mathcal O_X(-D)$, with its original
inclusion into $\mathcal O_X$. The auxiliary system is the actual tensor
$L^m\otimes D\mathcal O_X$. Its selected section must be composed with
this original inclusion to define the actual cyclic root section of
$L^m$. The whole branch ideal, including multiplicities, must be retained.

\begin{lemma}[A section of the original invertible ideal retains its entire product]
\label{lem:kn-nadel-auxiliary-ideal-section-product}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSectionsIdeal.ideal_eq_span_of_frame,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSectionsIdeal.ideal_section_product_ideal,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSectionsIdeal.ideal_section_product}
For the original invertible ideal $D$ and any actual whole section
$\delta:\mathcal O_X\to D\mathcal O_X$, the whole zero ideal of its
composite with the original inclusion is
\[
 I(\delta\,\iota_D)=D\,I(\delta).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose actual affine frames of the original ideal module. The entire
original ideal on each such open is generated by the original inclusion
of the inverse-frame unit: the native ideal-module section equivalence
identifies every original ideal element with an actual section, and the
frame reconstructs that section from its actual coefficient. If $d$ is
this original generator and $c$ is the actual coefficient of $\delta$,
linearity of the genuine original inclusion gives coefficient $cd$ for
the composite. The original whole zero-ideal frame formula gives $(c)$
and $(cd)$. The original product ideal is $(d)(c)=(dc)$. These entire
original ideal equalities glue on the actual affine frame cover.
\end{proof}

\label{lem:kn-nadel-auxiliary-tensor-inclusion-untwist}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSectionsMonoidal.untwist,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSectionsMonoidal.untwist_inclusion}

isomorphism $M\otimes D\mathcal O_X\simeq D\mathcal O_X$ under which
the original tensor-of-inclusion map is exactly the original inclusion,
followed by the inverse original frame. The entire original maps agree.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual native explicit-tensor comparison, tensor the original

the native right-tensor inclusion by its already proved comparison.
Naturality of the actual right unitor, tensor interchange, equality of
the two unitors on the unit, and actual left-unitor naturality give the
claimed entire map equality. No tensor or section matching is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The true auxiliary section has its exact whole branch product]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-auxiliary-whole-branch-product}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSections.branchSection,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSections.zeroIdeal_branch_of_frame,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSections.branchSection_pullback,
EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelAuxiliaryBranchSections.zeroIdeal_branch}
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-auxiliary-ideal-section-product,lem:kn-nadel-auxiliary-tensor-inclusion-untwist,lem:kn-nadel-bertini-zero-ideal-arbitrary-pullback}
For an original invertible line $M$, original invertible ideal $D$, and
actual section $\alpha:\mathcal O_X\to M\otimes D\mathcal O_X$, let
$s:\mathcal O_X\to M$ be its composite with the original tensor of the
original inclusion. Then the entire original ideal sheaves satisfy
\[
 I(s)=D\,I(\alpha).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $M$ has an actual whole frame, apply the preceding true untwisting map
to $\alpha$. Invariance of entire original zero ideals under the actual
line isomorphisms reduces the assertion to the first lemma. In general,
choose the actual affine frame cover of the original $M$. Native open
pullback of the genuine tensor-ideal inclusion, with the original
ideal-module pullback comparison, identifies the actual pulled composite
with the same tensor-inclusion composite on that original open. The
arbitrary-pullback whole zero-ideal theorem retains both original section
ideals. Apply the framed result there. The actual top-section ring
isomorphism preserves products and the original ideal restrictions, so
the resulting entire original ideal equalities glue on the original
cover. There is no radical or support replacement.
\end{proof}
