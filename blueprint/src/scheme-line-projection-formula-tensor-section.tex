\section{The original projection formula on every native pure tensor}

\begin{lemma}[Native pure-tensor readback of the original PF]
\label{lem:line-pf-native-pure-tensor-readback}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeLineProjectionFormulaUnit.lineProjectionFormulaObj_hom_tensorSection}
\uses{lem:line-pf-native-hom-component}
\leanok
For the original scheme morphism $g:Y\to X$, original invertible line
$L$, arbitrary original module $N$ on $Y$, and any original open $U$,
the original projection-formula hom sends
$b\otimes m$ to $b\otimes\eta_L(m)$ for every actual section
$b\in\Gamma(g^{-1}U,N)$ and $m\in\Gamma(U,L)$.
Every section and open remains literal, with no chosen field model,
basis, frame or coefficient-matching hypothesis.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding component formula.  The existing native pullback
inverse-tensor comparison sends the native unit image of a pure tensor
to the pure tensor of the two unit images.  Functoriality of the actual
tensor section then applies the original counit to its first factor.
The original adjunction right triangle identifies that factor with the
same original $b$.  The second factor remains the native unit image of
the original $m$.  This is exactly the claimed section equation.
\end{proof}
