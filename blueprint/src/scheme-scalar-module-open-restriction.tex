\section{Literal scalar sections and original open restriction}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:fc-scalar-module-open-restriction}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeScalarModuleOpenRestriction.nativeSectionsIso,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeScalarModuleOpenRestriction.openRestrictIso,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeScalarModuleOpenRestriction.openRestrictIso_apply}
\leanok
\uses{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-module-presheaf,prop:scheme-module-open-sections-kernel}
For an original $p:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$, any original module $M$
and any original open $U$, the literal $R$-module of sections agrees
with the native module of sections over the spectrum's coordinate
ring, restricted along the genuine spectrum section isomorphism.
Every section retains its actual original presheaf transport under this identification.
It is consequently isomorphic to the literal whole $R$-module of
sections of $M|_U$ on the original open subscheme $U$ over $R$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Both first modules have the same underlying original sections. Their
actions agree because restricting the original map on top sections is
its original map from the base top open to $U$. The identity on the
original additive group is therefore an $R$-linear isomorphism.
Apply it on each side of the native whole open-restriction section
isomorphism, after restricting scalars through the actual spectrum
section isomorphism. At the open subscheme's top, use the literal top
coefficient comparison. This gives the claimed entire module
isomorphism, with no chosen section or trivialization.
\end{proof}
