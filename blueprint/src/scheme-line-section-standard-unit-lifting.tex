\section{Actual standard-unit whole-section and point-fiber lifting}
The inputs are the actual original adjunction unit on sections and an
original generating family. The unit is assumed surjective. No cut
ampleness, Serre bound, or final local generation is asserted.

\begin{lemma}[The same whole section lifts under the original unit]
\label{lem:original-standard-unit-whole-section-lift}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeLineSectionStandardUnitLifting.exists_wholeSection_lift_of_standardUnit_surjective}
\leanok
For any original scheme morphism $g:Y\to X$ and module $M$, if the
actual original pullback--pushforward adjunction unit at $M$ is onto on
top-open sections, every actual whole $q:\mathcal O_Y\to g^*M$ is the
actual pullback of some original whole $t:\mathcal O_X\to M$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take the original value of $q$ at one. Apply the native top-open
pushforward transport and the stated surjectivity to choose an original
section of $M$, and take its native associated whole morphism $t$.
The existing component formula for the original adjunction unit on a top
section identifies its value with the original pulled $t$ on the
preimage of the top open. Original morphism naturality across the
canonical preimage-top equality transports this identity to the same
original top open of $Y$. The original section/unit-Hom equivalence
then gives the whole equality $g^*t=q$. The scalar and top-open
transports are those of the actual original unit; no chosen comparison
or desired whole-map equality is an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[An original generating family has a nonzero original fiber generator]
\label{lem:original-generating-family-nonzero-fiber}
For any original invertible $N$ on $Y$, any original generating family
$G$ of $N$, and any original point $y$, one of the same original whole
generator maps has nonzero pullback to the canonical residue-field point.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Pull the original generating family to the original residue spectrum.
The existing local-spectrum line trivialization gives a whole frame of
the actual pulled line. Transport the same pulled generating family
along this actual frame. The existing unit-coefficient theorem for an
original local-spectrum unit generating family supplies an original
index with unit coefficient. Native generator pullback and epimorphic
image identities identify its original whole generator with the
pullback of the same original generator followed by that frame.
A zero pullback would make the coefficient zero, contradicting its
being a unit in the original residue field.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A lifted original cut generator stays nonzero at its original ambient point]
\label{lem:original-standard-unit-generator-lift-nonzero-fiber}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeLineSectionStandardUnitLifting.exists_nonzero_pointFiber_lift_of_standardUnit_surjective}
\leanok
For any original $g:Y\to X$, original invertible $M$, original generating
family of $g^*M$, and actual surjectivity of the standard original unit
on top sections, every original $y\in Y$ has an original whole
$t:\mathcal O_X\to M$ whose original residue pullback at $g(y)$ is
nonzero.
\uses{lem:original-standard-unit-whole-section-lift,lem:original-generating-family-nonzero-fiber}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the original nonzero fiber generator at $y$ and lift its whole
map by the first lemma. The native scheme residue-field square is
$\operatorname{Spec}(\kappa(y)\leftarrow\kappa(g(y)))\to X$ equal to
$i_y$ followed by the same original $g$. Native pullback-composition
and equal-map section identities preserve this exact square. If the
original residue pullback of $t$ at $g(y)$ were zero, its further
pullback would be zero, contradicting the nonzero original generator
at $y$ after cancellation of the native whole comparison isomorphisms.
No residue-field isomorphism or fiber matching is required.
\end{proof}
