\begin{lemma}[Naturality of the exact original line trace]
\label{lem:kn-original-line-trace-naturality}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalCanonicalTrace.originalLineTrace_naturality}
\leanok
The original inverse-PF trace associated to any fixed actual structure trace
commutes with every morphism between the original invertible line modules.
No projection-formula, component, or line-trace naturality certificate is an input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Precompose with the original invertible line projection formula and its native
unit isomorphism. Pure tensor sections determine this source map. On a section
$b\otimes m$, the projection formula gives $b$ times the native pullback unit
of $m$. Naturality of the original adjunction unit carries it to $b$ times the
unit of the image of $m$. The already proved all-coefficient trace formula then
gives the scalar trace of $b$ times that image. Linearity of the original module
morphism gives exactly the other composite. Cancel the source isomorphism.
\end{proof}
