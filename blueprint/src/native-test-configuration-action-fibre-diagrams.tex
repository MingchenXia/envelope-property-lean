\begin{theorem}[Whole original action--fibre equations]
\label{thm:native-test-configuration-action-fibre-diagrams}

The original group-times-original-fibre inclusion, followed by the
original action, is precisely the inverse whole original unit-open
trivialization followed by the original total-space inclusion.
Consequently the original entire unit open has an actual lift to
the original group action product whose action composite is its
original inclusion and whose projection composite factors through
the same original parameter-one fibre. The resulting entire map
to the original $X$ is exactly the original product trivialization's
$X$ projection. No action/fibre factorization certificate is given.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine original source action to the original inclusion
of its parameter-one fibre, keeping the same original group
coordinate. The whole projection equation is the original product
projection identity. On the actual Laurent pullback, the inverse
whole unit-fibre isomorphism is by construction $(g,z)\mapsto gz$.
The actual Laurent-pullback projection to the original model is
equivariant. Its composite with that inverse is therefore exactly
the same original group-times-fibre inclusion followed by the
original total-space action. Compose this equality with the
actual original unit-open-to-fibre-product isomorphism. Its two
whole inverse identities give the original unit-open inclusion,
while the projection identity gives the same original fibre
factorization. Finally, the original source $X\times\mathbf G_m$
trivialization was constructed by the same actual fibre-product
isomorphism, the original source fibre-one identification, and
product symmetry. Its first projection is consequently exactly
this same fibre factor followed by the original identification
with $X$, proving the final entire-morphism equation.
\end{proof}
