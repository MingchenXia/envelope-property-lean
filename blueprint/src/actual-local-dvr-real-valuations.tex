\subsection{Actual local DVR centers determine the entire original real valuation}

\begin{lemma}[The whole original local valuation overring is the original DVR]
\label{lem:actual-local-valuation-overring}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LocalDVRRealValuations.localMap_bijective}
\leanok
Let $R$ be an original valuation ring in its SAME actual fraction field $K$.
For an original real-valued valuation $v$ of $K$, any actual local ring map
$R\to O_v$ whose composite with the original inclusion into $K$ is the
original fraction-field coefficient map is bijective. In particular the
ENTIRE original ring $R$ is the actual integer ring of $v$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The native valuation-ring local-overring theorem makes the actual map from
$R$ onto the whole image of $O_v$ in $K$ bijective. The original inclusion of
$O_v$ is injective, so the given local map itself is bijective. Its original
coefficient triangle identifies the actual $v$-bounded elements of $K$ with
the whole image of $R$, proving the integer-ring assertion. No surjectivity,
valuation preservation, or identification of local centers is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Every genuine local DVR center gives a positive whole real scale]
\label{lem:actual-local-dvr-real-scale}
\uses{lem:actual-local-valuation-overring}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LocalDVRRealValuations.exists_positive_realScale}
\leanok
If the SAME original ring $R$ is a DVR, the above actual local coefficient
map internally determines $s>0$ such that the ENTIRE original real valuation
$v$ is exactly the genuine real scaling at $s$ of the native original
maximal-ideal valuation of $R$ on its SAME fraction field $K$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual original uniformizer $\pi$. Its real value is strictly
positive by injectivity of the original fraction-field map and is strictly
less than one by locality of the actual map into $O_v$. Set
$s=-\log v(\pi)>0$. The actual native DVR valuation of this SAME $\pi$ is
$\exp(-1)$, whose genuine real scale at $s$ equals $v(\pi)$ by equality of
actual logarithms. Every actual original unit has value one under BOTH
valuations. Original DVR factorization of every nonzero ring element as a
unit times a power of $\pi$ proves equality on ALL original ring elements,
including zero. Every original fraction is a quotient of such original
elements; the valuations preserve the quotient, proving equality on the
ENTIRE original field without any regularity or completeness assumption.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original real parameter is unique]
\label{lem:actual-local-dvr-real-scale-unique}
\uses{lem:actual-local-dvr-real-scale}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.LocalDVRRealValuations.existsUnique_positive_realScale}
\leanok
The genuine real scaling parameter of the SAME native original DVR
valuation is unique, including among all nonnegative real parameters.
Thus the original real valuation with its actual local DVR center determines
one unique positive original parameter.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Evaluate the actual whole valuations on an original uniformizer. Its native
value is $\exp(-1)$, so the true real logarithm at parameter $s$ is $-s$.
Equality of the original valuations therefore gives equality of their
parameters, and the positive parameter constructed above is unique.
\end{proof}
