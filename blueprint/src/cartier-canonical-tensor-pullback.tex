\section{Composition of the original explicit tensor-pullback comparisons}

\begin{lemma}[Naturality of the genuine explicit tensor identification]
\label{lem:cartier-actual-monoidal-explicit-tensor-naturality}
\lean{ModularCurves.cartierMonoidalTensorObjIso_hom_natural}
\leanok
The canonical identification between the actual coherent tensor of two
original module sheaves and their explicit sheafified tensor commutes
with arbitrary original morphisms in both module factors.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The inverse identification is the original sheafification inverse
tensorator followed by the tensor of the two actual sheafification
counits. Naturality of that true inverse tensorator moves the actual
sheafified tensor of the two original morphisms through its first
factor. Naturality of each actual counit then moves their tensor
through its last factor. Functoriality of the tensor identifies the
two resulting composites. This proves inverse naturality; composing
with the actual identification on the source and target gives the
corresponding direct naturality.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual canonical explicit tensor pullback respects composition]
\label{lem:cartier-canonical-explicit-tensor-pullback-composition}
\uses{lem:cartier-actual-monoidal-explicit-tensor-naturality,lem:cartier-actual-pullback-inverse-tensorator-composition,lem:cartier-ratio-canonical-pullback}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.canonicalTensorPullbackIso_comp}
\leanok
For genuine original scheme morphisms $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$, the
actual canonical tensor pullback comparison for $f\circ g$ is the two
successive original canonical comparisons, followed by the genuine
pullback-composition isomorphisms on both original module factors.
The equality holds on the entire original module sheaves.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand each canonical comparison as original pullback of the inverse
explicit-tensor identification, the genuine inverse pullback tensorator
and the actual explicit-tensor identification on its original pulled
factors. The two intermediate original identifications cancel inside
pullback. Move the last identification through the tensor of the true
factor-composition maps by the preceding actual naturality theorem.
The true inverse-tensorator composition identity now replaces the two
successive inverse tensorators by the actual tensor-composition map
followed by the original composite-pullback inverse tensorator.
Finally naturality of the actual native pullback-composition comparison
moves that tensor-composition map through the first original inverse
explicit-tensor identification. The resulting composite is exactly
the original pullback-composition map on the explicit tensor followed
by the canonical comparison for composite pullback.
\end{proof}
