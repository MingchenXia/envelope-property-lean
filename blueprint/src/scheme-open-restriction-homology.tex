\section{Native homology of the original open restriction}
\label{sec:scheme-open-restriction-homology}

Let $j:U\to X$ be ANY original open immersion of schemes.
The restriction functor below is the original native module
restriction, with its actual original coefficient maps. No
quasicoherence, affineness, finiteness, nonemptiness or exactness
certificate is assumed.

\begin{lemma}[The original restriction preserves native homology]
\label{lem:scheme-open-restriction-preserves-homology}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeOpenRestrictionHomology.preservesHomology}
The original restriction functor preserves genuine native
homology of every original short complex.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original module restriction is the actual sheaf pushforward
along the open-image functor with scalar restriction by the
inverse actual open-immersion coefficient isomorphism. Its
original section maps preserve addition. Native evaluation
jointly reflects limits of presheaves of modules, and scalar
restriction preserves limits. Passing through the faithful
limit-reflecting sheaf forgetful functor therefore proves that
this SAME native restriction preserves limits. The actual
restriction--pushforward adjunction makes it a left adjoint,
so it also preserves colimits. Its true finite-limit and
finite-colimit preservation give native homology preservation.
This argument includes empty schemes and empty opens.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The entire original complex and its native comparison]
\label{prop:scheme-open-restriction-complex-homology}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeOpenRestrictionHomology.restrictedComplex,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeOpenRestrictionHomology.homologyIso}
For every original cochain complex $K$ of module sheaves on $X$
and every $q\geq0$, the actual native restricted complex has
\[
 H^q(j^*K)\simeq j^*H^q(K).
\]
Here $j^*$ denotes the original native open restriction, and
both homology objects are the original native categorical ones.
\uses{lem:scheme-open-restriction-preserves-homology}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine native map-of-homological-complexes functor
to the SAME original complex, using actual zero-map
preservation. Apply its actual native short-complex homology
comparison to the original row in degree $q$. The preceding
lemma proves that this comparison is an isomorphism. Thus its
kernel, boundary, projection and coefficient maps are those of
the original restriction and original complex, rather than a
chosen replacement complex or a supplied comparison.
\end{proof}

The next use is the SAME original global Frobenius de Rham
complex. Its actual affine complex comparison and canonical
Cartier sheaf descent still require their own full map proofs;
this generic exactness statement does not assert them or any
Deligne--Illusie splitting or vanishing theorem.
