\subsection{Genuine good-open isomorphisms of the original Rees scheme}
\begin{theorem}[The actual Rees projection is an isomorphism off each original generator]
\label{scheme:actual-rees-good-open-isomorphism}

\uses{scheme:actual-rees-coordinate-cover,scheme:actual-rees-projection-surjective,algebra:actual-rees-chart-away-equivalence}
Let $A$ be any commutative ring and $g_i$ any original family, with any actual
index $j$ (including $g_j=0$ and zero-divisor or nilpotent coefficients). The SAME original Rees scheme projection becomes a
genuine isomorphism over the original principal open $D(g_j)$ of
$\operatorname{Spec}A$. No finite-family, Noetherianity, field or comparison
certificate is needed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First show that the whole preimage of $D(g_j)$ lies in the original coordinate
chart $D_+(Y_j)$. Lift an arbitrary actual source point into the proved genuine
coordinate cover, say the chart of $Y_i$. In this SAME actual chart,
the whole original homogeneous relation gives
$g_i(Y_j/Y_i)=g_j$. The true coefficient triangle identifies membership over
$D(g_j)$ with nonmembership of the ORIGINAL coefficient $g_j$ in the actual
chart prime. The relation then forces $Y_j/Y_i$ outside that prime. The true
Proj chart intersection formula says precisely that the original source
point lies in $D_+(Y_j)$. This proves the whole scheme-open inclusion using
actual original prime ideals.

The whole original chart map into $A[1/g_j]$ is the genuinely proved original
inclusion, and $A[1/g_j]$ is genuinely its localization at the original
coefficient $g_j$. Its Spec map followed by the actual $j$-chart immersion
is an open immersion into the original Rees scheme. Every original
coefficient commutes, so its WHOLE projection triangle is the canonical
Spec map $\operatorname{Spec}A[1/g_j]\to\operatorname{Spec}A$.
The actual localization image is the chart principal open of $g_j$; the
preceding entire preimage inclusion shows this is exactly the original
projection preimage of $D(g_j)$. The actual open-immersion pullback criterion
therefore gives a genuine pullback square with the identity on
$\operatorname{Spec}A[1/g_j]$. The base-changed original projection is thus
an isomorphism, including its original coefficient and section maps.
\end{proof}
