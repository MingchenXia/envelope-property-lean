\section{The original ordered product on the sheaf Cech terms}

\begin{definition}[The ordered product with its original section values]
\label{def:native-cech-ordered-cup}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechAlexanderWhitneyProduct.orderedCup, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechAlexanderWhitneyProduct.cup_pi, EnvelopeProperty.DI.CechAlexanderWhitneyProduct.cup_tensorSection_pi}
Let $k$ have prime characteristic $p$, let $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$
be the original morphism, and put $F:X\to Y=X^{(p)}$ and
$E^q=F_*\Omega^q_{X/k}$, with the original coefficient action through
$F^\#$. Let $(U_i)_{i\in I}$ be any family of opens of $Y$.
For each tuple $t$ put $U_t=\bigcap_a U_{t_a}$ and
$Q(M,U_t)=j_{t,*}(M|_{U_t})$. The original ordinary sheaf Cech term is
$\check C^j(M)=\prod_{t\in I^{j+1}}Q(M,U_t)$.
There is a canonical morphism
\[
 \check C^j(E^q)\otimes\check C^{j'}(E^r)
       \longrightarrow\check C^{j+j'}(E^{q+r}).
\]
Its projection to a tuple $t$ of length $j+j'+1$ is the original
exterior product after the native prefix and suffix projections
and their restrictions to $U_t$. The prefix uses positions
$0,\ldots,j$ and the suffix positions $j,\ldots,j+j'$.
On every ambient open $W$, and every pair of whole original sections,
the image of their original tensor section is exactly the original
exterior product on $U_t\cap W$ of those two restricted projections.
No smoothness, finiteness, or covering assertion is needed for this
construction.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The full tuple intersection lies in both its prefix and suffix
intersections. Project to those original factors and use the original
factor restriction maps to reach $Q(E^q,U_t)$ and $Q(E^r,U_t)$.
On $W$, both factor section objects are the original exterior
sections on $F^{-1}(U_t\cap W)$. Their ambient scalar acts first
by restriction from $W$ to $U_t\cap W$, and then by the actual
coefficient map $F^\#$. The original whole-section exterior product
is bilinear for this common action. It commutes with every original
restriction. Hence its tensor lift is an actual morphism from the
original tensor presheaf to $Q(E^{q+r},U_t)$.

The original sheafification adjunction extends this morphism to the
actual tensor sheaf. Its unit sends each pure tensor of original
sections to that same tensor section, so the extended map has
exactly the stated whole-section value. Applying the original
product universal property over the output tuples gives the stated
morphism. Each original product projection recovers its defining
factor map. Restriction and the tensor unit retain the original
values, proving the formula on every $W$, including empty opens.
\end{proof}
