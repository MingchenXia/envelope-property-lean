\begin{definition}[Canonical coefficient corner isomorphisms retain the whole original maps]
\label{def:arithmetic-fixed-model-coefficient-corners}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ArithmeticFixedSmoothModel.topScalarIso,EnvelopeProperty.ArithmeticFixedSmoothModel.topScalarIso_hom_stage,EnvelopeProperty.ArithmeticFixedSmoothModel.bottomScalarIso,EnvelopeProperty.ArithmeticFixedSmoothModel.bottomScalarIso_hom_stage,EnvelopeProperty.ArithmeticFixedSmoothModel.top_bottom_scalar_triangle}
\leanok
For an actual algebra $R\to L$, the top subalgebra is canonically the SAME
$L$. If the original map is injective, its bottom subalgebra is canonically
the SAME $R$. Contravariance gives actual spectrum isomorphisms from
$\operatorname{Spec}L$ to the coefficient-system limit corner and from
$\operatorname{Spec}R$ to its bottom-stage corner. Their composites with
EVERY actual coefficient-stage map are the original coefficient maps as
whole morphism equalities, including the whole bottom/limit triangle.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Use the native top-subalgebra equivalence, whose underlying map is the
original subtype inclusion. For the bottom use the native equivalence
from injectivity of the original algebra map. Its inverse is the original
map into the bottom subalgebra. Applying its inverse/forward identity and
then the actual subtype inclusion proves the whole original coefficient
identity on EVERY bottom-ring element. The spectrum isomorphisms are the
native images of these actual ring equivalences. Contravariant functoriality
and the actual coefficient inclusions give every displayed whole stage
triangle; equality is checked on every coefficient, not a selected family.
\end{proof}
