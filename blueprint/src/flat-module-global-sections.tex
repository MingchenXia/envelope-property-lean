\begin{theorem}[Whole original quasicoherent global sections commute with flat base change]
\label{thm:fc-flat-module-global-sections}

\uses{scheme:existing-affine-module-pullback-sections,lem:fc-module-flat-base-change-affine-open,lem:fc-scheme-scalar-module-presheaf,lem:fc-scheme-module-flat-base-change-map,lem:fc-flat-kernel-base-change,lem:fc-affine-module-flat-base-change,lem:fc-module-pullback-open-restriction,prop:fc-finite-cech-flat-base-change}
Let $X$ be an original compact separated scheme over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$, let $M$ be any original quasicoherent module on
$X$, and let $S$ be an actual flat $R$-algebra. Put
$X_S=X\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}S$ and retain its
original projections $g$ and $q$. The canonical original map is an
$S$-linear equivalence
\[
 S\otimes_R\Gamma(X,M)\ \simeq\ \Gamma(X_S,g^*M).
\]
For every original $s\in S$ and $m\in\Gamma(X,M)$ its value is
$q^*(s)$ times the actual original pullback-unit section of $m$.
The comparison uses the literal original modules, original coefficient
maps and original pullback, including every pure tensor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual finite affine open cover of the original compact
scheme. Separatedness makes each actual nonempty finite tuple
intersection affine; its original inverse image under $g$ is the
corresponding actual tuple intersection on $X_S$. Apply the preceding
affine comparison to these original intersections. Tensor product
commutes with the finite products, so these comparisons give whole
linear equivalences in degrees zero and one of the actual base-linear
Cech complexes. Their genuine restriction naturality gives the whole
first-differential square, using its actual alternating-sum formula.

The sheaf condition identifies the entire original global-section
module, with its original base action and actual augmentation, with the
kernel of the first Cech differential on each side. Apply the preceding
flat-kernel theorem to the derived square and compose these whole
kernel identifications. There is no supplied H0 comparison, Cech
matching assumption, or global-section isomorphism certificate.
The augmentation maps retain actual restrictions, so the affine pure
tensor formula and the sheaf uniqueness theorem give the stated global
formula. The actual natural section map comes from the original pullback
adjunction unit on every open. Its genuine isomorphism at the top,
combined with the original whole top-coefficient identifications,
gives the stated equivalence between literal global-section modules.
The original tensor-map formula and actual top restriction transport
give the displayed coefficient-times-pullback-unit identity for every
original pure tensor. The literal original $R$ and $S$ actions are
retained through their actual spectrum section isomorphisms.
\end{proof}
