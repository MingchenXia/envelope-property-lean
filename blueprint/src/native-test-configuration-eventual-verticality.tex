\section{Eventual verticality of the literal test-configuration base ideals}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:native-test-configuration-unit-open-principal}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.unitOpen_eq_principal}
For the original test configuration $\pi:\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}k[t]$,
the whole noncentral open is $\pi^{-1}D(t)$, where $t$ is viewed as the
actual global section of the affine parameter line.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Under the affine global-section isomorphism for $k[t]$, the principal open
defined by the section corresponding to $t$ is exactly the spectrum basic
open of the original polynomial $t$. Taking its inverse image under $\pi$
gives the defining noncentral open of the original total space.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:native-test-configuration-eventual-verticality}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.exists_eventual_powerBaseIdeal_comap_eq_top}
Let $\pi:\mathcal X\to\mathbf A^1_k$ be the literal original test
configuration, $\mathcal M$ an invertible module on its original total
space, and $L$ an ample invertible module on the original fibre $X$.
Suppose the original action supplies its line-module isomorphism and the
original fibre-one identification identifies $\mathcal M$ with $L$.
Then there exists one $N$ such that for every $m\ge N$ the base ideal
$\mathfrak b_m$ generated by \emph{all} global sections of
$\mathcal M^{\otimes m}$ restricts to the unit ideal on
$\mathcal X\setminus\mathcal X_0$.
\uses{lem:native-test-configuration-unit-open-principal,lem:scheme-ample-line-eventual-generation,prop:native-test-configuration-line-power-generation,prop:principal-open-base-ideal-unit}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The geometric ample criterion gives one bound for finite global generation
of every original $L^{\otimes m}$ beyond that bound. Fix such an $m$ and
one of its generating families. The line-module comparison constructed
from the source action and the fibre-one identification, followed by its
actual tensor-power comparison, identifies
$\mathcal M^{\otimes m}|_{\mathcal X\setminus\mathcal X_0}$ with the
pullback of $L^{\otimes m}$ along the original projection to $X$.
Pulling back the family and transporting it through this whole module
isomorphism generates the original restricted power.

The original projectivity of $\pi$ makes the total space quasi-compact
and separated, since the original parameter base is affine. The preceding
lemma identifies the noncentral open with the principal open of the
actual parameter section. Apply the principal-open base-ideal theorem:
localization of the whole global-section module shows that the original
all-global-section evaluation is surjective on this generated principal
open, and its image ideal therefore restricts to the unit ideal.
This image is precisely $\mathfrak b_m$. The ample bound is independent
of $m$, giving the asserted eventual verticality.
\end{proof}
