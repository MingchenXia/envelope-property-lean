\section{Generic frames on an open containing the valued point}

\begin{definition}\label{def:scheme-support-line-frame}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSupportLineFrames.supportGenericFactor, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSupportLineFrames.supportGenericFactor_comp, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSupportLineFrames.supportGenericFrame}
\leanok
Let $X$ be a scheme, $x$ a point of $X$, and $U$ an open containing
$x$. The canonical map $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(x)\to X$ factors
uniquely through $U$. A trivialization of a module $M$ on $U$ therefore
induces a trivialization of its entire residue-field pullback.
\uses{cor:actual-line-model-norm-local-frame}
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Every point of $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(x)$ maps to $x$, so the range
of its canonical map is contained in the range of $U\to X$. The
open-immersion lifting property gives the factor and its exact
composition with the inclusion. Two factors are equal because an
open immersion is a monomorphism. Pull the given module trivialization
along this factor and compose with the native pullback-composition
and structure-unit isomorphisms. This constructs a frame on the
whole residue-field module.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:scheme-support-frame-model-norm}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSupportLineFrames.supportGenericFrame_eq_localFrameGenericIso, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSupportLineFrames.modelLineNorm_eq_supportFrameCoefficient_mul, EnvelopeProperty.SchemeSupportLineFrames.supportFrameUnit_modelLineNorm_pos}
\leanok
Let $X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ be proper, with a valuation on $R$
bounded by one. Let $x$ be a valued point above this valuation, $M$
an invertible module, and $U$ any open containing the support of $x$.
For a frame on $M|_U$, let $e$ be its induced residue-field frame and
$b=e^{-1}(1)$. Then $\|b\|_M>0$ and, for every residue-field section $s$,
\[
 \|s\|_M=|e(s)|\,\|b\|_M.
\]
The proper valuative center is not required to belong to $U$.
If it does belong to $U$, this support-induced frame equals the
frame obtained from the actual valuative-center factor.
\uses{def:scheme-support-line-frame,cor:actual-line-model-norm-local-frame,prop:generated-line-generic-frame-norms}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The induced frame is a linear isomorphism on all residue-field
sections. Its inverse is linear, so $s=e(s)b$. Apply scalar
homogeneity of the genuine proper-model norm to obtain the formula.
The inverse image of one is nonzero: applying the frame sends it to
one in the residue field. The genuine model norm detects zero, hence
its norm is positive. If the center is in $U$, both the support
factor and the center-induced generic factor compose with $U\to X$
to the canonical residue-field map. Monomorphy makes these factors
equal. Substitution in the native pullback frame makes the two whole
frames equal. No metric comparison or frame-preservation equality is
an input.
\end{proof}
