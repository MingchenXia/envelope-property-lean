\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartBaseAlgebra, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartBaseLinearIso, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.baseReesRestriction, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartBaseLinearIso_naturality, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.cechChartModule, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.cechFactorIso, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.orderedChartModule, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.orderedCechTermIso, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.cechFactorIso_face, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartTermProjection, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.orderedCechTermIso_projection, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartFaceRestriction, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartCoface, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartCoface_projection, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.orderedCechTermIso_coface, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartDifferential, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.orderedCechTermIso_differential, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartDifferential_comp, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.chartComplex, EnvelopeProperty.FcReesCechChartSections.orderedCechComplexIso}
\uses{lem:fc-rees-formal-chart-sheaf, lem:fc-rees-power-decomposition}
For a separated original scheme and any family of original affine opens, the ACTUAL
ordered structure-module Cech complex of the actual schematic Rees family on their
inverse images is genuinely isomorphic to the explicit Rees chart complex.
This is an isomorphism over the actual global functions on the original Rees
coefficient spectrum. Its terms are products of the actual Rees algebras of the
original affine intersections; its cofaces are the literal original-chart Rees
restrictions, and its differential is their explicit alternating sum.
No scalar compatibility or complex comparison is supplied as an assumption.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The whole original coefficient triangle and naturality of
$\Gamma\operatorname{Spec}$ prove that the genuine chart section comparison is
linear for the ACTUAL coefficient action on the ACTUAL structure-module presheaf.
The actual chart restrictions are algebra homomorphisms over those same original
base global functions. Their genuine module naturality square is proved.
Inverse image commutes with the actual finite intersection indexing each Cech
factor. The proved affine chart comparison therefore identifies each actual factor;
taking products gives the whole term isomorphism, retaining every original factor
projection. Naturality identifies every actual face map with literal original-chart
Rees restriction. Product projections prove the WHOLE coface diagram, and finite
alternating sums prove the WHOLE differential diagram. These genuine diagrams
derive square-zero for the explicit Rees differential and construct the actual
cochain-complex isomorphism. No graded cohomology, finite-generation or formal-H0
conclusion is asserted here; those remaining comparisons are separate producers.
\end{proof}

\uses{lem:fc-rees-cech-chart-sections, lem:fc-rees-power-decomposition}
For a finite family of affine opens of a separated original scheme, the ACTUAL
ordered structure-module Cech complex of its ACTUAL schematic Rees family,
after genuine restriction to the original coefficient ring, is isomorphic to
the explicit direct-sum original ideal-power complex. Each original Rees degree
has exactly the original affine-intersection ideal-power sections and the
alternating differential of the actual original ideal-power restrictions.
Every original coefficient, original degree and original scalar action is retained.
No graded-comparison or scalar-compatibility certificate is assumed.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The actual original scalar homomorphism into the global functions of the Rees
spectrum is constructed through the genuine affine section isomorphism. Its full
coefficient identity proves that restricting the ACTUAL Rees chart coefficient
action recovers the derived original chart scalar action on every element.
This gives the genuine base-linear whole-term comparison. The finite Rees term
decomposition from Lemma~\ref{lem:fc-rees-power-decomposition} then identifies
EVERY original intersection coefficient in its original degree. Literal original
ideal-power restrictions construct each degree coface, and their direct sum is
proved equal to the WHOLE actual original Rees coface by every actual coefficient.
Alternating sums prove the WHOLE differential diagram. Actual square-zero and
these genuine diagrams construct the cochain-complex isomorphism. Evaluating
at a single direct-sum inclusion derives square-zero in each actual ideal-power
degree and hence constructs each genuine degree complex. Passing this comparison
to homology, proving the graded Rees scalar action and applying proper/projective
cohomological finiteness remain separate steps; no formal-H0 result is asserted.
\end{proof}
