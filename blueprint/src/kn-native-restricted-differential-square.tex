\section{The original native exterior differential after open restriction}

For a morphism $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, write $\omega_f^{(n)}$
for its $n$th exterior cotangent module. For $a:Z\to X$, write
$d_a:a^*\omega_f^{(n)}\to\omega_{f\circ a}^{(n)}$ for the actual
exterior pullback differential. No smoothness or invertibility of these
modules is implicit. In the formulas below, $u;v$ means first $u$, then $v$.

\begin{lemma}[The original inverse pullback differential square]
\label{lem:kn-native-restricted-differential-inverse-square}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.fixed_pullback_differential_square}
\uses{lem:kn-blowup-canonical-whole-forward-square}
\leanok
Let $k$ be a field and let
$g:A\to X$, $\pi:Y\to X$, $p:B\to A$, and $v:B\to Y$
be arbitrary scheme morphisms with an actual equality
$w:g\circ p=\pi\circ v$. Fix $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ and $n\geq0$.
The native pullback-composition and equality comparisons give
\[
 \chi:p^*g^*\omega_f^{(n)}\simeq v^*\pi^*\omega_f^{(n)}.
\]
Let $H:\omega_{f\circ\pi\circ v}^{(n)}\to
\omega_{f\circ g\circ p}^{(n)}$ be transport along the original
commuting square and associativity equalities. Then
\[
 v^*d_\pi;d_v;H=\chi^{-1};p^*d_g;d_p.
\]
Here $\chi^{-1}$ is the original composition comparison for $v,\pi$,
the inverse comparison for $w$, and the inverse composition comparison
for $p,g$, in that order.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the whole forward native differential square with its two sides
exchanged and equality $w^{-1}$. Equality elimination identifies the
pullback congruence for $w^{-1}$ with the inverse of the original
congruence for $w$. Compose the resulting equality with the inverse of
its final associativity transport. Adjacent equality transports cancel;
the remaining transports compose to the original field-structure
equality defining $H$. The other path is exactly the displayed inverse
word. Thus every differential, comparison and structural equality is
the original one. No differential is inverted in this argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Restriction naturality for an original comparison word]
\label{lem:kn-native-restricted-differential-word-naturality}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.composeOriginalInverseWord,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.pushed_square_after_native_comparison_original_indices,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.restriction_square_original_word_indices}
\leanok
Let $R,F$ be functors between categories and let $e:R\simeq F$ be a
natural isomorphism. Suppose $d:M\to N$, $V:F(N)\to T_0$,
$H:T_0\to T$, and $P:L\to T$ are morphisms. Let
$W:F(M)\to L$ be the specified well-typed word $s;r;z;a$.
If the original square satisfies
\[
 F(d);V;H=W;P,
\]
then
\[
 R(d);e_N;V;H=e_M;W;P.
\]
In particular the maps $s,r,z,a$ can be the individual original
pullback-composition, equality and differential maps, with their original
intermediate objects retained.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Naturality gives $R(d);e_N=e_M;F(d)$. Associate the remaining composite
and substitute the supplied square equality. Associativity yields
$e_M;W;P$. Forming $W$ from its original individual arrows does not
change this proof. This is a conditional categorical naturality lemma;
the actual geometric square in the theorem below is produced by the
preceding lemma.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The fixed native comparison data on a whole chart]
\label{def:kn-native-restricted-differential-chart-data}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.cartesianComparison,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.pullbackComparison,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.restrictedPulledDifferential,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.restrictedSourceDifferential,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.restrictedPulledIso,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.restrictedSourceIso,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.fixed_inverse_iso_word}
\uses{sc0-open-differential:pullback-isomorphism,lem:kn-structure-trace-chart-cartesian}
\leanok
On an actual Cartesian whole chart, take $g$ to be its base open
immersion and $v$ its source open immersion. Use the actual Cartesian
restriction--pushforward isomorphism, the pullback comparison $\chi$
above, and the fixed restriction--pullback isomorphism
$\rho_v:\operatorname{Res}_v\simeq v^*$.
The specified pulled and source differential maps are respectively
\[
 \rho_{v,\pi^*\omega_f^{(n)}};\chi^{-1};p^*d_g,
 \qquad
 \rho_{v,\omega_{f\circ\pi}^{(n)}};d_v;H.
\]
The fixed pulled and source comparison isomorphisms are separately
constructed from the same specified native comparison data, using the
actual open differential isomorphisms. For arbitrary composable
isomorphisms, their inverse composite has the usual reversed inverse
word. This construction and abstract inverse-word identity do not assert
a further chart-specific identification of the displayed maps with
the comparison homomorphisms.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The original Cartesian square gives its whole-module base-change
comparison. Compose the native pullback-composition and square-equality
isomorphisms to obtain $\chi$. The open-immersion differential theorem
makes $d_g$ and $d_v$ isomorphisms. Use their actual isomorphisms in the
specified native comparison words; define the displayed morphisms by
the separately specified native maps. In an abstract category, expansion
of the inverse composite uses $(abc)^{-1}=c^{-1}b^{-1}a^{-1}$, giving the
generic inverse-word identity. These constructions choose no compatible
pair of maps. The chart-specific homomorphism identification and the
pushed differential square are separate comparisons.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Functorial assembly from specified comparison isomorphisms]
\label{lem:kn-native-restricted-differential-functorial-assembly}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.finish_fixed_native_iso_square}
\leanok
Let $F_1,F_2,G_1,G_2$ be composable functors and
$e:F_1F_2\simeq G_1G_2$ a specified natural comparison, with the
composition convention that $F_1F_2$ first applies $F_1$.
Suppose $d:A\to B$ and a given square
\[
 G_1(d);b=a;d'
\]
are supplied. Let $e_A,e_B$ be specified isomorphisms with homomorphisms
$a,b$. If the specified target comparisons $T,S$ are exactly
$e_A$ and $e_B$ transported through $e$ and $G_2$, respectively, then
\[
 F_2(F_1(d));S=T;G_2(d').
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply $G_2$ to the supplied square, using preservation of composition.
Naturality of $e$ transports that equality to $F_1F_2$. Substitute the
given identities for $S,T$ and for the homomorphisms of $e_A,e_B$.
The result is the asserted equality. Its hypotheses include the square
and the identities of the specified comparisons; this lemma alone does
not supply those data for a geometric chart.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual restricted native differential square]
\label{thm:kn-native-restricted-differential-general-square}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelGlobalStructureTrace.WholeFreeChart.NativeDifferentialSquareData.general_restricted_differential_square}
\uses{lem:kn-native-restricted-differential-inverse-square,lem:kn-native-restricted-differential-word-naturality}
\leanok
In the arbitrary scheme square of the first lemma, assume only that
$v$ is an open immersion. For the original restriction functor and its
fixed comparison $\rho_v$ with pullback, the entire native square is
\[
 \operatorname{Res}_v(d_\pi);
 \rho_{v,\omega_{f\circ\pi}^{(n)}};d_v;H
 =
 \rho_{v,\pi^*\omega_f^{(n)}};\chi^{-1};p^*d_g;d_p.
\]
The field, schemes, maps, commuting-square equality, exterior degree and
every comparison are the original ones. No Cartesian hypothesis,
smoothness, finite map, invertible differential or trace is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first lemma produces the original pullback differential square from
the actual geometric commuting-square equality. Apply the restriction
naturality lemma with $R=\operatorname{Res}_v$, $F=v^*$,
$e=\rho_v$, and the original inverse word for $\chi^{-1};p^*d_g$.
Its supplied square is precisely the one already produced in the first
lemma. Therefore the conclusion is the displayed equality. The only
extra geometric requirement is that $v$ be an open immersion, so that
this fixed restriction comparison exists.
\end{proof}
