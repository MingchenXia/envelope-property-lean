\section{Actual tensor and dual functoriality of the Cartier ratio sheaf}
\begin{lemma}[Whole explicit tensor and sheaf dual preserve actual isomorphism laws]
\label{lem:cartier-actual-tensor-dual-functorial}
\uses{lem:actual-cartier-ratio-line-sheaf}
\leanok
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.tensorObjCongr_refl,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.tensorObjCongr_trans,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.dualIsoObj_refl,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.dualIsoObj_trans}
The actual sheafified tensor comparison preserves genuine identity and
composition isomorphisms, and the actual sheaf dual preserves identities
and reverses genuine compositions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the tensor, take the original whole underlying presheaf-module
isomorphisms, tensor them using the genuine monoidal category of
presheaves, and apply actual sheafification. The original tensor identity
and tensor-composition law and true functoriality of sheafification prove
the stated whole isomorphism equalities. For the sheaf dual, its original
map is precomposition by the actual whole sheaf morphism on every
original over-site. The genuine already proved dual identity and reversed
composition equations therefore give precisely the corresponding entire
isomorphism identities. No choice of local frames or comparison premise
is used.
\end{proof}
