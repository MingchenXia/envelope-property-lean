\section{The original parameter-one line on the normalized PL model}

\begin{lemma}[The entire original parameter-one fibre is noncentral]
\label{lem:pl-original-normalized-fibre-one-noncentral}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLOriginalModelCompatibility.range_fst_subset_basicOpen_X,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneInclusion,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneInclusion_range,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneNoncentralLift,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneNoncentralLift_inclusion}
\leanok
\uses{def:actual-normalized-pl-ratio-noncentral-trivialization}
Let $\nu:\widetilde{\mathcal X}\to\mathcal X$ be the normalization of the
simultaneous blowup of two original vertical ideals on $X\times\mathbb A^1$.
Let $\pi:\widetilde{\mathcal X}\to\mathbb A^1$ be its original structure map.
The canonical inclusion $i:\pi^{-1}(1)\to\widetilde{\mathcal X}$ factors
through the entire noncentral open $\pi^{-1}(D(t))$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The Cartesian square defining the original fibre gives
$\pi\circ i=1\circ q$, where $q$ is its canonical map to $\operatorname{Spec}k$.
The image of $t$ in the residue ring along the point $1$ is the unit $1$.
It therefore belongs to no prime ideal of that fibre. Hence the image of $i$
is contained in $\pi^{-1}(D(t))$. The universal property of the open immersion
gives a unique factorization through this open, with the same original fibre
inclusion as its composite.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same original normalized fibre one is the original variety]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-fibre-one-variety}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLOriginalModelCompatibility.baseChangeFibreIso,EnvelopeProperty.PLOriginalModelCompatibility.baseChangeFibreIso_hom,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeProductFibreOneIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeProductFibreOneIso_hom,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneProductIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneProductIso_hom,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneNativeIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneNativeIso_hom,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousFibreOneOverIso}
\leanok
\uses{lem:pl-original-fibre-map-neighbourhood-isomorphism,lem:pl-original-normalized-fibre-one-noncentral,thm:actual-normalized-pl-model-noncentral-product}
Suppose $X$ is the original projectively normal variety and the two original
homogeneous ideals contain powers of the original parameter. The same original
normalized simultaneous model has its actual scheme-theoretic fibre at $1$
isomorphic to the original native scheme $X$. The isomorphism is exactly the
canonical original fibre inclusion followed by the original normalized-model
projection to $X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The normalization-blowup composite is an isomorphism over the entire original
noncentral open of $X\times\mathbb A^1$, by normality of the original variety
and the whole original ideal parameter-power comparison. The preceding two
lemmas therefore identify its actual fibre at $1$ with the actual product
fibre at $1$. The existing whole product-coordinate isomorphism identifies
this latter fibre with the base change of $X$ along
$\operatorname{Spec}k\xrightarrow{1}\mathbb A^1\to\operatorname{Spec}k$.
This composite is the identity because evaluation at $1$ preserves every
coefficient. Pullback pasting and the identity-base pullback give the required
isomorphism with $X$. The two original Cartesian projection equations, followed
by the original product-to-$X$ projection equation, give the claimed entire
formula for its morphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original ratio and projective line on the entire parameter-one fibre]
\label{thm:pl-original-normalized-fibre-one-line}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousRatioFibreOneIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTwistedRatioFibreOneIso,EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousTwistedRatioFibreOneIdentification}
\leanok
\uses{lem:pl-original-normalized-fibre-one-noncentral,thm:actual-normalized-pl-projective-twist-noncentral}
Suppose the two original homogeneous ideals contain powers of the original
parameter. On the entire canonical original fibre over $1$, the normalized
Cartier ratio has an explicit whole module trivialization. The normalized projective twist tensor
this ratio restricts to the original $O(n)$ pulled back along the same
original fibre-to-$X$ projection.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Factor the actual original fibre inclusion through the noncentral open by the
preceding lemma. Pull the whole noncentral ratio trivialization back along
this factorization. The canonical pullback composition isomorphism identifies
its source with the pullback along the original fibre inclusion; the unit
pullback isomorphism identifies its target with the original fibre structure
module. For the polarized line, pull back the whole noncentral projective-line
comparison along the same factorization. Pullback composition and the exact
factorization identity give the original composite projection to $X$.
No replacement fibre, chosen point or chart comparison is used.
\end{proof}
