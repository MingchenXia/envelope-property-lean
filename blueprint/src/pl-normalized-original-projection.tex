\subsection{The literal original projection of the actual normalized model}

\begin{lemma}[The whole polarized-model projection is the literal original composition]
\label{local:actual-normalized-original-projection}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousOriginalProjection_eq}
\leanok
\uses{local:actual-original-product-scheme-projection}
For the original ideal pair with finite original Gauss logarithms, the already
constructed polarized-model projection to the same original $X$ is literally
the composition of the actual normalization projection, the actual original
simultaneous blowup projection, and the original product projection.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand the already constructed projection and its actual product pullback
isomorphism. The genuine pullback pasting and symmetry projection identities
identify its whole original variety projection with the original product
projection. Associativity gives the asserted literal three-map composition.
No replacement model or supplied projection comparison is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original coefficient-base square]
\label{local:actual-normalized-original-projection-base}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.normalizedSimultaneousOriginalProjection_over_base}
\leanok
\uses{local:actual-normalized-original-projection}
This original composition followed by the original $k$-structure equals the
normalized model's actual $k[t]$-structure followed by the literal coefficient
map on spectra induced by $k\to k[t]$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original product projection satisfies that exact coefficient-base square.
Compose it with the literal original blowup and normalization maps and use
associativity and the definitions of their actual structure morphisms.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original restricted point is the whole literal projection]
\label{local:actual-normalized-original-projection-native-point}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeAnalyticHomeomorph_normalizedSimultaneousBaseRestrictionAt}
\leanok
\uses{local:actual-normalized-original-projection-base}
For every actual normalized-model point over the Gauss valuation of $k[t]$,
the derived original analytic restriction corresponds to precisely the entire
native point mapped by this literal composed scheme projection. In particular,
the original support and original residue valuation are both retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual product-point restriction is the whole original scheme projection
on prescribed-base native points. Apply this established equality to the
actual image of the normalized-model point under normalization and blowup.
Functoriality of whole native-point mapping identifies the resulting three
successive maps with the map of the actual composed original projection.
The Gauss valuation restricts to the trivial valuation on every original
coefficient, giving the required exact prescribed-base map.
\end{proof}
