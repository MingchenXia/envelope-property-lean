\section{Actual original support frames and compatible pullback powers}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:actual-original-support-power-frame}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeSupportPowerFrames.supportGenericFrame_power}
\leanok
\uses{thm:fc-actual-pulled-power-frame-factor-coherence,def:scheme-support-line-frame}
An actual original open frame raised through the genuine native
power pullback comparison induces exactly the genuine pulled
power of its original support residue frame, in every degree.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The support residue frame is factor transport through the actual
residue-field lift to the support open. Its whole factor triangle
is the native residue morphism. Apply the actual factor-power
frame identity to this triangle and the original open frame.
The resulting equality is precisely the assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:actual-local-pullback-power-frame}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeSupportPowerFrames.localPullbackFrame_power}
\leanok
\uses{lem:actual-factor-power-frame-composition,lem:fc-original-section-pullback-frame}
For any actual $f:Y\to X$, original open $U\subseteq X$ and frame
$e$ of the actual $U$-restriction of $L$, the local pullback frame
of the original power frame of $e$ equals the local residue-free
pullback of the genuine power comparison for $f$, followed by the
pulled power frame of the original local pullback frame.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $j:f^{-1}U\hookrightarrow Y$ and $q:f^{-1}U\to U$ for the
actual inclusion and restricted map. Their actual equality
$q\iota_U=jf$ is the original restriction square. The local
pullback frame is the native composition comparison for $jf$
followed by factor transport of $e$ through $q$. Apply the factor
power frame composition identity to these exact maps. Its two
sides are the asserted original local pullback power frames.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:actual-support-local-pullback-power-frame}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeSupportPowerFrames.supportGenericFrame_localPullbackPower}
\leanok
\uses{lem:actual-local-pullback-power-frame,lem:actual-original-support-power-frame,lem:fc-actual-factor-frame-tensor-naturality}
Let $y\in f^{-1}U$ be any actual support point. The support residue
frame obtained from the local pullback of the original power
frame of $e$ is the actual residue pullback of the canonical power
comparison for $f$, followed by the genuine pulled power frame
of the same original degree-one support frame. This holds for
all nonnegative degrees on the same original support open.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding local frame identity and transport it through
the actual support residue-field factor. Naturality of factor
transport moves the pulled original power comparison to the
native residue morphism. The preceding support-power identity
identifies the remaining factor frame with the genuine pulled
power of the same degree-one support frame. Thus the entire
original residue-frame identity follows without a new frame
choice or a frame-preservation assumption.
\end{proof}
