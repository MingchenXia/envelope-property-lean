\section{Specialization of the original natural cochain complex}

\begin{theorem}[Actual positive cochain homology specializes]
\label{thm:kn-derived-natural-cochain-specialization}
\uses{cor:kn-derived-cochain-homology-specialization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedNatural.exists_natural_homology_zero_neighborhood,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedNatural.generic_natural_homology_isZero_of_closed_fibre}
\leanok
Let $K$ be an actual nonnegative cochain complex over a Noetherian
ring, with flat terms and finite native homology.  Suppose its
actual term $K^{N+1}$ is zero.  In each original positive degree
$0<t\leq N$, vanishing of the native homology of its original
residue tensor complex persists on a produced principal open.
Over a domain it reaches the original fraction field.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Extend the actual original complex by zero to integer indices.
Its nonnegative terms and maps have the native extension
isomorphisms; its negative terms are actual zero objects.
Termwise flatness and all native homology finiteness follow from
these constructed comparisons.  Apply the integer-cochain
specialization producer at reverse degree $N-t$.  Because $t>0$,
all three original natural neighboring indices are retained by
the extension.  The actual scalar-extended segment isomorphism
therefore transports both the residue input and the field outputs.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Actual geometric fibres]

\end{remark}
