\section{Actual rank-one module sheaves of the Cartier ideals}

\begin{lemma}[A regular principal ideal is the original free rank-one module]
\label{lem:regular-generator-module-equiv}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.regularGeneratorModuleEquiv,EnvelopeProperty.regularGeneratorModuleEquiv_apply}
\leanok
For a commutative ring and an actual ideal generated by a nonzerodivisor
$g$, multiplication by $g$ is a genuine linear isomorphism from the
original ring to that actual ideal.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Multiplication lands in the ideal and is linear.  Generation by $g$
gives surjectivity.  If two multiples agree, their difference multiplied
by $g$ is zero; regularity gives equality of the two original scalars.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual ideal module has genuine local rank-one trivializations]
\label{lem:cartier-ideal-affine-module-trivializations}
\uses{lem:regular-generator-module-equiv}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.regularGeneratorModuleIso,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.IsInvertible.exists_affine_module_trivialization}
\leanok
The actual module sheaf associated to an effective Cartier ideal admits,
around every original point, an actual affine restriction isomorphic to
the actual structure sheaf.  The regular generator gives this concrete
isomorphism; no line-bundle comparison is an assumption.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Over the true affine restriction the original ideal module is the sheaf
associated to its global sections, by the actual localization theorem
for ideal sheaves.  Those sections are exactly the original ideal on
the affine open.  The preceding multiplication isomorphism identifies
them with the original affine section ring.  Apply the genuine tilde
functor to this linear isomorphism and then the actual affine counit
isomorphism.  Invertibility supplies such an affine open and regular
generator at every original point.
\end{proof}


\begin{lemma}[The existing locally free sheaf structure, with rank-one charts]
\label{lem:cartier-ideal-locally-free-module}
\uses{lem:cartier-ideal-affine-module-trivializations}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.regularGeneratorModuleOverIso,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.regularGeneratorFreeOverIso,AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.IsInvertible.isLocallyFree_toModules}
\leanok
The same actual Cartier ideal module sheaf is locally free in the existing
sheaf-of-modules interface.  Its local trivializations use the singleton
free sheaf and therefore have rank one.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Restrict the preceding affine-spectrum trivialization along the actual
isomorphism from the affine open to its spectrum, and use the true
restriction-composition and unit isomorphisms.  The existing equivalence
between sheaves on an open and sheaves on the over-site turns this into
an actual trivialization of the original module over that open.
The free sheaf on a singleton is the structure sheaf by the actual
singleton coproduct isomorphism.  Choose the actual regular-generator
open at every original point.  These opens cover the entire scheme,
and transport the single free generator by the proved local isomorphisms.
The resulting generator morphisms are isomorphisms, giving the existing
\texttt{IsLocallyFree} structure for the original ideal module sheaf.
\end{proof}
