\section{The original global-section maps in the infinitesimal Cech sequence}

\begin{lemma}[Canonical original global sections and ordered degree-zero cohomology]
\label{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-h0-cech}
\uses{thm:fibre-infinitesimal-ordered-cech-short-exact}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0Cech.globalSectionsIso,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0Cech.globalSectionsIso_naturality}
\leanok
For every original open cover and every original module sheaf, its
whole original global sections are canonically isomorphic, with their
original base coefficients, to the degree-zero cohomology of its
ordered Cech complex. This identification intertwines every original
module-sheaf morphism with its induced degree-zero cohomology map.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The sheaf gluing theorem identifies global sections with the kernel of
the first ordered Cech differential, by restricting the same global
section to every member of the original cover. A nonnegative cochain
complex has no boundaries in degree zero, so that kernel is its entire
degree-zero cohomology. The inclusion of this cohomology into the
original degree-zero cochain module is injective. Both ways round the
asserted naturality square have, at every ordered zero-tuple, the same
original restriction followed by the same given module-sheaf morphism;
its naturality says that this equals the reverse composite. The
injective cohomology inclusion therefore proves the whole naturality
square.
\end{proof}


\begin{corollary}[The genuine original global-section lifting obstruction]
\label{cor:fibre-infinitesimal-global-section-obstruction}
\uses{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-h0-cech,cor:fibre-infinitesimal-ordered-cech-long-exact}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0Cech.sectionBoundary,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0Cech.sectionBoundary_exact,EnvelopeProperty.FibreInfinitesimalH0Cech.sectionBoundary_eq_zero_iff}
\leanok
For an original affine open cover of a separated $X$, every whole
original section on the literal infinitesimal $X_n$ has a canonical
obstruction class in degree-one cohomology of the original ideal-power
Cech complex. This obstruction vanishes exactly when that section is
the original projection of a whole original global section on $X$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use the preceding canonical identification on the actual infinitesimal
pushforward module and follow it by the connecting morphism of the
proved actual Cech long exact sequence. The same identification on
$\mathcal O_X$ intertwines the literal original projection section map
with its induced cohomology map, by the proved whole naturality square.
Exactness of the long exact sequence at the third degree-zero term
therefore transports along these two actual isomorphisms. This gives
exactly the image of the whole original projection, with no section
lift assumed. Its value on every original section is consequently
zero precisely for sections that actually lift as asserted.
\end{proof}
