\begin{lemma}[The whole actual scalar pullback tower]
\label{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-tower-map}
\leanok
\uses{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-pullback}
For an actual coefficient tower $R\to S\to T$, the literal iterated
scheme pullback has a genuine morphism to the literal single final
scalar pullback. Its whole final coefficient projection is unchanged,
and its whole original scheme projection is the actual double
projection. Native scalar restriction through this morphism equals the
successive actual scalar restrictions on entire valued points.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The two actual pullback coefficient squares and the genuine original
coefficient tower identity give a commuting scheme square. Use the
actual pullback universal property with original double projection and
final structure morphism. Its two full projection identities give the
asserted whole triangles. Native functoriality applied to the entire
original projection triangle proves the whole valued-point identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole-scheme lifts through the genuine nontrivial closed extension]
\label{lem:fc-scheme-nontrivial-complete-scalar-lift}
\leanok
\uses{def:fc-nontrivial-complete-scalar-field,lem:fc-scheme-scalar-tower-map,lem:fc-scheme-gauss-scalar-lift,lem:fc-scheme-scalar-algebraic-completion-lift}
For every actual $F$-scheme, its whole native scalar restriction from
the literal $F_1$-scheme pullback is surjective. Here the same genuine
nontrivial complete algebraically closed extension $F_1/F$ is fixed by
$F$, independently of the original point or scheme.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Construct an actual Gauss scalar lift of the original whole point.
The actual Gauss field is complete. Its genuine valued algebraic
closure is algebraic over it, so the proved whole-scheme
algebraic-completion scalar lift lifts this entire point to the actual
iterated scalar pullback. Map it through the preceding genuine
scalar-tower morphism to the literal single $F_1$-pullback. The exact
whole native tower identity and the two actual lift identities recover
exactly the original whole point. No scalar-lift or composition
certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}
