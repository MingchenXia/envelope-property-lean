\section{Coherence of the actual canonical tensor pullback}

\begin{lemma}[The actual inverse tensorator respects genuine composite pullback]
\label{lem:cartier-actual-pullback-inverse-tensorator-composition}
\lean{AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.pullbackComp_inverse_tensorator}
\leanok
For actual scheme morphisms $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$, the genuine
inverse tensorator of pullback along $f\circ g$ agrees with the two
successive original inverse tensorators, under the true native
pullback-composition comparisons on the two original module factors.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine composition comparison for the actual pullback functors
is monoidal. Its tensor identity says that the tensorator of successive
pullback followed by that composition comparison on the original tensor
is the tensor of the two original factor comparisons followed by the
tensorator of composite pullback. Precompose the claimed identity with
the tensorator of successive pullback. The left composite cancels
successively its two inverse tensorators. On the right, use exactly
that monoidal tensor identity and then cancel the true composite
pullback tensorator with its inverse. The resulting two morphisms are
the same tensor of the original factor comparisons. Cancellation of
the original invertible successive-pullback tensorator proves the
claimed identity. No tensor comparison or coherence is assumed.
\end{proof}
