\section{Original section restriction for the same actual Witt maps}

Let $\kappa$ be perfect of characteristic $p$ and let $\widetilde f:\widetilde X\to\operatorname{Spec}W_2(\kappa)$ be the actual smooth pure-dimension-$n$ lifting. Retain its literal special fibre $f_0:X_0\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa$, its original geometric twist $Y$, and its original relative Frobenius. Write $M=\Omega^1_{Y/\kappa}$ and $E^q=F_*\Omega^q_{X_0/\kappa}$. For affine lifted opens $U\subseteq V$, write $U_0,V_0$ for their actual special-fibre inverse images and $W_U,W_V$ for their actual twisted opens. The two residual etale coordinate tuples may be any honest tuples of the declared length $n$; they need not match.

\begin{theorem}[The original whole section restriction squares]
\label{thm:actual-witt-geometric-section-restrictions}
\uses{def:actual-witt-geometric-xi-homotopy-sections}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.ActualWittGeometricXiHomotopy.xiSectionMap_restrict, EnvelopeProperty.DeligneIllusie.ActualWittGeometricXiHomotopy.homotopySectionMap_restrict}
\leanok
For an original actual lift $F$ on $V$, the canonical etale construction internally produces its same original restricted lift $F|_U$ and its entire original reduction square. For every $s\in\Gamma(W_V,M)$ one has
\[
 \operatorname{res}_{E^1}\bigl(\Xi_{V,F}(s)\bigr)
   =\Xi_{U,F|_U}\bigl(\operatorname{res}_M(s)\bigr).
\]
For two original lifts $F,G$ with their actual reductions, the same canonical restricted lifts satisfy
\[
 \operatorname{res}_{E^0}\bigl(\mathcal H_{V,FG}(s)\bigr)
   =\mathcal H_{U,F|_U,G|_U}\bigl(\operatorname{res}_M(s)\bigr).
\]
No selected target lift, reduction-model comparison, restriction identity, or matching-unit certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The existing actual Witt restriction theorem produces the canonical restricted lift and its entire original reduction, then proves the whole native individual-map square using the literal residue-section equivalences $\epsilon_U,\epsilon_V$. Its canonical zero-form theorem does the same for both lifts and their difference term. On the original source, the proved whole twisted-chart exterior restriction square, followed by the original degree-one exterior equivalence, is exactly the native cotangent restriction map. Thus the same source section has the same native value on both paths. On the target, the original geometric chart form isomorphism commutes with original exterior restriction and the actual relative-Frobenius preimage identification. The original exterior restriction is the underlying map of the native form restriction. Compose these three entire squares. This proves both displayed equalities on every original source section, including empty affine intersections. Each comparison is an existing original map or an internally derived actual lift square.
\end{proof}
