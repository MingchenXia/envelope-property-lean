\section{The original coherent ideal-power maps in Rees Cech coordinates}

\begin{lemma}[The original ideal-power inclusions in every Rees degree]
\label{lem:fibre-ideal-power-cech-inclusion-comparison}
\uses{lem:fibre-ideal-power-module-inclusions}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerCechMaps.inclusion_term,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerCechMaps.inclusion_complex,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerCechMaps.inclusion_homology}
\leanok
For a finite ordered affine family on a separated original scheme,
the canonical original coherent ideal-power Cech identifications
intertwine the whole sheaf inclusion from power $m$ to power $n\le m$
with the literal higher-to-lower inclusion on every Rees-degree Cech
term, on the entire complexes, and on their cohomology in every degree.
All coefficient actions are the original $R$ actions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On an original affine intersection, the proved coherent section
identification retains the underlying original function. The actual
sheaf inclusion and the literal ideal-power inclusion both retain
exactly that function, so the whole factor square commutes. Taking
the product over all ordered intersections proves the entire term
square. The actual maps and identifications commute with all original
restrictions and alternating differentials; the term squares therefore
assemble into the asserted whole cochain square. The naturality of
cohomology under original scalar restriction, followed by functoriality
of cohomology on that cochain square, proves the same comparison in
every cohomological degree.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Original coefficient multiplication in every Rees degree]
\label{lem:fibre-ideal-power-cech-multiplication-comparison}
\uses{lem:fibre-ideal-power-coefficient-multiplication,lem:fibre-ideal-power-cech-inclusion-comparison}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerCechMaps.multiply_term,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerCechMaps.multiply_complex,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerCechMaps.multiply_homology}
\leanok
Under the same identifications, actual multiplication by any original
$a\in I^e$ from power $d$ to power $e+d$ is the literal homogeneous
Rees multiplication, on every entire original Cech term, complex and
cohomology group.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual sheaf multiplication on an affine intersection multiplies
its entire original function by its original coefficient section. The
coefficient section equals the coefficient homomorphism of that same
actual Rees chart. Consequently its image under the whole section
identification is exactly literal homogeneous multiplication on the
same ideal power. These whole factor identities give the product term
identity. Functoriality of the unchanged alternating differential gives
the whole complex identity; naturality of cohomology under the original
scalar restriction and functoriality on the complex identity give the
asserted cohomology comparison in every degree.
\end{proof}
