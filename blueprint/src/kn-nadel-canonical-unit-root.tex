\section{Canonical trace on the whole tame unit-root layer}
\label{sec:kn-nadel-canonical-unit-root}

Let $A$ be a nonzero commutative ring.  Throughout this section the algebra is the entire actual monic quotient
$B=A[z]/(z^{m+1}-u)$, where $u\in A^\times$ and $m+1$ is invertible
in $A$.  No connected component, field factor, or chosen root is selected.

\begin{theorem}[The whole tame unit-root trace dual]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-canonical-unit-root-trace-dual}

The actual root and $J=(m+1)z^m$ are units in $B$.  The whole algebra
is etale over $A$, and native algebra trace gives a genuine $B$-linear
isomorphism
\[
 B\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Hom}_A(B,A),\qquad
 b\longmapsto(c\longmapsto\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}(cb)).
\]
Its true restriction/coextension counit is $\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}$
on every original element of the whole algebra.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original unit-root cover theorem proves the root unit and actual
etaleness from the invertible evaluated derivative.  The actual
positive-degree monic basis already proves the top-coefficient
multiplication pairing $\Lambda$ is perfect, and its native trace
calculation is $\operatorname{Tr}(b)=\Lambda(Jb)$ for every $b$.
Multiply the original regular $B$-module by the actual unit $J$ and
compose with that original dual-frame isomorphism.  Evaluation at
every $c$ gives the displayed native trace functional.  The native
adjunction counit evaluates at $1_B$, proving the whole trace formula.
All computations occur on the entire quotient algebra, even if it is
disconnected or splits as a finite product.
\uses{thm:actual-kummer-unit-root-finite-etale,lem:kn-nadel-root-trace-jacobian}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Retaining the unit-root layer after the actual ramified power cover]
\label{thm:kn-nadel-canonical-power-unit-composition}

adjoin the entire actual tame root of any original unit on that

coextension of the original polynomial base canonical module along
the actual composite coefficient map.  The whole counit is the
actual tame trace followed by the already proved ramified canonical
counit, on every actual top form.  Neither the unit layer nor any
of its components is discarded.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding actual unit-layer isomorphism to the original
polynomial differential frame on the ramified source.  Coextend the
already proved actual ramified-power canonical isomorphism along
the actual unit-root coefficient map.  Native coextension composition
then identifies this with coextension along the genuine whole

evaluates the resulting functional by first taking the whole tame
trace and then the original ramified counit.  The original power
action and the actual root coefficient map remain separate.
\uses{thm:kn-nadel-canonical-unit-root-counit,thm:kn-nadel-kummer-canonical-module}
\end{proof}

Let $R\to A\to B$ and $R\to A\to S$ be the original commutative-ring
towers, let $A\to S$ be formally etale, and put $C=S\otimes_A B$ with
its literal original left and right coefficient maps.  Given an actual
rank-$n$ differential frame of $\Omega_{B/R}$, there is an internally
constructed genuine comparison
\[
 S\otimes_A\bigwedge_B^n\Omega_{B/R}
   \xrightarrow{\sim}\bigwedge_C^n\Omega_{C/R}
\]
of $S$-modules.  On every original differential wedge, $1\otimes
(w_1\wedge\cdots\wedge w_n)$ maps to the wedge of its native
K\"ahler images in $C$.  The analogous original base comparison
$S\otimes_A\bigwedge_A^n\Omega_{A/R}\simeq
\bigwedge_S^n\Omega_{S/R}$ is also internally produced.
These comparisons require no flatness beyond original formal etaleness,
no chosen component, and no supplied Omega-model isomorphism.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First work on the actual ring pushout with its true four coefficient
maps.  Native pushout tensor cancellation gives
$C\otimes_B M\simeq S\otimes_A M$ for every original $B$-module
$M$, and its inverse sends $s\otimes m$ to $a_C(s)\otimes m$.
Every actual $C$-linear comparison can therefore be restricted and
composed to an $S$-linear comparison with this exact formula.
Formal etaleness of $A\to S$ is preserved by the actual pushout,
so the true map $B\to C$ is formally etale.  Apply native etale
K\"ahler and exterior base change to the original differential frame,
and compose with this tensor cancellation.  The original pure-tensor
formula and native wedge formula prove the claimed genuine
whole differential map.  For the literal tensor algebra, its native
pushout instance is produced internally; tensor balance and the
original scalar towers prove equality of the two original $R$-actions.
Applying the same native etale exterior construction directly to
$A\to S$ gives the original base comparison.  If $A\to S$ is
additionally faithfully flat, these are the actual module comparisons
needed to transport canonical maps to the proved descent
functor; the overlap equality is a separate proof obligation.
\uses{thm:kn-nadel-canonical-unit-root-differentials}
\end{proof}

Faithfully flat canonical descent still requires the actual overlap
identifications and actual tensor/Omega comparisons for the original

duality and its canonical normalization; it does not identify a
singular normalized cover with a smooth polynomial component or
assert global canonical gluing or Nadel vanishing.
