\section{The original parameter has its whole unit-power identity in the actual auxiliary chart}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:original-auxiliary-whole-unit-chart}
Retain the original characteristic-zero normalized coefficient-slice hypotheses and original nonzero parameter $t$ in the maximal ideal of the unchanged original DVR. There is an actual adapted etale surface chart of the original normalized model with auxiliary coordinate $\pi$, together with a smaller actual affine neighborhood of the same original trait center, on which the original parameter section is a whole unit times the $e$th power of this very auxiliary coordinate. Here $e>0$ is the unchanged original order of $t$ in $V$. The whole original trait factors through the smaller neighborhood, the auxiliary morphism remains smooth of relative dimension one, and its scalar in the original $V$ is the same irreducible element as before.

\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual original uniformizer trait chart. Let $x$ be its actual trait center, let $\tau$ be the original parameter section pulled back from the original normalized structure map, and let $p$ be the actual auxiliary coordinate section. The canonical map $\mathcal O_{A,x}\to V$ is the actual native ring isomorphism proved for this same original trait. It sends the germs of $\tau,p$ to the unchanged original $t,\pi$, by the entire original polynomial diagrams. In $V$ the already derived identity is $t=v\pi^e$, with $v$ a unit and $e>0$ the original order of $t$.

Carry $v$ back through this same native local-ring isomorphism to a unit $u$ in $\mathcal O_{A,x}$. Injectivity of the original local map gives the actual germ equality $\tau_x=u p_x^e$. Lift $u$ and $u^{-1}$ simultaneously to two sections on a single actual affine neighborhood of $x$. Their product has germ one. The chart is an open subscheme of the original integral model and is integral; its germ map on a nonempty open is injective. Therefore these two sections multiply to one on the entire neighborhood, so the first is an actual whole unit $w$. The same germ injectivity, applied to the displayed local parameter equality, proves the entire actual section identity $\tau=w p^e$ there.

Restriction of the etale coordinate map to this actual open remains etale, and the auxiliary parameter morphism remains smooth of relative dimension one. Every prime of the unchanged original trait specializes to its closed point, so the whole original trait factors through the smaller neighborhood. Composing with the original open immersion gives exactly its unchanged original projection. The auxiliary coordinate and scalar uniformizer were merely restricted, and the original integer $e$ was never changed. In particular this whole-open identity imposes no reducedness or multiplicity-one condition on the original parameter fibre.
\end{proof}
