\subsection{Genuine native evaluation points are nonisolated on etale curve charts}
\label{subsec:etale-curve-nonisolation}

\begin{lemma}[Nonisolation through actual continuous injections and open maps]
\label{topology:actual-open-map-nonisolation}
For arbitrary topological spaces, a continuous injection sends a
nonisolated point to a nonisolated point, and a point over a
nonisolated image under an open map is nonisolated. No separation
condition is needed.
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a continuous injection, the preimage of the image singleton is
exactly the original singleton; openness of the former singleton
would therefore imply openness of the latter. For an open map,
the image of the source singleton is exactly the image singleton,
so openness of the source singleton would contradict nonisolation
of its image. These are ordinary topological lemmas; in the native
applications the continuity, injectivity and openness are proved
from the genuine morphisms and Gauss family, not supplied as native
geometry certificates.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Actual native points of original rational evaluators]
\label{scheme:actual-native-rational-evaluation}

\uses{scheme:relative-valued-point-functor,scheme:relative-affine-valued-point-homeomorphism}
For an arbitrary commutative ring $R$, prescribed seminorm $v$,
actual $R$-algebra $A$ and genuine evaluator $e:A\to_R R$, the
native evaluation point on $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is the actual
affine comparison inverse of the seminorm $a\mapsto v(e(a))$.
Every original section has precisely this value, and actual affine
scheme morphisms transport these points by whole evaluator
composition.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Pull back the actual relative point $v$ of $R$ along the entire
original algebra map $e$. Its coefficient law makes this a true
fixed-base seminorm of $A$. Apply the genuine affine native
homeomorphism inverse; no topology is declared through this point.
The inverse law identifies its full seminorm with $v\circ e$.
The proved original-ring/global-section value formula then gives
$v(e(a))$ for every whole original $a$, including all coefficients.
For a genuine algebra homomorphism $A\to_R B$, actual affine
native naturality identifies its whole image seminorm with the
pullback of $v\circ e$ and hence with $v\circ e\circ\varphi$.
Affine-homeomorphism injectivity proves equality of ENTIRE native
points, not just support equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual line evaluation points have no open singleton]
\label{scheme:actual-native-line-evaluation-nonisolation}
\leanok
\uses{topology:actual-open-map-nonisolation,scheme:actual-native-rational-evaluation,spectrum:centered-gauss-whole-ray}
For every actual $a\in R$, evaluation at $a$ in the true native
fixed-$v$ space of $\mathbb A^1_R$ is nonisolated: its singleton
is not open and its punctured neighborhood filter is nontrivial.
The coefficient ring and seminorm are arbitrary, including finite
fields and trivial valuations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the already constructed genuine translated Gauss family over
the actual coefficient point $v$. Its whole value on $T-a$ is
exactly its nonnegative real radius, so it is injective. The
original Gauss-coordinate formulas prove continuity at EVERY
radius, including zero. At zero its entire seminorm equals
$v\circ\operatorname{eval}_a$, by the actual polynomial
translation and original zero coefficient evaluation identity.

Zero is nonisolated in the nonnegative real radius space: every
neighborhood contains a strictly positive radius, since zero lies
in the closure of the positive radii. Continuous injectivity
therefore implies nonisolation of the entire radius-zero seminorm.
The genuine affine native homeomorphism carries this whole point
to the actual line evaluation point, preserving nonisolation.
Finally the standard singleton/punctured-neighborhood identity
converts the proved absence of an open singleton into nontriviality
of the punctured neighborhood filter. No nontrivial absolute value,
infinitude, algebraic closure, completeness or coefficient bound
is required.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine etale curve charts give nonisolated rational native points]
\label{scheme:actual-etale-curve-rational-nonisolation}
\leanok
\uses{topology:actual-open-map-nonisolation,scheme:actual-native-line-evaluation-nonisolation,scheme:actual-general-etale-native-open-map,scheme:relative-open-embedding}
A genuine etale morphism to $\mathbb A^1_R$ makes every native
point mapping to an actual line evaluation point nonisolated in
its fixed-$v$ native source. In particular, for an actual etale
$R[T]\to_R A$, EVERY genuine rational evaluator $A\to_R R$
gives such a nonisolated original native point. The same conclusion
holds for an original point admitting such an actual etale chart
on a genuine scheme open neighborhood. These are actual etale
charts, not assumed open native maps or supplied comparison
certificates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The proved general etale native theorem derives openness of the
actual morphism on the ENTIRE fixed-base spaces from its genuine
algebraic etaleness. If the source singleton were open, its actual
image singleton would be open. The given whole point image is the
line evaluation point, contradicting its proved nonisolation.

For an actual affine rational evaluator $e:A\to_R R$ and original
etale algebra map $\varphi:R[T]\to_R A$, evaluator composition
is the actual polynomial evaluation at the original scalar
$e(\varphi(T))$. Its coefficient equality is the genuine algebra
law; equality on $T$ and all original coefficients determines the
ENTIRE polynomial homomorphism. The preceding whole evaluator
naturality therefore derives the required native image identity,
rather than assuming a native comparison or openness certificate.

If the etale chart is defined on an actual scheme open containing
the original support, the genuine relative restriction
homeomorphism gives its exact whole native preimage. Apply the
preceding chart result there. Its original inclusion into the
whole native space is a continuous injection, so the first
nonisolation lemma preserves nonisolation of the original point.
This establishes the local topological nonisolation input relevant
to the curve step, without constructing a curve potential,
asserting a local homeomorphism of etale native maps, or inferring
native density merely from scheme-support density.
\end{proof}
