\section{Finiteness in each original ideal-power degree}

\begin{lemma}[Finite cohomology of every original ideal power]
\label{lem:fc-formal-functions-degree-finite}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FormalFunctionsDegreeFinite.orderedIdealPowerCech_finite, EnvelopeProperty.FormalFunctionsDegreeFinite.originalIdealPowerCech_finite, EnvelopeProperty.FormalFunctionsDegreeFinite.degreeCech_finite}
\uses{lem:fc-rees-cech-ideal-power-sections}
Let $R$ be Noetherian and let $X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ admit a
projective factorization.  On every finite ordered affine cover of $X$,
each cohomology group of each original ideal-power sheaf $I^d\mathcal O_X$
is a finite $R$-module.  The same conclusion holds for every cohomology
group of the literal degree-$d$ Rees Čech complex.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A projective morphism is of finite type, so $X$ is locally Noetherian.
Consequently each extended ideal-power sheaf is finitely presented and
quasicoherent.  Projective coherent cohomology finiteness applies to
these actual sheaves: in positive degree it gives finiteness for the
unordered affine-cover Čech complex, and the canonical ordered-cover
comparison gives the ordered result.  In degree zero the ordered Čech
cohomology is the global-section module, whose finiteness follows from
the degree-zero projective cohomology theorem.  The affine section
isomorphism identifies the original base ring with the global sections
of its spectrum, so restriction along this isomorphism preserves finite
generation.  Finally the ideal-power Čech comparison identifies this
same finite module with the literal original Rees-degree cohomology.
\end{proof}
