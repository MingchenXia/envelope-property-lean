\section{Grading of the actual tensor-power base ideals}

\begin{proposition}[The actual tensor-power base ideals form a graded sequence]
\label{prop:actual-line-power-base-ideal-grading}
Let $L$ be any original invertible sheaf. For its actual recursively
formed tensor powers, put $\mathfrak a_m=\mathfrak b(L^{\otimes m})$.
For every $m,n\geq0$, $\mathfrak a_m\mathfrak a_n\subseteq
\mathfrak a_{m+n}$.
\uses{lem:actual-base-ideal-iso-transport,prop:actual-invertible-base-ideal-multiplication}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.TensorPowerBaseIdealGrading.tensorPower_isInvertible,EnvelopeProperty.TensorPowerBaseIdealGrading.tensorPowerAddIso,EnvelopeProperty.TensorPowerBaseIdealGrading.powerBaseIdeal,EnvelopeProperty.TensorPowerBaseIdealGrading.powerBaseIdeal_mul_le}
\leanok
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The canonical associators and structure-sheaf unitors construct the
isomorphism $L^{\otimes m}\otimes L^{\otimes n}\simeq
L^{\otimes(m+n)}$ by induction on $n$. Apply whole base-ideal
multiplication and the preceding isomorphism invariance. No finite family,
local frame, or supplied graded-sequence certificate is needed. This is
the actual algebraic grading input to the paper's test-configuration
formula; the analytic norm identity and full envelope formula remain
separate obligations.
\end{proof}
