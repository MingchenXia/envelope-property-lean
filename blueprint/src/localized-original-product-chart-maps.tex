\subsection{Whole original maps from selected charts into actual product localizations}

\begin{lemma}[Original fractions construct the selected-to-product localization map]
\label{algebra:actual-localized-original-product-chart-map}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.exists_originalProductChartLocalizationMap}
\leanok
\uses{algebra:actual-product-rees-chart-ratios,algebra:actual-rees-degree-zero-chart-equivalence}
Let $A$ be ANY commutative DOMAIN, let $I$ be finite and let original families
$G_i:J_i\to A$ be given.  Fix an actual selection $J$ with original
$P_J=\prod_iG_i(J_i)\ne0$, an index $i$, and an actual selected $d\in J_i$
with $G_i(d)\ne0$.  In the ORIGINAL product chart $B_J$, put
$h=P_{J[i:=d]}/P_J$, its true homogeneous coordinate ratio.  Construct a
genuine injective $A$-algebra map from the ACTUAL localization
$B_J[1/h]$ into the SAME original fraction field, extending the whole
original product-chart map.  Construct also an ACTUAL $A$-algebra map
\[
 \operatorname{ReesChart}(G_i,d)\longrightarrow B_J[1/h]
\]
whose ENTIRE composite into that SAME field is the ORIGINAL selected-chart
map.  No localization, injectivity, scalar action, fraction comparison or
chart map is supplied as a certificate.  Individual $J_i$ may be infinite.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original product-chart map $\iota:B_J\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Frac}A$
is genuinely injective.  The actual replacement-ratio formula gives
$\iota(h)=G_i(d)/G_i(J_i)\ne0$, so the real principal-localization universal
property constructs its whole extension $\tau:B_J[1/h]\to\operatorname{Frac}A$.
This extension is injective: if two original numerators have equal images
after localization, applying $\tau$ gives equality under $\iota$;
conversely equality under $\iota$ gives equality of the original numerators
and hence of their localization images.  The true localization injectivity
criterion proves injectivity on EVERY localization element.

For each original $k\in J_i$, use the real localization fraction with numerator
the ORIGINAL product-chart replacement coordinate $P_{J[i:=k]}/P_J$ and
denominator the ORIGINAL $h$.  Its actual $\tau$ image is
\[
 \frac{G_i(k)/G_i(J_i)}{G_i(d)/G_i(J_i)}=G_i(k)/G_i(d).
\]
All cancellations use the two actual nonzero original factors.
Consequently the entire original selected-chart fraction-adjoin algebra is
contained in the ACTUAL image of $\tau$.  Compose its genuine original
fraction-chart isomorphism with this literal subalgebra inclusion and the
inverse of the actual injective-map range isomorphism of $\tau$.  This gives
the claimed whole algebra map.  Its entire field-composite identity follows
from the true inverse laws on EVERY selected-chart element, retaining each
original coefficient and coordinate.  No generator-value inference replaces
equality of whole ring homomorphisms.
\end{proof}
