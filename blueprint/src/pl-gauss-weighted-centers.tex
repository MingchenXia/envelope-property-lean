\section{Every existing weighted Gauss point has its true original closed center}

Keep the ORIGINAL integral $Y$ and proper original
$f:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}k[t]$, with $k$ a field. At an actual original
$p\in Y$ put $S=\mathcal O_{Y,p}$ and let $t_p$ be the image of the
original polynomial variable under the ORIGINAL stalk coefficient map.
For the point theorem assume the genuine Noetherian stalk and original
parameters $\mathfrak m_S=(\pi,z)$, $\pi$ prime, $\pi\nmid z$,
$t_p\ne0$ and $\pi\mid t_p$. These are precisely honest local geometric
data of the existing point construction. No DVR, valuation family,
field square, norm comparison or center certificate is supplied.
For $N>0$ retain its SAME old actual constructed weighted chart DVR $V_N$
and its original map $j_N:S\to V_N$.

\begin{definition}[The original weighted DVR morphism]
\label{def:gauss-weighted-original-dvr-morphism}
\uses{thm:gauss-regular-surface-original-dvr}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussWeightedCenters.weightedMorphism}
\leanok
The actual whole scheme morphism is
\[
 q_N^{\rm mod}:\operatorname{Spec}V_N
    \xrightarrow{\operatorname{Spec}j_N}\operatorname{Spec}S
    \xrightarrow{Y.\operatorname{fromSpecStalk}(p)}Y.
\]
All rings, coefficients and morphisms are the original constructed ones.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Use the old original quotient-localization coefficient map and the
canonical actual stalk-spectrum map. Composition gives exactly the
displayed original morphism, without choosing a substitute model.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual weighted DVR closed point maps to the actual original point]
\label{lem:gauss-weighted-original-closed-point}
\uses{def:gauss-weighted-original-dvr-morphism,thm:gauss-regular-surface-original-dvr}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussWeightedCenters.weightedMorphism_closedPoint}
\leanok
For every $N>0$, the TRUE closed point satisfies
\[
 q_N^{\rm mod}(\mathfrak m_{V_N})=p.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The old actual coefficient-center theorem gives
$j_N^{-1}(\mathfrak m_{V_N})=\mathfrak m_S$. Thus the actual affine
spectrum map takes the genuine closed point of $V_N$ to the genuine
closed point of $S$, by equality of their original prime ideals.
The actual stalk-spectrum map sends that SAME closed point to $p$.
Their original composite therefore sends the original weighted closed
point to $p$. No existence of a different point with this center is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The SAME existing normalized native weighted point has true center p]
\label{thm:gauss-weighted-existing-native-true-center}
\uses{def:gauss-weighted-original-dvr-morphism,lem:gauss-weighted-original-closed-point,thm:gauss-regular-surface-original-dvr,lem:gauss-native-original-stalk-point-sequence,def:gauss-surface-actual-scaled-field-points,lem:gauss-surface-parameter-positive-vertical-order,thm:gauss-surface-actual-fixed-radius-points,thm:actual-dvr-fraction-point,scheme:native-coefficient-restriction,berkovich:actual-dvr-native-model-point,berkovich:actual-dvr-native-model-center,alggeom:original-stalk-coefficient-map,alggeom:original-stalk-coefficient-triangle,spectrum:gauss-valuation,lem:actual-specified-field-generic-morphism,scheme:valued-point-pullback,scheme:affine-valued-point-homeomorphism,spectrum:affine-seminorm}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.GaussWeightedCenters.center_nativeWeightedPoint}
\leanok
For EVERY actual $N>0$ and EVERY $0<r<1$, the SAME already defined
original point
\[
 y_N=\operatorname{nativeWeightedPoint}(f,p,\pi,z,N,r)
\]
over the exact radius-$r$ Gauss valuation has TRUE proper-model center
\[
 \mathfrak c_r(y_N)=p.
\]
This holds for every positive index, not merely eventually. The original
point and its normalization by its TRUE original parameter order are
unchanged; no pure-power expression $t_p=u\pi^e$ is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The old producer internally supplies the actual Noetherian weighted DVR
$V_N$, original embedding $e_N:V_N\to K=\operatorname{Frac}S$, and
actual fraction-ring structure on this SAME original $K$. Its EVERY
coefficient law says $e_Nj_N(s)=s/1$ for all original $s\in S$.
The original $k$-action is the restriction of the actual polynomial
stalk coefficient map; the coefficient law gives its actual scalar
tower through $V_N$ and $K$.

The actual positive weighted order $d_N(t_p)$ internally gives
\[
 c_N=-\log r/d_N(t_p)>0.
\]
By its OLD definition, the whole original
field norm used in $y_N$ is exactly the old DVR-fraction norm of THIS
$V_N$ at scale $c_N$, through THIS $e_N$. It is not a supplied matching
norm. On EVERY actual $a\in V_N$ its value is the original DVR norm of
$a$, by the old whole-fraction coefficient theorem.

The original polynomial stalk triangle identifies
$f\circ q_N^{\rm mod}$ with the actual spectrum map of the original
coefficient map $k[t]\to S\to V_N$. The genuine generic map into $Y$
used by the original $y_N$ construction is exactly
$\operatorname{Spec}K\to\operatorname{Spec}V_N\to Y$:
the EVERY-$S$ coefficient law above and the canonical original
stalk/function-field fraction isomorphism identify their whole ring
maps, and actual stalk-specialization naturality gives the whole scheme
triangle. This comparison is derived on the SAME original coefficients.

Apply the old native base-point restriction identity to EVERY $V_N$
coefficient of that DVR-fraction point. It identifies the WHOLE existing
original $y_N$ with the old native DVR point obtained from THIS
$q_N^{\rm mod}$ and THIS positive scale. The latter's bounded original
base coefficients follow from its original nonnegative DVR orders.
If the two presentations record different base valuations, their equal
whole underlying native point gives equal base points; evaluation of
every original polynomial proves the valuations equal. Proof irrelevance
then identifies their proper centers, retaining the actual Gauss bound.
The old native DVR model-center theorem gives its center as
$q_N^{\rm mod}(\mathfrak m_{V_N})$, and the preceding lemma identifies
that literal image with $p$. Thus the unchanged $y_N$ has TRUE center $p$.
No support or convergence statement is used to infer the center, and no
uniqueness of valuations from a two-dimensional center is claimed.
\end{proof}

This is the actual geometry needed to compute the old proper model-line

construct global surface/SNC data, nefness or LocalPositivity.
