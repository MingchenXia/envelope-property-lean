\section{The actual proper-model norm is independent of the valuative frame}

\begin{lemma}[Actual original valuative frames give the same line norm]
\label{lem:scheme-line-actual-model-norm-frame-independent}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.centerFrameLineNorm_independent}
\leanok
\uses{def:scheme-line-actual-center-frame-norm,lem:actual-unit-module-automorphism-scalar}
Any two actual global frames of the original line over its genuine
proper residue-field valuation-ring center give the same norm on
every section of the actual original generic-line module.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let the two original valuative frames be $e,e'$. Their actual ratio
$e^{-1}e'$ is a whole structure-module automorphism over the genuine
valuation ring $V_y$. Hence it is multiplication by an original global
unit $u$ of $\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}V_y,\mathcal O)$, identified
with a unit of $V_y$. The actual structure-module scalar pullback
identity says that the two original generic frames differ by
multiplication by the actual image of $u$ in $\kappa(y)$. The actual
affine global-section naturality square identifies this scalar with
the original inclusion $V_y\hookrightarrow\kappa(y)$ applied to $u$.
A unit and its inverse both belong to the original valuation ring,
so both have valuation at most one. Their product has valuation one;
therefore $|u|_y=1$. The generic coefficient in frame $e'$ is the
coefficient in frame $e$ times this actual scalar, so its norm is
unchanged by the original valuation multiplication law. This proves
equality for every section of the same original generic line.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Actual norm of the original proper-model line]
\label{def:scheme-line-actual-model-norm}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelLineNorm}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelLineNorm_eq_frame}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelLineNorm_eq_zero_iff}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelLineNorm_add_le_max}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelLineNorm_smul}
\leanok
\uses{lem:scheme-line-actual-model-norm-frame-independent,lem:scheme-line-actual-center-frame}
For an actual original invertible module sheaf $\mathcal L$, define
its proper-model norm at the original valued point by taking any
actual valuative frame and its just constructed original generic-line
norm. This norm is independent of that choice, vanishes exactly at
zero, is nonarchimedean, and scales by the original residue-field
valuation for every original scalar.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The actual valuative frame exists by original invertibility, properness
and local-spectrum triviality. The preceding lemma identifies the
norms of every two such frames, so the definition is independent of
which existing original frame is chosen. Apply the original frame
norm's proved zero, nondegeneracy, addition and scalar identities to
that chosen actual frame. Every identity is on the entire original
generic-line section module, which is unchanged in this construction.
\end{proof}
