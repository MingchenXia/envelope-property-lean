\section{Actual original homogeneous forms have whole ideal-tensor lifts}

Write $P=\mathbf P_R^n$, $D=(\widetilde J).\operatorname{comap}j$,
and $L=j^*\mathcal O(d)$, always using the existing original positive-power
line with pivot $0$, original tensor module and actual ideal module.
The general tensor module $L\otimes D.\operatorname{toModules}$ need not
be invertible.  The following two complete nodes have frozen native verification.

\begin{lemma}[The two existing original homogeneous coefficients agree]
\label{lem:resume-native-form-original-homogeneous-coefficient}
\uses{lem:fc-original-homogeneous-form-coefficients}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.BlowupHomogeneousTautologicalLine.nativeFormSection_eq_homogeneousCoefficient}
\leanok
For every homogeneous form $F$ of degree $d\in\mathbb N$ and every
original coordinate open $D_+(X_i)$, the existing whole section
$\operatorname{nativeFormSection}_i(F)$ is exactly the existing original
homogeneous coefficient $F/X_i^d$.  This includes $F=0$ and $d=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first section is the image of the original polynomial
de-homogenization under the native coordinate-to-section ring
isomorphism.  The inverse whole coordinate-ring isomorphism takes that
de-homogenization to the actual homogeneous fraction $F/X_i^d$.
The existing native homogeneous-chart section isomorphism is exactly
the original fraction-to-section map.  Apply these two whole-map
identities to $F$.  All coefficient structures, fractions and section
maps are the original ones.  Homogeneity alone is used; no nonzero
condition or replacement of the degree by a total degree is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine whole lift into the original ideal tensor module]
\label{thm:resume-original-form-whole-ideal-tensor-lift}
\uses{lem:resume-native-form-original-homogeneous-coefficient,lem:resume-original-homogeneous-whole-scalar,lem:blowup-projective-pulled-forms,lem:blowup-projective-subordinate-cover,thm:blowup-projective-global-map,thm:fc-original-homogeneous-form-section,lem:fc-original-homogeneous-form-frame-coefficient,def:actual-line-frame-via-factor,lem:fc-original-line-section-frame-naturality,lem:fc-original-line-section-fibre,lem:fc-original-local-section-scalar,lem:fc-original-section-pullback-frame,lem:actual-ideal-module-whole-kernel,lem:actual-ideal-module-pullback-map,sc0-tensor-obj-morphisms:mono,sc0-tensor-ideal-shuffle:inclusion}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.BlowupHomogeneousTautologicalLine.exists_originalFormIdealSection}
\leanok
Let $R$ be any commutative ring, $j:X\to P$ any original morphism,
$J$ an original homogeneous ideal, and $F\in J$ homogeneous of its
original degree $d\in\mathbb N$.  Put $s_F$ equal to the value at one
of the existing original whole homogeneous section morphism of $F$
pulled along $j$.  There exists an actual whole original section
\[
 b_F\in\Gamma(X,L\otimes D.\operatorname{toModules})
\]
whose image under the literal original tensor-ideal inclusion is exactly
$s_F$.  All coefficients, frames and scalar actions are the original ones.
No matching of local lifts or existence of a whole lift is an input.
The result includes degree zero, zero forms, empty opens and arbitrary
commutative coefficient rings, without flatness or a Cartier hypothesis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Cover $X$ by all original affine opens subordinate to some
$j^{-1}D_+(X_i)$.  On such an open $U$, let $e_i$ denote the actual pulled
$X_i^d$ frame of $L$.  Transport the actual original $X_i^d$ frame through the genuine
restricted-scheme factorization $U\to D_+(X_i)\to P$, using the native
pullback-composition and structure-unit isomorphisms.  The preceding
coefficient identification, the original homogeneous-section frame
theorem, and whole factor-frame scalar naturality show that the original
section has coefficient $g_F=j^*(F/X_i^d)$.  The native restriction
section-ring square and its actual top-section isomorphisms identify
this coefficient with the original entire function on $U$.  Since $F\in J$, the exact
original quotient-ideal chart formula puts $g_F$ in the entire ideal
$D(U)$.  The original ideal-to-module construction therefore gives an
actual ideal-module section with included function $g_F$.

Take its pure tensor with $e_i$ using the original native tensor and
its canonical explicit sheaf-tensor comparison.  Under the literal
original tensor-ideal inclusion the result is $g_Fe_i$, exactly the
restriction of the same global pulled homogeneous section.  Tensoring
the actual ideal inclusion by the original invertible $L$ is monic;
the true right-unit isomorphism preserves this monicity.  Consequently
the two local tensor sections agree on every overlap, because their
images are restrictions of that same global section.  Ordinary sheaf
gluing supplies the actual whole section.  Sheaf extensionality gives
its asserted entire inclusion identity.   Empty opens, zero forms and degree zero satisfy these
same identities without additional hypotheses.
\end{proof}
