\section{The actual ideal-power transition and coefficient morphisms}

\begin{lemma}[The original ideal-power inclusions on the whole scheme]
\label{lem:fibre-ideal-power-module-inclusions}
\uses{lem:fibre-infinitesimal-categorical-kernel,lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-image-kernel}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.inclusion,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.inclusion_app,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.inclusion_inclusion,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.inclusion_self,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.inclusion_comp}
\leanok
For every $n\le m$ there is a canonical inclusion of the actual original
ideal-power modules $(I\mathcal O_X)^m\to(I\mathcal O_X)^n$. Its
underlying map on every open is inclusion of the same original
functions. Composing with the original inclusion into $\mathcal O_X$
gives the original $m$th-power inclusion. These maps are the identity
at equal orders and compose according to the original order relation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On every affine open the $m$th power of the actual original coefficient
image ideal is contained in its $n$th power. Thus every section of the
original $m$th ideal module satisfies the local membership condition
for the original $n$th ideal module. The inclusion followed by the
order-$n$ projection is zero on every whole open. The proved categorical
kernel property therefore constructs the desired morphism, leaving
every underlying function unchanged. Its composite with inclusion
recovers the original whole module inclusion. Both the equal-order
and composition laws follow by canceling the original monomorphism
into $\mathcal O_X$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original coefficient-power multiplication morphisms]
\label{lem:fibre-ideal-power-coefficient-multiplication}
\uses{lem:fibre-ideal-power-module-inclusions,lem:fibre-infinitesimal-global-image-filtration}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.coefficientEnd,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.coefficientEnd_app,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.multiply,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.multiply_app,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.multiply_inclusion,EnvelopeProperty.FibreIdealPowerTransitions.multiply_inclusion_square}
\leanok
Every actual coefficient $a\in I^e$ gives a morphism of original
module sheaves $(I\mathcal O_X)^d\to(I\mathcal O_X)^{e+d}$ which on
every original open is multiplication by the restriction of the
original global coefficient $a$. It commutes with all original
ideal-power inclusions whenever their source and target orders permit
the same whole coefficient multiplication. Its composite into
$\mathcal O_X$ is the original ideal-power inclusion followed by
multiplication by that same coefficient.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The restriction of the original global coefficient to any affine
open lies in the $e$th power of the actual original coefficient image
ideal, since extension commutes with ideal powers. Multiplying an
original section locally in its $d$th power gives a section locally
in its $(e+d)$th power. This is the local membership condition for the
entire original target ideal module. Multiplication is additive and
linear over the original structure ring, and commutes with every
restriction because the original coefficient itself is a global
section. It therefore defines the stated morphism of module sheaves.
Its composite into $\mathcal O_X$ is the same multiplication on every
whole section. The inclusion and multiplication squares have equal
underlying original section functions; injectivity of the original
ideal-module inclusion proves equality of the whole morphisms.
\end{proof}
