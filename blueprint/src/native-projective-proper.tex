\label{spectrum:normalized-projective-polynomial-bounds}

\leanok

\begin{proof}

its original coefficient value. Take the genuine finite support maximum. The
empty polynomial and zero ring cases are included. For eventual bounds, intersect
only the finitely many actual coefficient events for this fixed original $F$,
and take their finite maximum. No generator-value reconstruction is used.
\end{proof}
