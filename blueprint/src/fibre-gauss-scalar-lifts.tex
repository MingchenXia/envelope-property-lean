\subsection{Actual scalar lifts to the original completed Gauss field}

\begin{lemma}[A genuine completed Gauss coefficient field and tensor lift]
\label{lem:fc-gauss-scalar-tensor-lift}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussBasePoint, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussField, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussFieldAlgebra, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussField_val_algebraMap, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fc_gauss_comap_base, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussScalarCoefficient, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussScalarCoefficient_norm, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussOriginalEvaluation, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussScalarEval, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussScalarEval_tmul, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussScalarLift, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fcGaussScalarLift_baseChangeRestriction, EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSpectrum.fc_baseChangeRestriction_gauss_surjective}
\uses{lem:fc-scheme-scalar-affine-tensor}
For every actual coefficient ring with prescribed seminorm and radius
$r\ge0$, the completed residue field of its actual base-line Gauss
point is a genuine coefficient extension with the prescribed original
values. Every original algebra point has an actual scalar lift to this
field, retaining every original algebra value.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Whole Gauss naturality identifies the base Gauss point under the actual
polynomial coefficient map. Its genuine completed-field functor gives
an entire coefficient-preserving isometric map into the completed field
of the Gauss point above the original algebra point. Combine this map
with the original algebra evaluation into that same field through the
actual tensor universal property. Pull back its actual norm. On the
new coefficient field the whole isometric map gives the prescribed
norm; on every original algebra element the original constant
polynomial evaluation gives its exact original value. Thus actual
scalar restriction recovers the entire original point.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole-scheme Gauss scalar lifts]
\label{lem:fc-scheme-gauss-scalar-lift}
\leanok
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.SchemeScalarBaseChange.nativeMap_surjective_gauss}
\uses{lem:fc-gauss-scalar-tensor-lift,lem:fc-scheme-scalar-affine-tensor}
Every whole native point of any actual scheme lifts to its literal
scalar scheme pullback by the completed residue field of the original
base-line Gauss point. This holds for arbitrary coefficient rings and
$r\ge0$, and in particular for the complete-field extensions used
in Lemma~\ref{lem:fc-base-change}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a genuine original affine Spec chart through the support. The
actual chart structure morphism derives its coefficient action and
its exact whole Spec identity. Apply the preceding actual tensor
point lift and the true affine tensor scheme comparison. The actual
pullback morphism induced by the original chart inclusion preserves
the entire new coefficient structure and the whole original
projection. Its native image therefore recovers exactly the input
whole point. No affine-cover gluing certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}
