\lean{EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativeProductNormalizedPoint, EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.nativePolynomialLineProductPoint_nativeProductNormalizedPoint, EnvelopeProperty.ProjSubvariety.continuous_nativeProductNormalizedPoint}

normalized polynomial projective point $x$. Transporting $x$ through the

homeomorphism identities and the whole inverse-hom scheme identity.
Continuity follows from the actual continuous scheme maps and inverse

\end{proof}
