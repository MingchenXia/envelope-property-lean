No new central-fibre scheme or proxy is introduced. The scheme in these

\begin{lemma}[The original constant-form cut has its actual coefficient zero set]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-original-coefficient-cut-support}
\uses{def:pl-gauss-original-homogeneous-cut,lem:pl-gauss-original-homogeneous-cut-support,projective:native-coefficient-basic-opens}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionCentralFiber.coefficientCut_support}
\leanok
Let $R$ be any actual commutative ring, let $i:Y\to\mathbf P^n_R$ be
the original morphism, and let $r\in R$. For the literal constant form
$C(r)$ of degree zero, the SAME actual original cut satisfies
\[
 \left|\mathcal K_{C(r)}\right|
 = (\mathrm{nativeStructureMap}\circ i)^{-1}V(r),
\]
where the composition denotes $Y\xrightarrow{i}\mathbf P^n_R\to
\operatorname{Spec}R$. All original points and maps are retained,
including $r=0$ and nilpotents in $R$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual geometric ideal-pullback support formula makes the left
side the inverse image of the original projective hypersurface support.
The proved whole quotient support identifies that with $V_+(C(r))$.
At the original homogeneous prime of $i(y)$, this says exactly that
$C(r)$ belongs to that prime. The existing genuine native structure
coefficient-prime theorem equates this with membership of $r$ in the
actual coefficient prime of the SAME image point. That is precisely
membership of the original $y$ in the stated inverse image of $V(r)$.
Extensionality on original points gives the whole support equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual dominant coefficient structure internally makes the nonzero coefficient cut proper]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-original-nonzero-coefficient-cut-proper}
\uses{lem:pl-gauss-original-coefficient-cut-support}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionCentralFiber.coefficientCut_support_ne_top}
\leanok
If $R$ is a domain, $r\ne0$, and the actual original composite
$Y\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is dominant, then this SAME original cut
has proper support. No proper-support or nonempty-open certificate is
an input, and no absolute properness over a field is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual zero prime of the domain is a point of the actual open
$D(r)$ because $r\ne0$, so this original open is nonempty. Dominance
means that the SAME actual structure map has dense image. Its actual
image therefore meets $D(r)$, giving an original $y\in Y$ whose
coefficient prime avoids $r$. By the preceding exact support equality,
this actual point lies outside the original cut. It witnesses the
asserted proper support internally.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual original central coefficient-cut component has the exact whole dimension]
\label{thm:pl-gauss-original-parameter-cut-component-dimension}
\uses{lem:pl-gauss-original-nonzero-coefficient-cut-proper,thm:pl-gauss-original-polynomial-cut-whole-component-dimension}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionCentralFiber.original_parameter_component_dimension_add_one}
\leanok

original field $k$, and let $i:Y\to\mathbf P^n_{k[t]}$ be an actual

\[

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original polynomial ring $k[t]$ is a domain and its actual
parameter $t$ is nonzero. Apply the preceding lemma to the SAME
actual dominant coefficient map, deriving proper support of this
literal original constant-form cut. Its degree-zero homogeneity is

component. It derives the stated whole dimension for the SAME

coefficients or parameter.
\end{proof}
