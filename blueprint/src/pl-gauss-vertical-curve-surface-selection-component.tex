\section{Actual integral components containing the original closed subscheme}
All closed ideals, subschemes and maps in this section are the original
ones. The given scheme $C$ is integral, so both its nonemptiness and its
reducedness are available internally. No selected component or desired
factorization is an input. The constructions below do not yet assert
that the chosen component is horizontal or has a specified dimension.

\begin{lemma}[The actual image kernel of a reduced source is its vanishing ideal]
\label{lem:pl-gauss-reduced-original-image-kernel}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionComponent.reduced_source_kernel_eq_vanishingIdeal}
\leanok
For any actual original morphism $c:C\to Y$ with reduced $C$, its
whole kernel ideal sheaf equals the vanishing ideal of the actual
closed subset $\overline{c(C)}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The vanishing ideal of the whole original $C$ is its nilradical, hence
zero because $C$ is reduced. Push this whole ideal along the actual
map $c$. The original zero-ideal pushforward is precisely $c.\ker$.
The genuine vanishing-ideal pushforward theorem identifies the same
pushforward with the vanishing ideal of the closure of the actual
image of the whole $C$. These are equal whole ideal sheaves, without
assuming a radical-kernel or image-matching certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An actual integral component contains the whole original C]
\label{thm:pl-gauss-integral-component-through-original-closed-subscheme}
\uses{lem:pl-gauss-reduced-original-image-kernel,lem:fc-scheme-curve-dimensions}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionComponent.exists_integral_component_through_closed_subscheme}
\leanok
Let $c:C\hookrightarrow Y$ be an actual original closed immersion with
integral source. There is an actual irreducible component $Z$ of $Y$
such that $c(C)\subseteq Z$. Give $Z$ its existing reduced closed
subscheme $D$ and original closed immersion $e:D\hookrightarrow Y$.
Then $D$ is integral and there is an actual CLOSED immersion
$g:C\hookrightarrow D$ satisfying the WHOLE original triangle
$e\circ g=c$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The whole original $C$ is nonempty and irreducible, and the actual map
$c$ is continuous. Its actual image is therefore a nonempty
irreducible subset of $Y$. Extend this subset to a maximal irreducible
subset by the genuine topological irreducible-component theorem.
Such a component is closed. Equip this particular actual closed set
$Z$ with its existing vanishing-ideal subscheme $D$; the existing
reduced-closed-subscheme theorem makes it integral.

Since $Z$ is closed and contains the whole original image,
$\overline{c(C)}\subseteq Z$. Vanishing ideals reverse this actual
closed-set inclusion. The preceding whole image-kernel equality gives
$\mathcal I_Z\subseteq c.\ker$. The genuine closed-immersion lift
therefore constructs the actual $g:C\to D$ and its whole triangle.
Its composite with the original closed immersion $e$ is the original
closed immersion $c$, so closed-immersion cancellation makes $g$ a
closed immersion. Thus the chosen actual integral component contains
the entire original $C$ as a closed subscheme, including its original
map, without any incoming component, ideal or triangle certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The original polynomial cut has an actual integral component through C]
\label{cor:pl-gauss-original-cut-integral-component}
\uses{thm:pl-gauss-original-closed-cut-through-subscheme,thm:pl-gauss-integral-component-through-original-closed-subscheme}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.PLGaussVerticalCurveSurfaceSelectionComponent.exists_eventually_original_integral_cut_component}
\leanok
Let $R$ be an actual commutative algebra over an infinite field $k$,
let $i:Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_R$ and $c:C\hookrightarrow Y$
be the original closed immersions, and assume $C$ integral. Let
$(y_a)$ be a finite NONEMPTY family of actual points outside $c(C)$.
For every sufficiently large degree, the preceding actual homogeneous
cut has an actual integral reduced irreducible component $D$ and
original CLOSED immersions $g:C\hookrightarrow D$ and
$j:D\hookrightarrow Y$ such that $j\circ g=c$ and $j(D)$ avoids
every original $y_a$. The actual map $j$ factors through the SAME
constructed original polynomial cut, and that factor identifies $D$
with a reduced irreducible component of that actual cut.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply the proved original closed-cut producer. It internally gives,
in each sufficiently large SAME degree, an actual homogeneous $F$,
its actual ideal pullback cut $K$, and the actual closed immersion
$c_K:C\hookrightarrow K$ with the whole original triangle. Apply
the preceding component theorem to this particular $c_K$. It produces
an actual integral reduced component $D$ of that SAME $K$ and a closed
immersion $g:C\hookrightarrow D$. Compose its original component
inclusion $e:D\hookrightarrow K$ with the original $K\hookrightarrow Y$
to define $j$. Closed immersions compose. Associativity and the two
proved whole triangles give $j\circ g=c$. Every actual image point
of $D$ lies in the SAME original cut, which avoids every $y_a$, so
its image avoids them as well. No horizontality, dimension, normality,
whole-cut flatness or surface-family conclusion is asserted here.
\end{proof}
