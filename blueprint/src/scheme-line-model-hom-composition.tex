\section{Original model morphism norms respect actual composition}

\begin{lemma}[True whole original composition norm]
\label{lem:actual-model-hom-norm-composition}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelHomNorm_comp}
\leanok
\uses{def:scheme-line-actual-model-hom-norm,lem:scheme-line-actual-model-norm-one-section}
For actual morphisms $\alpha:M\to N$ and $\beta:N\to P$ of
original invertible modules on an original proper model, the
genuine model morphism norm of their whole original composite is
the product of their original model morphism norms.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take an actual section of the original residue-field pullback of
$M$ with genuine model norm one. Pulling back the actual original
composite equals the actual composite of the two pulled module
morphisms. The original norm scaling theorem computes the norm of
its image both directly and successively. Since the chosen actual
section has norm one, the direct calculation gives precisely the
model norm of the composite. The successive calculation gives the
product of the two original morphism norms. Commutativity of the
original scalar value monoid gives the stated order.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Identity and whole original isomorphisms]
\label{cor:actual-model-hom-norm-identity-isomorphism}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelHomNorm_id}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Berkovich.RelativeSchemeValuedPoint.modelHomNorm_iso}
\leanok
\uses{def:scheme-line-actual-model-hom-norm,lem:scheme-line-actual-model-norm-one-section,lem:scheme-line-actual-model-isomorphism-isometry}
The actual original identity has model morphism norm one. Every
actual whole original line-module isomorphism also has model
morphism norm one.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For the identity, the norm scaling theorem applied to an actual
norm-one section gives one as its morphism norm. For an actual
whole original isomorphism, the original model norm isometry
identity identifies the image of such a section as another actual
norm-one section. The same exact original norm scaling theorem
then gives morphism norm one.
\end{proof}
