\section{Whole tensor coherence for the original model comparisons}

Fix the original test configuration and original lines $M,H$ with whole
action comparisons $\alpha,\gamma$ and fibre-one comparisons with original
lines $L,K$.  The tensor action and fibre-one comparisons are the actual
inverse pullback tensorator, tensor of the two given comparisons, and
target pullback tensorator, in that order.

\begin{lemma}[Actual whole tensor comparison on the unit open]
\label{lem:native-tc-nef-fs-unit-open-tensor-coherence}
\leanok
\uses{lem:native-tc-nef-fs-whole-tensor-comparisons, thm:native-test-configuration-line-trivialization, lem:cartier-actual-pullback-inverse-tensorator-composition}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.unitOpenLineIso_tensor_comparisons}
The resulting whole unit-open isomorphism of $M\otimes H$ with the
pullback of $L\otimes K$ is the tensor of the two original whole unit-open
isomorphisms, conjugated by the actual source and target pullback
tensorators.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A monoidal natural isomorphism commutes with inverse tensorators: multiply
its monoidal tensor square on the left and right by the inverse
tensorators.  The inverse tensorator of a composite strong monoidal
functor is the mapped inner inverse tensorator followed by the outer
inverse tensorator.  Finally, inverse tensorators are natural for the
two original line isomorphisms.  Apply these three elementary identities
to the nine factors defining each original whole unit-open comparison.
Actual pullback composition is monoidal, while pullback congruence for
equal original scheme maps is monoidal by substitution of that equality.
Composing the nine squares and functoriality of the actual tensor product
give the stated whole original isomorphism.  No equality is asserted
only after evaluation, and no action is replaced by a character twist.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual whole tensor comparison on the product]
\label{lem:native-tc-nef-fs-product-tensor-coherence}
\leanok
\uses{lem:native-tc-nef-fs-unit-open-tensor-coherence, def:native-tc-product-original-line-iso}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.NativeTestConfiguration.TestConfiguration.productTotalLineIso_tensor_comparisons}
The product line isomorphism of the original tensor data is the actual
tensor of the two original product line isomorphisms, with the original
product-map and original-reference-map pullback tensorators.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the same three identities to the inverse product pullback
composition, the pullback of the whole unit-open comparison, the target
pullback composition and the final original-projection map congruence.
Insert the preceding whole unit-open tensor identity and compose the
four squares.
\end{proof}
