\section{The original projective ordered section complex}

\begin{lemma}[A bounded flat original section complex with finite homology]
\label{lem:kn-derived-projective-coordinate-complex}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedCech.exists_coordinate_complex}
\leanok
Let $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ be an actual flat morphism with a
projective factorization, where $R$ is Noetherian, and let $M$ be an
actual invertible module on $X$.  There is an original projective
coordinate cover whose actual ordered base-linear \v{C}ech complex
is bounded, has flat terms, and has finite native homology in every
degree.  The coefficient ring in this native construction is exactly
$\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}R,\mathcal O)$ with its action induced by $f$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the existing projective-factorization finiteness producer to choose
the original closed embedding and its ordered coordinate cover.  A
closed embedding makes every coordinate preimage affine.  Projectivity
also derives separatedness.  An invertible module is locally free and
finitely presented; flatness of the family makes its sections on every
affine intersection flat over the original affine base.  Finite
products retain flatness.  Ordered tuples above the cover cardinality
are empty, so the actual terms there vanish.  Finiteness of every
native homology module is the independently proved projective
coherent-finiteness theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The actual coordinate tensor homology specializes]
\label{cor:kn-derived-projective-coordinate-tensor-specialization}
\uses{lem:kn-derived-projective-coordinate-complex,thm:kn-derived-natural-cochain-specialization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.SpecializationDerivedCech.exists_coordinate_tensor_specialization}
\leanok
On the produced original coordinate cover, positive-degree native
homology vanishing of the actual residue tensor section complex
persists on a produced principal open for every field-valued point.
The coefficient ring and maps remain those of the native original
affine base.  No geometric fibre cohomology comparison is supplied.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply the constructed natural-cochain specialization producer to
the actual bounded flat section complex with its independently
finite native homology.  Beyond the cover bound the original
middle term itself is zero, so its actual tensor homology is zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Scope of the geometric producer]
\label{rmk:kn-derived-projective-coordinate-complex-scope}
This constructs the actual section complex, its algebraic
finiteness and flatness properties, and its actual tensor-homology
specialization.  The canonical original-$R$ coefficient action is
retained in Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-derived-original-scalar-coefficients}.
The actual nonflat ordered-complex comparison and its fibre
cohomology interpretation are proved in
Theorem~\ref{thm:kn-geometric-cech-base-change} and
Lemma~\ref{lem:kn-derived-geometric-fibre-cohomology}.
\end{remark}
