\section{Relative Nadel vanishing from the canonical trace of adapted covers}
\label{sec:kn-relative-nadel-assembly}

Throughout, $k$ is a field of characteristic zero, $R$ is a finite-type
$k$-algebra, and $X$ is an integral scheme with $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$
smooth of relative dimension $n$ and $\pi:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ a morphism
over $k$. For an ideal $I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ that is nonzero at the generic
point, let $\mu:Y\to X$ be its Hironaka principalization, so that
$I\cdot\mathcal O_Y=\mathcal O_Y(-E)$ for an SNC divisor $E$, and for
$q\in\mathbb Q_{\geq0}$ put
\[
 Q_q=\omega_{Y/k}\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-\lfloor qE\rfloor),\qquad
 \omega_{Y/k}=\textstyle\bigwedge^n\Omega^1_{Y/k}.
\]
The chapter derives relative ample Nadel vanishing from one local input, the
existence of the canonical trace on adapted cyclic covers. Kawamata--Viehweg
vanishing on $Y$ is obtained from relative Kodaira vanishing by the covering
trick, and local vanishing of $R^i\mu_*$ is replaced by the vanishing of all
positive twists by a relatively ample line. The setting is relative over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$: neither properness of $X$ over $k$ nor algebraic
closedness of $k$ is used.

\begin{definition}[The canonical trace on every adapted cover {\cite{EV92}}]
\label{def:kn-relative-nadel-cover-trace-input}
\uses{lem:kn-nadel-global-normalized-geometric-properties}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelAssembly.CoverCanonicalTraceInput}
\leanok
For a field $k$, the \emph{canonical trace input} is the following
proposition. Let $Y$ be an integral scheme with $g:Y\to\operatorname{Spec}k$
smooth of relative dimension $n$. Let $(F_i)_{i}$ be a finite family of Cartier
divisors on $Y$, let $a\in\mathbb Q$ with denominator $N$, let $L_i$ be
invertible, and let $\sigma_{i,0},\dots,\sigma_{i,n}$ be auxiliary sections of
$L_i^{\otimes N}\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-F_i)$ whose zero divisors together with
the $F_i$ form an SNC family and have empty common intersection for each $i$.
Let $p:Y'\to Y$ be the normalization of the fibre product of the cyclic covers
$t_{i,j}^N=\sigma_{i,j}$, let $\deg p=N^{(n+1)r}$, where $r$ is the number of
divisors $F_i$, be the product of the degrees of these cyclic covers, and let
$dp:p^*\omega_{Y/k}\to\omega_{Y'/k}$ be the differential. Then there is a morphism
$T:p_*\omega_{Y'/k}\to\omega_{Y/k}$ with
\[
 \omega_{Y/k}\xrightarrow{\ \eta\ }p_*p^*\omega_{Y/k}
   \xrightarrow{\ p_*(dp)\ }p_*\omega_{Y'/k}\xrightarrow{\ T\ }\omega_{Y/k}
 \;=\;\deg p\cdot\mathrm{id}_{\omega_{Y/k}},
\]
where $\eta$ is the unit of the adjunction $p^*\dashv p_*$. The proposition
contains no cohomology and does not require $Y$ to be proper.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Principalization over a finite-type affine base]
\label{lem:kn-relative-nadel-principalization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalization.structure_finiteType,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalization.structure_isSeparated,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalization.sequence,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalization.stage_smoothOfRelativeDimension,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalization.totalTransform_isIdealOfSncDivisor,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalization.composite_isProper,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalization.last_isIntegral}
\leanok
If $\pi$ is projective, then $f$ is separated and of finite type. If $f$ is
separated and of finite type, the principalization $\mu:Y\to X$ of $I$ is a
finite sequence of blow-ups in smooth centres; $\mu$ is proper, $Y$ is integral
and smooth of relative dimension $n$ over $k$, and $I\cdot\mathcal O_Y$ is the
ideal of an SNC divisor.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The structure map $\operatorname{Spec}R\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is of finite
type and affine, hence separated; $\pi$ is projective, hence proper. Thus
$f=\pi$ followed by this structure map is separated and of finite type.

Hironaka's principalization applies to the triple consisting of the smooth
separated finite-type $k$-scheme $X$, the ideal $I$, and the empty boundary;
$I$ is nonzero in every stalk, because $X$ is integral, so every stalk of
$\mathcal O_X$ injects into the function field, and $I$ is nonzero at the
generic point. It produces a sequence of blow-ups in centres smooth over $k$;
each stage is therefore smooth of relative dimension $n$ over $k$, and the
total transform of $I$ is the ideal of an SNC divisor. A finite composite of
blow-ups of locally noetherian schemes in closed centres is proper. The
composite is an isomorphism over the open set where $I=\mathcal O_X$, which
contains the generic point, so $Y$ is nonempty. A blow-up of an integral
scheme is either empty (when the centre is the whole scheme) or integral;
since $Y$ is nonempty, every stage, and in particular $Y$, is integral.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The twisted multiplier module is a pushforward]
\label{lem:kn-relative-nadel-twisted-multiplier}
\uses{lem:kn-relative-nadel-principalization}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalizationTwistedMultiplier.originalTwistedMultiplierIso,EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.NadelRelativePrincipalizationTwistedMultiplier.originalTwistedMultiplierPushforward_isQuasicoherent}
\leanok
Let $f$ be separated and of finite type and $A$ an invertible module on $X$.
Then
\[
 (\omega_{X/k}\otimes A)\otimes\mathcal J(q\cdot I)\;\simeq\;
 \mu_*\bigl(Q_q\otimes\mu^*A\bigr),
\]
where $\mathcal J(q\cdot I)$ is the multiplier ideal defined by all divisorial
valuations, and both sides are quasicoherent.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Since $\mu$ is a log resolution of $I$, the multiplier ideal is computed on
$Y$: $\omega_{X/k}\otimes\mathcal J(q\cdot I)\simeq\mu_*Q_q$. Tensoring with
$A$ and the projection formula $\mu_*(Q_q)\otimes A\simeq\mu_*(Q_q\otimes\mu^*A)$
for the invertible $A$ give the isomorphism, after rearranging the tensor
factors by associativity and symmetry. The multiplier ideal is a quasicoherent
ideal and $\omega_{X/k}\otimes A$ is invertible, so the left side is
quasicoherent, and so is the isomorphic right side.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Relative Kawamata--Viehweg vanishing on the principalization {\cite{EV92}}]
\label{thm:kn-relative-nadel-source-vanishing}
\uses{def:kn-relative-nadel-cover-trace-input,lem:kn-relative-nadel-principalization,thm:kn-relative-nadel-numerator-cover,thm:kn-relative-kodaira-characteristic-zero,thm:affine-pushforward-cohomology-vanishing,def:kn-nadel-twisted-bounded-canonical-unit,lem:kn-nadel-bounded-canonical-pushforward-unit-factor,thm:kn-nadel-whole-degree-scalar-isomorphism,lem:actual-cohomology-retract-vanishing}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelAssembly.relativeSourceVanishing_of_coverTrace}
\leanok
Assume the canonical trace input for $k$, that $R$ is of finite type over $k$,
and that $f$ is separated and of finite type. Let $M,B$ be invertible on $X$,
let $I\otimes B$ be generated by finitely many global sections, let $q\geq0$
and $d>0$, $t\geq0$ be integers with $dq=t$, and let
$M^{\otimes d}\simeq C\otimes B^{\otimes t}$ with $C$ ample over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$. Then
\[
 H^{r+1}\bigl(Y,\;Q_q\otimes\mu^*M\bigr)=0\qquad(r\geq0).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The numerator-cover theorem over $R$ produces positive exceptional weights,
an integer $N>0$ and an SNC ideal $K=\mathcal O_Y(-D)$ with
$\lfloor D/N\rfloor=\lfloor qE\rfloor$, an SNC presentation
$D=\sum_i b_iF_i$, and auxiliary sections as in the canonical trace input with
denominator $N$ of $1/N$. The adapted cover $p:Y'\to Y$ is finite, $Y'$ is
smooth of relative dimension $n$ over $k$, and the rounded cover line $P$ on
$Y'$ is ample over $\operatorname{Spec}R$ via $Y'\to Y\to X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$.

The composite $Y'\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is separated: $p$ is finite, hence
affine, $\mu$ is proper and $f$ is separated. Relative Kodaira vanishing
over $R$ gives $H^{r+1}(Y',\omega_{Y'/k}\otimes P)=0$. The scheme $Y$ is
quasi-compact, because $\mu$ is proper and $f$ is quasi-compact, and separated,
because $\mu$ and $f$ are; choose a finite affine open cover of $Y$. Since $p$
is affine and $\omega_{Y'/k}\otimes P$ is invertible, hence quasicoherent,
affine pushforward gives
$H^{r+1}(Y,p_*(\omega_{Y'/k}\otimes P))=0$.

Put $A=\mu^*M\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-\lfloor D/N\rfloor)$, an invertible module,
and let $a_i<N$ be the fractional residues of the $b_i$, so that $P$ is the
whole rounded line of $A$ with residues $(a_i)$. The twisted bounded canonical
unit $j:\omega_{Y/k}\otimes A\to p_*(\omega_{Y'/k}\otimes P)$ composed with the
inclusion into $p_*\omega_{Y'/k}\otimes A$ equals the twist by $A$ of
$\eta\,;\,p_*(dp)$. The canonical trace input gives $T$ with
$\eta\,;\,p_*(dp)\,;\,T=\deg p\cdot\mathrm{id}$, and $\deg p$ acts invertibly
because it is a nonzero integer in characteristic zero. Hence $j$ has a
retraction, the twist by $A$ of the inclusion followed by $T$ and by
$(\deg p)^{-1}$, transported through the projection formula. Cohomology
vanishing passes to retracts, so $H^{r+1}(Y,\omega_{Y/k}\otimes A)=0$.

Finally $\lfloor D/N\rfloor=\lfloor qE\rfloor$ gives
$Q_q\otimes\mu^*M\simeq\omega_{Y/k}\otimes A$ after rearranging the tensor
factors, which proves the vanishing.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Twisting the rational ample source]
\label{lem:kn-relative-nadel-twisted-source-data}
\uses{lem:kn-original-rational-ample-twist-data}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelAssembly.relativeTwistedSourceData}
\leanok
Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $X$ integral over $\operatorname{Spec}R$, $M$
invertible, $C$ and $H$ ample over $\operatorname{Spec}R$, $d>0$, and
$M^{\otimes d}\simeq C\otimes B^{\otimes t}$. For every $m\geq1$ the line
$M\otimes H^{\otimes m}$ is invertible, $C\otimes H^{\otimes md}$ is ample over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$, and
$(M\otimes H^{\otimes m})^{\otimes d}\simeq
 (C\otimes H^{\otimes md})\otimes B^{\otimes t}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A tensor product of invertible modules is invertible. Since $md>0$, the power
$H^{\otimes md}$ of the ample line $H$ is ample, and the tensor product of two
lines ample over $\operatorname{Spec}R$ is ample over $\operatorname{Spec}R$.
The isomorphism is
$(M\otimes H^{\otimes m})^{\otimes d}\simeq M^{\otimes d}\otimes H^{\otimes md}
 \simeq C\otimes B^{\otimes t}\otimes H^{\otimes md}$, followed by the symmetry
exchanging $B^{\otimes t}$ and $H^{\otimes md}$. The argument is the one over a
field and uses no property of the base ring.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The global twist family of the source]
\label{lem:kn-relative-nadel-source-global-twists}
\uses{thm:kn-relative-nadel-source-vanishing,lem:kn-relative-nadel-twisted-source-data,lem:kn-original-rounded-source-twist-isomorphism,lem:actual-cohomology-retract-vanishing}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelAssembly.relativeSource_globalTwists_of_coverTrace}
\leanok
Under the hypotheses of the preceding theorem, let $H$ be ample over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$. Then for every $r\geq0$ and every $m\geq1$,
\[
 H^{r+1}\bigl(Y,\;(Q_q\otimes\mu^*M)\otimes(\mu^*H)^{\otimes m}\bigr)=0 .
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix $m\geq1$. By the twisting lemma, $M\otimes H^{\otimes m}$, the ample
numerator $C\otimes H^{\otimes md}$, the same $B$, the same generating sections
of $I\otimes B$ and the same $q,d,t$ satisfy the hypotheses of the source
vanishing theorem, which gives
$H^{r+1}(Y,Q_q\otimes\mu^*(M\otimes H^{\otimes m}))=0$. Since $\mu^*$ is
monoidal and commutes with tensor powers,
$Q_q\otimes\mu^*(M\otimes H^{\otimes m})\simeq
 (Q_q\otimes\mu^*M)\otimes(\mu^*H)^{\otimes m}$, and cohomology vanishing is
invariant under isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Relative rational-ample multiplier vanishing]
\label{thm:kn-relative-nadel-multiplier-vanishing}
\uses{thm:kn-relative-nadel-source-vanishing,lem:kn-relative-nadel-source-global-twists,lem:kn-relative-nadel-twisted-multiplier,lem:fc-original-very-ample-is-ample,thm:projective-acyclic-original-pushforward-higher-vanishing,lem:actual-cohomology-retract-vanishing}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelAssembly.relativeMultiplierVanishing_of_coverTrace}
\leanok
Assume the canonical trace input for $k$, that $R$ is of finite type over $k$,
and that $f$ is separated and of finite type. Let $A,B$ be invertible, let
$I\otimes B$ be generated by finitely many global sections, let $q\geq0$ be
rational and $d>0$, $t\geq0$ integers with $dq=t$, and let
$A^{\otimes d}\simeq C\otimes B^{\otimes t}$ with $C$ ample over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$. Then
\[
 H^{r+1}\bigl(X,\;(\omega_{X/k}\otimes A)\otimes\mathcal J(q\cdot I)\bigr)=0
 \qquad(r\geq0).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $\mathcal F=Q_q\otimes\mu^*A$; it is invertible, hence quasicoherent, and
$\mu_*\mathcal F$ is quasicoherent by the multiplier lemma. Since $C$ is ample
over $\operatorname{Spec}R$, some positive power of $C$ is the pullback of
$\mathcal O(1)$ along a closed immersion $j:X\to\mathbb P^s_R$ over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$. The line $H=j^*\mathcal O(1)$ is very ample, hence
ample, over $\operatorname{Spec}R$. The source vanishing theorem gives
$H^{r+1}(Y,\mathcal F)=0$, and the global twist lemma gives the vanishing of
$H^{r+1}(Y,\mathcal F\otimes(\mu^*H)^{\otimes m})$ for all $r\geq0$ and
$m\geq1$. The projective Leray--\v{C}ech comparison for the proper $\mu$ and
the closed immersion $j$ therefore identifies the vanishing of
$H^{r+1}(Y,\mathcal F)$ with that of $H^{r+1}(X,\mu_*\mathcal F)$. The
multiplier lemma transports this vanishing to
$(\omega_{X/k}\otimes A)\otimes\mathcal J(q\cdot I)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Relative ample Nadel vanishing from the canonical trace]
\label{thm:kn-relative-ample-nadel-of-cover-trace}
\uses{thm:kn-relative-nadel-multiplier-vanishing,lem:kn-relative-nadel-principalization,def:c0n-relative-ample-nadel-input,def:kn-relative-nadel-cover-trace-input}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.Vanishing.RelativeNadelAssembly.relativeAmpleNadelVanishingInput_of_coverTrace}
\leanok
If the canonical trace input holds for $k$, then the relative ample Nadel input
holds for $k$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the data of the relative ample Nadel input: $\pi:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$
projective with $f$ smooth of relative dimension $n$ and $X$ integral,
invertible $A,B$, a finite generating family of $I\otimes B$, $c\geq0$ with
$dc=t$, and $A^{\otimes d}\simeq C\otimes B^{\otimes t}$ with $C$ ample over
$\operatorname{Spec}R$. By the principalization lemma, $f$ is separated and of
finite type. The multiplier vanishing theorem with $q=c$ gives the vanishing
of $H^{r+1}(X,(\omega_{X/k}\otimes A)\otimes\mathcal J(c\cdot I))$ for every
$r\geq0$, which is the asserted statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The canonical trace input is the only hypothesis of the last theorem. It is a
statement about the finite flat adapted covers of smooth $k$-schemes, with
no cohomology; it is not proved in this chapter, so the chapter does not by
itself establish Nadel vanishing.
\end{remark}
