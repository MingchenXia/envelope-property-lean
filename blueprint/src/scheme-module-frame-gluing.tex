\section{Original frame comparisons with a common transition}

\begin{lemma}[A common frame transition cancels]
\label{lem:scheme-original-common-frame-transition}
\uses{thm:scheme-original-module-isomorphism-gluing}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleFrameGluing.frameComparison,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleFrameGluing.frameComparison_eq_of_common_transition,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleFrameGluing.frameComparison_restrict}
\leanok
If two pairs of original frames of two module sheaves differ by the same
endomorphism of the original structure module, their coefficient-preserving
comparison isomorphisms are equal. Restricting this comparison to a
smaller open gives the comparison of the restricted original frames.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write the two frame pairs as $(e,g)$ and $(e',g')$ and the common
transition as $c$, so $e=e'c$ and $g=g'c$. Postcompose the comparison
$eg^{-1}$ and $e'(g')^{-1}$ with the monomorphism $g$. The first
composite is $e$, and the second is $e'(g')^{-1}g=e'c=e$. Hence the
comparisons agree. The equivalence between modules on the over-site
of an open and modules on its original open subscheme transports this
same equality. For restriction, the original over-site restriction
functor preserves both compositions and inverses; the unit comparison
inserted after the first restricted frame cancels its inverse before
the second frame. The proved actual frame-restriction identity therefore
gives exactly the comparison of the restricted original frames.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Matching original frame transitions construct a whole isomorphism]
\label{thm:scheme-original-frame-gluing}
\uses{lem:scheme-original-common-frame-transition,thm:scheme-original-module-isomorphism-gluing}
\lean{EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleFrameGluing.glueOfFrames,EnvelopeProperty.SchemeModuleFrameGluing.glueOfFrames_restrict}
\leanok
Suppose two original module sheaves have actual frames on an actual open
cover. If on every overlap both original frame systems have the same
transition endomorphism, their coefficient-preserving local comparisons
glue to an actual whole module isomorphism. Every restriction of this
isomorphism is the original coefficient-preserving comparison.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On each cover member compare the original frames by composing the first
frame with the inverse of the second. On an overlap restriction is
exactly the comparison of the original restricted frames. Their common
transition cancels by the preceding lemma, so the two original local
comparisons agree. The proved isomorphism gluing theorem constructs the
whole original isomorphism and identifies its restriction with every
prescribed original local comparison.
\end{proof}
